# False Cognates: Nouns, Adjectives and Adverbs

These Spanish words resemble English words, but the tempting translation is wrong in this context.

## Actual / Actual

**El presidente actual asumió el cargo en agosto.**

❌ The actual president took office in August.
✅ The current president took office in August.

English **actual** means **real** or **verdadero**:

**El costo real fue mayor que el estimado.**
✅ The actual cost was higher than the estimate.

## Actualmente / Actually

**Actualmente trabajo desde casa.**

❌ I actually work from home.
✅ I currently work from home.

English **actually** normally means **en realidad**:

**En realidad, la reunión es mañana.**
✅ Actually, the meeting is tomorrow.

## Sensible / Sensible

**Mi piel es sensible al frío.**

❌ My skin is sensible to cold.
✅ My skin is sensitive to cold.

English **sensible** means **sensato** or **razonable**:

**Esperar parece una decisión sensata.**
✅ Waiting seems like a sensible decision.

## Familiar / Familiar

**Un familiar me acompañó al hospital.**

❌ A familiar accompanied me to the hospital.
✅ A relative accompanied me to the hospital.

English **familiar** means **conocido** or **reconocible**:

**Esa canción me resulta conocida.**
✅ That song sounds familiar to me.

## Pariente / Parent

**Un pariente vino a visitarnos desde Cali.**

❌ A parent came to visit us from Cali.
✅ A relative came to visit us from Cali.

English **parent** means **padre** or **madre**:

**Cada padre o madre debe firmar el formulario.**
✅ Each parent must sign the form.

## Librería / Library

**Compré el libro en una librería del centro.**

❌ I bought the book at a downtown library.
✅ I bought the book at a downtown bookstore.

English **library** means **biblioteca**:

**Pedí prestado el libro en la biblioteca.**
✅ I borrowed the book from the library.

## Colegio / College

**Mi hija estudia en un colegio bilingüe.**

❌ My daughter attends a bilingual college.
✅ My daughter attends a bilingual school.

English **college** refers to a higher-education institution:

**Mi hermano estudia ingeniería en una universidad.**
✅ My brother studies engineering in college.

## Culto / Cult

**La profesora es una persona muy culta.**

❌ The teacher is a very cult person.
✅ The teacher is a very cultured person.

The noun **culto** can mean **cult** in a religious context:

**El documental analiza un culto secreto.**
✅ The documentary examines a secret cult.

## Conductor / Conductor

**El conductor estacionó el carro.**

❌ The conductor parked the car.
✅ The driver parked the car.

English **conductor** commonly refers to the person who directs an orchestra or works on a train:

**El director de orquesta levantó la batuta.**
✅ The conductor raised the baton.

## Paisaje / Passage

**El paisaje cambia durante la temporada de lluvias.**

❌ The passage changes during the rainy season.
✅ The landscape changes during the rainy season.

English **passage** means **pasaje**:

**Leí un pasaje del libro.**
✅ I read a passage from the book.

## Efectivo / Effective

**Necesito pagar en efectivo.**

❌ I need to pay in effective.
✅ I need to pay in cash.

As an adjective, **efectivo** can mean **effective**:

**El tratamiento fue efectivo.**
✅ The treatment was effective.

## Argumento / Argument

When **argumento** means the storyline of a movie, book, or play, English uses **plot**, not **argument**:

**El argumento de la película es simple.**

❌ The argument of the movie is simple.
✅ The plot of the movie is simple.

English **argument** can mean a disagreement or a reasoned case. Depending on the context, Spanish may use **discusión** or **argumento**.

The technical programming use is a true cognate:

**La función recibe dos argumentos.**
✅ The function takes two arguments.
