# **Disponible** → **Available** and **Free**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| disponible | available / free |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Diana está disponible esta tarde. | Diana is available this afternoon. |
| La sala está disponible y el profesor está libre. | The room is available, and the teacher is free. |

**Disponible** usually becomes **available** for a person, place, resource, or time. In a scheduling context, **free** is also natural.
