# Spanish **Entrar**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Entró a la oficina. | She entered the office. / She went into the office. |
| Entra, por favor. | Come in, please. |
| El sofá no entra por la puerta. | The sofa doesn't fit through the door. |
| Entró a trabajar a las ocho. | She started work at eight. |
| No quiero entrar en esa discusión. | I don't want to get into that argument. |

**Entrar** can become **enter, go/come in, fit, start**, or **get into**, depending on the construction.
