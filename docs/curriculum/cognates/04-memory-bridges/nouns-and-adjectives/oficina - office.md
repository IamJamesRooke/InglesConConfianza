# Noun Cognates: Shorter English Forms

These related English nouns are shorter than their Spanish equivalents. Learn each pair as a memory bridge rather than as a complete letter-by-letter rule.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| oficina | **office** | **La oficina queda en el segundo piso del edificio.**<br>The office is on the second floor of the building. |
| escuela | **school** | **La escuela organiza actividades para las familias.**<br>The school organizes activities for families. |

## False Cognates

In Colombian Spanish, **colegio** normally means **school**, not **college**. English **college** refers to a higher-education institution.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Mi hija estudia en un colegio bilingüe.** | My daughter attends a bilingual **school**. |
| **Mi hermano estudia ingeniería en una universidad.** | My brother studies engineering in **college**. |
