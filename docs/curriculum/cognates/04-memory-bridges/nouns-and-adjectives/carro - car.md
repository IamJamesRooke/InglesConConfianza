# Noun Cognates: Shorter English Forms

These English nouns are shorter than their related Spanish forms. Learn each pair as a memory bridge rather than as one complete spelling rule.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| carro | **car** | **Dejé el carro en el parqueadero del edificio.**<br>I left the car in the building's parking lot. |
| costo | **cost** | **El costo del servicio incluye los impuestos.**<br>The cost of the service includes taxes. |
| banco | **bank** | **El banco cierra a las cuatro de la tarde.**<br>The bank closes at four in the afternoon. |
| aeropuerto | **airport** | **El taxi nos llevó al aeropuerto temprano.**<br>The taxi took us to the airport early. |
