# Verb Cognates: **-ate**

Many Spanish verbs ending in **-ar** correspond to English verbs ending in **-ate**. Some related verbs, such as **traducir**, follow the same English ending without the Spanish **-ar** pattern.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| crear | **to create** | **Queremos crear un curso práctico para adultos.**<br>We want to create a practical course for adults. |
| comunicar | **to communicate** | **Los equipos deben comunicar sus decisiones con claridad.**<br>The teams must communicate their decisions clearly. |
| estimar | **to estimate** | **El arquitecto va a estimar el costo de la obra.**<br>The architect is going to estimate the cost of the project. |
| negociar | **to negotiate** | **La empresa quiere negociar un precio más bajo.**<br>The company wants to negotiate a lower price. |
| inmigrar | **to immigrate** | **Mis abuelos decidieron inmigrar a Colombia en 1950.**<br>My grandparents decided to immigrate to Colombia in 1950. |
| acelerar | **to accelerate** | **La inversión puede acelerar el crecimiento de la empresa.**<br>The investment can accelerate the company's growth. |
| traducir | **to translate** | **Laura va a traducir el contrato al inglés.**<br>Laura is going to translate the contract into English. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| participar | **to participate** | **Todos los estudiantes pueden participar en la actividad.**<br>All the students can participate in the activity. |
| celebrar | **to celebrate** | **Vamos a celebrar el cumpleaños de Ana este sábado.**<br>We are going to celebrate Ana's birthday this Saturday. |
| decorar | **to decorate** | **Ellos van a decorar el salón para la fiesta.**<br>They are going to decorate the room for the party. |
| eliminar | **to eliminate** | **Este filtro ayuda a eliminar las impurezas del agua.**<br>This filter helps eliminate impurities from the water. |
| operar | **to operate** | **Solo el personal capacitado puede operar esta máquina.**<br>Only trained personnel can operate this machine. |
| liberar | **to liberate** | **El ejército logró liberar la ciudad del control enemigo.**<br>The army managed to liberate the city from enemy control. |
