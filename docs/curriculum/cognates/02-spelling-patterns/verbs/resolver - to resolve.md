# Verb Cognates: **-olve**

In these cognates, Spanish verbs ending in **-olver** correspond to English verbs ending in **-olve**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| resolver | **to resolve** | **Los técnicos esperan resolver el problema esta tarde.**<br>The technicians hope to resolve the problem this afternoon. |
| disolver | **to dissolve** | **Debes disolver el polvo en agua caliente.**<br>You must dissolve the powder in hot water. |
| absolver | **to absolve** | **El tribunal decidió absolver al acusado por falta de pruebas.**<br>The court decided to absolve the defendant because of insufficient evidence. |
