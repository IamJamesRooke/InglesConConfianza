# Cognates: **-acto → -act**

Remove the final **-o** from Spanish **-acto** to find English **-act**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| acto | **act** | **El acto de apertura comienza a las nueve.**<br>The opening act begins at nine. |
| exacto | **exact** | **Necesitamos el número exacto de participantes.**<br>We need the exact number of participants. |
| impacto | **impact** | **La medida tuvo un impacto positivo en la comunidad.**<br>The measure had a positive impact on the community. |
| contacto | **contact** | **Marta es nuestro contacto principal en la empresa.**<br>Marta is our main contact at the company. |
| contrato | **contract** | **El abogado revisó el contrato antes de firmarlo.**<br>The lawyer reviewed the contract before signing it. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| abstracto | **abstract** | **El concepto parece abstracto sin un ejemplo.**<br>The concept seems abstract without an example. |
| compacto | **compact** | **Compramos un escritorio compacto para la oficina.**<br>We bought a compact desk for the office. |
| intacto | **intact** | **El paquete llegó intacto después del viaje.**<br>The package arrived intact after the journey. |

For Spanish **exacto / exacta**, everyday English normally uses **exact**; **precise** emphasizes exact measurement or wording. **Necesito la hora exacta. → I need the exact time.**
