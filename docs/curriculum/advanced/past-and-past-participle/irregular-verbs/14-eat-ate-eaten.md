# **Eat → Ate → Eaten, Beat → Beat → Beaten**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| eat | ate | eaten | comer |
| beat | beat | beaten | golpear / vencer |

**Eaten** and **beaten** rhyme even though their past forms do not.

**We ate before our team beat the rival; we have eaten, and they have been beaten.**
