# **Said, Bled, Bred, Fed, Fled, Led, Sped**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| say | said | said | decir |
| bleed | bled | bled | sangrar |
| breed | bred | bred | criar |
| feed | fed | fed | alimentar |
| flee | fled | fled | huir |
| lead | led | led | liderar / guiar |
| speed | sped | sped | acelerar |

All target forms rhyme with **red**. Written **said** does not rhyme with **paid**.

**She said the farmer bred and fed the horses before they fled.**
