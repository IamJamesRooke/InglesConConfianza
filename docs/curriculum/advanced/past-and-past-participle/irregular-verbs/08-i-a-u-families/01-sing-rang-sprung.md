# **Sing–Sang–Sung, Ring–Rang–Rung, Spring–Sprang–Sprung**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| sing | sang | sung | cantar |
| ring | rang | rung | sonar / llamar |
| spring | sprang | sprung | brotar / saltar |

Every column rhymes, and every row follows **i–a–u**.

**The bell rang after I had rung it, and everyone sang a song they had sung before.**
