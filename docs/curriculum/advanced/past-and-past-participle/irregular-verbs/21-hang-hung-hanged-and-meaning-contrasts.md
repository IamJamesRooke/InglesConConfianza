# **Hang → Hung/Hanged**: Meaning Changes the Form

| Meaning | Past = participle | Example |
|---|---|---|
| colgar una cosa | hung | **We hung the picture yesterday.** |
| ejecutar en la horca | hanged | **The prisoner was hanged.** |

Other contrasts to protect:

- **lie → lay → lain** means *acostarse*: **She has lain down**.
- **lay → laid → laid** needs an object and means *poner*: **She has laid the book down**.
- **bear → bore → born** refers to birth; **borne** refers to carrying or enduring.
- **bid → bid → bid** means offering a price; literary **bid → bade → bidden** means commanding or saying farewell.
- **shine → shone → shone** normally describes emitted light; **shined** is usual when someone polishes something: **She shined the shoes**.

These are meaning distinctions, not interchangeable spellings.
