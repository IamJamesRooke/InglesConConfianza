# **Take → Took → Taken**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| forsake | forsook | forsaken | abandonar |
| mistake | mistook | mistaken | confundir / equivocarse |
| shake | shook | shaken | sacudir |
| take | took | taken | tomar / llevar |

Compounds inherit **take → took → taken**: **overtake, retake, undertake**.

**I mistook the bottle she had taken for the one she had shaken.**
