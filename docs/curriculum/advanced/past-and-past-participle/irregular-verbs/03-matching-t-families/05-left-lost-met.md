# **Left, Lost, Met**: Three One-Offs

These do not rhyme, so treat them as three isolated micro-patterns.

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| leave | left | left | salir / dejar |
| lose | lost | lost | perder |
| meet | met | met | conocer / encontrarse |

Do not chant these as one family. Review each row independently.
