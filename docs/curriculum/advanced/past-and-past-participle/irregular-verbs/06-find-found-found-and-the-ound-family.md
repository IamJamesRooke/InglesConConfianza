# **Find → Found → Found** and the **-ound** Family

| Base | Past = participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| bind | bound | atar |
| find | found | encontrar |
| grind | ground | moler |
| wind `/waɪnd/` | wound `/waʊnd/` | enrollar |

The target forms rhyme. Do not confuse past **wound** `/waʊnd/` with the noun **wound** `/wuːnd/`, una herida.

**I found the cable wound around the machine that had ground the coffee.**
