# **Help → Helped**

After final `/p/`, **-ed** is pronounced `/t/`:

**help → helped**

| Course source | Base | Past and past participle | Spanish bridge |
|---|---|---|---|
| core verb | help | helped | ayudó / ayudado |
| core verb | stop | stopped | paró / parado |
| cognate reinforcement | develop | developed | desarrolló / desarrollado |

- Past: Laura **helped** the team and **stopped** at five.
- Past participle: Laura has **helped** the team and has **stopped** for the day.

**Stop** doubles its final consonant in writing, but both forms still attach the sound `/t/`: help + `/t/`, stop + `/t/`.
