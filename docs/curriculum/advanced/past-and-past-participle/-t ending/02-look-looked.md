# **Look → Looked**

After final `/k/`, **-ed** is pronounced `/t/`:

**look → looked**

| Course source | Base | Past and past participle | Spanish bridge |
|---|---|---|---|
| core verb | look | looked | miró / mirado |
| core verb | ask | asked | preguntó / preguntado |
| core verb | walk | walked | caminó / caminado |
| core verb | talk | talked | habló / hablado |
| core verb | work | worked | trabajó / trabajado |
| core verb | like | liked | gustó / gustado |

- Past: Laura **looked** at the schedule and **asked** about the meeting.
- Past participle: Laura has **looked** at the schedule and has **asked** about the meeting.

The final `/k/` may be written **k, ke**, or **-lk/-rk** in these words, but the ending is always `/t/`.
