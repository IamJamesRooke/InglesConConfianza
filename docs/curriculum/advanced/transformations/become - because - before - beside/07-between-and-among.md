# **Between** and **Among**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El banco está entre la farmacia y el café, y Ana está entre la multitud. | The bank is between the pharmacy and the café, and Ana is among the crowd. |

Use **between** for distinct, individually identifiable people, things, or points. Use **among** when someone or something is within a group viewed collectively.

**Between** is not restricted to exactly two when the relationships remain distinct: **an agreement between three companies**.
