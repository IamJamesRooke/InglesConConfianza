# **Become, Because, Before**, and **Beside**

These high-frequency words begin with **be-**, but their modern meanings are not reliably produced by adding one reusable prefix. Learn each complete word and its sentence pattern.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Become**](01-become.md) | Se convirtió en gerente. | She became a manager. |
| [**Because**](02-because.md) | Se fue porque estaba cansada. | She left because she was tired. |
| [**Before**](03-before.md) | Llámeme antes de la reunión. | Call me before the meeting. |
| [**Beside** and **Besides**](04-beside-and-besides.md) | Siéntese a mi lado; además, necesito hablar con usted. | Sit beside me; besides, I need to speak with you. |
| [**Behind**](05-behind.md) | El jardín queda detrás de la casa. | The garden is behind the house. |
| [**Below**](06-below.md) | La tienda queda debajo de la oficina. | The store is below the office. |
| [**Between** and **Among**](07-between-and-among.md) | El banco está entre la farmacia y el café. | The bank is between the pharmacy and the café. |
| [**Beyond**](08-beyond.md) | El pueblo queda más allá de las montañas. | The town is beyond the mountains. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Antes de convertirse en gerente, se sentaba entre nosotros porque su oficina quedaba detrás de la nuestra. | Before she became a manager, she sat between us because her office was behind ours. |
