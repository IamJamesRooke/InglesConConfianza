# Difficult → More Difficult → the Most Difficult

Most longer adjectives keep their spelling and add **more** or **the most**:

| Base | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|
| difficult | more difficult | the most difficult |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La segunda tarea es difícil. | The second task is difficult. |
| La segunda tarea es más difícil que la primera. | The second task is more difficult than the first one. |
| Esta es la tarea más difícil del curso. | This is the most difficult task in the course. |

Do not combine the two comparison patterns: use **more difficult**, not *difficulter* or *more difficulter*.
