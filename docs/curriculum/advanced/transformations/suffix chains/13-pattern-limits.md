# When the Pattern Is Not Mechanical

Suffixes help predict meaning, but English does not accept every imaginable combination.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El consejo no tiene precio, pero la factura sí muestra un precio. | The advice is priceless, but the bill does show a price. |

**Priceless** does not usually mean merely “without a price”; it means extremely valuable. Other forms may be understandable but rare, such as **meaningfulness** and **weakener**.

Use three checks:

1. Is the resulting word established?
2. Does its meaning remain transparent?
3. Is it the natural choice in this context?

The transformation is a memory bridge, not a guarantee.
