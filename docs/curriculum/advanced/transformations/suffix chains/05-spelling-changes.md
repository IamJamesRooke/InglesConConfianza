# Spelling Changes Before a Suffix

Some bases change spelling when a suffix is added.

| Base | Transformation | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **happy** | **happiness** | final **y → i** before **-ness** |
| **beauty** | **beautiful** | final **y → i** before **-ful** |
| **use** | **useful / useless** | keep the final **e** |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La belleza del diseño contribuye a su utilidad y a nuestra satisfacción. | The design's beauty contributes to its usefulness and our happiness. |

These are patterns for established words, not permission to attach every suffix to every base.
