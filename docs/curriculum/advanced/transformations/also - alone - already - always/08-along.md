# **Along**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Caminamos a lo largo del río. | We walked along the river. |

**Along** follows the length or course of something. With people, **come along** can mean accompany someone or make progress.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Acompáñenos; el proyecto avanza bien. | Come along with us; the project is coming along well. |
