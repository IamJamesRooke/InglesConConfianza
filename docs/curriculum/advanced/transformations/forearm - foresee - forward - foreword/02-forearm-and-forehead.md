# **Forearm** and **Forehead**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Se golpeó el antebrazo y se tocó la frente. | He bumped his forearm and touched his forehead. |

**Forearm** names the part between the elbow and wrist. **Forehead** names the part of the face above the eyes.

The spelling preserves **fore-**, but the pronunciation of **forehead** is commonly `/ˈfɔːr.hed/` or `/ˈfɒr.ɪd/`, depending on accent.
