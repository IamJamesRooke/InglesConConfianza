# **Foremost, Foreground**, and **Forefather**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| En primer plano aparece uno de los antepasados más importantes de la familia. | One of the family's foremost forefathers appears in the foreground. |

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **foremost** | most important or most prominent |
| **foreground** | the front part of a scene or image |
| **forefather** | an ancestor, especially from an earlier generation or tradition |

These words preserve a front/earlier picture but have established meanings that must be learned.
