# **Onward** and **Onwards**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Después de una pausa breve, seguimos adelante. | After a short break, we continued onward. |

**Onward/onwards** means continuing forward from a point in space or time.

Use **onward** before a noun: **the onward journey**. For direction after a verb, both **onward** and **onwards** are standard.
