# **Toward** and **Towards**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Caminamos hacia la estación, pero no llegamos. | We walked toward the station, but we didn't arrive. |

**Toward/towards** presents a direction and does not guarantee arrival. **To** normally presents the destination as the endpoint.

Both forms are standard. **Toward** is more common in American English, while **towards** is more common in British English.
