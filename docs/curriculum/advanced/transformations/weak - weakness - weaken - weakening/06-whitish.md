# **White → Whitish**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El cielo se volvió de un color blanquecino antes del amanecer. | The sky turned whitish before dawn. |

Add **-ish** to mean somewhat, approximately, or tending toward the color white.

**Whitish** does not mean completely white. Depending on tone and context, natural Spanish includes **blanquecino, blancuzco**, or **medio blanco**.

The final **e** disappears: **white + -ish → whitish**, not *whiteish*.
