# **White Out** and **Whiteout**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tape el número incorrecto con corrector y escriba el nuevo. | White out the incorrect number and write the new one. |

The verb **white out** means cover writing with white correction material. As a verb, it is written as two words.

The noun **whiteout** can name extremely poor visibility caused by snow or a white correction product:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La ventisca produjo una pérdida total de visibilidad. | The snowstorm caused a whiteout. |

Brand names and regional preferences vary for correction products; **correction fluid** is the transparent general term.
