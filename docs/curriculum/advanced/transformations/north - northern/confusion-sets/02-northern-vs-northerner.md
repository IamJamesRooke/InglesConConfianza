# **Northern** vs **Northerner**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Es una escritora norteña, pero no es del norte. | She's a northern writer, but she isn't a northerner. |

**Northern** describes a thing or person associated with the north. A **northerner** is a person from or living in the north.

The parallel person words are **southerner, easterner**, and **westerner**:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La guía es del sur y los visitantes son del oeste. | The guide is a southerner, and the visitors are westerners. |
