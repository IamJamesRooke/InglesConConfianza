# **Southern**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Exploraron la región sur del país. | They explored the southern region of the country. |

**Southern** describes a place, part, climate, culture, or other thing associated with the south. Depending on context, it can mean **del sur, meridional**, or **sureño**.

Use **south** for the direction itself and **southern** before the thing being described.
