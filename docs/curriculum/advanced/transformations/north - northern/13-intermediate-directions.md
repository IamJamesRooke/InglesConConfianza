# Intermediate Directions

The four intermediate directions combine two cardinal directions.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Bogotá queda al sureste de Medellín, y Bucaramanga queda al noreste de Bogotá. | Bogotá is southeast of Medellín, and Bucaramanga is northeast of Bogotá. |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| noreste | **northeast** |
| noroeste | **northwest** |
| sureste | **southeast** |
| suroeste | **southwest** |

The descriptive forms add **-ern** to the complete direction: **northeastern, northwestern, southeastern**, and **southwestern**.
