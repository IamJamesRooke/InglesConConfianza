# **Southward** and **Southwards**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Las aves volaron hacia el sur durante el invierno. | The birds flew southward during the winter. |

**Southward/southwards** means in the direction of the south. The shorter **south** is also natural: **The birds flew south**.

Use **southward** before a noun: **the southward route**. For direction after a verb, both **southward** and **southwards** are standard.

See the productive [**-ward/-wards** transformation](../to - towards/README.md).
