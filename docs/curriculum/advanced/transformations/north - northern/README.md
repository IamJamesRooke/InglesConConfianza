# **North → Northern** and the Cardinal-Direction Family

Each cardinal direction supports three useful forms: the direction itself, an **-ern** description, and a **-ward(s)** direction of movement.

| Base direction | Descriptive form | Movement form |
|---|---|---|
| [**north**](01-north.md) | [**northern**](02-northern.md) | [**northward(s)**](03-northward-and-northwards.md) |
| [**south**](04-south.md) | [**southern**](05-southern.md) | [**southward(s)**](06-southward-and-southwards.md) |
| [**east**](07-east.md) | [**eastern**](08-eastern.md) | [**eastward(s)**](09-eastward-and-eastwards.md) |
| [**west**](10-west.md) | [**western**](11-western.md) | [**westward(s)**](12-westward-and-westwards.md) |

## Core Pattern

| Meaning | North example | Spanish bridge |
|---|---|---|
| direction or position | **north** | norte / al norte |
| relative area or origin | **northern** | del norte / septentrional / norteño |
| movement in that direction | **northward(s)** | hacia el norte |

The pattern repeats with **south/southern/southward**, **east/eastern/eastward**, and **west/western/westward**.

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La carretera del norte atraviesa la región septentrional y luego gira hacia el norte. | The north road crosses the northern region and then turns northward. |
| La tormenta del sur se desplazó hacia el este por la parte oriental del país. | The southern storm moved eastward across the eastern part of the country. |
| Viajamos hacia el oeste desde el sur y terminamos en la región occidental. | We traveled westward from the south and ended up in the western region. |
