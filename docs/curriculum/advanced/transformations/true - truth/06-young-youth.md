# **Young → Youth**

**Young** describes a person, animal, organization, or thing near the beginning of its life. **Youth** can name the stage of being young or young people as a group.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La empresa todavía es joven. | The company is still young. |
| Pasó su juventud en Bogotá. | She spent her youth in Bogotá. |
| La organización trabaja con jóvenes. | The organization works with young people. |

For ordinary individuals, **young people** is often clearer than collective **youth**. The spelling change **young → youth** is irregular and must be learned as a family.
