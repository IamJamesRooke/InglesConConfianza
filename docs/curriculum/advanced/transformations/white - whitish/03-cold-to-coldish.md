# **Cold → Coldish**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El café está algo frío y la habitación está más o menos limpia. | The coffee is coldish, and the room is clean-ish. |

In informal English, **-ish** can soften a description: somewhat, approximately, or not completely.

Speakers may attach it creatively to familiar descriptions: **tallish, smallish, okay-ish, ready-ish**. A hyphen is common when the improvised form would otherwise look unclear.

**Ish** can even stand alone as a very informal hedge:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| —¿Está listo? —Más o menos. | “Is it ready?” “Ish.” |
