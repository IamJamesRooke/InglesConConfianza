# **Six → Sixish**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Llegaré como a las seis con unas veinte personas. | I'll arrive at sixish with twentyish people. |

In informal English, add **-ish** to a time, age, or quantity to mean approximately.

| Base | Approximate form |
|---|---|
| **six** | **sixish** |
| **twenty** | **twentyish** |
| **forty** | **fortyish** |

A hyphen can improve readability, especially with numerals: **6-ish, 20-ish**. These forms are conversational, not ideal for exact schedules or formal measurements.
