# **Child → Childish**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Su curiosidad era infantil, pero su queja fue inmadura. | Her curiosity was childlike, but her complaint was childish. |

With nouns, **-ish** can mean like or characteristic of the base. The resulting meaning may become positive, negative, or specialized.

| Family | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **child → childish** | immature; often negative |
| **fool → foolish** | showing poor judgment |
| **self → selfish** | concerned mainly with oneself |
| **style → stylish** | fashionable or showing style |
| **fever → feverish** | having or resembling a fever |

Because tone changes across words, learn these as established vocabulary rather than applying **-ish** mechanically.
