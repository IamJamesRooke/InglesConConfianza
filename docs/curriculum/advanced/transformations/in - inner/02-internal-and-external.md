# **Internal** and **External**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El técnico revisó los componentes internos y los cables externos. | The technician checked the internal components and the external cables. |

**Internal** means located or happening within something. **External** means located or coming from outside it. These forms are common in technical, medical, organizational, and formal contexts.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La empresa resolvió el problema interno sin contratar ayuda externa. | The company solved the internal problem without hiring external help. |

For ordinary physical parts, **inner/outer** often sounds more natural: **the inner pocket**, **the outer wall**.
