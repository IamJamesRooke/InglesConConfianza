# The **Inner Circle** and **Outer Space**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Solo su círculo íntimo sabía que quería estudiar el espacio exterior. | Only her inner circle knew that she wanted to study outer space. |

An **inner circle** is a small, trusted, or influential group close to a person or organization. **Outer space** is the region beyond Earth's atmosphere.

Both expressions extend the core contrast between what is closer to the center and what lies beyond the outside.
