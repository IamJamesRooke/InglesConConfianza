# **All the Way**

**All the way** means for the complete distance or until the final point.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El autobús se averió, así que tuvimos que caminar todo el trayecto desde la terminal hasta el hotel. | The bus broke down, so we had to walk all the way from the terminal to the hotel. |

The named starting and ending points establish the complete-distance meaning.
