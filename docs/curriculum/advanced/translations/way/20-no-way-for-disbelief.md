# **No Way!** for Disbelief

As a reaction, **No way!** can express surprise or disbelief rather than refusal.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| —Gané dos entradas para la final. —¡No puede ser! Llevaban meses agotadas. | “I won two tickets to the final.” “No way! They had been sold out for months.” |

The sold-out event makes the news difficult to believe.
