# **Way Too**

**Way too** intensifies **too** and means that something exceeds an acceptable limit by a large amount.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Cincuenta diapositivas para una presentación de diez minutos son demasiadas; debemos eliminar por lo menos treinta. | Fifty slides for a ten-minute presentation are way too many; we need to remove at least thirty. |

The source also gives **way too easy** and **way too much**. In every case, **way** strengthens the idea of excess already expressed by **too**.
