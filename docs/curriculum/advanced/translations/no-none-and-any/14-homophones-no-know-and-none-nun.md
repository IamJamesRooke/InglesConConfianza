# Homophones: **No / Know** and **None / Nun**

**No** and **know** are homophones; **none** and **nun** are another homophone pair.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Sé que ninguna monja dijo que no. | I know that no nun said no, and none of them knew why. |

The sentence also contrasts **know** with its past form **knew**, which sounds like **new**.
