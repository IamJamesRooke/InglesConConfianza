# Pronunciation: **Less** and **Lest**

**Less** ends in `/s/`; formal **lest**, meaning to prevent something undesirable, adds `/t/`. They aren't homophones.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Lleva menos equipaje para evitar perder el último bus. | Take less luggage lest you miss the last bus. |

**Lest** is formal; everyday English uses **so you don't**.
