# When Spanish **Que** Does Not Become Connector **That**

After verbs such as **want, need, ask**, and **tell**, English often uses **person + to + base verb**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Quiero que Laura lo haga y necesito que Carlos la ayude. | I want Laura to do it, and I need Carlos to help her. |

Do not say *I want that Laura does it* in the standard pattern taught here.
