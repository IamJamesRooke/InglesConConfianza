# **That Is** and **That Is To Say**

Use **that is** or formal **that is to say** to clarify or restate something.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Nos reuniremos el próximo día hábil, es decir, el lunes. | We'll meet on the next business day—that is, Monday. |

The abbreviation **i.e.** represents Latin *id est* and means **that is**, not **for example**.
