# **That's Why, That's How**, and **That's What**

These expressions connect a result, method or identification to the previous context.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Perdimos el último bus; por eso llegamos tarde. | We missed the last bus; that's why we were late. |
| Laura convirtió el archivo a PDF; así resolvió el problema. | Laura converted the file to PDF; that's how she solved the problem. |
| Necesito una respuesta clara; eso es lo que espero recibir. | I need a clear answer; that's what I expect to receive. |

**That's** is the standard contraction of **that is** or **that has**, depending on what follows: **That's important → that is**; **That's been difficult → that has**.
