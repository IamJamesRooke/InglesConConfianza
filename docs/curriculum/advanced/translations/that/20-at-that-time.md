# **At That Time**

**At that time** means **en ese momento** or **en aquella época**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| En aquella época trabajaba de noche, así que no podía asistir a las clases. | At that time, I worked nights, so I couldn't attend the classes. |

Depending on the timeline, natural alternatives include **then**, **back then**, and **at the time**.
