# **Not That + Description**

Use **not that** to reject or lower a degree that someone expects.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El examen no estuvo tan difícil; terminé veinte minutos antes. | The exam wasn't that difficult; I finished twenty minutes early. |

The direct patterns are:

- **no tan ocupado → not that busy**
- **no tanto tráfico → not that much traffic**
- **no tantos carros → not that many cars**
