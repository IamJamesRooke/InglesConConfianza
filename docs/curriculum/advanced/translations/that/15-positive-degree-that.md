# Positive Degree **That**

In questions, conditions and comparisons, **that** can mean **tan** or **así de** by referring to a degree already understood.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿De verdad está así de lejos? Si queda tan lejos, tomemos un taxi. | Is it really that far? If it's that far, let's take a taxi. |

Informal **I didn't know it was that expensive** means the price was higher than the speaker expected.
