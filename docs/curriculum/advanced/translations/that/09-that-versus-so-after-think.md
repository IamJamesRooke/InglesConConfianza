# **That** Versus **So** After **Think**

**I think that...** introduces the idea. **I think so** replaces an idea already understood.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Creo que Laura va a ganar. | I think that Laura will win. |
| —¿Va a ganar Laura? —Creo que sí. | “Will Laura win?” “I think so.” |

**I think it's like that** means **creo que es así** when describing a manner, arrangement or process.
