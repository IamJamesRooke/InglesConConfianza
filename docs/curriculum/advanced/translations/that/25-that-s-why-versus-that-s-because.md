# **That's Why** Versus **That's Because**

**That's why** introduces a result. **That's because** introduces its cause.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El bus se averió; por eso llegamos tarde. | The bus broke down; that's why we were late. |
| Llegamos tarde porque el bus se averió. | We were late; that's because the bus broke down. |

Follow the direction of the explanation:

- cause → **that's why** → result;
- result → **that's because** → cause.
