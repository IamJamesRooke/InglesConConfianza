# **That + Thing**

Use **that** before one person or thing that is distant, already identified, or being contrasted with something nearer.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ese libro que está junto a la ventana es el que necesito. | That book by the window is the one I need. |

The basic set is:

| Distance | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| nearer | **this book** | **these books** |
| farther | **that book** | **those books** |

Distance can be physical, temporal, emotional or conversational: **that day**, **that problem**, **that idea you mentioned**.
