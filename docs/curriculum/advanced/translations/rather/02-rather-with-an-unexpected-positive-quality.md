# **Rather** With an Unexpected Positive Quality

Before a positive adjective, **rather** can show that a good quality is stronger or more surprising than expected.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Pensé que la conferencia sería aburrida, pero resultó bastante interesante y tomé cinco páginas de notas. | I thought the lecture would be boring, but it was rather interesting, and I took five pages of notes. |

The contrast with the speaker's expectation gives **rather interesting** a positive meaning.
