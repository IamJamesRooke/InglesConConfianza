# **Rather Too**

**Rather too** means that something exceeds an acceptable limit, although the criticism is expressed mildly.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ciento cincuenta dólares me parece un precio algo excesivo para una cena sin bebidas, así que busquemos otro restaurante. | One hundred and fifty dollars is rather too expensive for a dinner without drinks, so let us find another restaurant. |

Here **rather** softens the criticism while **too expensive** establishes that the price is unacceptable.
