# Uses of **Rather**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Rather** Meaning Fairly or Quite](01-rather-meaning-fairly-or-quite.md) | Es bastante importante guardar una copia del contrato porque el original puede perderse. | It is rather important to keep a copy of the contract because the original could get lost. |
| [**Rather** With an Unexpected Positive Quality](02-rather-with-an-unexpected-positive-quality.md) | Pensé que la conferencia sería aburrida, pero resultó bastante interesante y tomé cinco páginas de notas. | I thought the lecture would be boring, but it was rather interesting, and I took five pages of notes. |
| [**Rather** Before a Thought or Feeling Verb](03-rather-before-a-thought-or-feeling-verb.md) | Aunque los demás criticaron el diseño, a mí me gusta bastante porque es sencillo y fácil de usar. | Although everyone else criticized the design, I rather like it because it is simple and easy to use. |
| [**Rather Too**](04-rather-too.md) | Ciento cincuenta dólares me parece un precio algo excesivo para una cena sin bebidas, así que busquemos otro restaurante. | One hundred and fifty dollars is rather too expensive for a dinner without drinks, so let us find another restaurant. |
| [**Rather a + Adjective + Noun**](05-rather-a-plus-adjective-plus-noun.md) | Fue una sorpresa bastante agradable que todos llegaran temprano a pesar del tráfico. | It was rather a pleasant surprise that everyone arrived early despite the traffic. |
| [**Would Rather + Base Verb**](06-would-rather-plus-base-verb.md) | Prefiero revisar el contrato hoy porque mañana no tendré tiempo para leerlo con calma. | I would rather review the contract today because tomorrow I will not have time to read it carefully. |
| [**Would Rather A Than B**](07-would-rather-a-than-b.md) | Preferiría trabajar desde casa en vez de pasar tres horas diarias en el tráfico. | I would rather work from home than spend three hours in traffic every day. |
| [**Would Rather Not**](08-would-rather-not.md) | Preferiría no hablar del aumento durante la cena porque es un asunto privado. | I would rather not discuss the raise during dinner because it is a private matter. |
| [**Would You Rather...?**](09-would-you-rather.md) | ¿Preferirías presentar el informe hoy o esperar hasta que tengamos las cifras definitivas mañana? | Would you rather present the report today or wait until we have the final figures tomorrow? |
| [**Would Rather + Different Subject**](10-would-rather-plus-different-subject.md) | Preferiría que Laura dirigiera la reunión de mañana porque conoce mejor al cliente. | I would rather Laura led tomorrow's meeting because she knows the client better. |
| [**Would Rather + Different Subject + Negative**](11-would-rather-plus-different-subject-plus-negative.md) | Preferiría que ustedes no enviaran el documento todavía porque contiene cifras sin verificar. | I would rather you did not send the document yet because it contains unverified figures. |
| [**Would Rather Have + Past Participle**](12-would-rather-have-plus-past-participle.md) | Habría preferido tomar el vuelo de la mañana porque el de la noche fue cancelado. | I would rather have taken the morning flight because the evening flight was canceled. |
| [**Would Rather + Subject + Had + Past Participle**](13-would-rather-plus-subject-plus-had-plus-past-participle.md) | Habría preferido que Carlos me hubiera llamado antes de firmar porque el contrato contenía un error. | I would rather Carlos had called me before signing because the contract contained an error. |
| [**Rather Than**](14-rather-than.md) | En vez de cancelar el proyecto por falta de dinero, redujimos el alcance y terminamos las funciones esenciales. | Rather than cancel the project because of insufficient funding, we reduced its scope and completed the essential features. |
| [**Not X but Rather Y**](15-not-x-but-rather-y.md) | El retraso no fue causado por falta de esfuerzo, sino por un error en los datos del cliente. | The delay was not caused by a lack of effort but rather by an error in the client's data. |
| [**Or Rather**](16-or-rather.md) | La reunión duró dos horas, o mejor dicho, una hora y cincuenta y ocho minutos. | The meeting lasted two hours, or rather, one hour and fifty-eight minutes. |
| [Sentence-Opening **Rather**](17-sentence-opening-rather.md) | La caída de las ventas no demuestra que el producto haya fracasado; más bien, indica que necesitamos cambiar la estrategia de publicidad. | The drop in sales does not prove that the product has failed; rather, it indicates that we need to change the advertising strategy. |
