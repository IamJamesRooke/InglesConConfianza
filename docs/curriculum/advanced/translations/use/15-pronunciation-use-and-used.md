# Pronunciation: **Use, Used**, and **Used To**

The noun **use** ends in `/s/`; the verb **use** ends in `/z/`; **used to** normally has `/st/`.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Antes usaba una herramienta útil, pero ya no la uso. | I used to use a useful tool, but I don't use it anymore. |

The noun and verb spellings are identical but their final sounds differ.
