# Emphatic **Just**

Informal **just** can intensify a feeling, reaction, or command.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Simplemente me encanta esta canción, así que déjame escucharla. | I just love this song, so just lemme listen to it. |

**Lemme** represents the informal spoken reduction of **let me**; use **let me** in standard writing.
