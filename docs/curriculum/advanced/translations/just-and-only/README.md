# Uses of **Just** and **Only**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Only** Meaning No More Than](01-only-meaning-no-more-than.md) | Solo tengo diez minutos, así que dime únicamente lo importante. | I've only got ten minutes, so tell me only the important part. |
| [**Just** Meaning **Only**](02-just-meaning-only.md) | Solo necesito esto; no tienes que traer nada más. | I just need this; you don't have to bring anything else. |
| [**Just** Meaning Exactly](03-just-meaning-exactly.md) | Eso es exactamente lo que necesitaba. | That's just what I needed. |
| [**Just** for the Recent Past](04-just-recently.md) | Acabo de terminar el informe. | I've just finished the report. |
| [**Just Now**](05-just-now.md) | Laura llamó hace un momento, pero ahora está en una reunión. | Laura called just now, but she's in a meeting now. |
| [**Just Over, Just Under**, and **Just Around**](06-just-over-under-and-around.md) | La reunión duró poco más de una hora y asistieron poco menos de cien personas. | The meeting lasted just over an hour, and just under a hundred people attended. |
| [**Just in Case**](07-just-in-case.md) | Lleva un paraguas por si acaso llueve. | Take an umbrella just in case it rains. |
| [**Just Because...**](08-just-because.md) | Solo porque sea barato no significa que sea de mala calidad. | Just because it's cheap doesn't mean it's poor quality. |
| [**Just As**](09-just-as.md) | Hazlo exactamente como te mostré. | Do it just as I showed you. |
| [**Just Enough**](10-just-enough.md) | Tenemos apenas el tiempo suficiente para tomar el último bus. | We've got just enough time to catch the last bus. |
| [**Just Right**](11-just-right.md) | El café no está ni muy caliente ni muy frío; está perfecto. | The coffee isn't too hot or too cold; it's just right. |
| [**Only One** and **The Only One**](12-only-one-and-the-only-one.md) | Solo queda una entrada y Laura es la única que puede usarla. | There's only one ticket left, and Laura's the only one who can use it. |
| [**Only If**](13-only-if.md) | Firmaré únicamente si el abogado aprueba el contrato. | I'll sign only if the lawyer approves the contract. |
| [**If Only**](14-if-only.md) | Ojalá hubiera leído el contrato antes de firmarlo. | If only I'd read the contract before signing it. |
| [**Not Only...But Also**](15-not-only-but-also.md) | Laura no solo corrigió el informe, sino que también explicó el error. | Laura not only corrected the report but also explained the mistake. |
| [**Only After** and **Only Then**](16-only-after-and-only-then.md) | Solo después de leer el contrato entendí el riesgo. | Only after reading the contract did I understand the risk. |
| [**Just** Meaning Fair](17-just-meaning-fair.md) | La decisión fue justa porque todos tuvieron la misma oportunidad. | The decision was just because everyone had the same opportunity. |
| [**Justice, Fairness, Unjust**, and **Unfair**](18-justice-fairness-unjust-and-unfair.md) | La justicia exige imparcialidad, y castigarla sin escucharla sería injusto. | Justice requires fairness, and punishing her without listening would be unjust. |
| [**Only Too**](19-only-too.md) | Con mucho gusto revisaré el documento una vez más. | I'll be only too happy to review the document one more time. |
| [Pronunciation: **Just, Justice**, and **Jest**](20-pronunciation-just-justice-and-jest.md) | La justicia no es solo una broma; tiene que ser justa. | Justice isn't just a jest; it's gotta be just. |
| [**Only** Position Changes the Meaning](21-only-position-changes-the-meaning.md) | Solo Laura llamó a Carlos; Laura solo llamó a Carlos; Laura llamó únicamente a Carlos. | Only Laura called Carlos; Laura only called Carlos; Laura called only Carlos. |
| [**Just About**](22-just-about.md) | Ya casi termino y hay aproximadamente veinte personas esperando. | I've just about finished, and there are just about twenty people waiting. |
| [Emphatic **Just**](23-emphatic-just.md) | Simplemente me encanta esta canción, así que déjame escucharla. | I just love this song, so just lemme listen to it. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Solo necesito esto y acabo de encontrar exactamente lo que necesitaba. | I only need this, and I've just found just what I needed. |
| Solo porque queda una entrada no significa que Laura sea la única que pueda usarla; firmará únicamente si es justo. | Just because there's only one ticket left doesn't mean Laura's the only one who can use it; she'll sign only if it's fair. |
| La justicia no es solo una broma: debe ser justa. Ojalá tuviéramos el tiempo justo para hacer lo correcto. | Justice isn't just a jest: it's gotta be just. If only we had just enough time to do the right thing. |
