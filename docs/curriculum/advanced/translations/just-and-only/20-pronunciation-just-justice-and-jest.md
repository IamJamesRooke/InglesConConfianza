# Pronunciation: **Just, Justice**, and **Jest**

**Just** has `/ʌ/`; **jest** has `/e/`; **justice** keeps the vowel of **just**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La justicia no es solo una broma; tiene que ser justa. | Justice isn't just a jest; it's gotta be just. |

None of the three words is a homophone of another.
