# **Only** Position Changes the Meaning

Place **only** directly before the person, thing, or action being limited whenever ambiguity matters.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Solo Laura llamó a Carlos; Laura solo llamó a Carlos; Laura llamó únicamente a Carlos. | Only Laura called Carlos; Laura only called Carlos; Laura called only Carlos. |

The three sentences limit the caller, the action, and the person called. In casual speech, stress often clarifies a less exact position.
