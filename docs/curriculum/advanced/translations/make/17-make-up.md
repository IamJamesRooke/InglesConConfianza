# **Make Up**

**Make up** can invent, reconcile, or form a whole.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Inventó una excusa, luego hizo las paces con Laura y juntos formaron la mitad del equipo. | He made up an excuse, made up with Laura, and together they made up half the team. |

Cosmetics are also **makeup**, one word as a noun.
