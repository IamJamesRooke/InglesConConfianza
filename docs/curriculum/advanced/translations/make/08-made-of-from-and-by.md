# **Made Of, Made From**, and **Made By**

**Made of** keeps the visible material, **made from** names a transformed source, and **made by** identifies the creator.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La mesa está hecha de madera, el papel está hecho de madera y ambos fueron hechos por Laura. | The table's made of wood, the paper's made from wood, and both were made by Laura. |
