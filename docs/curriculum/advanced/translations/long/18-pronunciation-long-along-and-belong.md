# Pronunciation: **Long, Along**, and **Belong**

All three share the stressed ending `/lɔŋ/` or `/lɑŋ/`, depending on accent. None has a common homophone.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mientras caminábamos por el largo sendero, cantamos todo el camino para sentir que pertenecíamos allí. | As we walked along the long trail, we sang all along to feel that we belonged there. |

The repeated endings build fluency without claiming a false homophone.
