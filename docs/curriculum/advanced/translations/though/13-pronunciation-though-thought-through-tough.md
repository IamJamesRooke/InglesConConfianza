# Pronunciation: **Though, Thought, Through**, and **Tough**

These similar spellings have four different vowels or endings: **though, thought, through, tough**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Aunque pensé bien el problema difícil, lo resolví por completo. | Though I thought through the tough problem, I solved it thoroughly. |

**Though** has no common exact homophone; **thoroughly** adds another high-confusion spelling.
