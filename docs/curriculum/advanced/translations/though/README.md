# Uses of **Though**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Though** Meaning Despite the Fact](01-though-meaning-despite-the-fact.md) | Aunque estaba cansada, terminó el informe. | Though she was tired, she finished the report. |
| [**Although**](02-although.md) | Aunque el apartamento es pequeño, tiene mucha luz. | Although the apartment's small, it's got plenty of natural light. |
| [Sentence-Final **Though**](03-sentence-final-though.md) | El apartamento es pequeño. Sin embargo, tiene mucha luz. | The apartment's small. It's got plenty of natural light, though. |
| [**Even Though**](04-even-though.md) | Aunque llovió toda la mañana, jugamos el partido. | Even though it rained all morning, we played the game. |
| [**Even If**](05-even-if.md) | Incluso si llueve mañana, jugaremos el partido. | Even if it rains tomorrow, we'll play the game. |
| [**Even If** Versus **Even Though**](06-even-if-versus-even-though.md) | Aunque ya está lloviendo, saldré; incluso si empeora, no cambiaré de plan. | Even though it's already raining, I'll go out; even if it gets worse, I won't change my plans. |
| [**But...Not..., Though**](07-but-not-though.md) | Me gusta el diseño, pero no compraría ese color. | I like the design, but I wouldn't buy that color, though. |
| [**As Though**](08-as-though.md) | Habla como si supiera todo, pero ni siquiera leyó el informe. | He talks as though he knew everything, but he didn't even read the report. |
| [Description + **Though**](09-adjective-though.md) | Por difícil que fuera, no abandonamos el proyecto. | Difficult though it was, we didn't abandon the project. |
| [**Though** Versus **However**](10-though-versus-however.md) | El plazo era corto; sin embargo, terminamos. Fue estresante. | The deadline was short; however, we finished. It was stressful, though. |
| [Informal Spelling **Tho**](11-tho-in-informal-writing.md) | Fue una buena película, aunque un poco larga. | It was a good movie—kinda long, tho. |
| [**All the Same**](12-all-the-same.md) | Sé que es tarde, pero de todos modos voy a llamar. | I know it's late, but I'm going to call all the same. |
| [Pronunciation: **Though, Thought, Through**, and **Tough**](13-pronunciation-though-thought-through-tough.md) | Aunque pensé bien el problema difícil, lo resolví por completo. | Though I thought through the tough problem, I solved it thoroughly. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Aunque ya está lloviendo, saldré; incluso si empeora, no cambiaré de plan. | Even though it's already raining, I'll go out; even if it gets worse, I won't change my plans. |
| El apartamento es pequeño; sin embargo, tiene mucha luz. No lo compraría, aunque sí me gusta. | The apartment's small; however, it's bright. I wouldn't buy it, though I do like it. |
| Aunque pensé bien el problema difícil, lo resolví por completo; fue bastante agotador, eso sí. | Though I thought through the tough problem thoroughly, I solved it; it was pretty exhausting, though. |
