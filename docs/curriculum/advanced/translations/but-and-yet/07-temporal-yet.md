# Temporal **Yet**

In questions and negative statements, **yet** commonly means **ya**, **aún** or **todavía** and usually appears at the end.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| —¿Ya terminaste el informe? —No, todavía no lo he terminado. | “Have you finished the report yet?” “No, I haven't finished it yet.” |

In the question, Spanish naturally uses **ya**. In the negative answer, the same English word becomes **todavía** or **aún**.
