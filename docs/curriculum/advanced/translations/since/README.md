# Uses of **Since**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Since** With a Starting Time](01-since-with-a-starting-time.md) | Trabajo aquí desde 2022. | I've worked here since 2022. |
| [**Since** With a Complete Event](02-since-with-a-complete-event.md) | No la he visto desde que cambió de trabajo. | I haven't seen her since she changed jobs. |
| [**For** Versus **Since**](03-for-versus-since.md) | Vivo aquí desde 2020, así que llevo seis años en este barrio. | I've lived here since 2020, so I've been in this neighborhood for six years. |
| [**Ever Since**](04-ever-since.md) | Desde que tuve el accidente, siempre he tomado el bus. | I've taken the bus ever since I had the accident. |
| [**Since Then**](05-since-then.md) | Renuncié en enero y desde entonces trabajo por mi cuenta. | I quit in January, and I've worked for myself since then. |
| [**It's Been...Since...**](06-its-been-since.md) | Han pasado dos años desde que nos vimos. | It's been two years since we saw each other. |
| [**Since** Meaning **Because**](07-since-meaning-because.md) | Ya que estamos aquí, mejor esperemos un rato. | Since we're already here, we might as well wait for a while. |
| [**Since When?**](08-since-when.md) | ¿Desde cuándo te gusta levantarte a las cinco? | Since when have you liked getting up at five? |
| [**Long Since**](09-long-since.md) | Ese sistema quedó obsoleto hace mucho tiempo. | That system has long since become obsolete. |
| [**Not Since...**](10-not-since.md) | No había visto una tormenta así desde que era niña. | I hadn't seen a storm like that since I was a child. |
| [**Since the Last Time**](11-since-the-last-time.md) | El barrio ha cambiado mucho desde la última vez que vine. | The neighborhood's changed a lot since the last time I came. |
| [Homophones: **Since** and **Sins**](12-homophones-since-and-sins.md) | Desde que confesó sus pecados, no ha vuelto a mentir. | Since he confessed his sins, he hasn't lied again. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Vivo aquí desde 2020, no la veo desde que renunció y han pasado dos años desde nuestra última conversación. | I've lived here since 2020, I haven't seen her since she quit, and it's been two years since our last conversation. |
| Ya que estamos aquí desde temprano, bien podríamos esperar; después de todo, nada ha cambiado desde entonces. | Since we've been here since early this morning, we might as well wait; after all, nothing's changed since then. |
| Desde que confesó sus pecados, no ha mentido; ¿desde cuándo es tan honesto? | Since he confessed his sins, he hasn't lied; since when has he been so honest? |
