# **A Lot Of, Lots Of**, and **Plenty Of**

**A lot of** and **lots of** express a large quantity; **plenty of** means more than enough.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tenemos muchos libros y más que suficiente tiempo para revisarlos. | We've got lots of books and plenty of time to review them. |

**A pile of** is literal for a physical pile and informal exaggeration elsewhere.
