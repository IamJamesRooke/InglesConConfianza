# **Sometimes, Sometime**, and **Some Time**

**Sometimes** means occasionally, **sometime** means at an unspecified time, and **some time** means a period.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| A veces practico sola; deberíamos reunirnos algún día y dedicar algo de tiempo a practicar. | Sometimes I practice alone; we should meet sometime and spend some time practicing. |
