# **The Only One** and **The Only Thing**

Use **the only one** for the unique person or countable item and **the only thing** for one unique fact, object, action or concern.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Natalia fue la única que recordó la contraseña. | Natalia was the only one who remembered the password. |
| Lo único que necesito es acceso al documento. | The only thing I need is access to the document. |

Do not translate every Spanish **lo único** mechanically as **the only thing**; choose the noun that the context requires.
