# **Which One?** and **Which Ones?**

Use singular **which one?** to choose one item and plural **which ones?** to choose more than one.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| —¿Cuál quieres? —La pequeña. | “Which one do you want?” “The small one.” |
| —¿Cuáles quieres? —Las pequeñas. | “Which ones do you want?” “The small ones.” |

The plural marker moves to **ones**: **the small ones**, not *the smalls*.
