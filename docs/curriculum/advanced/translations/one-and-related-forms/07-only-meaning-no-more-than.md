# **Only** Meaning No More Than

Before a quantity, **only** limits it and often means **solo**, **solamente** or **apenas**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Solo queda una entrada, así que no podemos asistir los dos. | There's only one ticket left, so we can't both attend. |

The position of **only** matters: **only one ticket** limits the number of tickets.
