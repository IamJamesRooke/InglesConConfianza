# **Kind Of** Versus **A Kind Of**

Without an article, informal **kind of** can soften a description. With **a**, **a kind of + noun** identifies a category or describes something approximately as a category.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La bebida es un tipo de té fermentado, y su sabor es algo ácido. | The drink is a kind of fermented tea, and its flavor is kind of sour. |

The first **kind of** means **un tipo de**; the second means **algo**. In formal writing, prefer a more precise word such as **somewhat**, **approximately** or **a type of** when it expresses the intended meaning.
