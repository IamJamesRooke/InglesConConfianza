# **Kind Of** Meaning Somewhat

In informal English, **kind of** before an adjective or verb means **algo**, **un poco**, **más o menos** or **como que**. It makes the statement less direct or less exact.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La reunión es algo importante porque allí decidiremos el presupuesto, pero no es obligatorio asistir. | The meeting is kind of important because we will decide the budget there, but attendance is not mandatory. |

**Kind of important** normally weakens **important**. It does not reliably mean **bastante importante**, which can express a stronger degree similar to **quite important**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Más o menos entiendo la explicación, pero todavía no podría explicársela a otra persona. | I kind of understand the explanation, but I still couldn't explain it to someone else. |

The second clause shows that the speaker's understanding is incomplete.
