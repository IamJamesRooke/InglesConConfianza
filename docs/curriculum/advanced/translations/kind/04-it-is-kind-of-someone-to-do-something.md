# **It Is Kind of Someone to Do Something**

Use **it is kind of someone to + base verb** to praise a considerate action.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Fue muy amable de parte de Andrés traerme el almuerzo cuando supo que no podía salir de la oficina. | It was very kind of Andrés to bring me lunch when he learned that I could not leave the office. |

Here **of Andrés** identifies the person whose action was kind. Do not confuse this structure with the softener **kind of** below.
