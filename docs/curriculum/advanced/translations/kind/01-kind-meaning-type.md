# **Kind** Meaning Type

As a noun, **kind** means **tipo**, **clase** or **especie**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Este tipo de café crece bien en climas fríos y lluviosos. | This kind of coffee grows well in cold, rainy climates. |

Use **kind of + noun** to identify a category. In questions, **what kind of...?** asks which category something belongs to.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿Qué tipo de música escucha Natalia cuando está trabajando? | What kind of music does Natalia listen to while she is working? |

After **this** and **that**, use singular **kind**; after **these**, **those** and numbers, use plural **kinds**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La tienda vende tres tipos de pan sin gluten, pero este tipo es el más económico. | The store sells three kinds of gluten-free bread, but this kind is the least expensive. |
