# Informal Spoken **Kinda**

**Kinda** represents the informal spoken reduction of **kind of** when it means **algo**, **más o menos** or **como que**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Estoy algo cansada, así que creo que me voy a ir a casa temprano. | I'm kinda tired, so I think I'll head home early. |

The standard written form is **kind of**. Use **kinda** in casual messages or when representing relaxed speech, not in formal writing. It is an informal phonetic spelling rather than a standard contraction: **I'm** and **I'll** are standard contractions.

Do not use **kinda** when **kind** names a category. Write **this kind of music**, not *this kinda music*, in standard English.
