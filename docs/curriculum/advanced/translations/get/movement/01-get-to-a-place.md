# **Get To** a Place

Conversational **get to + place** means arrive at or reach the destination: before, the person wasn't there; afterward, they were.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Llegamos al aeropuerto justo antes de que cerraran la puerta. | We got to the airport just before they closed the gate. |

Compare **get to the airport**, **arrive at the airport**, and **reach the airport**. **Reach** takes no **to**.
