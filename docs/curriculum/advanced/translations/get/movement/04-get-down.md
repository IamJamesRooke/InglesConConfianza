# **Get Down**

As movement, **get down** can mean descend, lower yourself, or move into a crouching position.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Bájate de la mesa y agáchate para que la pelota no te golpee. | Get down from the table and get down so the ball doesn't hit you. |

Vehicle exit normally uses **get out of** or **get off**, not general **get down**.
