# **Get On** and **Get Onto** Public Transport

Use **get on/onto** for boarding a bus, train, plane, bicycle, motorcycle, or other transport you ride on or can move around inside.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Subimos al avión después de montar en el bus equivocado. | We got on the plane after getting on the wrong bus. |

**Onto** emphasizes the movement; **on** is the usual conversational choice.
