# **Get Up**

**Get up** means move from lying or sitting to an upright position. In a morning routine, it means leave the bed after waking.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me desperté a las seis, pero no me levanté de la cama hasta las seis y media. | I woke up at six, but I didn't get up until six thirty. |

**Wake up** is stop sleeping; **get up** is physically rise.
