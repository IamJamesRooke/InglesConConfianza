# **Get Off** Public Transport

Use **get off** for leaving a bus, train, plane, bicycle, or similar transport.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Nos bajamos del bus, pero olvidamos bajarnos del tren en nuestra estación. | We got off the bus, but we forgot to get off the train at our stop. |

Keep the contrast visible: **get in/out of a car** but **get on/off a bus**.
