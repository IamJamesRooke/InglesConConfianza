# **Gotten**: Past Participle

In American English, many receive and obtain meanings use **get → got → gotten** in perfect forms.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| He recibido tres mensajes y por fin he conseguido una cita. | I've gotten three messages and have finally gotten an appointment. |

British English normally uses **got** instead. American **have got** is also preserved in possession and obligation patterns, which belong to later parts of the map.
