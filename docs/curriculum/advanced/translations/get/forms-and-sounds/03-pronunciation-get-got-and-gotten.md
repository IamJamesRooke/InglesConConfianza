# Pronunciation: **Get, Got**, and **Gotten**

**Get** has `/e/`, **got** has `/ɑ/` in common American pronunciation, and **gotten** adds an unstressed final syllable. None has a common homophone.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Conseguí lo que recibí, pero todavía no he conseguido lo que quería obtener. | I got what I received, but I haven't gotten what I wanted to get yet. |

The intentionally repetitive sentence contrasts present **get**, past **got**, and participle **gotten**.
