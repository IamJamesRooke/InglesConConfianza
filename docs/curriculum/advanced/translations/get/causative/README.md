# Causative **Get**

Use causative **get** when someone causes, persuades, arranges, or ensures an action or result.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Get Someone To Do Something**](01-get-someone-to-do-something.md) | Quiero que alguien arregle la puerta, así que conseguiré que mi hermano la arregle. | I want someone to fix the door, so I'll get my brother to fix it. |
| [**Get Someone To Do** Versus **Have Someone Do**](02-get-someone-to-do-versus-have-someone-do.md) | Conseguí que Ana pintara la puerta y le pedí al técnico que arreglara la ventana. | I got Ana to paint the door, and I had the technician fix the window. |
| [Person Versus Result](03-get-someone-to-do-it-versus-get-it-done.md) | Conseguiré que alguien arregle el informe y me aseguraré de que quede listo. | I'll get someone to fix the report, and I'll get it done. |
| [**Do It, Get It Done**, and **Have It Done**](04-do-it-get-it-done-and-have-it-done.md) | Hazlo tú, asegúrate de que quede listo o mándalo a hacer. | Do it, get it done, or have it done. |
| [Finished, Sent, or Sold](05-get-or-have-something-finished-sent-or-sold.md) | Tenemos que terminar el informe, enviar el paquete y vender el carro. | We've gotta get the report finished, the package sent, and the car sold. |
| [**Did It** Versus **Got It Done**](06-did-it-versus-got-it-done.md) | Ellos vendieron el carro, pero consiguieron que un agente vendiera la casa. | They sold the car, but they got the house sold by an agent. |
| [**Make Sure I Do It** Versus **It Gets Done**](07-make-sure-i-do-it-versus-it-gets-done.md) | Me aseguraré de hacerlo yo; si no puedo, me aseguraré de que quede listo de todos modos. | I'll make sure I do it; if I can't, I'll make sure it gets done anyway. |
| [**Had Him Killed** Versus **Got Him Killed**](08-had-him-killed-versus-got-him-killed.md) | El jefe mandó a matar a Leo; el informante causó la muerte de Leo al revelar dónde estaba. | The boss had Leo killed; the informant got Leo killed by revealing his location. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Quiero que alguien lo haga, conseguiré que Ana lo haga y me aseguraré de que quede listo. | I want someone to do it, I'll get Ana to do it, and I'll get it done. |
| Yo lo arreglé, conseguí que Ana lo arreglara y mandé a arreglarlo. | I fixed it, I got Ana to fix it, and I had it fixed. |
| Me aseguraré de hacerlo yo; si no puedo, me aseguraré de que se haga y conseguiré que quede listo. | I'll make sure I do it; if I can't, I'll make sure it gets done and I'll get it done. |
| Alguien lo hará, así que se hará; yo me aseguraré de que se haga. | Somebody will do it, so it will get done; I'll make sure it gets done. |
| El jefe mandó a matarlo, pero el informante causó su muerte sin dar la orden. | The boss had him killed, but the informant got him killed without giving the order. |
