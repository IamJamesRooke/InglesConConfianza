# **Get** or **Have Something Finished, Sent**, or **Sold**

The result pattern is productive: **get/have + thing + completed form**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tenemos que terminar el informe, enviar el paquete y vender el carro, así que hagamos que todo quede listo esta semana. | We've gotta get the report finished, get the package sent, and get the car sold this week. |

**Get** often emphasizes achieving the result, sometimes after effort. **Have** more clearly suggests arranging for someone else to produce it:

- **I'll get the package sent today.**
- **I'll have the package sent by my assistant.**

The playful rhythm—**get it finished, get it sent, get it sold**—helps fix the reusable frame.
