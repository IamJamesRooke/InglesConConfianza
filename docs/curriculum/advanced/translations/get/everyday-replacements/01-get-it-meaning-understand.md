# **Get It**: Understand

In conversation, **get** can mean understand an idea, explanation, joke, or situation.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Al principio no entendía el chiste, pero ahora sí lo entiendo. | I didn't get the joke at first, but now I get it. |

Context separates several common meanings:

- **I got it.** — I understood it or received it.
- **I've got it.** — I understand, possess it, or will handle it.
- **You got it.** — I understand your request and will do it.

The playful progression is **I didn't get it, then I got it, and now I've got it.**
