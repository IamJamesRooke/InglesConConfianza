# Common **Get + Past Participle** Events

The conversational **get** passive is especially natural for events, changes, and things that happen to someone.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Lo contrataron, lo ascendieron y luego lo despidieron porque lo descubrieron mintiendo. | He got hired, got promoted, and then got fired because he got caught lying. |

Other common forms include **get paid, get hurt, get invited, get dressed**, and **get stuck**.

Don't replace every neutral **be** passive with **get**. **The book was written in 1925** states a historical fact; **The book got written in 1925** would unusually emphasize that the writing actually happened.
