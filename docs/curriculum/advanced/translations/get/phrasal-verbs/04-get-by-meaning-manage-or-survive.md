# **Get By**: Manage or Survive

**Get by** means manage to live or continue with only enough money, knowledge, time, or other resources.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No ganamos mucho, pero nos alcanza para sobrevivir hasta fin de mes. | We don't earn much, but we get by until the end of the month. |

It usually means **manage** rather than survive a life-threatening event. Add **on** for the available resource: **We can get by on very little money**.
