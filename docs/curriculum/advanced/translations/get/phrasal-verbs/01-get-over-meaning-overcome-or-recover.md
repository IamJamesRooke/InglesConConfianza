# **Get Over**: Overcome or Recover

**Get over** can replace **overcome** for a difficulty and can also mean recover from an illness or an emotional experience.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Superó el miedo, pero todavía no se ha recuperado de la ruptura. | She got over her fear, but she still hasn't gotten over the breakup. |

**Overcome** is more formal and normally emphasizes defeating a difficulty. In everyday speech, **get over** is also natural for illnesses, shocks, and breakups.
