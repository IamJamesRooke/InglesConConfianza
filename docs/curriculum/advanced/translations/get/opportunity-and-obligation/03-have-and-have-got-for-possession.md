# **Have** and **Have Got** for Possession

For present possession, **have** and **have got** normally mean the same thing.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tengo dos entradas, así que tenemos la oportunidad de ver el concierto. | I've got two tickets, so we get to see the concert. |

- **I have two tickets.**
- **I've got two tickets.**

In **I've got**, **'ve** means **have**. Although **got** looks past, the complete expression describes what I possess now.
