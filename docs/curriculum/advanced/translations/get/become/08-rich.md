# **Be Rich** and **Get Rich**

**Be rich** describes wealth. **Get rich** means become rich or enriquecerse.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Era pobre, se hizo rico lentamente y ahora es rico. | He was poor, he slowly got rich, and now he's rich. |

**Get rich quick** is a common fixed idea for becoming rich rapidly, often through a questionable plan.
