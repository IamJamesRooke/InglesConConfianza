# **Be Dark** and **Get Dark**

**Be dark** describes darkness. **Get dark** describes the light changing.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Estaba soleado, luego oscureció y ahora está oscuro. | It was sunny, then it got dark, and now it's dark. |

The minimal change keeps the timeline clear: **It isn't dark, it's getting dark, and soon it'll be dark.**
