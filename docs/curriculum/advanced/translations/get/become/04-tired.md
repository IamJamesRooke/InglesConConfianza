# **Be Tired** and **Get Tired**

**Be tired** describes tiredness. **Get tired** describes becoming tired.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tenía mucha energía, pero me cansé y ahora estoy cansado. | I was full of energy, but I got tired, and now I'm tired. |

**I'm tired of waiting** means estoy cansado de esperar; **I'm getting tired of waiting** means that frustration is increasing.
