# **Be Ready** and **Get Ready**

**Be ready** means estar listo. **Get ready** means alistarse or prepararse.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No estaba listo, así que me alisté y ahora estoy listo. | I wasn't ready, so I got ready, and now I'm ready. |

The natural instruction is **Get ready—we've gotta go!**
