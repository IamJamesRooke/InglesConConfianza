# **Be Busy** and **Get Busy**

**Be busy** describes being occupied. **Get busy** means become busy or start working actively.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La mañana estaba tranquila, luego me ocupé y ahora estoy muy ocupado. | My morning was quiet, then I got busy, and now I'm really busy. |

As an instruction, **Get busy!** means start working, not merely enter a busy state.
