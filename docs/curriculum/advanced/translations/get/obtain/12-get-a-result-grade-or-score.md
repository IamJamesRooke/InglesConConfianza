# **Get a Result, Grade**, or **Score**

Use **get** for a result produced by a process, test, game, or evaluation.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Obtuve un buen resultado, saqué una nota alta y conseguí diez puntos. | I got a good result, got a high grade, and got ten points. |

The exact Spanish verb changes, but conversational English can reuse **get**.
