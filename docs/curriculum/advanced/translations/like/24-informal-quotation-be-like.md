# Informal Quotation **Be Like**

In conversation, **be like** can introduce someone's words, reaction or attitude.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Le mostré el precio y ella reaccionó: “¡De ninguna manera voy a pagar eso!” | I showed her the price, and she was like, “There's no way I'm paying that!” |

This is highly informal. Use **said**, **replied**, or **reacted** in neutral and formal writing.
