# Pronunciation and Sound Contrast

**Like** is pronounced `/laɪk/`. It has no common standard-English homophone.

The useful near-sound contrast is:

- **like** `/laɪk/`
- **lick** `/lɪk/`
- **lake** `/leɪk/`

> **Mike likes hiking by the lake but dislikes licking lime ice.**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| A Mike le gusta caminar junto al lago, pero no le gusta lamer hielo de lima. | Mike likes hiking by the lake but dislikes licking lime ice. |

The long moving vowel `/aɪ/` distinguishes **like** from short `/ɪ/` in **lick** and `/eɪ/` in **lake**.
