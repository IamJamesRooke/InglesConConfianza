# Homophones: **Mean** and **Mien**

**Mean** and rare **mien**, meaning a person's appearance or manner, are homophones.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No quería parecer cruel, pero su expresión seria hizo que lo pareciera. | He didn't mean to seem mean, but his serious mien made him look mean. |

The playful sentence uses **mean** for intend and unkind before the rare homophone.
