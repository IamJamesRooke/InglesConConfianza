# **Be Well** and **Be Good**

**Be well** means be healthy. **Be good** usually means behave properly or have a good quality.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Espero que estés bien y que los niños se porten bien mientras descansas. | I hope you're well and the kids are good while you rest. |

Spanish **estar bien** can also mean **be okay**, depending on context.
