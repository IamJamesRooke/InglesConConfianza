# **Well-Known** and Other **Well-** Descriptions

Use **well- + past participle** before a noun for something done or recognized to a high degree.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Es un libro muy conocido y bien escrito sobre una científica respetada. | It's a well-known, well-written book about a well-respected scientist. |

The hyphen is normally omitted after the noun: **The book is well known.**
