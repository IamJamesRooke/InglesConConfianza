# Pronunciation: **Such** and **Suck**

**Such** ends in `/tʃ/`; **suck** ends in `/k/`. They aren't homophones.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Un cambio tan repentino puede hacer que una bomba succione demasiado aire. | Such a sudden change can make a pump suck in too much air. |

The sentence deliberately repeats the short vowel while contrasting the endings.
