# **Never Mind**

Use **never mind** to withdraw a request, dismiss a concern, or say that something isn't important now.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| —¿Qué querías preguntarme? —No importa; ya encontré la respuesta. | “What did you wanna ask me?” “Never mind; I've already found the answer.” |

Tone matters: a sharp **Never mind!** can sound annoyed.
