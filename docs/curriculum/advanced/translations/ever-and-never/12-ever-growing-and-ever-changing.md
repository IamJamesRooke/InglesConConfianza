# **Ever-Growing** and **Ever-Changing**

Before a description ending in **-ing**, **ever-** can mean continually.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tenemos que adaptarnos a una lista de requisitos que crece y cambia constantemente. | We've gotta adapt to an ever-growing, ever-changing list of requirements. |

These compounds are more common in formal or polished writing than in relaxed conversation.
