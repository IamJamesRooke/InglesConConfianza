# **As Well As**

Use **as well as** to add a related person or thing, often with less emphasis than **and**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Natalia habla portugués además de inglés, así que puede atender a ambos clientes. | Natalia speaks Portuguese as well as English, so she can help both clients. |

It can also compare ability: **She speaks Portuguese as well as her teacher does.** Context distinguishes addition from equal quality.
