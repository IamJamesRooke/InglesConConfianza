# **Not As...As**

Use **not as...as** when the first person or thing has a lower degree than the second.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Este informe no es tan claro como el anterior, así que tendremos que revisarlo. | This report isn't as clear as the previous one, so we'll have to revise it. |

**Not so...as** is also possible, especially in formal British English, but **not as...as** is the neutral everyday form.
