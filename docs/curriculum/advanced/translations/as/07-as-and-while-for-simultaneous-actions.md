# **As** and **While** for Simultaneous Actions

Use **as** or **while** when actions happen at the same time. **As** often highlights one action changing during the other.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mientras salíamos del edificio, empezó a llover con más fuerza. | As we were leaving the building, it started raining harder. |

**While we were leaving** is also natural and focuses simply on the shared time.
