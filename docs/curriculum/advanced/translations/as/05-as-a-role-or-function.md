# **As** for a Real Role or Function

Use **as** for the role, job, or function something truly has.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Laura trabaja como médica y usa su teléfono como linterna durante las visitas nocturnas. | Laura works as a doctor and uses her phone as a flashlight during night visits. |

Use **like** for similarity: **She talks like a doctor** means her manner resembles a doctor's; it doesn't say that she is one.

The same role pattern appears after verbs such as **know, describe, regard**, and **choose**: **The neighborhood is known as La Candelaria.**
