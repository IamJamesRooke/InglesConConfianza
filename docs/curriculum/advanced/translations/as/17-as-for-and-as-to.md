# **As For** and **As To**

Use **as for** to shift attention to a topic. Formal **as to** means **about** or **regarding**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| En cuanto al precio, todavía no hemos tomado una decisión sobre cuánto cobrar. | As for the price, we haven't made a decision as to how much to charge. |

In everyday English, **about** is usually more natural than **as to**.
