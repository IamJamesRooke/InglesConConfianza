# **As Usual, As Expected**, and **As Planned**

Use **as + expected action or condition** to say that reality matches a familiar pattern, expectation, or plan.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Como de costumbre, Laura llegó temprano y, como estaba previsto, empezó la reunión a las ocho. | As usual, Laura arrived early and, as planned, started the meeting at eight. |

Other common forms include **as expected**, **as promised**, and **as agreed**.
