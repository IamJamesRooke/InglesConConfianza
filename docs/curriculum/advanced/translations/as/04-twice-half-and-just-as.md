# **Twice, Half**, and **Just As...As**

Words before **as...as** can measure or emphasize the comparison.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El segundo apartamento cuesta el doble, pero es apenas la mitad de grande que el primero. | The second apartment costs twice as much, but it's only half as big as the first one. |

Use **just as...as** for emphatic equality: **The smaller room is just as useful as the larger one.**
