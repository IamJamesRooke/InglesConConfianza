# Pronunciation: **Else** and **Elves**

**Else** ends in `/ls/`; **elves** adds a `/v/`. They aren't homophones.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿Qué más podrían esconder los elfos en otra parte? | What else could the elves hide elsewhere? |

The playful sentence repeats **else / elves / elsewhere**.
