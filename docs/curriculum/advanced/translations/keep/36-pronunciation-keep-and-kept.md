# Pronunciation: **Keep** and **Kept**

**Keep** has `/iː/`; **kept** has `/e/` and ends in `/pt/`. Neither has a common homophone.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Guardé el secreto mientras ella dormía, lloraba y avanzaba sigilosamente. | I kept the secret while she slept, wept, and crept ahead. |

The playful family **kept / slept / wept / crept** reinforces the vowel and final consonant cluster.
