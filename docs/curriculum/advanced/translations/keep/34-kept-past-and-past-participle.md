# **Kept**: Past and Past Participle

**Keep** is irregular: **keep → kept → kept**. The simple past uses **kept** alone; perfect forms use **have/has/had kept**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ayer guardé el recibo y siempre he cumplido mis promesas. | I kept the receipt yesterday, and I've always kept my promises. |

The same form **kept** serves as both simple past and past participle.
