# Uses of **Keep**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Keep** Something](01-keep-something.md) | Guarda el recibo por si necesitas cambiar el producto. | Keep the receipt in case you need to exchange the product. |
| [**Keep It** Meaning Not Return It](02-keep-meaning-not-return.md) | Ya no necesito el libro; puedes quedártelo. | I don't need the book anymore—you can keep it. |
| [**Keep the Change**](03-keep-the-change.md) | Quédese con el cambio; no necesito las monedas. | Keep the change; I don't need the coins. |
| [**Keep Doing Something**](04-keep-doing.md) | Sigue practicando aunque el ejercicio parezca difícil. | Keep practicing even though the exercise seems difficult. |
| [**Keep Something in a State**](05-keep-something-in-a-state.md) | Mantén la puerta cerrada y la comida fría. | Keep the door closed and the food cold. |
| [**Keep Someone Waiting**](06-keep-someone-waiting.md) | Siento haberte hecho esperar tanto tiempo. | I'm sorry I kept you waiting so long. |
| [**Keep Safe, Calm, Warm**, and **Clean**](07-keep-safe-calm-warm-and-clean.md) | Mantén la calma, abrígate y guarda los documentos en un lugar seguro y limpio. | Keep calm, keep warm, and keep the documents safe and clean. |
| [**Keep a Secret**](08-keep-a-secret.md) | ¿Puedes guardar el secreto hasta que hagamos el anuncio? | Can you keep the secret until we make the announcement? |
| [**Keep a Promise** and **Keep Your Word**](09-keep-a-promise-and-your-word.md) | Prometió llamar y cumplió su palabra. | She promised to call, and she kept her word. |
| [**Keep in Mind**](10-keep-in-mind.md) | Ten en cuenta que el banco cierra temprano los viernes. | Keep in mind that the bank closes early on Fridays. |
| [**Keep an Open Mind**](11-keep-an-open-mind.md) | Mantén la mente abierta hasta que escuches ambas versiones. | Keep an open mind until you've heard both sides. |
| [**Keep Track** and **Keep Records**](12-keep-track-and-keep-records.md) | Lleva un registro de los gastos y guarda los recibos. | Keep track of the expenses and keep records of every payment. |
| [**Keep an Eye On**](13-keep-an-eye-on.md) | Vigila mi bolso mientras voy al baño. | Keep an eye on my bag while I go to the restroom. |
| [**Keep Someone Posted**](14-keep-someone-posted.md) | Mantenme informada sobre cualquier cambio en el horario. | Keep me posted about any changes to the schedule. |
| [**Keep Time**](15-keep-time.md) | El reloj no marca bien la hora y el baterista tampoco mantiene el ritmo. | The clock doesn't keep time well, and neither does the drummer. |
| [**Keep From Doing Something**](16-keep-from-doing.md) | La lluvia nos impidió salir de la casa. | The rain kept us from leaving the house. |
| [**Keep To**](17-keep-to.md) | Sigamos el horario y limitémonos al tema principal. | Let's keep to the schedule and keep to the main topic. |
| [**Keep Going**](18-keep-going.md) | Sigue adelante; ya casi llegamos. | Keep going; we're almost there. |
| [**Keep On Doing**](19-keep-on-doing.md) | Siguió interrumpiéndome aunque le pedí que parara. | He kept on interrupting me even though I asked him to stop. |
| [**Keep It Up**](20-keep-it-up.md) | Tu pronunciación está mejorando; sigue así. | Your pronunciation's improving—keep it up. |
| [**Keep Up**](21-keep-up.md) | Camina más despacio; no puedo seguirte el ritmo. | Walk more slowly; I can't keep up. |
| [**Keep Up With**](22-keep-up-with.md) | Me cuesta mantenerme al día con todos los correos. | It's hard to keep up with all the emails. |
| [**Keep in Touch**](23-keep-in-touch.md) | Mantengámonos en contacto después de que termine el curso. | Let's keep in touch after the course ends. |
| [**Keep Away From**](24-keep-away-from.md) | Mantén a los niños alejados de la carretera. | Keep the children away from the road. |
| [**Keep Out**](25-keep-out.md) | Mantén el agua fuera de la caja y no entres al cuarto de máquinas. | Keep water out of the box, and keep out of the engine room. |
| [**Keep Off**](26-keep-off.md) | No pises el césped y evita el azúcar esta semana. | Keep off the grass and keep off sugar this week. |
| [**Keep Down**](27-keep-down.md) | Baja la voz y trata de mantener los costos bajos. | Keep your voice down and try to keep costs down. |
| [**Keep Back**](28-keep-back.md) | Manténganse atrás y no oculten ninguna información. | Keep back from the edge, and don't keep any information back. |
| [**Keep At It**](29-keep-at-it.md) | Todavía cometes errores, pero sigue intentándolo. | You're still making mistakes, but keep at it. |
| [**Keep Together**](30-keep-together.md) | Mantengan al grupo unido y guarden todos los recibos juntos. | Keep the group together and keep all the receipts together. |
| [**Keep Someone Company**](31-keep-someone-company.md) | Me quedaré contigo mientras esperas el bus. | I'll keep you company while you wait for the bus. |
| [**Keep to Yourself**](32-keep-to-yourself.md) | Carlos suele mantenerse apartado y guarda sus opiniones para sí mismo. | Carlos tends to keep to himself and keeps his opinions to himself. |
| [**For Keeps**](33-for-keeps.md) | Esta vez el libro es tuyo para siempre. | This time, the book is yours for keeps. |
| [**Kept**: Past and Past Participle](34-kept-past-and-past-participle.md) | Ayer guardé el recibo y siempre he cumplido mis promesas. | I kept the receipt yesterday, and I've always kept my promises. |
| [**Kept** in Passive and State Patterns](35-kept-in-passive-and-state-patterns.md) | Los documentos se guardaron bajo llave y nos hicieron esperar afuera. | The documents were kept locked up, and we were kept waiting outside. |
| [Pronunciation: **Keep** and **Kept**](36-pronunciation-keep-and-kept.md) | Guardé el secreto mientras ella dormía, lloraba y avanzaba sigilosamente. | I kept the secret while she slept, wept, and crept ahead. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Sigue practicando, mantén la calma, ten en cuenta el plazo y asegúrate de mantenerme informada. | Keep practicing, keep calm, keep the deadline in mind, and make sure you keep me posted. |
| La lluvia nos impidió avanzar, pero seguimos adelante, nos mantuvimos al día con el grupo y seguimos en contacto. | The rain kept us from moving, but we kept going, kept up with the group, and kept in touch. |
| Ayer guardé el recibo, cumplí mi promesa y mantuve el secreto que siempre había guardado. | Yesterday I kept the receipt, kept my promise, and kept the secret I'd always kept. |
