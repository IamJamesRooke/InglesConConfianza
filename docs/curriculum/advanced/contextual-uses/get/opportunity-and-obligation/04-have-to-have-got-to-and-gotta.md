# **Have To, Have Got To**, and **Gotta**

**Have to** and **have got to** express necessity or obligation. In informal speech, **have got to** is often reduced to **gotta**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tengo que terminar esto hoy porque tenemos que enviarlo mañana. | I've gotta finish this today because we have to send it tomorrow. |

- **I have to do it.**
- **I've got to do it.**
- **I gotta do it.** — informal representation of speech

**Gotta** is useful in dialogue and casual messages, but use **have to** or **have got to** in formal writing.

Don't use **gotta** for possession: **I've got a ticket** means tengo una entrada; **I've gotta get a ticket** means tengo que conseguir una entrada.
