# **Be Better** and **Get Better**

**Be better** describes an improved state. **Get better** describes improvement.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Estaba enferma, se mejoró y ahora está mucho mejor. | She was sick, she got better, and now she's much better. |

**Get better** works for health, skills, situations, and conditions: **My English is getting better.**
