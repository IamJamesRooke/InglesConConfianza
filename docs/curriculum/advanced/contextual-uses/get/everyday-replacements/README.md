# Everyday Replacements with **Get**

These uses replace a more specific everyday verb when context makes the intended result clear.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Get It**: Understand](01-get-it-meaning-understand.md) | Al principio no entendía el chiste, pero ahora sí lo entiendo. | I didn't get the joke at first, but now I get it. |
| [**Get Something To Someone**](02-get-something-to-someone-or-somewhere.md) | Te enviaré el informe mañana y haré que los originales lleguen a la oficina. | I'll get the report to you tomorrow and get the originals to the office. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Al principio no lo entendía, luego lo entendí y ahora lo tengo claro; te lo haré llegar mañana. | I didn't get it at first, then I got it, and now I've got it; I'll get it to you tomorrow. |
