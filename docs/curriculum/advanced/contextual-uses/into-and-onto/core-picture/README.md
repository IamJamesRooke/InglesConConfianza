# Core Picture of **Into** and **Onto**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Into an Interior](01-into-an-interior.md) | Entró al cuarto. | She walked into the room. |
| [Onto a Surface](02-onto-a-surface.md) | Saltó sobre la cama. | She jumped onto the bed. |
| [Dynamic vs Static](03-direction-vs-position.md) | Entró al cuarto y ahora está adentro. | She went into the room and is in it now. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El gato saltó dentro de la caja, salió y después saltó sobre la mesa, donde todavía está. | The cat jumped into the box, came out, and then jumped onto the table, where it is still sitting. |
