# Movement with **Into** and **Onto**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Come, Go, Walk](01-come-go-walk.md) | Entró caminando al edificio. | She walked into the building. |
| [Run and Drive](02-run-and-drive.md) | Entró corriendo y metió el carro al garaje. | She ran in and drove into the garage. |
| [Get Into](03-get-into.md) | Súbase al carro. | Get into the car. |
| [Jump and Fall](04-jump-and-fall.md) | Saltó al agua y cayó al piso. | She jumped into the water and fell onto the floor. |
| [Put, Throw, Push, Pull](05-move-an-object.md) | Empujó la caja dentro del cuarto. | She pushed the box into the room. |
| [Turn Into](06-turn-into-a-place.md) | Gire para entrar a esa calle. | Turn into that street. |
| [Take Into](07-take-into.md) | Llevó la caja a la oficina. | She took the box into the office. |
