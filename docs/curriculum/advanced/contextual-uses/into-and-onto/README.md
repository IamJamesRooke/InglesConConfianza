# Uses of **Into** and **Onto**

**Into** combines direction with an interior: movement goes from outside to inside. **Onto** combines direction with a surface: movement goes from off a surface to on it.

| Section | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Core Picture](core-picture/README.md) | Saltó dentro de la piscina y después subió al muelle. | She jumped into the pool and then climbed onto the dock. |
| [Movement](movement/README.md) | Entró al cuarto y puso la caja sobre la mesa. | She walked into the room and put the box onto the table. |
| [Changes and Extensions](changes-and-extensions/README.md) | El agua se convirtió en hielo y pasamos al siguiente tema. | The water turned into ice, and we moved on to the next topic. |
| [Confusion Sets](confusion-sets/README.md) | Está en el carro porque se subió al carro. | She's in the car because she got into the car. |

## Whole-Topic Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Entró al cuarto, puso los documentos sobre la mesa y se sentó en la silla. | She walked into the room, put the documents onto the table, and sat on the chair. |
| El agua se convirtió en hielo, el carro chocó contra una pared y la policía investigó el accidente. | The water turned into ice, the car ran into a wall, and the police looked into the accident. |
