# **Side** and Related Words

**Side** names one part, face, or position of something. It also helps build words and expressions whose meaning may stay physical or become figurative.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Side**](01-side.md) | Este lado de la caja está mojado, pero el otro está seco. | This side of the box is wet, but the other side is dry. |
| [**Inside** and **Outside**](02-inside-and-outside.md) | El interior de la caja está seco, pero la parte exterior está mojada. | The inside of the box is dry, but the outside is wet. |
| [**Upside** and **Downside**](03-upside-and-downside.md) | La ventaja es el salario; la desventaja es el horario. | The upside is the salary; the downside is the schedule. |
| [The **Front Side** and the **Back Side**](04-front-side-and-back-side.md) | Escriba su nombre en la parte delantera y la fecha en la parte de atrás. | Write your name on the front side and the date on the back side. |
| [The **Other Side** and **Across**](05-the-other-side-and-across.md) | El banco queda al otro lado de la calle, frente a la farmacia. | The bank is on the other side of the street, across from the pharmacy. |
| [**By** and **Beside**](06-by-and-beside.md) | Siéntese junto a Ana, al lado de la ventana. | Sit by Ana, beside the window. |
| [**Besides**](07-besides.md) | Además del salario, el trabajo ofrece seguro médico. | Besides the salary, the job offers health insurance. |
| [**Beside** vs **Besides**](confusion-sets/README.md) | Siéntese a mi lado; además, necesito hablar con usted. | Sit beside me; besides, I need to speak with you. |

## Word-Building Picture

| Parts | Main idea |
|---|---|
| **in + side → inside** | the inner part or position |
| **out + side → outside** | the outer part or position |
| **up + side → upside** | figuratively, an advantage |
| **down + side → downside** | figuratively, a disadvantage |

The parts make the contrast memorable, but **upside** and **downside** are commonly used for the figurative meanings **advantage** and **disadvantage**. Learn them as a pair.

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El interior está seco, pero la parte exterior está mojada. | The inside is dry, but the outside is wet. |
| La ventaja está en la parte delantera; la desventaja está escrita en la parte de atrás. | The upside is on the front side; the downside is written on the back side. |
| El banco queda al otro lado de la calle, frente a la farmacia; además, hay un cajero al lado de la entrada. | The bank is on the other side of the street, across from the pharmacy; besides, there's an ATM beside the entrance. |
