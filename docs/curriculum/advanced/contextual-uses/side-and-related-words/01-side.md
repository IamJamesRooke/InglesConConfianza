# **Side**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Este lado de la caja está mojado, pero el otro lado está seco. | This side of the box is wet, but the other side is dry. |

**Side** can name one of two contrasting parts, one face of an object, or an area next to something.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Hay una puerta a cada lado del pasillo. | There's a door on each side of the hallway. |
| Ana está a mi lado. | Ana is by my side. |

Use **on this/that side of** for a position: **on this side of the river**. Use **by someone's side** for a position next to that person.
