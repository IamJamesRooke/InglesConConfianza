# **The Way Someone Does Something**

**The way + clause** describes how someone does something or how something happens.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me gusta cómo Laura explica los problemas porque siempre usa ejemplos concretos. | I like the way Laura explains problems because she always uses concrete examples. |

Spanish naturally uses **cómo** or **la manera en que**; it does not need a literal translation with **camino**.
