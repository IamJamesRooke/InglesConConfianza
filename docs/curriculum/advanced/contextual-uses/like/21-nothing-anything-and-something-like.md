# **Nothing Like, Anything Like**, and **Something Like**

These combinations express different degrees of similarity or approximation.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El resultado no se parece en nada al diseño original. | The result is nothing like the original design. |
| ¿El nuevo sistema se parece en algo al anterior? | Is the new system anything like the old one? |
| Necesitamos algo parecido a una autorización escrita. | We need something like written authorization. |

After a number, **something like** means approximately: **Something like fifty people attended → Asistieron unas cincuenta personas**.
