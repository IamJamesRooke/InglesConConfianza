# Predictable Extensions with **Back**

Here **back** keeps a visible meaning: return to a place/person or move in a rearward direction. With an object, **back** is a separable particle: **put the book back / put it back**, not *put back it*.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Go, Come**, and **Get Back**](01-go-come-and-get-back.md) | Vuelve allá, regresa acá y vuelve a casa antes de las diez. | Go back there, come back here, and get back home before ten. |
| [**Walk, Run**, and **Drive Back**](02-walk-run-and-drive-back.md) | Regresó caminando, volvió corriendo y condujo de regreso. | She walked back, ran back, and drove back. |
| [**Bring, Take, Give**, and **Put Back**](03-bring-take-give-and-put-back.md) | Tráeme el libro, llévalo allá, devuélveselo a Ana y ponlo en el estante. | Bring the book back to me, take it back there, give it back to Ana, and put it back on the shelf. |
| [**Throw, Push**, and **Pull Back**](04-throw-push-and-pull-back.md) | Devuelve la pelota, empuja la silla hacia atrás y retira la cortina. | Throw the ball back, push the chair back, and pull the curtain back. |
| [**Look** and **Turn Back**](05-look-and-turn-back.md) | Mira hacia atrás y da la vuelta antes del puente. | Look back and turn back before the bridge. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Vuelve corriendo, tráeme la pelota, devuélvela y luego ponla otra vez en la caja. | Run back, bring the ball back to me, throw it back, and then put it back in the box. |
