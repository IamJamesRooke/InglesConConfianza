# Confusion Sets with **Back**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Back** and **Behind**](01-back-and-behind.md) | La mochila está en la parte de atrás del carro, detrás del asiento. | The backpack is in the back of the car, behind the seat. |
| [**Go Back** and **Come Back**](02-go-back-and-come-back.md) | Vuelve allá y luego regresa acá. | Go back there and then come back here. |
| [**Bring Back** and **Take Back**](03-bring-back-and-take-back.md) | Tráeme la taza y después llévala a la cocina. | Bring the cup back to me and then take it back to the kitchen. |
| [Pronunciation: **Back** and **Bag**](04-pronunciation-back-and-bag.md) | Vuelve por la bolsa negra. | Go back for the black bag. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ve a la parte de atrás, recoge la bolsa que está detrás de la silla y tráemela. | Go to the back, pick up the bag behind the chair, and bring it back to me. |
