# **Upper** and **Lower**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Los vasos están en el estante superior y los platos en el estante inferior. | The glasses are on the upper shelf, and the plates are on the lower shelf. |

**Upper** and **lower** identify relative parts or levels. **Up** and **down** usually express direction or position, while **high** and **low** describe level or height.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Levante la mano derecha y mantenga el brazo izquierdo más abajo. | Raise your right hand and keep your left arm lower. |

**Lower** is also a verb meaning move something down: **Lower the box onto the bottom shelf.**
