# **Upward** and **Upwards**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mire hacia arriba y mueva la palanca hacia arriba. | Look upward and move the lever upwards. |

**Upward** and **upwards** mark direction toward a higher position. **Upwards** is especially common in British English; **upward** is standard in both British and American English.

Only **upward** normally goes before a noun: **an upward movement**, not *an upwards movement*.

Compare the base direction and the extended form:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mire arriba y apunte hacia arriba. | Look up and point upward. |
