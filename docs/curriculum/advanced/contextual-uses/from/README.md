# Uses of **From**

**From** points back to a source, starting point, origin, cause, separation, or prevention. Its reusable picture is movement or difference away from a starting point.

| Section | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Core Picture](core-picture/README.md) | Caminó desde la estación hasta la oficina. | She walked from the station to the office. |
| [Core Uses](core-uses/README.md) | Soy de Bogotá y trabajo de ocho a cinco. | I'm from Bogotá and work from eight to five. |
| [Fixed Connections](fixed-connections/README.md) | Esto evita que el agua entre. | This prevents water from getting in. |
| [Confusion Sets](confusion-sets/README.md) | Te lo compré a ti, no para ti. | I bought it from you, not for you. |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
