# Movement Verbs with **From**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ana vino del parque, caminó desde la esquina y corrió desde la entrada cuando empezó a llover. | Ana came from the park, walked from the corner, and ran from the entrance when it started to rain. |

Common combinations include **come, go, get, run, walk, drive, fly**, and **travel from** a starting point.
