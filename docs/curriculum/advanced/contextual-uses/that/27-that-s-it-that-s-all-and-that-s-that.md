# **That's It, That's All**, and **That's That**

- **That's it** can identify the correct thing or announce completion.
- **That's all** means there is nothing more.
- **That's that** emphatically closes a discussion or decision.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Esa es la llave que buscaba. Eso es todo; podemos irnos. | That's it—the key I was looking for. That's all; we can leave. |
| La decisión es definitiva y se acabó el asunto. | The decision is final, and that's that. |
