# Uses of **That**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**That + Thing**](01-that-plus-thing.md) | Ese libro que está junto a la ventana es el que necesito. | That book by the window is the one I need. |
| [**That** Standing Alone](02-that-standing-alone.md) | Me gusta eso, pero no me gusta aquello que está junto a la puerta. | I like that, but I don't like that one by the door. |
| [**That** Versus **It**](03-that-versus-it.md) | —Compré una bicicleta nueva. —¡Eso es genial! ¿Cuándo la vas a usar? | “I bought a new bicycle.” “That's great! When are you going to use it?” |
| [Connector **That** After Thinking and Speaking](04-connector-that-after-thinking-and-speaking.md) | Creo que Laura sabe que la reunión empieza a las nueve. | I think that Laura knows that the meeting starts at nine. |
| [Optional Connector **That**](05-optional-connector-that.md) | El gerente confirmó que los cambios que solicitó el cliente estarán listos mañana. | The manager confirmed that the changes the client requested will be ready tomorrow. |
| [Connector **That** After a Description](06-connector-that-after-a-description.md) | Estoy segura de que el archivo está listo y me alegra que Laura lo haya revisado. | I'm sure that the file is ready, and I'm glad that Laura reviewed it. |
| [Noun + Connector **That**](07-noun-plus-connector-that.md) | La posibilidad de que el vuelo sea cancelado cambia nuestro plan. | The possibility that the flight will be canceled changes our plan. |
| [When Spanish **Que** Does Not Become Connector **That**](08-when-spanish-que-does-not-become-connector-that.md) | Quiero que Laura lo haga y necesito que Carlos la ayude. | I want Laura to do it, and I need Carlos to help her. |
| [**That** Versus **So** After **Think**](09-that-versus-so-after-think.md) | Creo que Laura va a ganar. | I think that Laura will win. |
| [Relative **That**](10-relative-that.md) | Necesito algo que funcione sin internet y una persona que pueda instalarlo. | I need something that works without internet and someone who can install it. |
| [Relative **That** Versus **Which**](11-relative-that-versus-which.md) | El archivo que contiene las cifras definitivas está en esta carpeta. | The file that contains the final figures is in this folder. |
| [When Relative **That** Can Disappear](12-when-relative-that-can-disappear.md) | Esa idea que dijiste que no era tan importante en ese momento es la que cambió todo; por eso me gusta. | That idea that you said wasn't that important at that time is the idea that changed everything; that's why I like that. |
| [Prepositions and Relative **That**](13-prepositions-and-relative-that.md) | Esa es la persona con la que hablé y el tema al que me referí. | That's the person that I spoke to and the topic that I referred to. |
| [**Not That + Description**](14-not-that-plus-description.md) | El examen no estuvo tan difícil; terminé veinte minutos antes. | The exam wasn't that difficult; I finished twenty minutes early. |
| [Positive Degree **That**](15-positive-degree-that.md) | ¿De verdad está así de lejos? Si queda tan lejos, tomemos un taxi. | Is it really that far? If it's that far, let's take a taxi. |
| [**So...That**](16-so-that.md) | Estaba tan cansada que se quedó dormida en el bus. | She was so tired that she fell asleep on the bus. |
| [**Such...That**](17-such-that.md) | Fue una reunión tan larga que tuvimos que pedir la cena. | It was such a long meeting that we had to order dinner. |
| [Purpose **So That**](18-purpose-so-that.md) | Te envío las dos versiones para que puedas compararlas. | I'm sending you both versions so that you can compare them. |
| [**Like That** and **That Way**](19-like-that-and-that-way.md) | No organices los archivos así; si lo haces de esa manera, nadie podrá encontrarlos. | Don't organize the files like that; if you do it that way, nobody will be able to find them. |
| [**At That Time**](20-at-that-time.md) | En aquella época trabajaba de noche, así que no podía asistir a las clases. | At that time, I worked nights, so I couldn't attend the classes. |
| [**Now That**](21-now-that.md) | Ahora que tenemos las cifras definitivas, podemos enviar el informe. | Now that we have the final figures, we can send the report. |
| [Reason and Condition Expressions](22-reason-and-condition-expressions.md) | Dado que el cliente aprobó el diseño, podemos publicarlo. | Given that the client approved the design, we can publish it. |
| [**The Fact That**](23-the-fact-that.md) | El hecho de que Laura hablara con el cliente evitó otro retraso. | The fact that Laura spoke to the client prevented another delay. |
| [**That's Why, That's How**, and **That's What**](24-that-s-why-that-s-how-and-that-s-what.md) | Perdimos el último bus; por eso llegamos tarde. | We missed the last bus; that's why we were late. |
| [**That's Why** Versus **That's Because**](25-that-s-why-versus-that-s-because.md) | El bus se averió; por eso llegamos tarde. | The bus broke down; that's why we were late. |
| [Emphatic **It Is...That**](26-emphatic-it-is-that.md) | Fue Laura quien encontró el error, no Carlos. | It was Laura that found the error, not Carlos. |
| [**That's It, That's All**, and **That's That**](27-that-s-it-that-s-all-and-that-s-that.md) | Esa es la llave que buscaba. Eso es todo; podemos irnos. | That's it—the key I was looking for. That's all; we can leave. |
| [**That Is** and **That Is To Say**](28-that-is-and-that-is-to-say.md) | Nos reuniremos el próximo día hábil, es decir, el lunes. | We'll meet on the next business day—that is, Monday. |
| [**That Said**](29-that-said.md) | El plan es costoso. Dicho esto, podría ahorrarnos dinero a largo plazo. | The plan is expensive. That said, it could save us money in the long run. |
| [**And All That**](30-and-all-that.md) | Hablamos del presupuesto, del cronograma y todo eso. | We talked about the budget, the schedule, and all that. |
| [**That Of** and **Those Of**](31-that-of-and-those-of.md) | El clima de Bogotá es más frío que el de Cartagena. | Bogotá's climate is colder than that of Cartagena. |
| [Pronunciation](32-pronunciation.md) | Esa idea que dijiste que no era tan importante en ese momento es la que cambió todo; por eso me gusta. | That idea that you said wasn't that important at that time is the idea that changed everything; that's why I like that. |
| [No Common Homophone: **That, Than**, and **Then**](33-no-common-homophone-that-than-and-then.md) | Esa idea que dijiste que no era tan importante en ese momento es la que cambió todo; por eso me gusta. | That idea that you said wasn't that important at that time is the idea that changed everything; that's why I like that. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Esa idea que dijiste que no era tan importante en ese momento es la que cambió todo; por eso me gusta. | That idea that you said wasn't that important at that time is the idea that changed everything; that's why I like that. |
| Creo que ese es el archivo que Laura dijo que necesitaba, pero el archivo que está sobre la mesa es el que quiero que Carlos revise. | I think that's the file Laura said she needed, but the file that is on the table is the one I want Carlos to review. |
| Ahora que sabemos que funciona así, envíalo para que el equipo pueda usarlo; dicho esto, no supongas que todos entenderán el proceso. | Now that we know it works that way, send it so that the team can use it; that said, don't assume that everyone will understand the process. |
