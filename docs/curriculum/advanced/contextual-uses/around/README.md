# Contextual Uses of **Around**

The core picture of **around** is surrounding something, moving along different sides, or being in the nearby area. It also commonly means approximately.

| Section | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Core Uses](core-uses/README.md) | Hay unas veinte personas alrededor de la mesa. | There are about twenty people around the table. |
| [Core-Verb Movement](predictable-extensions/README.md) | Caminamos por el parque, miramos a nuestro alrededor y dimos la vuelta. | We walked around the park, looked around, and turned around. |
| [Whole Expressions](whole-expressions/README.md) | Encontró una manera de evitar la regla y dejó de perder el tiempo. | She found a way around the rule and stopped messing around. |
