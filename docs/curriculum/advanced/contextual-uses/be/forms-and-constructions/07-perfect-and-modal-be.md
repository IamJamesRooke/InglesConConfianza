# Perfect and Modal Forms of **Be**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ana ha estado aquí, podría estar cansada y debería estar en casa antes de las ocho. | Ana has been here, could be tired, and should be home before eight. |

Use **have/has been** for perfect forms and bare **be** after modals: **will, would, could, should, may**, or **might be**.
