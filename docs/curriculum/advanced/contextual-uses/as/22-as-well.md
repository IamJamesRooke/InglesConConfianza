# **As Well** Meaning **Too**

At the end of a positive statement, **as well** means **también**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Laura va a revisar el contrato y yo también voy a revisarlo. | Laura's going to review the contract, and I'll review it as well. |

**Too** is more common in everyday American English. **As well** is especially common in British English and can sound slightly more polished.
