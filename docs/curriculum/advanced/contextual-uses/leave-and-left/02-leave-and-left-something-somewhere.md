# **Leave** and **Left** Something Somewhere

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Lo dejo aquí todos los días, y ayer también lo dejé aquí. | I leave it here every day, and yesterday I left it here too. |

With an object and place, **leave** means put or keep something there instead of taking it: **lo dejo aquí** is **I leave it here**, while **lo dejé aquí** is **I left it here**.
