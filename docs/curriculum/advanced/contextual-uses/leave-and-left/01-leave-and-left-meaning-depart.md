# **Leave** and **Left** Meaning Depart

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Salgo a las cinco todos los días, pero ayer salí a las seis. | I leave at five every day, but yesterday I left at six. |

**Leave** means *salir* or *irse* when someone departs. Its simple past and past participle are both **left**: **I leave**, **I left**, and **I've left**.
