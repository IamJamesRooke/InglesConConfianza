# **Derecho**: **Straight** or **Right**?

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Sigue derecho dos cuadras y después gira a la derecha. | Go straight for two blocks and then turn right. |

In directions, Colombian Spanish distinguishes **derecho** for continuing straight from **a la derecha** for turning right. English cannot use **right** for both: **go straight**, but **turn right**.
