# **Look Forward To**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Espero con ilusión conocerlos el lunes. | I look forward to meeting you on Monday. |

**Look forward to** means await something with pleasure or anticipation, not physically look ahead. Because **to** is part of this fixed connection, use a noun or an **-ing** form after it: **our meeting** or **meeting you**, not *meet you*.
