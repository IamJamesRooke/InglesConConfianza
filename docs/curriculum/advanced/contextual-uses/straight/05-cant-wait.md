# **Can't Wait**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tengo muchas ganas de verlos; ¡no veo la hora! | I can't wait to see you! |

**Can't wait to** expresses strong, enthusiastic anticipation and is more informal than **look forward to**. It normally takes **to + base verb**: **can't wait to see**, not *can't wait to seeing*.
