# Core Uses of **In** and **Out**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Inside and Outside](01-inside-and-outside.md) | Los niños están adentro y los perros afuera. | The children are inside, and the dogs are outside. |
| [Time, Place, and Language](02-time-place-and-language.md) | En enero viviré en Bogotá y trabajaré en inglés. | In January, I'll live in Bogotá and work in English. |
| [A Period from Now](03-in-a-few-minutes.md) | Terminaré en unos minutos. | I'll finish in a few minutes. |
| [The **Out-** Prefix](04-out-prefix.md) | Nos superó en rendimiento, velocidad y estrategia. | She outperformed, outran, and outthought us. |

| Spanish | English |

| En enero estaré dentro de la oficina en Bogotá, hablando en inglés con alguien que me supera en estrategia. | In January, I'll be inside the office in Bogotá, speaking in English with someone who outthinks me. |
