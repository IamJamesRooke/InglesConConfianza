# **About, Around**, and **Of** Confusion Sets

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**About** and **Around** for Approximation](01-about-vs-around-for-approximation.md) | Llegaron unas cien personas alrededor de las diez. | About a hundred people arrived around ten. |
| [**Hear About** and **Hear Of**](02-hear-about-vs-hear-of.md) | Conozco la empresa y me enteré de su nuevo proyecto. | I've heard of the company, and I heard about its new project. |
| [**Think About** and **Think Of**](03-think-about-vs-think-of.md) | Considera el plan y dime qué opinión te merece. | Think about the plan and tell me what you think of it. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Conocía la empresa, pero me enteré de su nuevo proyecto alrededor de las diez y pensé en él durante una hora. | I'd heard of the company, but I heard about its new project around ten and thought about it for an hour. |
