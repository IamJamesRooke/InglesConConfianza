# Contextual Uses of **About**

**About** commonly introduces a topic, gives an approximate quantity, or appears in fixed expressions. In many topic connections it corresponds to Spanish **de** or **sobre**.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**About** a Topic](01-about-a-topic.md) | Hablamos del curso. | We talked about the course. |
| [**About** Meaning Approximately](02-about-meaning-approximately.md) | Llegaron unas cien personas. | About a hundred people came. |
| [**Be About**](03-be-about.md) | El libro trata de una familia bogotana. | The book is about a family from Bogotá. |
| [**Be About To**](04-be-about-to.md) | Estoy a punto de salir. | I'm about to leave. |
| [**Think, Know**, and **Worry About**](05-think-know-and-worry-about.md) | Pienso en el problema, sé del riesgo y me preocupa el costo. | I think about the problem, know about the risk, and worry about the cost. |
| [**Hear About**](06-hear-about.md) | Me enteré del cambio ayer. | I heard about the change yesterday. |
| [**About, Around**, and **Of**](confusion-sets/README.md) | Sé de la empresa y me enteré de unos veinte cambios. | I know of the company and heard about around twenty changes. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El libro trata de una empresa con unos cien empleados. | The book is about a company with about a hundred employees. |
| Estoy a punto de llamar porque me enteré del problema y estoy preocupado por Ana. | I'm about to call because I heard about the problem and I'm worried about Ana. |
