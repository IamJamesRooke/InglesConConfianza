# **Think, Know**, and **Worry About**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Pienso en el problema, sé del riesgo y me preocupa el costo. | I think about the problem, know about the risk, and worry about the cost. |

Memorize these verb + **about** connections. They introduce the topic receiving thought, knowledge, or concern; they are not separable phrasal verbs.
