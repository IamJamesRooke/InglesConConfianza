# Core Uses of **On** and **Off**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Position and Separation](01-position-and-separation.md) | El libro está sobre la mesa; quítalo. | The book is on the table; take it off. |
| [Days and Dates](02-days-dates-and-weekends.md) | Nos vemos el lunes 1 de enero. | I'll see you on Monday, January 1. |
| [Transport and Floors](03-transport-and-floors.md) | Estoy en el bus y trabajo en el segundo piso. | I'm on the bus, and I work on the second floor. |
| [States and **On and Off**](04-states-and-on-and-off.md) | La máquina está encendida, apagada e intermitente. | The machine is on, off, and working on and off. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El lunes estaba en el bus cuando la luz del segundo piso se encendió y apagó intermitentemente. | On Monday, I was on the bus when the light on the second floor went on and off. |
