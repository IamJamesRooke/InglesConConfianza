# Idiomatic **Put On** and **Put Off**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Fingió una sonrisa, pero su actitud me quitó las ganas y aplazamos la reunión. | She put on a smile, but her attitude put me off, so we put off the meeting. |

**Put on** can mean pretend; **put someone off** discourages or repels; **put something off** postpones.
