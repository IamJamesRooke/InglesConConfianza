# Contextual Uses of **On** and **Off**

**On** often suggests contact, support, continuation, or activation. **Off** often suggests separation, removal, interruption, or deactivation. Start with that picture, then learn the combinations whose meanings cannot be predicted.

| Section | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Core Uses](core-uses/README.md) | La luz está encendida el lunes, pero se apaga de vez en cuando. | The light is on on Monday, but it goes off now and then. |
| [Core Picture](core-picture/README.md) | Súbete al bus, ponte la chaqueta y enciende el teléfono. | Get on the bus, put on your jacket, and turn on your phone. |
| [Predictable Extensions](predictable-extensions/README.md) | Limpia la mesa, sigue trabajando y después pasa al siguiente punto. | Clean off the table, keep on working, and then move on. |
| [Fixed Connections](fixed-connections/README.md) | Concéntrate en el plan porque dependemos de que funcione. | Focus on the plan because we're counting on it to work. |
| [Whole Expressions](whole-expressions/README.md) | ¡Vamos! No presumas; cancelaron la reunión porque se activó la alarma. | Come on! Don't show off; they called off the meeting because the alarm went off. |

## Whole-Topic Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Súbete, ponte el cinturón y enciende el carro; cuando lleguemos, apágalo, quítate el cinturón y bájate. | Get on, put on your seat belt, and turn the car on; when we arrive, turn it off, take the belt off, and get off. |
| Planeo seguir trabajando en el informe porque mi equipo depende de mí. | I plan on keeping on working on the report because my team is counting on me. |
| La alarma se activó, la reunión se canceló y el presumido finalmente dejó de presumir. | The alarm went off, the meeting was called off, and the show-off finally stopped showing off. |
