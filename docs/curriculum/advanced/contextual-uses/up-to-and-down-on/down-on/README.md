# Uses of **Down On**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Look Down On](01-look-down-on.md) | Menosprecia a los principiantes. | She looks down on beginners. |
| [Come Down On](02-come-down-on.md) | El jefe lo criticó duramente. | The boss came down on him hard. |
| [Crack Down On](03-crack-down-on.md) | Las autoridades tomaron medidas estrictas contra el fraude. | The authorities cracked down on fraud. |
| [Cut Down On](04-cut-down-on.md) | Estoy reduciendo el azúcar. | I'm cutting down on sugar. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La gerente no menosprecia a los empleados, pero los critica con dureza si no reducen los errores repetidos. | The manager doesn't look down on employees, but she comes down on them hard if they don't cut down on repeated errors. |
