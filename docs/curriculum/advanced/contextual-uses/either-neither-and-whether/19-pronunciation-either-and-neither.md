# Pronunciation: **Either** and **Neither**

Both words have two standard first vowels: `/ˈiːðər/` or `/ˈaɪðər/`, and `/ˈniːðər/` or `/ˈnaɪðər/`.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Cualquiera de las dos pronunciaciones sirve; ninguna es incorrecta. | Either pronunciation works; neither one is wrong. |

Speakers may use either variant regardless of country.
