# The **-wise** Suffix

Informal **topic-wise** means regarding that topic or in that aspect.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| En cuanto al dinero, estamos bien, pero el tiempo sí es un problema. | Money-wise, we're fine, but time-wise, we've got a problem. |

Common conversational forms include **money-wise, time-wise**, and **work-wise**.
