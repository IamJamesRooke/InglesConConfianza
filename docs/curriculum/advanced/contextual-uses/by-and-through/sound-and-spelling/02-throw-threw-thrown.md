# **Throw, Threw, Thrown**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Lanzo la pelota todos los días, la lancé ayer y ya la he lanzado hoy. | I throw the ball every day, threw it yesterday, and have already thrown it today. |

The three forms are **throw–threw–thrown**. **Threw** rhymes with **through**; **throw** and **thrown** use a different vowel.
