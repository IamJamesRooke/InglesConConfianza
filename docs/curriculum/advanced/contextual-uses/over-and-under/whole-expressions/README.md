# Whole Expressions with **Over** and **Under**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Be Over**](01-be-over.md) | La reunión terminó. | The meeting is over. |
| [**Start Over** and **Do Over**](02-start-over-and-do-over.md) | Empieza de nuevo y hazlo otra vez. | Start over and do it over. |
| [**Go Over** and **Look Over**](03-go-over-and-look-over.md) | Repasemos el plan y revisemos el contrato. | Let's go over the plan and look over the contract. |
| [**Get Over** and **Take Over**](04-get-over-and-take-over.md) | Se recuperó y asumió el control del proyecto. | She got over it and took over the project. |
| [**Turn Over**](05-turn-over.md) | Voltea la hoja y entrégale las llaves a Ana. | Turn over the page and turn the keys over to Ana. |
