# Core Uses

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Over, Above, Under**, and **Below**](01-over-above-under-and-below.md) | El cuadro está encima del sofá y el gato está debajo de la mesa. | The picture is over the sofa, and the cat is under the table. |
| [**Over Here** and **Over There**](02-over-here-and-over-there.md) | Ven por aquí y deja la caja por allá. | Come over here and leave the box over there. |
| [More or Less Than a Limit](03-more-or-less-than-a-limit.md) | Es para mayores de 18 y pesa menos de 20 kilos. | It's for people over 18, and it weighs under 20 kilos. |
| [Across a Period of Time](04-across-a-period-of-time.md) | Ha mejorado durante los últimos años. | It has improved over the last few years. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La lámpara está encima de la mesa de allá; cuesta poco más de cien dólares y ha durado más de diez años. | The lamp is over the table over there; it costs just over a hundred dollars and has lasted over ten years. |
