# **Over, Above, Under**, and **Below**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La lámpara está sobre la mesa, el estante está más arriba y el gato duerme debajo de la mesa. | The lamp is over the table, the shelf is above it, and the cat is sleeping under the table. |

**Over** and **under** commonly relate one thing directly to another. **Above** and **below** simply mark a higher or lower position and need not suggest direct alignment. **Underneath** strongly emphasizes a position directly under something; **beneath** has the same basic physical meaning but is less common and more formal.
