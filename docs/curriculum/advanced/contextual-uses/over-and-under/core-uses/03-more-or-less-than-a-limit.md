# More or Less Than a Limit

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El límite es de 20 kilos: mi maleta pesa un poco más y la tuya pesa menos. | The limit is 20 kilos: my suitcase is just over it, and yours is under it. |

With ages, prices, distances, amounts, and other limits, **over** means more than and **under** means less than. **Just over** and **just under** mean only slightly more or less.
