# Movement with **Over** and **Under**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ven, pasa por encima del puente, conduce por debajo del paso elevado y tráeme la caja. | Come over, walk over the bridge, drive under the overpass, and bring the box over to me. |

With movement verbs such as **go, come, get, walk, run, drive, take**, and **throw**, the physical path usually makes **over** or **under** predictable. Context distinguishes a path across something from movement toward a nearby person: **come over to me**.
