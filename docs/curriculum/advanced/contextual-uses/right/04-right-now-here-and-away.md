# **Right Now, Right Here**, and **Right Away**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Necesito la respuesta aquí mismo, ahora mismo, así que envíamela de inmediato. | I need the answer right here, right now, so send it right away. |

**Right** adds precision or immediacy: **right now** means at this exact moment, **right here** means in this exact place, and **right away** means immediately.
