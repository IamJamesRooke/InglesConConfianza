# **At** Points in an Event or Process

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Al principio de la reunión explicaron el objetivo y al final anunciaron la decisión. | At the beginning of the meeting they explained the goal, and at the end they announced the decision. |

**At the beginning/end of** points to the starting or finishing edge of a specific event or period. It does not always mean one exact instant: **at the beginning of the year** normally means near the start of the year, not necessarily precisely on January 1. English normally says **in the middle of**, because the middle is viewed as a surrounding part rather than an edge.
