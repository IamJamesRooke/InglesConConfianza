# Whole Expressions with **At**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Come At**](01-come-at.md) | El perro se me abalanzó. | The dog came at me. |

## Phrasal-Verb Note

**Come at** is often included in broad phrasal-verb lists because its aggressive meaning is idiomatic. More precisely, it is an inseparable verb + preposition expression: **at** must stay before its object.

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El perro se me abalanzó, pero su dueño lo detuvo antes de que atacara. | The dog came at me, but its owner stopped it before it attacked. |
