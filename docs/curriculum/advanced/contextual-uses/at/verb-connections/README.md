# Verb Connections with **At**

These are verb + preposition connections: **at** introduces an object and cannot move after it. They are often loosely called phrasal verbs, but they do not behave like separable particle verbs such as **turn the light off**.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Look At** and **Stare At**](01-look-at-and-stare-at.md) | Mira la foto, pero no te quedes mirando fijamente a la persona. | Look at the photo, but don't stare at the person. |
| [**Aim At** and **Point At**](02-aim-at-and-point-at.md) | Apunta al blanco y señálalo. | Aim at the target and point at it. |
| [**Be Good At**](03-be-good-at.md) | Es buena resolviendo problemas. | She's good at solving problems. |

## Core-Verb Note

**Look at** uses the core verb **look**. The idiomatic core-verb connection **come at** is separated under [Whole Expressions](../whole-expressions/README.md) because it can mean approach aggressively or attack.

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mira el blanco al que estoy apuntando y no señales al estudiante que es bueno lanzando. | Look at the target I'm aiming at, and don't point at the student who's good at throwing. |
