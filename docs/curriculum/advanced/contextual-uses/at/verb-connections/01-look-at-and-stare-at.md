# **Look At** and **Stare At**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mira la foto, pero no te quedes mirando fijamente a la persona que aparece en ella. | Look at the photo, but don't stare at the person in it. |

**Look at** directs your eyes toward something. **Stare at** means look for a long time with fixed attention and can sound rude when the object is a person.
