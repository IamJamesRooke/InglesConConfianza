# **In** or **On** Planes and Cars

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Estoy en el avión, pero mi hermano está en el carro. | I'm on the plane, but my brother is in the car. |

Passengers normally travel **on** a plane, bus, train, or other shared transport, but **in** a car or taxi. **In the plane** emphasizes the physical interior, especially with a small aircraft; **on the car** means on its exterior surface.
