# **In, At**, or **On** the Corner

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La silla está en la esquina del cuarto, yo espero en la esquina de las dos calles y el café queda en esa esquina. | The chair is in the corner of the room, I'm waiting at the corner of the two streets, and the café is on that corner. |

Use **in the corner** for an inside angle, **at the corner** for a meeting point or intersection, and **on the corner** for a building or site located there.
