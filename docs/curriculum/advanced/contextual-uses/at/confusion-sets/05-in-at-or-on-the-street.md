# **In, At**, or **On** a Street

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Hay un taxi en la calle, el café queda en la Calle 72 y nos vemos en la Calle 72 con la Séptima. | There's a taxi in the street, the café is on 72nd Street, and we'll meet at 72nd Street and Seventh Avenue. |

Use **in the street** for the roadway, **on + street name** for an address or location along it, and **at** for a specific address or intersection. English does not normally use *at the street* as a general location.
