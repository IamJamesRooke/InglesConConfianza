# **In, At**, or **On** the Table

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Estoy en la mesa, el plato está sobre la mesa y hay un cajón oculto dentro de la mesa. | I'm at the table, the plate is on the table, and there's a hidden drawer in the table. |

Use **at the table** for someone positioned there for an activity and **on the table** for its surface. **In the table** is only natural when something is inside its structure, such as silverware in a built-in drawer.
