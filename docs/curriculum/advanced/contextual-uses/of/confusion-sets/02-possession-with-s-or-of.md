# Possession with **'s** or **Of**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La leche de John está en la puerta del refrigerador. | John's milk is in the door of the refrigerator. |

English usually prefers **person + 's** for a human possessor and often uses **of** for a thing or longer phrase: **John's milk**, but **the door of the refrigerator**. Everyday English also naturally says **the refrigerator door**.
