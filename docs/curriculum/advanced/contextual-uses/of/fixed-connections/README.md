# Fixed Connections with **Of**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Because Of**](01-because-of.md) | Cancelaron la clase debido a la lluvia. | They canceled class because of the rain. |
| [**Instead Of**](02-instead-of.md) | Tomamos el bus en vez del carro. | We took the bus instead of the car. |
| [**Hear Of**](03-hear-of.md) | Había oído hablar de la empresa. | I'd heard of the company. |
| [**Take Advantage Of**](04-take-advantage-of.md) | Aprovechamos la oportunidad. | We took advantage of the opportunity. |
| [**The Best Way Of**](05-the-best-way-of.md) | Esta es la mejor forma de aprenderlo. | This is the best way of learning it. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Debido a la lluvia, aprovechamos el bus en vez del carro del que habíamos oído hablar. | Because of the rain, we took advantage of the bus instead of the car we'd heard of. |
