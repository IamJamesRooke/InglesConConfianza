# **Far Too** and Informal **Way Too**

**Far too** and **way too** strengthen the idea of excess.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Dos horas es demasiado tiempo para una reunión que podría durar veinte minutos. | Two hours is far too long for a meeting that could take twenty minutes. |

Informal conversation commonly uses **way too long**. Prefer **far too long** or simply **too long** in formal writing.
