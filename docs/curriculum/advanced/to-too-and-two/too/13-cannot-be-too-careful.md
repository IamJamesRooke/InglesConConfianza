# **Cannot Be Too Careful**

**You cannot be too careful** is a special pattern meaning that any amount of care is justified.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No se puede ser demasiado cuidadoso al verificar un número de cuenta antes de transferir dinero. | You can't be too careful when checking an account number before transferring money. |

Although the sentence is negative, it recommends maximum care.
