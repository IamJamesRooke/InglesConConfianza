# Contextual Uses of **In** and **Out**

**In** often points toward an interior, inclusion, or participation. **Out** often points toward an exterior, exclusion, disappearance, or completion.

| Section | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Core Uses](core-uses/README.md) | En enero estaré en Bogotá, dentro de la oficina, hablando en inglés. | In January, I'll be in Bogotá, inside the office, speaking in English. |
| [Core Picture](core-picture/README.md) | Déjame entrar al carro y luego déjame salir. | Let me get in the car, and then let me get out. |
| [Predictable Extensions](predictable-extensions/README.md) | Se nos acabó la leche, así que limpia el recipiente y llénalo. | We ran out of milk, so clean out the container and fill it up. |
| [Fixed Connections](fixed-connections/README.md) | Creo en el plan, estoy interesado en probarlo y me apunto. | I believe in the plan, I'm interested in trying it, and I'm in. |
| [Whole Expressions](whole-expressions/README.md) | Averigua cómo resolverlo y comprueba si todo salió bien. | Find out how to figure it out, and check whether everything worked out. |

## Whole-Topic Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Entré, respiré, descubrí que se nos había acabado el tiempo y salí corriendo. | I went in, breathed in, found out we'd run out of time, and ran out. |
| Creo en participar, así que me apunté, llené el formulario y lo entregué. | I believe in participating, so I'm in: I filled out the form and handed it in. |
| Averigua qué omitieron, señala el error y resuélvelo antes de cerrar sesión. | Find out what they left out, point out the mistake, and figure it out before logging out. |
