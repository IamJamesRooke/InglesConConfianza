# **At Night**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Trabajo de noche, pero descanso por la mañana y estudio por la tarde. | I work at night, but I rest in the morning and study in the afternoon. |

English normally says **at night**, but **in the morning, in the afternoon**, and **in the evening**. Use **in the night** only when referring to something that happens during a particular night.
