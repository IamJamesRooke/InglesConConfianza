# Contextual Uses of **At**

Spanish **en** can become **at, in**, or **on**. **At** usually treats a place or time as a point, an activity, or a general situation rather than emphasizing an interior or surface.

| Section | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Core Uses](core-uses/README.md) | Estoy en el trabajo y la reunión es a las diez. | I'm at work, and the meeting is at ten. |
| [**At / In / On** Confusion Sets](confusion-sets/README.md) | Estoy en el avión, en mi asiento, con el bolso en el piso. | I'm on the plane, in my seat, with my bag on the floor. |
| [Verb Connections](verb-connections/README.md) | Mira el blanco, apunta hacia él y señálalo. | Look at the target, aim at it, and point at it. |
| [Whole Expressions](whole-expressions/README.md) | El perro se me abalanzó, pero su dueño lo detuvo. | The dog came at me, but its owner stopped it. |

## Whole-Topic Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Nos vemos en la tienda a las diez; estaré en la esquina, no dentro de la tienda. | Meet me at the store at ten; I'll be at the corner, not in the store. |
| Estoy en el avión, sentada en mi asiento, mirando el mapa que está sobre la mesa. | I'm on the plane, sitting in my seat, looking at the map on the table. |
| Es buena apuntando al blanco, pero no te quedes mirándola fijamente. | She's good at aiming at the target, but don't stare at her. |
