# Uses of **By** and **Through**

**By** commonly places something beside a point, makes it pass that point, or sets a deadline at that point. **Through** commonly follows a path into, across the inside of, and out beyond something.

| Section | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Core Picture](core-picture/README.md) | Pasó junto al túnel y después caminó por dentro de él. | She went by the tunnel and then walked through it. |
| [Uses of **By**](by/README.md) | Envíelo antes de las doce por correo. | Send it by twelve by email. |
| [Uses of **Through**](through/README.md) | Trabajamos durante toda la noche y resolvimos el problema mediante la negociación. | We worked through the night and solved the problem through negotiation. |
| [Fixed Connections](fixed-connections/README.md) | Pensémoslo bien antes de llevarlo a cabo. | Let's think it through before we follow through. |
| [Sound and Spelling](sound-and-spelling/README.md) | Lanzó la pelota por la ventana y revisó todo minuciosamente. | She threw the ball through the window and checked everything thoroughly. |
| [Confusion Sets](confusion-sets/README.md) | Termine antes del viernes; puede trabajar hasta el jueves. | Finish by Friday; you can work until Thursday. |

## Whole-Topic Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Pase por la recepción antes de las cinco y envíeme el formulario por correo electrónico. | Go by reception by five and send me the form by email. |
| Atravesamos el túnel en carro, pasamos toda la noche revisando el informe y finalmente superamos la crisis. | We drove through the tunnel, worked through the night reviewing the report, and finally got through the crisis. |
| Aunque lanzó la pelota por la ventana, revisó minuciosamente el vidrio y no encontró daños. | Although he threw the ball through the window, he thoroughly checked the glass and found no damage. |
