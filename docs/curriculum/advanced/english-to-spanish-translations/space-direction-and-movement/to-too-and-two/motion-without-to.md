# Motion Destinations Without **To**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| Go home, come here, and arrive home before six. | Ve a casa, ven aquí y llega a casa antes de las seis. |

English omits **to** before **home, here**, and **there** after movement verbs: **go home, come here, get there, arrive home**. Ordinary destinations retain a preposition: **go to work, come to the office, arrive at the station**, and **arrive in Bogotá**.
