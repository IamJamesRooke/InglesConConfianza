# **Me Too**

Use informal **me too** as a short agreement with a positive statement.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| —Quiero ir a Cartagena. —Yo también. | “I want to go to Cartagena.” “Me too.” |

The fuller pattern repeats the matching short verb: **So do I**.

For negative agreement, neutral English uses **me neither** or a **neither** pattern:

- **I don't drive.**
- **Me neither. / Neither do I.**
