# Uses of **Up To**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Maximum](01-maximum.md) | Pueden venir hasta veinte personas. | Up to twenty people can come. |
| [Responsibility](02-its-up-to-you.md) | Depende de ti. | It's up to you. |
| [Activity](03-what-are-you-up-to.md) | ¿Qué estás haciendo? | What are you up to? |
| [Secret Activity](04-up-to-something.md) | Algo trama. | He's up to something. |
| [Standard](05-up-to-standard.md) | No cumple con la norma. | It isn't up to standard. |
| [Capable or Willing](06-be-up-to-doing.md) | No me siento capaz de hacerlo. | I don't feel up to doing it. |
| [Catch Up To](07-catch-up-to.md) | Alcancé al bus. | I caught up to the bus. |
| [Lead Up To](08-lead-up-to.md) | Los eventos previos a la reunión. | The events leading up to the meeting. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Depende de ti aceptar hasta veinte personas, siempre que su trabajo cumpla con la norma y te sientas capaz de dirigir el grupo. | It's up to you to accept up to twenty people, provided their work is up to standard and you feel up to leading the group. |
