# Spanish Translations of English **Play**

English **play** uses different Spanish structures for games, sports, instruments, media, acting, roles, pretending, and manipulation.

| Lesson | English | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| [**Play → Jugar** a Game](01-games.md) | The children are playing a game. | Los niños están jugando un juego. |
| [**Play → Jugar** a Sport](02-sports.md) | We play football on Saturdays. | Jugamos fútbol los sábados. |
| [**Play → Tocar** an Instrument](03-instruments.md) | She plays the piano. | Ella toca el piano. |
| [**Play → Reproducir / Poner** Media](04-media.md) | Play the video again. | Reproduce el video otra vez. |
| [**Play → Interpretar** a Role](05-act-a-role.md) | He plays the doctor in the series. | Él interpreta al médico en la serie. |
| [**Play a Role → Desempeñar Un Papel**](06-have-a-role.md) | Trust plays an important role. | La confianza desempeña un papel importante. |
| [**Play → Jugar A / Fingir**](07-pretend.md) | The children played doctor. | Los niños jugaron al doctor. |
| [**Play With → Jugar Con / Manipular**](08-manipulate.md) | Don't play with her feelings. | No juegues con sus sentimientos. |
