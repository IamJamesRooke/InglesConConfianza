# **Be Angry** and **Get Angry**

**Be angry** describes the emotion. **Get angry** means become angry or enojarse.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Estaba tranquilo, pero me enojé y ahora estoy enojado. | I was calm, but I got angry, and now I'm angry. |

Use **angry with someone** and **angry about something**: **She got angry with me about the delay.**
