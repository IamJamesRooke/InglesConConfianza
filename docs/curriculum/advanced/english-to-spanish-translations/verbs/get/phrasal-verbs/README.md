# Phrasal Verbs with **Get**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Get Over**](01-get-over-meaning-overcome-or-recover.md) | Superó el miedo, pero todavía no se recupera de la ruptura. | She got over her fear, but she still hasn't gotten over the breakup. |
| [**Get Along**](02-get-along-meaning-have-a-good-relationship.md) | No siempre estamos de acuerdo, pero nos llevamos bien. | We don't always agree, but we get along well. |
| [**Get Away**](03-get-away-meaning-escape.md) | Intentó escapar, pero no logró huir de la policía. | He tried to escape, but he couldn't get away from the police. |
| [**Get By**](04-get-by-meaning-manage-or-survive.md) | No ganamos mucho, pero nos alcanza para sobrevivir. | We don't earn much, but we get by. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Superé la ruptura, escapé, sobreviví con poco dinero y seguí llevándome bien con todos. | I got over the breakup, got away, got by on little money, and kept getting along with everyone. |
