# **Get Away**: Escape

**Get away** can replace **escape** when someone leaves danger, capture, or an unwanted situation.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El ladrón intentó escapar, pero no logró huir de la policía. | The thief tried to escape, but he couldn't get away from the police. |

Use **get away from** before the person, place, or danger being escaped. **Escape** can take a direct object—**escape the police**—but **get away** needs **from**: **get away from the police**.
