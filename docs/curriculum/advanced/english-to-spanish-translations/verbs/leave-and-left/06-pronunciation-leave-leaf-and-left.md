# Pronunciation: **Leave, Leaf**, and **Left**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Dejó una hoja a la izquierda antes de irse. | She left a leaf on the left before leaving. |

These are a sound contrast, not homophones. **Leave** ends in voiced `/v/`, **leaf** ends in unvoiced `/f/`, and **left** has the shorter vowel `/ɛ/` and ends in `/ft/`.
