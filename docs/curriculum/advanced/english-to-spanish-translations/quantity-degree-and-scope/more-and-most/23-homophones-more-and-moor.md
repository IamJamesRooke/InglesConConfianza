# Homophones: **More** and **Moor**

**More** and **moor**, an open area of high land or the act of securing a boat, are homophones in many accents.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Necesitamos más cuerda para amarrar el barco cerca del páramo. | We need more rope to moor the boat near the moor. |

**Most** and **must** are not homophones; their vowels differ.
