# **Get Along**

**Get along** can mean have a good relationship or manage/progress.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me llevo bien con mis compañeros y nos las arreglamos bien a pesar del retraso. | I get along with my coworkers, and we're getting along well despite the delay. |

For the progress of a project or task, **coming along well** is usually more natural.
