# **Justice, Fairness, Unjust**, and **Unfair**

**Justice** and **fairness** name the ideas; **unjust** and **unfair** describe their opposites.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La justicia exige imparcialidad, y castigarla sin escucharla sería injusto. | Justice requires fairness, and punishing her without listening would be unjust. |

**Unfair** is the more common everyday description.
