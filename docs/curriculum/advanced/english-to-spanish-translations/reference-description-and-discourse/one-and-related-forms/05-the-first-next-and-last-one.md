# **The First, Next** and **Last One**

Add **one** or **ones** after words such as **first**, **next** and **last** when they replace countable nouns.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El primer ejercicio fue fácil, los dos siguientes fueron difíciles y el último fue imposible. | The first exercise was easy, the next two were difficult, and the last one was impossible. |

With an explicit number, English normally says **the next two**, not *the next two ones*. Without the noun or a number, use forms such as **the first one**, **the next one**, **the last one**, **the first ones** and **the last ones**.
