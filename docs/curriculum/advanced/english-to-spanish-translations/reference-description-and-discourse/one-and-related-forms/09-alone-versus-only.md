# **Alone** Versus **Only**

**Alone** means **solo**, without another person. **Only** limits a number or possibility.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Diana fue sola a la conferencia porque era la única de su equipo con una entrada. | Diana went to the conference alone because she was the only one on her team with a ticket. |

The first idea concerns company; the second concerns uniqueness.
