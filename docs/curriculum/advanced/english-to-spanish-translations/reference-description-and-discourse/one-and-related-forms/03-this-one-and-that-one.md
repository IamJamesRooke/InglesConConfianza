# **This One** and **That One**

Use **this one** for a nearby singular item and **that one** for a more distant singular item.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Esta taza está rota, pero esa está en perfecto estado. | This cup is broken, but that one is in perfect condition. |

English can also say simply **this** and **that** when the item is clear. Adding **one** emphasizes the choice among similar objects.
