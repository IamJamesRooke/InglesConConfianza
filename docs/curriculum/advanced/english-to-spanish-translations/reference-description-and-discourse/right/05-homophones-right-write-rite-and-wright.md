# Homophones: **Right, Write, Rite**, and **Wright**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El dramaturgo escribirá a la derecha el nombre correcto del rito. | The playwright will write the right name of the rite on the right. |

**Right**, **write**, **rite**, and **wright** are pronounced `/raɪt/`. **Right** can mean *derecha*, *correcto*, or *derecho*; **write** means *escribir*; a **rite** is a formal ceremony. A **wright** is a maker or builder, but the word is now mainly seen in compounds such as **playwright** and in surnames.
