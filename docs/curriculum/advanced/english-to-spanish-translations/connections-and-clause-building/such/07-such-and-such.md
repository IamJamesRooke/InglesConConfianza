# **Such and Such**

Informal **such and such** replaces a name or detail that is unknown, unimportant, or deliberately omitted.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Dijo que nos reuniéramos con cierta persona en cierto lugar, pero no dio detalles claros. | He said to meet so-and-so at such-and-such a place, but he didn't give clear details. |
