# Uses of **Either, Neither**, and **Whether**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Either...Or**](01-either-or.md) | Podemos reunirnos o el lunes o el martes. | We can meet either Monday or Tuesday. |
| [**Neither...Nor**](02-neither-nor.md) | Ni el lunes ni el martes me sirven. | Neither Monday nor Tuesday works for me. |
| [**Either One**](03-either-one.md) | —¿Cuál quieres? —Cualquiera de los dos. | “Which one do you want?” “Either one.” |
| [**Neither One**](04-neither-one.md) | —¿Cuál de los dos libros compraste? —Ninguno de los dos. | “Which of the two books did you buy?” “Neither one.” |
| [**Either Of**](05-either-of.md) | Cualquiera de los dos libros sirve para la clase. | Either of the two books works for the class. |
| [**Neither Of**](06-neither-of.md) | Ninguno de los dos libros está disponible. | Neither of the two books is available. |
| [**Both, Either**, and **Neither**](07-both-either-and-neither.md) | Ambos libros son buenos, cualquiera sirve y ninguno está en la biblioteca. | Both books are good, either one works, and neither one is in the library. |
| [Positive Agreement: **Me Too** and **So Do I**](08-positive-agreement-me-too.md) | —Me gusta este libro. —A mí también. | “I like this book.” “Me too. / So do I.” |
| [Negative Agreement: **Me Neither** and **Neither Do I**](09-negative-agreement-me-neither.md) | —No me gusta ese libro. —A mí tampoco. | “I don't like that book.” “Me neither. / Neither do I.” |
| [**Not...Either**](10-not-either.md) | Laura no va a asistir y Carlos tampoco va a ir. | Laura isn't going to attend, and Carlos isn't going either. |
| [**Any** Versus **Either**](11-any-versus-either.md) | Puedes escoger cualquiera de los dos libros o cualquier libro de toda la biblioteca. | You can choose either book or any book in the entire library. |
| [**Either Way**](12-either-way.md) | Podemos ir en bus o caminar; de cualquier manera, llegaremos antes de las ocho. | We can take the bus or walk; either way, we'll arrive before eight. |
| [**Neither Here Nor There**](13-neither-here-nor-there.md) | Que no te guste el color no viene al caso; estamos hablando del precio. | The fact that you don't like the color is neither here nor there; we're discussing the price. |
| [**Whether...Or Not**](14-whether-or-not.md) | No sé si Laura va a asistir o no. | I don't know whether Laura's going to attend or not. |
| [**Whether A or B**](15-whether-a-or-b.md) | Tenemos que decidir si viajamos hoy o mañana. | We have to decide whether to travel today or tomorrow. |
| [**If** Versus **Whether**](16-if-versus-whether.md) | Hablamos de si debíamos ir y de si debíamos esperar. | We talked about whether to go and whether to wait. |
| [**Whether It Be**](17-whether-it-be.md) | Ya sea por dinero o por tiempo, tenemos que reducir el alcance. | Whether it be money or time, we've gotta reduce the scope. |
| [Homophones: **Whether** and **Weather**](18-homophones-whether-and-weather.md) | No sé si el clima permitirá que viajemos. | I don't know whether the weather will let us travel. |
| [Pronunciation: **Either** and **Neither**](19-pronunciation-either-and-neither.md) | Cualquiera de las dos pronunciaciones sirve; ninguna es incorrecta. | Either pronunciation works; neither one is wrong. |
| [**Whether, Either**, and **Neither** Together](20-whether-either-or-neither.md) | No sé si elegir uno de los dos o ninguno. | I don't know whether to choose either one or neither one. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ambos libros son buenos: cualquiera de los dos sirve, pero ninguno está disponible. | Both books are good: either one works, but neither one is available. |
| —No sé si viajará por el clima. —Yo tampoco; no me sirve ni el lunes ni el martes. | “I don't know whether she'll travel because of the weather.” “Me neither; neither Monday nor Tuesday works for me.” |
| No sé si escoger uno de los dos o ninguno; de cualquier manera, cualquiera de las dos pronunciaciones es correcta. | I don't know whether to choose either one or neither one; either way, either pronunciation is correct. |
