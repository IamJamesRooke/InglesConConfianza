# **Like** Meaning **Gustar**

Use **like + person or thing** for something someone enjoys or views positively.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me gusta este café porque tiene un sabor suave. | I like this coffee because it has a mild flavor. |

Spanish places the liked thing after **gusta/gustan**. English makes the person who experiences the feeling the subject:

- **Me gusta el café → I like coffee.**
- **A Laura le gustan estos libros → Laura likes these books.**
