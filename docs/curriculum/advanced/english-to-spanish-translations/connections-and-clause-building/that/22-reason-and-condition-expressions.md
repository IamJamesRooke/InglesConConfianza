# Reason and Condition Expressions

Several expressions use **that** to introduce a reason, fact or condition:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Dado que el cliente aprobó el diseño, podemos publicarlo. | Given that the client approved the design, we can publish it. |
| Considerando que es festivo, hay bastante tráfico. | Considering that it's a holiday, there's quite a lot of traffic. |
| Siempre que todos estén de acuerdo, firmaremos hoy. | Provided that everyone agrees, we'll sign today. |

**In that** gives a specific respect in which a statement is true:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Los dos trabajos son parecidos porque ambos requieren contacto diario con clientes. | The two jobs are similar in that both require daily contact with clients. |
