# **That Of** and **Those Of**

In formal comparisons, **that of** and **those of** prevent repetition.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El clima de Bogotá es más frío que el de Cartagena. | Bogotá's climate is colder than that of Cartagena. |
| Los costos de este año son mayores que los del año pasado. | This year's costs are higher than those of last year. |

Use singular **that of** for one repeated thing and plural **those of** for several.
