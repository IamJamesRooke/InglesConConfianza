# Noun + Connector **That**

After a noun, **that** can explain the content of a fact, idea, claim, possibility, evidence, news or belief.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La posibilidad de que el vuelo sea cancelado cambia nuestro plan. | The possibility that the flight will be canceled changes our plan. |

Compare content **that** with relative **that**:

- **the news that Laura resigned**: the news is “Laura resigned”;
- **the news that surprised us**: the news performed the action **surprised**.
