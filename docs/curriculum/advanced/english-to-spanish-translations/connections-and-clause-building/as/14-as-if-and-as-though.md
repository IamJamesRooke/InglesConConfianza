# **As If** and **As Though**

Use **as if** or **as though** to describe how something seems.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Habla como si supiera la respuesta, pero ni siquiera leyó la pregunta. | He talks as if he knew the answer, but he didn't even read the question. |

The past form **knew** can mark an unreal comparison. **As though** has the same core meaning and may sound slightly more formal.
