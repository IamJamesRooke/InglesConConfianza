# **As Far As**

Use **as far as** for physical extent or to limit a statement to someone's knowledge or concern.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Hasta donde sé, el sendero llega hasta el río, pero no pasa de allí. | As far as I know, the trail goes as far as the river, but no farther. |

Common frames include **as far as I know**, **as far as I can tell**, and **as far as I'm concerned**.
