# Spanish Translations of English **despite and in spite of**

| Lesson | English | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| [noun](01-noun.md) | Despite the rain, we left. | A pesar de la lluvia, salimos. |
| [ing](02-ing.md) | In spite of being tired, she worked. | A pesar de estar cansada, trabajó. |
| [clause-contrast](03-clause-contrast.md) | Although it rained, we left. | Aunque llovió, salimos. |
| [fact-that](04-fact-that.md) | Despite the fact that it rained, we left. | A pesar de que llovió, salimos. |
