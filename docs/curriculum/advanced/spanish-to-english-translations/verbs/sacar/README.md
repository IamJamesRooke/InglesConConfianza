# Translations of Spanish **Sacar**

Spanish **sacar** covers removal, obtaining results, photographs, grades, deductions, publication, and bringing something out.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Sacar → Take Out / Remove**](01-take-out-or-remove.md) | Saca la basura y saca las llaves del bolso. | Take out the trash and remove the keys from the bag. |
| [**Sacar → Get / Obtain**](02-get-or-obtain.md) | Saqué una cita para el lunes. | I got an appointment for Monday. |
| [**Sacar → Take** a Photo](03-take-a-photo.md) | Saqué una foto del atardecer. | I took a photo of the sunset. |
| [**Sacar → Get** a Grade](04-get-a-grade.md) | Saqué cinco en el examen. | I got a five on the exam. |
| [**Sacar → Figure Out / Deduce**](05-figure-out-or-deduce.md) | No pude sacar la respuesta con esos datos. | I couldn't figure out the answer from that data. |
| [**Sacar → Bring Out / Release**](06-bring-out-or-release.md) | La empresa sacó un producto nuevo. | The company released a new product. |
