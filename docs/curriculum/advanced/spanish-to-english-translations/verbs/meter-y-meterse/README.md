# Translations of Spanish **Meter** and **Meterse**

Spanish **meter** and **meterse** cover putting inside, inserting, involving, entering, interfering, fitting into schedules, and getting into trouble.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Meter → Put In / Put Into**](01-put-in.md) | Mete los documentos en la carpeta. | Put the documents in the folder. |
| [**Meter → Insert**](02-insert.md) | Meta la tarjeta en la máquina. | Insert the card into the machine. |
| [**Meterse En → Get Into / Enter**](03-get-into.md) | Se metió en el carro rápidamente. | She got into the car quickly. |
| [**Meter A Alguien En → Involve**](04-involve.md) | No me metas en sus problemas. | Don't involve me in their problems. |
| [**Meterse En → Interfere / Meddle**](05-interfere.md) | No te metas en mi vida. | Don't interfere in my life. |
| [**Meter → Fit / Squeeze In**](06-fit-in-a-schedule.md) | Puedo meterte en la agenda a las cuatro. | I can fit you into the schedule at four. |
| [**Meterse A / En → Get Into**](07-get-involved.md) | Se metió a estudiar medicina. | She went into medicine. |
| [**Meterse En Problemas → Get Into Trouble**](08-get-in-trouble.md) | Se metió en problemas por mentir. | He got into trouble for lying. |
