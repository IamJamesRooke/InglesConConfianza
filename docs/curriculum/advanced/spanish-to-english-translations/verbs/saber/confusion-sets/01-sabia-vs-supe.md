# **Sabía** and **Supe**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No sabía la verdad, pero ayer supe qué pasó durante la reunión. | I didn't know the truth, but yesterday I found out what happened during the meeting. |

Imperfect **sabía** describes an existing state of knowledge (**knew**). Preterite **supe** often marks entry into that state (**found out/learned**).
