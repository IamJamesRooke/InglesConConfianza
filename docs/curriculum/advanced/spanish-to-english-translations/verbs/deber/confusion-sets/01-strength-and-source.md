# **Must, Have To, Should**, and **Be Supposed To**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Debes presentar el informe hoy, deberías revisarlo primero y debes llegar a la oficina a las diez. | You must submit the report today, should review it first, and are supposed to arrive at the office at ten. |

**Must/have to** express strong necessity, **should** gives advice, and **be supposed to** points to a rule, arrangement, or expectation.
