# **Deber Hacer → Must / Have To**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Debo terminar el informe hoy porque el cliente lo necesita mañana. | I must finish the report today because the client needs it tomorrow. |

Both **must** and **have to** express strong necessity. **Have to** is more versatile in conversation and across tenses; **must** often sounds formal, emphatic, or like the speaker is presenting the requirement.
