# Negative Obligation Contrasts

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Mustn't**: Prohibition](01-mustnt-prohibition.md) | No debes entrar. | You mustn't enter. |
| [**Don't Have To**: No Necessity](02-dont-have-to.md) | No tienes que esperar. | You don't have to wait. |
| [**Shouldn't**: Advice Against](03-shouldnt.md) | No deberías preocuparte. | You shouldn't worry. |
| [Past No Necessity](04-didnt-have-to-and-neednt-have.md) | No tuve que pagar y no era necesario haber pagado. | I didn't have to pay, and I needn't have paid. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No debes entrar, no tienes que esperar afuera y no deberías preocuparte. | You mustn't enter, you don't have to wait outside, and you shouldn't worry. |
