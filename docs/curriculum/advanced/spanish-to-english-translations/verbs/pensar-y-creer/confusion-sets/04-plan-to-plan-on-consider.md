# **Plan To, Plan On**, and **Consider**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Planeo viajar, cuento con viajar en diciembre y estoy considerando quedarme un mes. | I plan to travel, plan on traveling in December, and am considering staying for a month. |

Use **plan to + base verb**, but **plan on + -ing** and **consider + -ing**. **Plan on** can emphasize expecting or relying on an action as part of the plan.
