# **I've Met** and **I've Known**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| He conocido a Ana, pero conozco a Carlos desde hace diez años. | I've met Ana, but I've known Carlos for ten years. |

**I've met Ana** confirms at least one encounter. **I've known Carlos for ten years** describes a relationship continuing across that period. Do not use *I've met Carlos for ten years*.
