# Translations of Spanish **Coger**

In Colombian Spanish, **coger** is an ordinary high-frequency verb for taking, catching, picking up, grabbing, and transport. Its regional sexual meaning requires explicit awareness.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Coger → Take**](01-take.md) | Coge una silla y siéntate. | Take a chair and sit down. |
| [**Coger → Catch**](02-catch.md) | Cogió la pelota con una mano. | She caught the ball with one hand. |
| [**Coger → Pick Up**](03-pick-up.md) | Cogí las llaves del piso. | I picked up the keys from the floor. |
| [**Coger → Grab**](04-grab.md) | Cogió el bolso y salió corriendo. | She grabbed the bag and ran out. |
| [**Coger → Take / Catch** Transport](05-transport.md) | Cogemos el bus a las siete. | We take the bus at seven. |
| [**Coger → Catch** an Illness](06-catch-an-illness.md) | Cogí una gripa durante el viaje. | I caught a cold during the trip. |
| [**Coger A Alguien → Catch / Reach**](07-coger-a-alguien.md) | La policía cogió al ladrón. | The police caught the thief. |
| [Regional Warning for **Coger**](08-regional-warning.md) | En Colombia, “coger el bus” es una expresión normal. | In Colombia, **coger el bus** is a normal expression. |
