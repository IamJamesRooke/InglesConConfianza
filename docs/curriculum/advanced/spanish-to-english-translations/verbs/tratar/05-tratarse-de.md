# **Tratarse De → Be About**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿De qué se trata el libro? | What is the book about? |

Impersonal **tratarse de** identifies the subject or essential nature of something. English commonly uses **be about** or **involve**. With an explicit subject, Spanish normally uses **tratar de**: **El libro trata de una familia bogotana.**
