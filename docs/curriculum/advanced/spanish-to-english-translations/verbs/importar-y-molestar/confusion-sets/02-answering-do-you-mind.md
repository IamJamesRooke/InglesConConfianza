# Answering **Do You Mind...?**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| —¿Te importa si me siento aquí? —No, adelante. | “Do you mind if I sit here?” “No, go ahead.” |

Answer the objection: **No, I don't mind** grants permission; **Yes, I do mind** refuses it. To avoid ambiguity, add **go ahead** or **I'd rather you didn't**.
