# Translations of Spanish **Enterarse De**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [find-out](01-find-out.md) | Me enteré de la noticia ayer. | I found out the news yesterday. |
| [hear](02-hear.md) | Me enteré de que Ana se fue. | I heard that Ana left. |
| [learn](03-learn.md) | Se enteró del cambio por correo. | She learned about the change by email. |
| [false-enter](04-false-enter.md) | Enterarse no significa entrar. | **Enterarse** does not mean **enter**. |
