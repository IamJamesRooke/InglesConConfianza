# **Desear/Ojalá → Wish**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ojalá tuviera más tiempo y desearía no tener que salir hoy. | I wish I had more time, and I wish I didn't have to leave today. |

Use **wish + past form** for a present situation you want to be different. **Wish someone + noun** is used in greetings: **I wish you good luck**. For a possible desired future result, **hope** is usually more natural.
