# Translations of Spanish **Quedar** and **Quedarse**

Spanish **quedar** and **quedarse** map to several English structures involving what remains, location, fit, arrangements, staying, and resulting appearance.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Quedar → Remain / Be Left**](01-remain-and-be-left.md) | Quedan dos entradas. | Two tickets remain. / There are two tickets left. |
| [**Quedar → Fit / Suit**](02-fit-and-suit.md) | La chaqueta me queda bien. | The jacket fits me well. / The jacket looks good on me. |
| [**Quedar → Be Located**](03-location.md) | El banco queda frente al parque. | The bank is located across from the park. |
| [**Quedar → Arrange to Meet**](04-arrange-to-meet.md) | Quedamos a las ocho frente al teatro. | We arranged to meet at eight in front of the theater. |
| [**Quedarse → Stay**](05-stay.md) | Me quedo en casa esta noche. | I'm staying home tonight. |
| [**Quedar → Turn Out / Look**](06-turn-out-or-look.md) | La presentación quedó muy bien. | The presentation turned out very well. |
