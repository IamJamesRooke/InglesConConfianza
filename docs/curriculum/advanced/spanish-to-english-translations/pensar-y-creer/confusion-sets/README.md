# **Pensar** and **Creer** Confusion Sets

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Think** and **Believe**](01-think-vs-believe.md) | Creo que dice la verdad y le creo. | I think she's telling the truth, and I believe her. |
| [**Think About** and **Think Of**](02-think-about-vs-think-of.md) | Considera el plan y dime qué opinión te merece. | Think about the plan and tell me what you think of it. |
| [**Believe** and **Believe In**](03-believe-vs-believe-in.md) | Le creo a Ana y creo en ella. | I believe Ana, and I believe in her. |
| [**Plan To, Plan On**, and **Consider**](04-plan-to-plan-on-consider.md) | Planeo viajar, cuento con viajar y estoy considerando quedarme. | I plan to travel, plan on traveling, and am considering staying. |
| [**Think So** and **Believe So**](05-think-so-and-believe-so.md) | —¿Funcionará? —Creo que sí. | “Will it work?” “I think so.” |
| [Pronunciation: **Think** and **Sink**](06-pronunciation-think-and-sink.md) | Creo que el barco se hundirá. | I think the ship will sink. |
| [Spelling and Sound: **Believe** and **Belief**](07-believe-and-belief.md) | Creo en esa creencia. | I believe in that belief. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Creo que Ana dice la verdad, le creo y creo en ella, así que pienso seguir su plan. | I think Ana is telling the truth, believe her, and believe in her, so I plan to follow her plan. |
