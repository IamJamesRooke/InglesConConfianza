# Nouns Related to **Pensar** and **Creer**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Compartió una idea, explicó su plan y defendió su creencia. | She shared a thought, explained her plan, and defended her belief. |

**Pensamiento** can be **thought** or **thinking**, **plan** is **plan**, and **creencia** is **belief**. The verb is **believe**; the noun is **belief**.
