# **Owe** and **Own**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Le debo dinero a Ana, pero soy dueño del carro que ella quiere comprar. | I owe Ana money, but I own the car she wants to buy. |

**Owe** `/oʊ/` means have a debt or obligation. **Own** `/oʊn/` means possess. The final `/n/` distinguishes the sound as well as the meaning.

| Form | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| verb | deber | **owe** |
| verb | poseer / ser dueño de | **own** |
| person | dueño/a / propietario/a | **owner** |
| state or right | propiedad / titularidad | **ownership** |
