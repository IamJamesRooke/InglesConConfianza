# Homophones: **Ought** and **Aught**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Hasta donde sabemos, deberíamos revisar la cuenta otra vez. | For aught we know, we ought to check the bill again. |

**Ought** and **aught** are pronounced alike, but **aught** meaning anything is rare or old-fashioned and mainly survives in expressions such as **for aught I know**.

The practical contrast is spelling and meaning: **ought to** expresses what should happen.
