# **Own, Owner**, and **Ownership**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ana es la dueña, es propietaria de la tienda y conserva la propiedad total del negocio. | Ana is the owner, owns the store, and retains full ownership of the business. |

**Own** is the verb for possessing something, **owner** is the person or organization that possesses it, and **ownership** is the state or legal right of possession.

Spanish chooses among **ser dueño/a de, poseer, ser propietario/a de**, and **propiedad**, depending on the sentence.
