# Duty and Related Words

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Deber → Duty**](01-duty.md) | Es mi deber proteger esta información. | It's my duty to protect this information. |
| [**Deberes → Duties / Homework**](02-duties-and-homework.md) | Cumplió con sus deberes y luego hizo la tarea. | She performed her duties and then did her homework. |
| [**Obligación → Obligation**](03-obligation.md) | Tenemos la obligación legal de informar el cambio. | We have a legal obligation to report the change. |
| [**Responsabilidad → Responsibility**](04-responsibility.md) | Es mi responsabilidad revisar el informe. | It's my responsibility to review the report. |
| [Modern Uses of **Shall**](05-shall.md) | ¿Empezamos? Empezaremos de inmediato. | Shall we start? We shall begin immediately. |
| [**Own, Owner**, and **Ownership**](06-own-owner-and-ownership.md) | Ana es la dueña, es propietaria de la tienda y conserva la propiedad total. | Ana is the owner, owns the store, and retains full ownership. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Es mi deber pagarle a la dueña lo que le debo antes de asumir la propiedad del local. | It's my duty to pay the owner what I owe her before taking ownership of the premises. |
