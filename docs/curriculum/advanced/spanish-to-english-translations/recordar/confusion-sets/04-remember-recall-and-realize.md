# **Remember, Recall**, and **Realize**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Recordé el nombre, logré recordar el número y me di cuenta del error. | I remembered the name, managed to recall the number, and realized the mistake. |

**Remember/recall** retrieve information from memory. **Realize** means become aware of or understand a fact, so it normally translates **darse cuenta**, not **recordar**.
