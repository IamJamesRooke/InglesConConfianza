# **Remind Of, Remind About**, and **Remind To**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Esta canción me recuerda a Ana; recuérdame la cita y recuérdame llamarla. | This song reminds me of Ana; remind me about the appointment and remind me to call her. |

Use **remind someone of + association**, **remind someone about + topic/event**, and **remind someone to + action**.
