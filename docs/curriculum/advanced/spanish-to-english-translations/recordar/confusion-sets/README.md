# **Recordar** Confusion Sets

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Remember** and **Remind**](01-remember-vs-remind.md) | Recordé la cita y se la recordé a Ana. | I remembered the appointment and reminded Ana about it. |
| [**Remember To Do** and **Remember Doing**](02-remember-to-do-vs-doing.md) | Recordé llamar a Ana y recuerdo haberla llamado. | I remembered to call Ana, and I remember calling her. |
| [**Remind Of, Remind About**, and **Remind To**](03-remind-patterns.md) | Me recuerda a Ana; recuérdame la cita y recuérdame llamar. | It reminds me of Ana; remind me about the appointment and remind me to call. |
| [**Remember, Recall**, and **Realize**](04-remember-recall-and-realize.md) | Recordé el nombre, logré recordar el número y me di cuenta del error. | I remembered the name, recalled the number, and realized the mistake. |
| [**Remember** and **Forget**](05-remember-vs-forget.md) | Recuerda las llaves y no olvides cerrar. | Remember the keys, and don't forget to lock up. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Recordé llamar a Ana, recuerdo haber hablado con ella y le recordé la reunión antes de darme cuenta del error. | I remembered to call Ana, remember talking to her, and reminded her about the meeting before realizing the mistake. |
