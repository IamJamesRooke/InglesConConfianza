# **Recuerdo** as a Noun

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tengo un recuerdo bonito de Cartagena y compré un recuerdo para Ana. | I have a beautiful memory of Cartagena, and I bought a souvenir for Ana. |

Spanish **recuerdo** can mean a mental **memory**, a physical **souvenir**, or a greeting in plural expressions such as **recuerdos a Ana → give my regards to Ana**.
