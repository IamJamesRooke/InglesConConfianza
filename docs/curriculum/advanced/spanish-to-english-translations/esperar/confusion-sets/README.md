# **Esperar** Confusion Sets

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Wait, Hope**, and **Expect**](01-wait-hope-and-expect.md) | Espero el bus, espero que no llueva y calculo que llegará a las diez. | I'm waiting for the bus, I hope it doesn't rain, and I expect it to arrive at ten. |
| [**Hope** and **Wish**](02-hope-vs-wish.md) | Espero que venga; ojalá ya estuviera aquí. | I hope she comes; I wish she were already here. |
| [**Wait For** and **Expect**](03-wait-for-vs-expect.md) | Espero a Ana porque espero que llegue a las diez. | I'm waiting for Ana because I expect her to arrive at ten. |
| [**Can't Wait** and **Look Forward To**](04-cant-wait-and-look-forward-to.md) | Tengo muchas ganas de conocerla y espero con ilusión nuestra reunión. | I can't wait to meet her, and I'm looking forward to our meeting. |
| [Homophones: **Wait** and **Weight**](05-homophones-wait-and-weight.md) | Espera mientras reviso el peso de la caja. | Wait while I check the weight of the box. |
