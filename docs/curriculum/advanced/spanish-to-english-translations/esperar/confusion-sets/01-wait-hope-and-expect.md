# **Wait, Hope**, and **Expect**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Espero el bus, espero que no llueva y calculo que llegará a las diez. | I'm waiting for the bus, I hope it doesn't rain, and I expect it to arrive at ten. |

Use **wait** for remaining until something happens, **hope** for wanting a possible result, and **expect** for believing a result is probable or planned.
