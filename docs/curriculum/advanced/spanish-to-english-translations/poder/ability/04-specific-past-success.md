# Specific Past Success: **Was Able To**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Aunque la puerta estaba trabada, ayer pude abrirla. | Although the door was stuck, I was able to open it yesterday. |

For one successful completed action, **was/were able to** is often clearer than **could**. In the negative, **couldn't** works naturally: **I couldn't open it**.
