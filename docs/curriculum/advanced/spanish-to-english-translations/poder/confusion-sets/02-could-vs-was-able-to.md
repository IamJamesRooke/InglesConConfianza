# **Could** vs **Was Able To**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| De niño podía nadar, y ayer logré cruzar el río por primera vez. | As a child, I could swim, and yesterday I was able to cross the river for the first time. |

**Could** describes general past ability. **Was/were able to** or **managed to** normally marks one completed success. Negative **couldn't** works for a failed attempt.
