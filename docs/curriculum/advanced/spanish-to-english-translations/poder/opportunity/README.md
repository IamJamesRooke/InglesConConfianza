# **Poder** as Opportunity

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Present Opportunity](01-get-to.md) | Puedo trabajar desde casa. | I get to work from home. |
| [Past Opportunity](02-got-to.md) | Pude conocer Cartagena. | I got to visit Cartagena. |
| [Missed Opportunity](03-didnt-get-to.md) | No pude hablar con Ana. | I didn't get to speak with Ana. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ayer pude visitar el museo, pero no alcancé a ver la exposición nueva. | Yesterday I got to visit the museum, but I didn't get to see the new exhibition. |
