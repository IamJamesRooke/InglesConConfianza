# Possible Events: **May, Might, Could**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Puede que Ana llegue tarde. | Ana may arrive late. |
| Podría haber tráfico. | There might be traffic. |
| El paquete podría llegar hoy. | The package could arrive today. |

**May, might**, and **could** can all mark uncertain possibility. They do not mean ability in these examples.
