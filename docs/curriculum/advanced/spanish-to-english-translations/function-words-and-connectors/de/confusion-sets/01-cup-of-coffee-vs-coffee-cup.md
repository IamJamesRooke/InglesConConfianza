# **Cup Of Coffee** and **Coffee Cup**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La taza de café está llena, pero la taza para café está rota. | The cup of coffee is full, but the coffee cup is broken. |

**A cup of coffee** identifies the contents. **A coffee cup** identifies the type or purpose of the container. Spanish may use **taza de café** for either, so context decides.
