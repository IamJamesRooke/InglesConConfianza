# **Importar** and **Molestar** Confusion Sets

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**It Doesn't Matter, I Don't Care**, and **I Don't Mind**](01-doesnt-matter-dont-care-dont-mind.md) | No importa, no me interesa y no me molesta. | It doesn't matter, I don't care, and I don't mind. |
| [Answering **Do You Mind...?**](02-answering-do-you-mind.md) | —¿Te importa si me siento? —No, adelante. | “Do you mind if I sit down?” “No, go ahead.” |
| [**Bother, Disturb**, and **Annoy**](03-bother-disturb-and-annoy.md) | Perdón por interrumpirte; el ruido me está fastidiando. | Sorry to disturb you; the noise is annoying me. |
| [False Cognate: **Molestar** and **Molest**](04-false-cognate-molestar-and-molest.md) | El ruido me molesta. | The noise bothers me. |
| [**Care About** and **Care For**](05-care-about-vs-care-for.md) | Me importa Ana y la cuido cuando está enferma. | I care about Ana and care for her when she's sick. |
| [Homophones: **Mind** and **Mined**](06-homophones-mind-and-mined.md) | No me importa dónde extrajeron el carbón. | I don't mind where they mined the coal. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No me molesta esperar y no importa dónde, pero me importa Ana y no quiero molestarla. | I don't mind waiting, and it doesn't matter where, but I care about Ana and don't want to bother her. |
