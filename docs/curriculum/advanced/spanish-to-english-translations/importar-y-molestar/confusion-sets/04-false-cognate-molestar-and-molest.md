# False Cognate: **Molestar** and **Molest**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El ruido me molesta, así que voy a pedir que bajen el volumen. | The noise bothers me, so I'm going to ask them to turn it down. |

English **molest** does not mean ordinary annoyance. It normally refers to sexual abuse or assault, especially of a child. Use **bother, disturb, annoy**, or **irritate** for everyday Spanish **molestar**.
