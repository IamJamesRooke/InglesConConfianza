# **It Doesn't Matter, I Don't Care**, and **I Don't Mind**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No importa cuál elijamos; no me interesa el color y no me molesta esperar. | It doesn't matter which one we choose; I don't care about the color, and I don't mind waiting. |

**It doesn't matter** says the choice or fact has no effect. **I don't care** says the speaker has no interest and can sound dismissive. **I don't mind** says the speaker does not object.
