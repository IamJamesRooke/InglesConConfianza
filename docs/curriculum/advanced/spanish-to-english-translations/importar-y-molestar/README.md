# Translations of Spanish **Importar** and **Molestar**

Spanish **importar** and **molestar** overlap in questions with **mind**, but their other meanings separate into importance, care, objection, inconvenience, interruption, and annoyance.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Importar → Matter**](01-importar-meaning-matter.md) | Esto importa mucho. | This matters a lot. |
| [**Importar → Care**](02-importar-meaning-care.md) | Me importa lo que pase. | I care about what happens. |
| [**Importar/Molestar → Mind**](03-mind.md) | ¿Te importa si abro la ventana? | Do you mind if I open the window? |
| [**Molestar → Bother**](04-molestar-meaning-bother.md) | Perdón por molestarte. | Sorry to bother you. |
| [**Molestar → Disturb**](05-molestar-meaning-disturb.md) | No molestes a Ana mientras trabaja. | Don't disturb Ana while she's working. |
| [**Molestar → Annoy/Irritate**](06-annoy-and-irritate.md) | Ese ruido me molesta. | That noise annoys me. |
| [Literal **Importar → Import**](07-importar-goods.md) | La empresa importa café colombiano. | The company imports Colombian coffee. |
| [**Matter, Care, Mind**, and **Bother**](confusion-sets/README.md) | No me importa esperar, pero me molesta el ruido. | I don't mind waiting, but the noise bothers me. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Esto me importa porque importa mucho para el proyecto. | I care about this because it matters a lot to the project. |
| ¿Te importa si cierro la puerta? No quiero molestarte, pero el ruido me molesta. | Do you mind if I close the door? I don't want to bother you, but the noise annoys me. |
