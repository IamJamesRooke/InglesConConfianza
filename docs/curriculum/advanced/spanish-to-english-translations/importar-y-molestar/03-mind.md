# **Importar/Molestar → Mind**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿Te importa si abro la ventana? No, no me molesta. | Do you mind if I open the window? No, I don't mind. |

Use **mind + noun/-ing** for objection or inconvenience. After **Do you mind if...?**, **No** grants permission because it means “I do not object.”
