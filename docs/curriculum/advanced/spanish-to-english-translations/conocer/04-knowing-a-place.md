# Knowing a Place

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Conozco bien Bogotá porque he estado allí muchas veces. | I know Bogotá well because I've been there many times. |

Use **know + place** for familiarity with it. Use **have been to + place** to state the life experience of visiting it. **Conocí Bogotá en 2018** is usually **I visited/saw Bogotá for the first time in 2018**, not *I knew Bogotá*.
