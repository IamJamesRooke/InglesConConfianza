# **De** Translation Confusion Sets

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Cup Of Coffee** and **Coffee Cup**](01-cup-of-coffee-vs-coffee-cup.md) | La taza de café está vacía, pero la taza para café está limpia. | The cup of coffee is empty, but the coffee cup is clean. |
| [**Of** and **From**](02-of-vs-from.md) | La estatua de piedra vino de Italia. | The stone statue came from Italy. |
| [**About** and **Of**](03-about-vs-of.md) | Me enteré del libro, pero nunca había oído hablar del autor. | I heard about the book, but I'd never heard of the author. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me enteré del libro de Ana, una historia sobre una familia de Colombia escrita por una autora de Bogotá. | I heard about Ana's book, a story about a family from Colombia written by an author from Bogotá. |
