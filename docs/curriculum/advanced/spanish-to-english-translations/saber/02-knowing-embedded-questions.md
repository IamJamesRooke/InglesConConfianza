# Knowing What, Where, When, Why, or Whether

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Sé dónde vive y por qué se fue, pero no sé si regresará. | I know where she lives and why she left, but I don't know whether she'll return. |

After **know**, keep statement word order: **I know where she lives**, not *I know where does she live*. Spanish **si** becomes **if** or **whether** in an embedded yes/no question.
