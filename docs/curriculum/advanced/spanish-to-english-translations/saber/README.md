# Translations of Spanish **Saber**

Spanish **saber** covers factual knowledge, knowing how to do something, becoming informed, and several fixed expressions. English chooses among **know, know how, can, find out, learn, hear**, and **taste like**.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Knowing Facts and Answers](01-knowing-facts-and-answers.md) | Sé la respuesta y sé que Ana viene. | I know the answer, and I know Ana is coming. |
| [Knowing What, Where, When, Why, or Whether](02-knowing-embedded-questions.md) | Sé dónde vive, pero no sé si viene. | I know where she lives, but I don't know whether she's coming. |
| [**Saber + Infinitive**](03-saber-plus-infinitive.md) | Sé nadar y cocinar. | I can swim and cook. |
| [**Supe → Found Out**](04-supe-meaning-found-out.md) | Ayer supe la verdad. | Yesterday I found out the truth. |
| [**Saber De**](05-saber-de.md) | Sé bastante de diseño, pero no sé nada de Ana. | I know a lot about design, but I haven't heard anything from Ana. |
| [**Saberse** Something](06-saberse-something.md) | Me sé la canción de memoria. | I know the song by heart. |
| [**Saber A → Taste Like**](07-saber-a-meaning-taste-like.md) | Esto sabe a chocolate. | This tastes like chocolate. |
| [Fixed Expressions with **Saber**](08-fixed-expressions.md) | Que yo sepa, nadie sabe qué pasó. | As far as I know, nobody knows what happened. |
| [**Saber, Conocer, Know**, and **Find Out**](confusion-sets/README.md) | Conocía a Ana, sabía la respuesta y ayer supe la verdad. | I knew Ana, knew the answer, and found out the truth yesterday. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Conozco a Ana, sé dónde trabaja y sé usar el sistema que diseñó. | I know Ana, know where she works, and know how to use the system she designed. |
| No sabía la respuesta, pero ayer supe la verdad y ahora ya la sé. | I didn't know the answer, but yesterday I found out the truth, and now I know it. |
| Que yo sepa, ella sabe mucho de café y puede reconocer uno que sabe a chocolate. | As far as I know, she knows a lot about coffee and can recognize one that tastes like chocolate. |
