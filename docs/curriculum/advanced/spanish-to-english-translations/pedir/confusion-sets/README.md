# **Pedir** Confusion Sets

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Ask** and **Ask For**](01-ask-vs-ask-for.md) | Hice una pregunta y pedí una respuesta. | I asked a question and asked for an answer. |
| [**Ask For** and **Order**](02-ask-for-vs-order.md) | Pedí ayuda y pedí el almuerzo. | I asked for help and ordered lunch. |
| [**Ask For** and **Request**](03-ask-for-vs-request.md) | Pedí una copia y luego la solicité formalmente. | I asked for a copy and then formally requested it. |
| [**Borrow** and **Lend**](04-borrow-vs-lend.md) | Le pedí prestado el libro a Ana y ella me lo prestó. | I borrowed the book from Ana, and she lent it to me. |
| [**Order** Meaning Purchase or Command](05-order-purchase-vs-command.md) | Pedí el almuerzo y le ordené al equipo que esperara. | I ordered lunch and ordered the team to wait. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Le pregunté a Ana qué pedir, le pedí una recomendación y luego pedí el almuerzo. | I asked Ana what to order, asked her for a recommendation, and then ordered lunch. |
