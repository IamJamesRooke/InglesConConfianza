# Formal **Yourself** and **Yourselves**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Usted se ve en el espejo, y ustedes se ven en la foto. | You see yourself in the mirror, and you see yourselves in the photo. |

With formal **usted**, reflexive **se** becomes **yourself**. With **ustedes**, it becomes **yourselves**. English uses **you** for both singular and plural, so the reflexive pronoun makes the number visible.
