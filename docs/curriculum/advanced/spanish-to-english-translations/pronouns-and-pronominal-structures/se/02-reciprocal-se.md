# Reciprocal **Se**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Juan y María se ayudan, pero no se ven todos los días. | Juan and María help each other, but they don't see each other every day. |

When two or more people act on one another, Spanish **se** becomes **each other** or **one another**. Context distinguishes this reciprocal meaning from the reflexive meaning **themselves**.
