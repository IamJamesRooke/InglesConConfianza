# Pronominal Verbs with **Se**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| María se llama Ana, pero su hermano se llama Juan. | Her name is Ana, but her brother's name is Juan. |

In a pronominal verb such as **llamarse**, **se** is part of the Spanish verb structure. English may express the same meaning with a different construction rather than translating **se** as **himself, herself**, or **themselves**.
