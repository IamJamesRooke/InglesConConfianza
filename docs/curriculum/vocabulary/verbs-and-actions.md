### Translations of Spanish **Bajar**

Spanish **bajar** can become **go down, come down, lower, get off, download, turn down, fall, take down**, or **lose weight**. Each lesson keeps **subir** visible as the useful inverse contrast; the same contrasts are indexed under **subir**.

| Lesson | Main English mappings |
|---|---|
| movement | go down / come down |
| moving something | lower |
| transport | get off |
| files | download |
| settings | turn down |
| prices and levels | fall |
| take something down | take down |
| weight | lose weight |

#### **Subir / Bajar → Go or Come Up / Down**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Sube a mi oficina y luego baja a recepción. | Come up to my office, and then go down to reception. |

English chooses **come** toward the speaker and **go** away from the speaker, plus **up/down**.

#### **Subir / Bajar → Raise / Lower**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Sube la caja y baja la mesa con cuidado. | Raise the box and lower the table carefully. |

With a direct object, use transitive **raise/lower**, not intransitive **rise/fall**.

#### **Subir / Bajar → Get On / Off**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Subimos al bus y bajamos en la siguiente parada. | We got on the bus and got off at the next stop. |

Use **get on/off** for public or shared transport. Use **get into/out of** a car.

#### **Subir / Bajar → Upload / Download**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Sube el archivo y baja la versión nueva. | Upload the file and download the new version. |

Digital direction determines **upload** versus **download**.

#### **Subir / Bajar → Turn Up / Down**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Baja la música y sube la calefacción. | Turn down the music and turn up the heat. |

For adjustable settings, use **turn up/down, raise/lower**, or a specific verb.

#### **Subir / Bajar → Rise / Fall**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Los precios subieron, pero la inflación bajó. | Prices rose, but inflation fell. |

Without a direct object, levels **rise/go up** and **fall/go down**.

#### **Subir / Bajar Algo → Bring Up / Take Down**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Sube el café y baja estos documentos. | Bring the coffee up and take these documents down. |

English combines **bring/take** with direction according to the speaker's location.

#### **Subir / Bajar De Peso → Gain / Lose Weight**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Subió de peso y luego bajó cinco kilos. | She gained weight and then lost five kilos. |

English uses **gain weight** and **lose weight**, not *go up/down of weight*.

### Translations of Spanish **Buscar**

Spanish **buscar** maps to **look for, search for**, or formal **seek**. English separates the process of searching from the result **find**.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| Look For, Search For, Seek | Busco una solución. | I'm looking for / seeking a solution. |
| Look For vs Find | Busco las llaves y luego las encuentro. | I look for the keys and then find them. |

#### **Buscar** Confusion Sets

- **Look For** vs **Find**

#### **Buscar → Look For / Search For / Seek**

Spanish **buscar** usually needs **look for** in everyday English, **search for** for a systematic search, and formal **seek** for an abstract goal.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Busco mis llaves. | I'm looking for my keys. |
| La policía busca pruebas. | The police are searching for evidence. |
| Buscamos una solución. | We're seeking a solution. |

##### **Look For** and **Find**

Use **look for** during the search and **find** when you discover the person or thing.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| buscar | **to look for** |
| encontrar | **to find** |

**Busco mis llaves antes de salir.**<br>
I look for my keys before leaving.

**Encuentro mis llaves debajo de la mesa.**<br>
I find my keys under the table.

English does not use *search* as a direct translation in every context:

**I look for my keys.**<br>
Not: *I search my keys.*
