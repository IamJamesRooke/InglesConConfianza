## Core vocabulary

### Spanish **Allí** and **Ahí**

Spanish **allí** and **ahí** both commonly become **there**. This family keeps the closely related location words together while preserving the broader contextual uses of **ahí**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La tienda está allí. | The store is there. |
| Ponlo ahí. | Put it there. |

- Contextual translations of **ahí**

#### Spanish **Ahí**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ponlo ahí. | Put it there. |
| Vive por ahí. | She lives somewhere around there. |
| Ahí fue cuando entendí. | That was when I understood. |
| De ahí viene el problema. | That is where the problem comes from. |

**Ahí** can become **there, around there**, or part of a complete **that is when/where** expression.

### Translations of Spanish **Casa**

- **Casa** → **At Home**, **Home**, and **From Home**

#### **Casa** → **At Home**, **Home**, and **From Home**

Spanish **casa** appears in three essential English location patterns:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| en casa | at home |
| a casa | home |
| de casa | from home |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Estoy en casa. | I am at home. |
| Voy a casa. | I am going home. |
| Trabajo desde casa. | I work from home. |

Do not use **to** before **home** after a movement verb: **go home**, not *go to home*.

### Spanish **Dirección**

Spanish **dirección** becomes **address** or **direction** depending on whether it means location information or orientation.

- **dirección** → address or direction.md)

#### **Address** and **Direction**

Spanish **dirección** has two common English translations.

| Meaning | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| location information | Necesito la dirección de la oficina. | I need the office address. |
| way or orientation | La oficina está en esa dirección. | The office is in that direction. |

Use **address** for the information needed to locate a home, office, or website. Use **direction** for the way something faces or the route toward it.

Do not ask for *the direction of the office* when you need its street address.

### Translations of Spanish **Lugar**

Spanish **lugar** usually becomes **place**. The lesson also keeps **plaza** visible as a nearby false-friend and location contrast without creating a separate top-level source folder.

- **lugar** → place; contrast with **plaza**

This map separates Spanish **lugar** from **plaza**, which do not both become English **place**.

- **lugar** and **plaza**

#### **Place** and **Plaza**

Use **place** for general Spanish **lugar**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Es un buen lugar para la reunión. | It is a good place for the meeting. |
| Nos vemos en ese lugar después de la clase. | I'll see you in that place after class. |
| Nos vemos en ese punto después de la clase. | I'll see you at that spot after class. |

English **plaza** is not the general word for **lugar**. It normally names a public square or a commercial complex:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Nos vemos en la plaza principal. | I will see you in the main plaza. |

Use **place** as the reusable foundational noun.

### Spanish **Piso**

Spanish **piso** becomes **floor** for the surface or building level, and **apartment** for the home.

- **piso** → floor or apartment.md)

#### Translations of **Piso**

Spanish **piso** can refer to a surface, a level of a building, or an apartment. English distinguishes them.

| Meaning | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| surface under your feet | Las llaves están en el piso. | The keys are on the floor. |
| level of a building | La oficina está en el segundo piso. | The office is on the second floor. |
| apartment | Vivo en un apartamento pequeño. | I live in a small apartment. |

In foundational American English, use **apartment**, not **floor**, for the home itself.

## Supplemental vocabulary

### Translations of Spanish **Cerca**

- **Cerca** → **Near** and **Close To**

#### **Cerca** → **Near** and **Close To**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| cerca de un lugar | near a place |
| cerca de un lugar | close to a place |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La oficina está cerca del parque. | The office is near the park. |
| La oficina está cerca del parque. | The office is close to the park. |

**Near** does not take **to** in the foundational pattern: **near the park**, not *near to the park*.

### Translations of Spanish **Lejos**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| far from | El aeropuerto está lejos de mi oficina. | The airport is far from the office. |

#### **Lejos De** → **Far From**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| lejos de un lugar | far from a place |
| El aeropuerto está lejos de mi oficina. | The airport is far from my office. |

**Far** takes **from** when naming the reference point.
