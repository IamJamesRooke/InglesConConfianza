# The **Power** Family

| Form | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| **power** | La información da poder. | Information gives people power. |
| **powerful** | Es una herramienta poderosa. | It's a powerful tool. |
| **powerless** | Sin acceso, me sentí impotente. | Without access, I felt powerless. |
| **powerlessness** | Su impotencia generó frustración. | Their powerlessness caused frustration. |

The true family branches from **power**:

- **power + -ful → powerful**
- **power + -less → powerless**
- **powerless + -ness → powerlessness**
