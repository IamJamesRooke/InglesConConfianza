# The **Meaning** Family

| Form | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| **meaning** | No entiendo el significado. | I don't understand the meaning. |
| **meaningful** | Tuvimos una conversación significativa. | We had a meaningful conversation. |
| **meaningless** | La contraseña es una secuencia sin sentido. | The password is a meaningless sequence. |
| **meaningfulness** | Valoramos lo significativo de la conversación. | We valued the conversation's meaningfulness. |
| **meaninglessness** | La falta de sentido del mensaje nos confundió. | The meaninglessness of the message confused us. |

**Meaningfulness** is correct but less common than natural alternatives such as **importance, value**, or **significance**, depending on context.
