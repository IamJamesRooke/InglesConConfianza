# **Eastward** and **Eastwards**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La tormenta se desplazó hacia el este durante la noche. | The storm moved eastward during the night. |

**Eastward/eastwards** means in the direction of the east. The shorter **east** is also natural: **The storm moved east**.

Use **eastward** before a noun: **the eastward route**. For direction after a verb, both **eastward** and **eastwards** are standard.

See the productive [**-ward/-wards** transformation](../to - towards/README.md).
