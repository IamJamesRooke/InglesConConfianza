# **Straight Forward, Straight Ahead**, and **Straightforward**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mire derecho hacia adelante; las instrucciones son sencillas. | Look straight ahead; the instructions are straightforward. |

**Straight ahead** is the usual expression for a physical direction directly in front of you. **Straight forward** can literally combine the two direction words, but **straight ahead** is normally clearer.

The single word **straightforward** means clear, direct, or uncomplicated:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Nos dio una respuesta directa y una explicación sencilla. | She gave us a straightforward answer and a straightforward explanation. |
