# **Afterward** and **Afterwards**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Terminamos el informe y descansamos después. | We finished the report and rested afterward. |

**Afterward/afterwards** means **después**. Both forms are standard; **afterward** is more common in American English and **afterwards** in British English.

This is a time word, not a physical direction. It connects one event to a later moment.
