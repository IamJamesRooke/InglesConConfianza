# The **White** Family

**White** can generate a description, a quality noun, a change verb, and several forms of that verb.

| Form | Transformation | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| **white** | base color | blanco |
| [**whitish**](../white - whitish/01-white-to-whitish.md) | **white + -ish** | blanquecino / blancuzco |
| [**whiteness**](07-whiteness.md) | **white + -ness** | blancura |
| [**whiten**](08-whiten.md) | **white + -en** | blanquear(se) |
| [**whitening**](09-whitening-and-whitened.md) | **whiten + -ing** | blanqueamiento / blanqueando |
| [**whitened**](09-whitening-and-whitened.md) | **whiten + -ed** | blanqueado |
| [**whitener**](10-whitener.md) | **whiten + -er** | blanqueador |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La tela blanquecina perdió su blancura, así que usamos un blanqueador para blanquearla; después del blanqueamiento, quedó más blanca. | The whitish fabric had lost its whiteness, so we used a whitener to whiten it; after the whitening, it looked whiter. |

## Family Tree

```text
white ── whitish
  ├───── whiteness
  └───── whiten ── whitening
                 ├─ whitened
                 └─ whitener
```

English does not normally use *whitely*. Use a natural phrase such as **in white**, **with a white color**, or **pale**, depending on the meaning.
