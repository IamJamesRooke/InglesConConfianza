# **Therefore**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Llovió; por lo tanto, cancelamos el viaje. | It rained; therefore, we canceled the trip. |

**Therefore** introduces a logical result and means **por lo tanto / por eso**.

Although its spelling ends in **fore**, modern learners should learn **therefore** as one complete connector. It is not a model for freely adding **fore-** to another word.
