# Time Words

Foundational vocabulary for now, sequence, parts of the day, and relative time.

## Family index

| Spanish family | Priority |
|---|---|
| **hora** | Core |
## Core Time Words: **Ahora, Pronto, Temprano**, and **Tarde**

These high-frequency time words are foundational vocabulary. **Ahora** remains especially important even though its central translation is simply **now**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ahora | now |
| pronto | soon |
| temprano | early |
| más temprano | earlier |
| tarde | late |
| más tarde | later |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Lo estoy haciendo ahora. | I am doing it now. |
| Voy a hacerlo pronto. | I am going to do it soon. |
| Llegué temprano. | I arrived early. |
| Llegué más temprano que Laura. | I arrived earlier than Laura. |
| Llegué tarde. | I arrived late. |
| Voy a llamarte más tarde. | I am going to call you later. |

Spanish **tarde** can mean **afternoon** or **late**. Context determines which English word is needed.

## Core vocabulary

### Translations of Spanish **Hora**

- **Hora** → **Hour** or **Time**
- Basic units and clock time — reusable structure reference

#### **Hora** → **Hour** or **Time**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Esperé una hora. | I waited for an hour. |
| ¿Qué hora es? | What time is it? |

Use **hour** for a sixty-minute unit. Use **time** when **la hora** means the clock time. The broader clock system lives in structure.
