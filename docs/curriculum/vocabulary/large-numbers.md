# Large Numbers

Hundreds through trillions, including the billion/billón false-cognate warning.
## Hundreds, Thousands, Millions, and Beyond

| Number | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| 100 | cien | one hundred |
| 1,000 | mil | one thousand |
| 1,000,000 | un millón | one million |
| 1,000,000,000 | mil millones | one billion |
| 1,000,000,000,000 | un billón | one trillion |

Do not make **hundred, thousand, million, billion**, or **trillion** plural after an exact number:

- **two hundred pages**
- **five thousand pesos**
- Not: *two hundreds pages*

Use a plural with **of** for an approximate large quantity: **hundreds of pages** and **thousands of people**.

### Building a large number

Read 1,523 by moving from the largest unit to the smallest:

**one thousand five hundred twenty-three**

British English commonly inserts **and** before the final tens or units:

**one thousand five hundred and twenty-three**

Both patterns are standard. The course uses the compact American pattern by default.

### False cognates: **billion** and **billón**

English **one billion** is **mil millones**, not Spanish **un billón**. In standard Spanish, **un billón** is a million millions, which English calls **one trillion**.
