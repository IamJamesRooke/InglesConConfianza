# Social Expressions

Polite requests, thanks, apologies, attention-getters, and responses.
## Expressions

This folder contains a deliberately small set of conversational chunks that should be learned as complete expressions rather than translated word by word.

| Function | Core expressions |
|---|---|
| making a request polite | **please** |
| expressing gratitude | **thank you / thanks** |
| responding to gratitude | **you're welcome / no problem** |
| apologizing or getting attention | **I'm sorry / excuse me** |

The curriculum keeps this category small. Additional idioms and fixed expressions can be evaluated during later curation.

## Spanish **Disculpar**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Discúlpame por llegar tarde. | Excuse me for being late. |
| No puedo disculpar su comportamiento. | I can't excuse his behavior. |
| La profesora disculpó la ausencia. | The teacher excused the absence. |
| Disculpa, ¿me puedes ayudar? | Excuse me, can you help me? |

**Disculpar** can mean **excuse** or **forgive**, and **disculpa/disculpe** is also a polite attention-getter.

## Spanish **Perdonar**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Perdóname por mentir. | Forgive me for lying. |
| Perdona la interrupción. | Excuse the interruption. |
| El banco perdonó parte de la deuda. | The bank forgave part of the debt. |
| Perdón, no te escuché. | Sorry, I didn't hear you. |

**Perdonar** can become **forgive** or **excuse**. Noun/interjection **perdón** often becomes **sorry** or **excuse me**.

## **Please**

Use **please** to make a request more polite.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| por favor | please |

**Please** can go before the request or at the end:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Por favor, ayúdame. | Please help me. |
| Ayúdame, por favor. | Help me, please. |

The end position normally uses a comma in writing.

## **I'm Sorry** and **Excuse Me**

| Spanish | English | Core use |
|---|---|---|
| lo siento | I'm sorry | apology or sympathy |
| perdón / disculpe | excuse me | getting attention or interrupting |
| perdón | I'm sorry | apologizing for a small mistake |
| qué pena | I'm sorry / excuse me | polite apology in Colombian Spanish |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Lo siento. Llegué tarde. | I'm sorry. I arrived late. |
| Perdón, esa es mi llave. | Excuse me, that is my key. |

Colombian **qué pena** frequently softens a request or apology. Choose **I'm sorry** when apologizing and **excuse me** when politely getting attention or interrupting.

## **Thank You for Doing Something**

Use **thank you for + verb-ing** to thank someone for an action.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| gracias por ayudarme | thank you for helping me |
| gracias por llamarme | thank you for calling me |
| gracias por explicarlo | thank you for explaining it |

After **for**, use the **-ing** form:

- **Thank you for helping me.**
- Not: *Thank you for help me.*

The expression is learned as one complete pattern: **thank you for doing something**.

## **Thank You, Thanks**, and Responses

| Spanish | English | Use |
|---|---|---|
| gracias | thank you | neutral |
| gracias | thanks | more informal |
| con gusto / de nada | you're welcome | standard response |
| no hay problema | no problem | informal response |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Gracias por el café. | Thank you for the coffee. |
| Gracias. | Thanks. |
| De nada. | You're welcome. |

Write **you're welcome**, not *your welcome*. **You're** is the contraction of **you are**.
