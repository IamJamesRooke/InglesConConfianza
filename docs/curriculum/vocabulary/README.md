# Vocabulary

This pillar contains the deliberately limited core lexicon, productive systems for acquiring additional words independently, and language-specific maps for meanings that do not transfer directly between Spanish and English.

| Area | Focus |
|---|---|
| [Core](core/README.md) | High-reuse verbs, nouns, descriptions, domains, and expressions used to build and practice English structure. |
| [Cognates](cognates/README.md) | Reliable inference patterns, spelling changes, memory bridges, and confusion sets. |
| [Word Building](word-building/README.md) | Productive English word families and visible form changes. |
| [Spanish to English](spanish-to-english/README.md) | Choose an English form from the meaning and function of a Spanish word or construction. |
| [English to Spanish](english-to-spanish/README.md) | Use Spanish contrasts to expose the contextual meanings of English words and constructions. |

Vocabulary folders do not represent a list to memorize before using the language. Structure lessons should introduce only the words needed to make their contrasts concrete.

The directional folders identify the learner-facing explanation, not separate courses. Their translation maps and confusion sets prevent predictable interference that structure and ordinary vocabulary study cannot resolve by themselves.
