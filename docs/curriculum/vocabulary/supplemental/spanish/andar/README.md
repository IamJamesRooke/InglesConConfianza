# Spanish **Andar**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Anda cinco kilómetros cada día. | She walks five kilometers every day. |
| Anda por toda la ciudad. | He goes all over the city. |
| Anda buscando trabajo. | She is looking for work. |
| El reloj no anda. | The clock doesn't work. |
| Anda con Mateo. | She is seeing Mateo. |

**Andar** can become **walk, go around, be doing, work**, or **be seeing someone**, depending on the construction.
