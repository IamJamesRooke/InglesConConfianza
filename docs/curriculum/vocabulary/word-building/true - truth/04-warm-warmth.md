# **Warm → Warmth**

**Warm** describes a comfortable degree of heat or a friendly emotional quality. **Warmth** names that quality.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La habitación está cálida. | The room is warm. |
| Sentimos la calidez del sol por la ventana. | We felt the sun's warmth through the window. |
| Laura recibió al grupo con calidez. | Laura welcomed the group with warmth. |

The noun is **warmth**, not *warmness* in these ordinary meanings.
