# **Northward** and **Northwards**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Después de la tormenta, las aves volaron hacia el norte. | After the storm, the birds flew northward. |

**Northward/northwards** means in the direction of the north. The shorter **north** is also natural: **The birds flew north**.

Use **northward** before a noun: **the northward route**. For direction after a verb, both **northward** and **northwards** are standard.

See the productive [**-ward/-wards** transformation](<../to - towards/README.md>).
