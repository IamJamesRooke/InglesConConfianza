# **Westward** and **Westwards**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Viajamos hacia el oeste para ver el atardecer. | We traveled westward to watch the sunset. |

**Westward/westwards** means in the direction of the west. The shorter **west** is also natural: **We traveled west**.

Use **westward** before a noun: **the westward route**. For direction after a verb, both **westward** and **westwards** are standard.

See the productive [**-ward/-wards** transformation](<../to - towards/README.md>).
