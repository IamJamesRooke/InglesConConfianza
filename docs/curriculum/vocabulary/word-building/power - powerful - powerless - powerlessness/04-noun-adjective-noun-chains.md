# Noun → Description → Quality Noun

A noun can produce a description with **-ful** or **-less**, and that description can produce a quality noun with **-ness**.

| Base noun | Description | Quality noun |
|---|---|---|
| **power** | **powerless** | **powerlessness** |
| **use** | **useful** | **usefulness** |
| **use** | **useless** | **uselessness** |
| **meaning** | **meaningless** | **meaninglessness** |
| **care** | **careless** | **carelessness** |
| **hope** | **hopeless** | **hopelessness** |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Su descuido causó el error, y la inutilidad de la copia de seguridad nos dejó impotentes. | Her carelessness caused the error, and the backup's uselessness left us powerless. |

Not every possible-looking chain is equally common. Confirm that the resulting word is established and natural.
