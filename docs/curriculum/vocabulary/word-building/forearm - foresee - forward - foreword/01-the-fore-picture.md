# The **Fore-** Picture

**Fore-** commonly contributes the idea before, earlier, or at the front.

| Picture | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| front part | **forearm** | front/lower part of the arm |
| earlier knowledge | **foresee** | see or anticipate beforehand |
| advance warning | **forewarn** | warn beforehand |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Previmos el problema y advertimos al equipo con anticipación. | We foresaw the problem and forewarned the team. |

The pattern helps with established words, but it does not license every imaginable **fore-** combination.
