# **Foretell** and **Forewarn**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Nadie puede predecir el resultado, pero podemos advertirle al equipo con anticipación sobre el riesgo. | No one can foretell the outcome, but we can forewarn the team about the risk. |

**Foretell** means predict or tell beforehand. **Forewarn** means warn in advance.

The fixed expression **forewarned is forearmed** means that advance warning helps someone prepare. Here **forearmed** means prepared in advance, not related to the body-part noun **forearm**.
