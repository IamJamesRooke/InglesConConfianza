# **All Right** and **Alright**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Todo está bien; la respuesta es aceptable. | Everything's all right; the answer is all right. |

**All right** is the established two-word form. **Alright** is common in informal writing but is still avoided by some editors and style guides.

The source's bridge **to be all right → estar bien** is useful, but this expression begins with **all**, not a productive prefix **al-**.
