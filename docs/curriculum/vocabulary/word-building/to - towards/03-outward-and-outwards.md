# **Out → Outward(s)**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mire afuera y luego extienda los brazos hacia afuera. | Look outside, and then stretch your arms outward. |

**Out** supplies the core exterior picture. **Outward/outwards** adds movement or orientation away from the inside or center.

Use **outward** before a noun: **an outward movement**. For direction after a verb, both **outward** and **outwards** are standard.
