# **Back → Backward(s)**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mire atrás antes de dar dos pasos hacia atrás. | Look back before taking two steps backward. |

**Back** supplies the core rear-direction picture. **Backward/backwards** marks movement or orientation toward the back.

Use **backward** before a noun: **a backward movement**. For direction after a verb, both forms are standard.
