# **In → Inward(s)**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mire adentro y luego dé un paso hacia adentro. | Look inside, and then step inward. |

**In** supplies the core interior picture. **Inward/inwards** adds movement or orientation toward the inside or center.

Use **inward** before a noun: **an inward movement**. For direction after a verb, both **inward** and **inwards** are standard.
