# **Homeward** and **Homewards**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Al anochecer, nos dirigimos hacia casa. | At dusk, we headed homeward. |

**Homeward/homewards** means in the direction of home.

Use **homeward** before a noun: **the homeward route**. For direction after a verb, both **homeward** and **homewards** are standard. In everyday conversation, the shorter **home** is usually more natural: **We headed home**.
