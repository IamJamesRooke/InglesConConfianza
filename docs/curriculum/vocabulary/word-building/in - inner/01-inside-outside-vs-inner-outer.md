# **Inside/Outside** vs **Inner/Outer**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Estoy afuera, pintando la pared exterior; Ana está adentro, pintando la pared interior. | I'm outside, painting the outer wall; Ana is inside, painting the inner wall. |

**Inside** and **outside** can tell us where someone or something is. **Inner** and **outer** normally describe which relative part or layer we mean.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Where are you? | I'm **inside**. |
| Which wall are you painting? | The **inner** wall. |

**The inside** and **the outside** can also name the whole interior or exterior part: **The inside of the box is clean, but the outside is dirty.**
