## Core vocabulary

### Translations of Spanish **Esposa**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| **Esposa** → **Wife** or **Spouse** | esposa | wife / spouse |

#### **Esposa** → **Wife** or **Spouse**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mi esposa trabaja desde casa. | My wife works from home. |
| Cada esposa puede solicitar una copia. | Each spouse can request a copy. |

Use **wife** when the female relationship matters and gender-neutral **spouse** in legal, formal, or general contexts.

### Translations of Spanish **Familia**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| **Familia** → **Family**, **Household**, or **Relatives** | familia | family / household / relatives |

#### **Familia** → **Family**, **Household**, or **Relatives**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mi familia vive en Bogotá. | My family lives in Bogotá. |
| Todos los miembros de la familia viven en la misma casa. | Everyone in the household lives in the same house. |
| Voy a visitar a mi familia. | I'm going to visit my relatives. |

Use **family** for the family as a group, **household** for the people living together, and **relatives** for family members considered individually.

### Translations of Spanish **Hermana**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| **Hermana** → **Sister** or **Sibling** | hermana | sister / sibling |

#### **Hermana** → **Sister** or **Sibling**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tengo una hermana. | I have a sister. |
| Cada hermana debe traer su identificación. | Each sibling must bring their identification. |

Use **sister** when the female relationship matters. Use gender-neutral **sibling** when referring to a brother or sister without specifying which.

### Translations of Spanish **Hermandad**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| **Hermandad** → **Brotherhood**, **Sisterhood**, or **Fraternity** | hermandad | brotherhood / sisterhood / fraternity |

#### **Hermandad** → **Brotherhood**, **Sisterhood**, or **Fraternity**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La hermandad entre los miembros es fuerte. | The brotherhood among the members is strong. |
| La hermandad de las mujeres creció con el proyecto. | The sisterhood among the women grew through the project. |
| La organización funciona como una fraternidad. | The organization functions as a fraternity. |

The natural English depends on the group and context: **brotherhood** can mean solidarity or a male group, **sisterhood** a female group or solidarity, and **fraternity** an organized brotherhood.

### Translations of Spanish **Hombre**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| **Hombre** → **Man**, **Guy**, or **Male** | hombre | man / guy / male |

#### **Hombre** → **Man**, **Guy**, or **Male**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Es un hombre amable. | He is a kind man. |
| Ese hombre vive al lado. | That guy lives next door. |
| El paciente es de sexo masculino. | The patient is male. |

Use **man** as the neutral adult noun, informal **guy** in conversation, and **male** when talking about sex or classification.

#### **Mujer** → **Woman**, **Lady**, or **Female**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Es una mujer amable. | She is a kind woman. |

| Lesson | Spanish | English |

#### **Niña** → **Child**, **Girl**, or **Kid**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|

### Translations of Spanish **Persona**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| **Persona** → **Person**, **People**, or **Someone** | persona | person / people / someone |

#### **Persona** → **Person**, **People**, or **Someone**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Es una persona amable. | She is a kind person. |
| Hay muchas personas en la sala. | There are many people in the room. |
| Necesito una persona que pueda ayudarme. | I need someone who can help me. |

Singular **persona** usually becomes **person**. English uses **people** for the ordinary plural and **someone** when the person is indefinite.
