# Function Words

High-frequency connectors, quantifiers, and adverbs kept as compact vocabulary references.

## Family index

| Spanish family | Priority |

| **poco** | Core |

## Core vocabulary

### Spanish **Casi**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Casi terminé. | I almost finished. |
| Hay casi cien personas. | There are nearly one hundred people. |
| Casi nunca salgo de noche. | I hardly ever go out at night. |

**Casi** normally becomes **almost** or **nearly**. With negative frequency it can become **hardly ever**. The separate **por poco** expression lives under **poco**.

### Translations of Spanish **Pero**

- **Pero** → **But**

#### **Pero** → **But**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| pero | but |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Quiero ir, pero tengo que trabajar. | I want to go, but I have to work. |

English **but** also has other uses; see the English **but** map for those contrasts.

### Spanish **Poco**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tengo poco tiempo. | I have little time. |
| Hay pocas sillas. | There are few chairs. |
| Es un poco difícil. | It is a little difficult. |
| Es poco práctico. | It is not very practical. |
| Por poco me caigo. | I almost fell. |

**Poco** can become **little, few, a little**, or negative-like **not very**, depending on number and position. **Por poco** commonly becomes **almost**.

### Translations of Spanish **Porque**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| Giving a Reason | porque | because / since / as |

Related reason connectors have their own mapping owners: **ya que** and **debido a que**.

#### **Porque** → **Because**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Porque tengo que terminar el informe. | Because I have to finish the report. |
| Se fue porque estaba cansada. | She left because she was tired. |

Spanish **porque** gives a reason. Depending on the connection and register, English can also use **since** or **as**.

### Translations of Spanish **Quizá**

Spanish **quizá** and its alias **quizás** introduce uncertainty. English commonly chooses **maybe**, **perhaps**, or **possibly**.

| Source | Target | Atomic mapping |
|---|---|---|
| **quizá** | **maybe** | open |
| **quizá** | **perhaps** | open |
| **quizá** | **possibly** | open |

#### **quizá** → **maybe**

Use **maybe** to introduce an uncertain possibility.

#### **quizá** → **perhaps**

Use **perhaps** as a slightly more formal alternative to **maybe**.

#### **quizá** → **possibly**

Use **possibly** when English makes the uncertainty explicit inside the clause.
