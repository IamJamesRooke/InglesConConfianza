# Verb Cognates: **-ide**

In these cognates, Spanish verbs ending in **-idir** or **-iar** correspond to English verbs ending in **-ide**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| decidir | **to decide** | **Tenemos que decidir la fecha antes del viernes.**<br>We have to decide on the date before Friday. |
| dividir | **to divide** | **La profesora va a dividir la clase en cuatro grupos.**<br>The teacher is going to divide the class into four groups. |
| residir | **to reside** | **El solicitante debe residir legalmente en Colombia.**<br>The applicant must legally reside in Colombia. |
| guiar | **to guide** | **Una experta local va a guiar al grupo por el museo.**<br>A local expert is going to guide the group through the museum. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| presidir | **to preside** | **La jueza va a presidir la audiencia.**<br>The judge is going to preside over the hearing. |
| coincidir | **to coincide** | **Las vacaciones escolares coinciden con el festival.**<br>The school vacation coincides with the festival. |
