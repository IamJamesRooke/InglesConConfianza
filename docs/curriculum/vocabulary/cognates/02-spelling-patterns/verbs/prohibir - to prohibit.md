# Verb Cognates: **-hibit**

In these cognates, Spanish verbs ending in **-hibir** correspond to English verbs ending in **-hibit**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| prohibir | **to prohibit** | **La ley prohíbe fumar dentro del edificio.**<br>The law prohibits smoking inside the building. |
| exhibir | **to exhibit** | **El museo va a exhibir varias obras de artistas colombianos.**<br>The museum is going to exhibit several works by Colombian artists. |
| inhibir | **to inhibit** | **El miedo puede inhibir la creatividad de los estudiantes.**<br>Fear can inhibit students' creativity. |
