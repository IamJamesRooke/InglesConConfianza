# Noun Cognates: **-que → -k / -ck**

In these cognates, the Spanish ending **-que** corresponds to English **-k** or **-ck**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| parque | **park** | **Los niños juegan en el parque después de la escuela.**<br>The children play in the park after school. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| tanque | **tank** | **El tanque contiene quinientos litros de agua.**<br>The tank contains five hundred liters of water. |
| bloque | **block** | **La oficina queda a un bloque de la estación.**<br>The office is one block from the station. |
| cheque | **check** | **Necesito depositar este cheque antes del viernes.**<br>I need to deposit this check before Friday. |
| ataque | **attack** | **El sistema bloqueó el ataque antes de que causara daños.**<br>The system blocked the attack before it caused damage. |
