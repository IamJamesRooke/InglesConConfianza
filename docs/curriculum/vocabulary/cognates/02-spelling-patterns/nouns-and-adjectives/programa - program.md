# Noun Cognates: **-ama → -am**

In these cognates, Spanish nouns ending in **-ama** correspond to English nouns ending in **-am**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| programa | **program** | **El programa ofrece clases gratuitas para adultos.**<br>The program offers free classes for adults. |
| diagrama | **diagram** | **Este diagrama muestra todas las etapas del proceso.**<br>This diagram shows every stage of the process. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| anagrama | **anagram** | **La palabra forma un anagrama del nombre de su hermana.**<br>The word forms an anagram of her sister's name. |
| telegrama | **telegram** | **La familia recibió un telegrama con la noticia.**<br>The family received a telegram with the news. |
| holograma | **hologram** | **El documento incluye un holograma para impedir falsificaciones.**<br>The document includes a hologram to prevent counterfeiting. |
