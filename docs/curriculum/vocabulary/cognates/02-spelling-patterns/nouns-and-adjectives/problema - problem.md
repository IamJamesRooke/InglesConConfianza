# Noun Cognates: **-ema → -em**

In these cognates, Spanish nouns ending in **-ema** correspond to English nouns ending in **-em**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| problema | **problem** | **El técnico encontró un problema en el sistema.**<br>The technician found a problem in the system. |
| sistema | **system** | **El nuevo sistema guarda los archivos automáticamente.**<br>The new system saves the files automatically. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| poema | **poem** | **La estudiante escribió un poema sobre su ciudad.**<br>The student wrote a poem about her city. |
| teorema | **theorem** | **La profesora demostró el teorema con un diagrama.**<br>The teacher demonstrated the theorem with a diagram. |
