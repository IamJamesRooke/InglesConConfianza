# Verb Cognates: **-press**

These Spanish and English cognates correspond to English verbs ending in **-press**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| comprimir | **to compress** | **El programa puede comprimir el archivo antes de enviarlo.**<br>The program can compress the file before sending it. |
| reprimir | **to repress** | **El régimen intentó reprimir las protestas.**<br>The regime tried to repress the protests. |
| suprimir | **to suppress** | **El medicamento ayuda a suprimir la tos.**<br>The medication helps suppress the cough. |
| expresar | **to express** | **Es importante expresar tus dudas con claridad.**<br>It is important to express your concerns clearly. |

## False Cognates

**Imprimir** means **to print**, not **to impress**. **To impress** normally means **impresionar**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Voy a imprimir el contrato esta tarde.** | I am going to **print** the contract this afternoon. |
| **La presentación impresionó a todos los clientes.** | The presentation **impressed** all the clients. |
