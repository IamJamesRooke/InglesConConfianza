# Cognates: English **-ult**

These related English words end in **-ult**, but they do not follow one complete letter-by-letter rule. Learn them as a memory family.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| difícil | **difficult** | **La última pregunta fue difícil para todos.**<br>The last question was difficult for everyone. |
| culto | **cult** | **El documental investiga un culto religioso.**<br>The documentary investigates a religious cult. |
| adulto | **adult** | **Cada adulto debe presentar su identificación.**<br>Each adult must show identification. |
| insulto | **insult** | **Su comentario fue un insulto para la familia.**<br>His comment was an insult to the family. |
| resultado | **result** | **El experimento produjo un resultado positivo.**<br>The experiment produced a positive result. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| tumulto | **tumult** | **La decisión provocó un tumulto fuera del edificio.**<br>The decision caused a tumult outside the building. |

## False Cognates

The noun **culto** can mean **cult**, but the adjective **culto/culta** normally means **cultured** or **well educated**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **El documental analiza un culto secreto.** | The documentary examines a secret **cult**. |
| **La profesora es una persona muy culta.** | The teacher is a very **cultured** person. |
