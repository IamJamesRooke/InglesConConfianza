# Direct Noun Cognates: **-or**

These Spanish and English nouns have the same spelling.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| color | **color** | **El color de la pared cambió después de secarse.**<br>The color of the wall changed after it dried. |
| favor | **favor** | **¿Puedes hacerme un favor antes de salir?**<br>Can you do me a favor before leaving? |
| honor | **honor** | **Fue un honor representar a la universidad.**<br>It was an honor to represent the university. |
