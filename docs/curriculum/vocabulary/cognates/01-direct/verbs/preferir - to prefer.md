# Verb Cognates: **-fer**

These Spanish and English cognates correspond to English verbs ending in **-fer**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| preferir | **to prefer** | **Prefiero tomar café sin azúcar.**<br>I prefer to drink coffee without sugar. |
| referirse a | **to refer to** | **El informe se refiere a los resultados del último trimestre.**<br>The report refers to the results from the last quarter. |
| transferir, consignar | **to transfer** | **Voy a transferir el dinero a tu cuenta esta tarde.**<br>I am going to transfer the money to your account this afternoon. |
| diferir | **to differ** | **Los dos planes difieren en precio y duración.**<br>The two plans differ in price and duration. |
| sufrir | **to suffer** | **Muchas familias sufrieron pérdidas durante la inundación.**<br>Many families suffered losses during the flood. |
| ofrecer | **to offer** | **La empresa va a ofrecer cursos gratuitos a sus empleados.**<br>The company is going to offer free courses to its employees. |
