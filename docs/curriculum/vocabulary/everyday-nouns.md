### Everyday people and entertainment

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| amigo / amiga | friend | Laura es mi amiga. → Laura is my friend. |
| amigo | buddy / pal (informal) | Hola, amigo. → Hi, buddy. |
| película | movie / film | Vimos una película anoche. → We watched a movie last night. |

**Friend** is the neutral choice. **Buddy** and **pal** are informal and friendly; English does not mark the friend's gender in these nouns.

Additional preserved example: **Es un buen pal. → He's a good pal.**

For **película**, **movie** is the usual everyday word in American English. **Film** is also standard and is common when discussing cinema as an art form: **La película ganó un premio en el festival. → The film won an award at the festival.** This is useful vocabulary, but the register choice does not require a standalone mapping family.

### Organizations and money

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| empresa / compañía | company | La empresa ofrece cursos. → The company offers courses. |
| negocio | business | Tiene un negocio pequeño. → She has a small business. |
| firma | firm | La firma publicó un informe. → The firm published a report. |
| dinero | money | Necesito dinero para el viaje. → I need money for the trip. |
| efectivo | cash | No tengo efectivo. → I don't have any cash. |
| fondos | funds | El proyecto necesita más fondos. → The project needs more funds. |

**Company** is the neutral organization word. **Business** can mean the organization or the activity of commerce, while **firm** is more formal.

Use **money** generally, **cash** for physical or immediately available money, and **funds** for money reserved for a purpose. These are neighboring vocabulary items rather than three contextual translations of one Spanish source.

Use the mapping folders for related high-value noun contrasts such as **cosa**, **persona**, **respuesta**, **llave**, **mesa**, **reserva**, and **precio**. The noun **pregunta** is indexed under **preguntar**.

### Duties and responsibilities

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| la tarea | homework | Mi hijo terminó la tarea. → My son finished his homework. |
| la obligación | obligation | Tenemos la obligación de informar el cambio. → We have an obligation to report the change. |
| la responsabilidad | responsibility | Es mi responsabilidad revisar el informe. → It's my responsibility to review the report. |

Colombian Spanish normally uses **la tarea** for school homework. **La obligación** emphasizes a requirement or commitment; **la responsabilidad** emphasizes a task or area for which someone is accountable.

## Core vocabulary

### Translations of Spanish **Cosa**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| **Cosa** → **Thing**, **Stuff**, or **Matter** | cosa | thing / stuff / matter |

#### **Cosa** → **Thing**, **Stuff**, or **Matter**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿Qué es esa cosa? | What is that thing? |
| Tengo muchas cosas que hacer. | I have a lot of stuff to do. |
| La puntualidad es una cosa importante. | Punctuality is an important matter. |

Use **thing** for a countable object or idea, **stuff** for an informal mass of things, and **matter** for an issue or concern.

### Translations of Spanish **Llave**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| **Llave** → **Key**, **Faucet**, or **Wrench** | llave | key / faucet / wrench |

#### **Llave** → **Key**, **Faucet**, or **Wrench**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La llave está sobre la mesa. | The key is on the table. |
| Cierra la llave del agua. | Turn off the water faucet. |
| Necesito una llave inglesa. | I need a wrench. |

The surrounding noun or action determines the meaning. **Llave inglesa** specifically becomes **wrench**, not *English key*.

### Translations of Spanish **Mesa**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| **Mesa** → **Table** or **Board** | mesa | table / board |

#### **Mesa** → **Table** or **Board**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La llave está sobre la mesa. | The key is on the table. |
| La mesa directiva aprobó el plan. | The board approved the plan. |

Use **table** for furniture or a work surface. In an organization, **mesa directiva** becomes **board**.

### Translations of Spanish **Respuesta**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| **Respuesta** → **Answer**, **Response**, or **Reply** | respuesta | answer / response / reply |

#### **Respuesta** → **Answer**, **Response**, or **Reply**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Sé la respuesta. | I know the answer. |
| La respuesta del gobierno fue lenta. | The government's response was slow. |
| Todavía no he recibido una respuesta a mi mensaje. | I still haven't received a reply to my message. |

Use **answer** for a question, **response** for a reaction or formal statement, and **reply** especially for a message or direct communication.

## Supplemental vocabulary

### Translations of Spanish **Luz**

Spanish **luz** can mean visible **light** or electrical **power**. The common outage expression **se fue la luz** requires a full English phrase.

| Source | Target | Atomic mapping |
|---|---|---|
| **luz** | **light** | open |
| **luz** | **power** | open |
| **se fue la luz** | **the power went out** | open |

#### **luz** → **light**

Use **light** for visible illumination.

#### **luz** → **power**

Use **power** when **luz** refers to electric service rather than illumination.

#### **se fue la luz** → **the power went out**

English normally describes the outage with **the power went out** or **the power was out**.

### Translations of Spanish **Precio**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| **Precio** → **Price**, **Cost**, or **Rate** | precio | price / cost / rate |

#### **Precio** → **Price**, **Cost**, or **Rate**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El precio del libro es veinte dólares. | The price of the book is twenty dollars. |
| El precio total del proyecto fue alto. | The total cost of the project was high. |
| ¿Cuál es el precio por hora? | What is the hourly rate? |

**Price** is the amount requested for a product, **cost** the amount required or paid, and **rate** an amount measured per hour, unit, or period.

### Translations of Spanish **Reserva** and **Reservación**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| **Reserva/Reservación** → **Reservation**, **Booking**, or **Reserve** | reserva / reservación | reservation / booking / reserve |

#### **Reserva/Reservación** → **Reservation**, **Booking**, or **Reserve**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Necesito una reservación para esta noche. | I need a reservation for tonight. |
| Hicimos una reserva en el hotel. | We made a booking at the hotel. |
| La reserva de dinero es para una emergencia. | The reserve of money is for an emergency. |

Use **reservation** for a planned place or service, **booking** as an everyday alternative, and **reserve** for an amount held back or saved.
