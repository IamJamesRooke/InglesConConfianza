# Sending and Receiving: **Take** and **Bring**

English chooses **take** or **bring** according to the direction of movement.

## **Take**

Use **take** when something moves from here to another place.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| llevar | **to take** |

**Lleva estos documentos a la oficina de Diana.**<br>
Take these documents to Diana's office.

## **Bring**

Use **bring** when something moves toward the speaker or the destination being discussed.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| traer | **to bring** |

**Trae esos documentos a mi oficina.**<br>
Bring those documents to my office.

Think about the direction:

**Take it there.**<br>
**Bring it here.**
