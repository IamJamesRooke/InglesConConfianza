# Moving: **Come Back** and **Go Back**

Both **volver** and **regresar** can correspond to **come back** or **go back**. English chooses according to the direction of the return.

## **Come back**

Use **come back** for a return toward the speaker or the place being discussed.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| volver, regresar | **to come back** |

**Vuelve a mi oficina después de la reunión.**<br>
Come back to my office after the meeting.

## **Go back**

Use **go back** for a return away from the speaker or to another place.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| volver, regresar | **to go back** |

**Regresa a tu oficina después de la reunión.**<br>
Go back to your office after the meeting.

Think about the direction:

**Come back here.**<br>
**Go back there.**
