# Handle Verbs: Wanting Somebody to Do Something

When you want, need, or would like another person to perform an action, put the person before the full infinitive.

**subject + want / need / would like + somebody + to + base verb**

Spanish normally expresses the same idea with **que** followed by a verb in the subjunctive.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| querer que alguien haga algo | **to want somebody to do something** |
| necesitar que alguien haga algo | **to need somebody to do something** |
| gustaría que alguien hiciera algo | **would like somebody to do something** |

## **Want somebody to**

**Quiero que Laura haga la reserva.**<br>
I want Laura to make the reservation.

## **Need somebody to**

**Necesito que Laura haga la reserva.**<br>
I need Laura to make the reservation.

## **Would like somebody to**

**Me gustaría que Laura hiciera la reserva.**<br>
I would like Laura to make the reservation.

Do not translate the Spanish **que** as **that** in this English pattern:

**I want Laura to make the reservation.**<br>
Not: *I want that Laura makes the reservation.*
