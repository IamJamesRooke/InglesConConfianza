# **To + Verb** or **Of + -ing**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tengo la oportunidad de practicar y una nueva forma de estudiar. | I have the opportunity to practice and a new way of studying. |

After some nouns, **de + infinitive** becomes **to + base verb**; after others it becomes **of + -ing**. Learn the English connection: **an opportunity to practice**, but **a way of practicing**. **A way to practice** is also natural.
