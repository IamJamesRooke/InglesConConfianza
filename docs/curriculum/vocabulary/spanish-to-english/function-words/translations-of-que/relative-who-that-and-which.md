# Relative **Que**: **Who, That**, and **Which**

Relative **que** connects a noun to information that identifies or describes it.

| Noun | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| person | La persona que trabaja con Laura. | The person who works with Laura. |
| person | La persona que necesito llamar. | The person that I need to call. |
| thing | El libro que necesito. | The book that I need. |
| thing | El libro que está sobre la mesa. | The book which is on the table. |

Use **who** naturally for people. **That** can introduce essential information about people or things. **Which** is used for things.

English can omit the relative word when it is the object:

- **The book that I need**
- **The book I need**

Do not omit it when it is the subject: **The book that is on the table**.
