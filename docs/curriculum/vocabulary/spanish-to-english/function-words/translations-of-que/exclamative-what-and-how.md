# Exclamative **Qué**: **What** and **How**

Accented **qué** can introduce an exclamation. English chooses **what** before a noun phrase and **how** before an adjective or adverb alone.

| Pattern | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| **qué + noun phrase** | ¡Qué buena idea! | What a good idea! |
| **qué + adjective** | ¡Qué interesante! | How interesting! |
| **qué + adverb** | ¡Qué temprano! | How early! |

Do not translate every exclamative **qué** as **what**:

- **What a good idea!**
- **How interesting!**
