# Reflexive **Se**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Juan se ve en el espejo, y Ana y María se ven en la foto. | Juan sees himself in the mirror, and Ana and María see themselves in the photo. |

When the subject acts on the same person or people named by the subject, Spanish **se** becomes an English reflexive pronoun such as **himself, herself**, or **themselves**. The English form must agree with the subject.
