# **Se** Replacing **Le** or **Les**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Le doy el libro a Ana: se lo doy porque lo necesita. | I give Ana the book: I give it to her because she needs it. |

Before **lo, la, los**, or **las**, Spanish changes **le** or **les** to **se**. Here **se** identifies the recipient, while the following pronoun identifies the thing.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Se lo doy. | I give it to him, her, you, or them. |
| Se la doy. | I give it to him, her, you, or them. |
| Se los doy. | I give them to him, her, you, or them. |
| Se las doy. | I give them to him, her, you, or them. |

English usually identifies the recipient through context or an explicit phrase because Spanish **se** alone does not reveal whether the recipient is **him, her, you**, or **them**.
