# Translations of Spanish **Subir** and **Bajar**

Spanish **subir** and **bajar** map to movement, raising and lowering, transport, digital transfer, settings, prices, and collection or delivery.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Subir / Bajar → Go or Come Up / Down**](01-go-or-come-up-and-down.md) | Sube a mi oficina y luego baja a recepción. | Come up to my office, and then go down to reception. |
| [**Subir / Bajar → Raise / Lower**](02-raise-or-lower.md) | Sube la caja y baja la mesa con cuidado. | Raise the box and lower the table carefully. |
| [**Subir / Bajar → Get On / Off**](03-get-on-or-off-transport.md) | Subimos al bus y bajamos en la siguiente parada. | We got on the bus and got off at the next stop. |
| [**Subir / Bajar → Upload / Download**](04-upload-or-download.md) | Sube el archivo y baja la versión nueva. | Upload the file and download the new version. |
| [**Subir / Bajar → Turn Up / Down**](05-volume-and-settings.md) | Baja la música y sube la calefacción. | Turn down the music and turn up the heat. |
| [**Subir / Bajar → Rise / Fall**](06-prices-and-levels.md) | Los precios subieron, pero la inflación bajó. | Prices rose, but inflation fell. |
| [**Subir / Bajar Algo → Bring Up / Take Down**](07-pick-up-or-drop-off.md) | Sube el café y baja estos documentos. | Bring the coffee up and take these documents down. |
| [**Subir / Bajar De Peso → Gain / Lose Weight**](08-weight.md) | Subió de peso y luego bajó cinco kilos. | She gained weight and then lost five kilos. |
