# Time and Perfect Forms

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Past Necessity: **Had To**](01-had-to.md) | Ayer tuve que trabajar. | I had to work yesterday. |
| [Past Advice: **Should Have**](02-should-have-done.md) | Debí haber llamado. | I should have called. |
| [Past Deduction: **Must Have**](03-must-have-done.md) | Debe haber olvidado la reunión. | She must have forgotten the meeting. |
| [Past Expectation: **Was Supposed To**](04-was-supposed-to.md) | Debía llegar a las diez. | She was supposed to arrive at ten. |
| [Present Perfect Necessity](05-have-had-to.md) | He tenido que trabajar mucho. | I've had to work a lot. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ayer tuve que empezar sin Ana; debía llegar a las diez, pero debe haber olvidado la reunión y yo debí haberla llamado. | Yesterday I had to start without Ana; she was supposed to arrive at ten, but she must have forgotten the meeting, and I should have called her. |
