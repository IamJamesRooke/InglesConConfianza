# **Deber** Confusion Sets

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Must, Have To, Should**, and **Supposed To**](01-strength-and-source.md) | Debes presentar el informe, deberías revisarlo y debes llegar a las diez. | You must submit the report, should review it, and are supposed to arrive at ten. |
| [Obligation and Deduction with **Must**](02-must-obligation-vs-deduction.md) | Debe llamar porque debe estar preocupada. | She must call because she must be worried. |
| [**Had To, Should Have, Must Have**, and **Was Supposed To**](03-past-forms.md) | Tuve que llamar, debí haber llamado, debe haber llamado y debía llamar. | I had to call, should have called, she must have called, and she was supposed to call. |
| [**Owe** and **Have To Pay**](04-owe-vs-have-to-pay.md) | Debo dinero y debo pagarlo hoy. | I owe money and have to pay it today. |
| [Homophones: **Owe** and **Oh**](05-homophones-owe-and-oh.md) | —Debo cincuenta dólares. —¡Oh! | “I owe fifty dollars.” “Oh!” |
| [Homophones: **Ought** and **Aught**](06-homophones-ought-and-aught.md) | Hasta donde sabemos, deberíamos revisar. | For aught we know, we ought to check. |
| [Pronunciation: **Must** and **Most**](07-pronunciation-must-and-most.md) | La mayoría debe presentar el formulario. | Most people must submit the form. |
| [**Owe** and **Own**](08-owe-vs-own.md) | Le debo dinero a Ana, pero soy dueño del carro. | I owe Ana money, but I own the car. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Debo dinero, tengo que pagarlo hoy, debería llamar primero y Ana debe estar preocupada porque yo debía pagar ayer. | I owe money, have to pay it today, should call first, and Ana must be worried because I was supposed to pay yesterday. |
