# **Saber A → Taste Like**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Este café sabe a chocolate, pero no sé qué ingredientes tiene. | This coffee tastes like chocolate, but I don't know what ingredients it has. |

In **saber a + flavor**, **saber** means have a particular taste. English uses **taste like + noun** or **taste + adjective**: **tastes like chocolate**, but **tastes sweet**.
