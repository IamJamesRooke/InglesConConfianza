# **Can't Wait** and **Look Forward To**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tengo muchas ganas de conocerla y espero con ilusión nuestra reunión. | I can't wait to meet her, and I'm looking forward to our meeting. |

Both express eager anticipation. **Can't wait to + verb** is enthusiastic and informal. **Look forward to + noun/-ing** is neutral and also common in professional messages.
