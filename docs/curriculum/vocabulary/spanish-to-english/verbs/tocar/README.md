# Translations of Spanish **Tocar**

Spanish **tocar** covers physical contact, instruments, turns, obligations, effects on someone, and contact with doors or bells.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Tocar → Touch**](01-touch.md) | No toques la pintura; todavía está húmeda. | Don't touch the paint; it's still wet. |
| [**Tocar → Play** an Instrument](02-play-an-instrument.md) | Ana toca el piano y Luis toca la guitarra. | Ana plays the piano, and Luis plays the guitar. |
| [**Tocar → Be Someone's Turn**](03-be-someones-turn.md) | Me toca a mí y después te toca a ti. | It's my turn, and then it's your turn. |
| [**Tocar → Have To**](04-have-to-or-be-ones-lot.md) | Me toca trabajar este sábado. | I have to work this Saturday. |
| [**Tocar → Affect / Concern**](05-be-affected.md) | Ese cambio nos toca a todos. | That change affects all of us. |
| [**Tocar → Knock / Ring**](06-knock-or-ring.md) | Tocaron la puerta y luego tocaron el timbre. | They knocked on the door and then rang the doorbell. |
