# Requests: **Can** and **Could**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿Puedes cerrar la ventana? | Can you close the window? |
| ¿Podrías cerrar la ventana, por favor? | Could you close the window, please? |

Here **can** and **could** make requests; they are not questions about physical ability. **Could** normally sounds less direct.
