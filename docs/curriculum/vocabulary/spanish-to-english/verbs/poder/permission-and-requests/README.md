# Permission and Requests with **Poder**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Asking Permission](01-asking-permission.md) | ¿Puedo entrar? | Can I come in? |
| [Giving Permission](02-giving-permission.md) | Puede entrar. | You may come in. |
| [Being Allowed](03-be-allowed-to.md) | Nos permiten entrar. | We're allowed to go in. |
| [Requests](04-requests.md) | ¿Podrías ayudarme? | Could you help me? |
| [Past Permission](05-past-permission.md) | De niño, podía acostarme tarde los sábados. | As a child, I was allowed to stay up late on Saturdays. |
| [**Let**](06-let-someone-do.md) | Me dejan hacerlo. | They let me do it. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿Podría entrar? Me dijeron que hoy sí me permiten visitar a Ana. | Could I come in? They told me I'm allowed to visit Ana today. |
