# Cardinal Numbers

Quantity words, pronunciation contrasts, and the extended family of two.
## Numbers

Numbers belong in Foundations because they provide essential quantity and order vocabulary for dates, prices, addresses, measurements, and everyday instructions.

| Area | Core examples |
|---|---|
| small cardinal numbers | **one, two, twelve** |
| teens and tens | **thirteen, thirty, ninety** |
| large cardinal numbers | **one hundred, one thousand, one million** |
| special formats | **3.14, phone numbers** |
| ordinal numbers | **first, second, twentieth** |
| fractions | **half, a third, a quarter** |

Cardinal numbers answer **How many?** Ordinal numbers identify position in an ordered series.

## Supplemental **Two** and Related Number Forms

English **two** maps directly to Spanish **dos**, so it does not need a canonical mapping bucket. This supplemental number set preserves the related contrasts with **the two, both, a couple, a pair, twins, twice, second, half**, and **double**.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| **Two** | Compré dos libros porque cada estudiante necesita uno. | I bought two books because each student needs one. |
| **The Two** Versus **Two** | Compré dos libros y le di los dos libros a Laura. | I bought two books and gave the two books to Laura. |
| **Both** | Leí ambos libros: el primero explica la teoría y el segundo muestra los ejemplos. | I read both books: the first one explains the theory, and the second one shows the examples. |
| **A Couple Of** | Dame un par de minutos para terminar este correo. | Give me a couple of minutes to finish this email. |
| **A Pair Of** | Necesito un par de zapatos negros para la entrevista. | I need a pair of black shoes for the interview. |
| **Twins** | Las gemelas se parecen mucho, pero tienen personalidades diferentes. | The twins look very similar, but they have different personalities. |
| **Twice** | Leí el contrato dos veces porque encontré un número diferente en cada copia. | I read the contract twice because I found a different number in each copy. |
| **Second, Half**, and **Double** | Las dos gemelas leyeron ambos libros dos veces. | The two twins read both books twice. |

### Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Las dos gemelas leyeron ambos libros dos veces. | The two twins read both books twice. |
| Compré un par de zapatos y un par de medias hace un par de días. | I bought a pair of shoes and a pair of socks a couple of days ago. |
| El segundo par cuesta el doble que el primero, así que comprar dos pares costaría el doble. | The second pair costs twice as much as the first one, so buying two pairs would cost double. |

## Numbers One to Twelve

Cardinal numbers can determine the exact quantity of a countable noun.

| Number | Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | uno | one | one cat |
| 2 | dos | two | two cats |
| 3 | tres | three | three cats |
| 4 | cuatro | four | four cats |
| 5 | cinco | five | five cats |
| 6 | seis | six | six cats |
| 7 | siete | seven | seven cats |
| 8 | ocho | eight | eight cats |
| 9 | nueve | nine | nine cats |
| 10 | diez | ten | ten cats |
| 11 | once | eleven | eleven cats |
| 12 | doce | twelve | twelve cats |

Use the singular noun after **one** and the plural after the other numbers.

## Numbers Thirteen to Ninety-Nine

### **-teen**

| Number | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | trece | thirteen |
| 14 | catorce | fourteen |
| 15 | quince | fifteen |
| 16 | dieciséis | sixteen |
| 17 | diecisiete | seventeen |
| 18 | dieciocho | eighteen |
| 19 | diecinueve | nineteen |

Stress the final syllable when the number is said alone or contrasted: thir**TEEN**, four**TEEN**.

### **-ty**

| Number | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | veinte | twenty |
| 30 | treinta | thirty |
| 40 | cuarenta | forty |
| 50 | cincuenta | fifty |
| 60 | sesenta | sixty |
| 70 | setenta | seventy |
| 80 | ochenta | eighty |
| 90 | noventa | ninety |

The tens normally stress the first syllable: **THIR**ty, **FOR**ty. Notice the spelling **forty**, not *fourty*.

Join a ten and a unit with a hyphen in writing: **twenty-one, forty-five, ninety-nine**.

Compare the sound carefully:

- We invited **fifteen** people, not **fifty**.
- The room holds **fourteen** students, not **forty**.

## **Two**

Use **two** to state the number.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Compré dos libros porque cada estudiante necesita uno. | I bought two books because each student needs one. |

## **The Two** Versus **Two**

**Two books** introduces any two books. **The two books** identifies two specific books already known.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Compré dos libros y le di los dos libros a Laura. | I bought two books and gave the two books to Laura. |

Once the books are known, English can replace the repeated noun: **I gave both to Laura**.

## **Both**

Use **both** when the statement applies to the complete set of two.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Leí ambos libros: el primero explica la teoría y el segundo muestra los ejemplos. | I read both books: the first one explains the theory, and the second one shows the examples. |

Compare:

- **I read two books**: the number is two;
- **I read both books**: I read the complete known set of two.

Common forms are **both books**, **both of the books**, and **both of them**.

## **A Couple Of**

**A couple of** commonly means two, but in relaxed speech it can also mean a small approximate number.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Dame un par de minutos para terminar este correo. | Give me a couple of minutes to finish this email. |

Here the speaker requests a short time, not necessarily exactly 120 seconds. Use **two minutes** when the exact number matters.

## **A Pair Of**

Use **a pair of** for two things designed, matched or treated as one set.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Necesito un par de zapatos negros para la entrevista. | I need a pair of black shoes for the interview. |

Some objects use the pair as their normal unit:

- **a pair of shoes / socks / gloves**
- **a pair of glasses**
- **a pair of scissors / pants**

Say **two pairs of shoes** for four individual shoes arranged as two pairs.

## **Twins**

**Twins** are two children born from the same pregnancy.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Las gemelas se parecen mucho, pero tienen personalidades diferentes. | The twins look very similar, but they have different personalities. |

Use **twin brothers**, **twin sisters**, or **twins**. Two siblings of different ages are not twins.

## **Twice**

**Twice** means **dos veces**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Leí el contrato dos veces porque encontré un número diferente en cada copia. | I read the contract twice because I found a different number in each copy. |

In normal conversation, prefer **twice** to **two times** unless the number needs contrastive emphasis.

**Twice as...as** means double the degree or amount:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El segundo apartamento cuesta el doble que el primero. | The second apartment costs twice as much as the first one. |

## **Second, Half**, and **Double**

Related two-based ideas use different words:

| Meaning | English example |
|---|---|
| position number 2 | **the second one** |
| one of two equal parts | **half of the book** |
| two times an amount | **double the price** |

Do not use **two** mechanically for these relationships.
