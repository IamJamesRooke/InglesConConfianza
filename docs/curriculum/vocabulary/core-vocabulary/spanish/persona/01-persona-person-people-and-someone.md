# **Persona** → **Person**, **People**, or **Someone**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Es una persona amable. | She is a kind person. |
| Hay muchas personas en la sala. | There are many people in the room. |
| Necesito una persona que pueda ayudarme. | I need someone who can help me. |

Singular **persona** usually becomes **person**. English uses **people** for the ordinary plural and **someone** when the person is indefinite.
