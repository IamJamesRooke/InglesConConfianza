# **Pagar** → **Pay**, **Pay For**, **Pay Back**, and **Pay Up**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| pagar | **to pay** |
| pagar por algo | pay for something |
| devolver dinero | pay back money |
| pagar todo | pay up |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Pago veinte mil pesos por el libro. | I pay twenty thousand pesos for the book. |

**Pay for** names what you purchase, **pay back** returns money, and **pay up** emphasizes completing a debt or payment.
