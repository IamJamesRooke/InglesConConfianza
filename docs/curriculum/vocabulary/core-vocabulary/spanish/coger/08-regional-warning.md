# Regional Warning for **Coger**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| En Colombia, “coger el bus” es una expresión normal. | In Colombia, **coger el bus** is a normal expression. |

In Colombia and Spain, **coger** is ordinary. In several Latin American countries it can be a vulgar sexual verb, so learners should recognize regional reactions and choose **tomar/agarrar** when needed.
