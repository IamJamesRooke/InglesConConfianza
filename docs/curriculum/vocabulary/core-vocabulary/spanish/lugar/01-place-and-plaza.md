# **Place** and **Plaza**

Use **place** for general Spanish **lugar**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Es un buen lugar para la reunión. | It is a good place for the meeting. |
| Nos vemos en ese lugar después de la clase. | I'll see you in that place after class. |
| Nos vemos en ese punto después de la clase. | I'll see you at that spot after class. |

English **plaza** is not the general word for **lugar**. It normally names a public square or a commercial complex:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Nos vemos en la plaza principal. | I will see you in the main plaza. |

Use **place** as the reusable foundational noun.
