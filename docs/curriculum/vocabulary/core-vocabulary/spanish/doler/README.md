# Spanish **Doler**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me duele la cabeza. | My head hurts. / I have a headache. |
| Le duelen las piernas. | Her legs hurt. |
| Me dolió lo que dijiste. | What you said hurt me. |
| Me duele haberla decepcionado. | I am sorry that I disappointed her. |

English normally makes the body part or affected person the subject. Physical pain, emotional hurt, and regret require different structures.
