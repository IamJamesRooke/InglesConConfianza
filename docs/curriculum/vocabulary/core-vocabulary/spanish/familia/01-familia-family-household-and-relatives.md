# **Familia** → **Family**, **Household**, or **Relatives**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mi familia vive en Bogotá. | My family lives in Bogotá. |
| Todos los miembros de la familia viven en la misma casa. | Everyone in the household lives in the same house. |
| Voy a visitar a mi familia. | I'm going to visit my relatives. |

Use **family** for the family as a group, **household** for the people living together, and **relatives** for family members considered individually.
