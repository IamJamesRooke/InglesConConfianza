# **Niña** → **Child**, **Girl**, or **Kid**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La niña necesita ayuda. | The child needs help. |
| La niña está jugando con su hermano. | The girl is playing with her brother. |
| Hay muchos niños en el parque. | There are many kids in the park. |

Use **child** when gender does not matter, **girl** when the female distinction matters, and informal **kid** in everyday conversation.
