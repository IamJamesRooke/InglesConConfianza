# **To Be**: **Be Going To**

Use **be going to + base verb** for a future plan or a prediction based on present evidence.

Spanish uses **ir a + infinitive** for the corresponding construction.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| voy a hacer algo | **I am going to do something** |
| va a hacer algo | **he/she is going to do something** |
| vas, vamos, van a hacer algo | **you/we/they are going to do something** |

## Future plan

**Voy a estudiar inglés esta noche.**<br>
I am going to study English tonight.

The contraction is natural in speech:

**I'm going to study English tonight.**

Keep **to** before the action:

**She is going to study.**<br>
Not: *She is going study.*
