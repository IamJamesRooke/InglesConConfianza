# Seeing: Showing Something to Somebody

Use **show + somebody + something** or **show + something + to + somebody**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| mostrarle algo a alguien | **to show somebody something** |

**Le muestro la foto a Camila.**<br>
I show Camila the photo.

Both English patterns are possible:

**I show Camila the photo.**<br>
**I show the photo to Camila.**
