# Communication Verbs: **Say** and **Tell**

Spanish **decir** can correspond to either **say** or **tell**.

## **Say something**

Use **say** when the focus is the words or message.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| decir algo | **to say something** |

**Sara dice que la reunión empieza a las nueve.**<br>
Sara says that the meeting starts at nine.

## **Tell somebody**

Use **tell** when you include the person receiving the message.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| decirle algo a alguien | **to tell somebody something** |

**Sara me dice la hora de la reunión.**<br>
Sara tells me the time of the meeting.

Use **say something** but **tell somebody**:

**Sara says something to me.**<br>
**Sara tells me something.**
