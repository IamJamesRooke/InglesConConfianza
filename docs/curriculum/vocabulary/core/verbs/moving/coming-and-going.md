# Moving: **Come** and **Go**

English chooses **come** or **go** according to the direction of movement.

## **Come**

Use **come** for movement toward the speaker or the place being discussed.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| venir | **to come** |

**Ven a mi oficina después del almuerzo.**<br>
Come to my office after lunch.

## **Go**

Use **go** for movement away from the speaker or toward another place.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ir | **to go** |

**Ve a la oficina de Diana después del almuerzo.**<br>
Go to Diana's office after lunch.

Think about the direction:

**Come here.**<br>
**Go there.**
