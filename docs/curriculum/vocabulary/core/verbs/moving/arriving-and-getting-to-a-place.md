# Moving: Arriving and Getting to a Place

English commonly uses **arrive** or the more conversational **get** for reaching a destination.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| llegar a un lugar | **to arrive at/in a place**, **to get to a place** |

## **Arrive at** and **arrive in**

Use **arrive at** for a specific place or point.

**Llegamos al hotel a las ocho.**<br>
We arrive at the hotel at eight.

Use **arrive in** for a city, region, or country.

**Llegamos a Bogotá el viernes.**<br>
We arrive in Bogotá on Friday.

Do not use **to** after **arrive**:

**We arrive at the hotel.**<br>
Not: *We arrive to the hotel.*

## **Get to**

Use **get to** for the common conversational form.

**Llegamos al hotel a las ocho.**<br>
We get to the hotel at eight.

Unlike **arrive**, **get** uses **to** before the destination.
