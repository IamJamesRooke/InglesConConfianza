# **Kind, Type**, and **Sort**

English **kind, type**, and **sort** can all classify something.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| un tipo de libro | a kind of book |
| un tipo de libro | a type of book |
| un tipo de libro | a sort of book |

**Kind** and **type** are the most neutral foundational choices. **Sort** is common in conversation and often feels slightly less formal.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿Qué tipo de café quieres? | What kind of coffee do you want? |
| Este es un tipo diferente de problema. | This is a different type of problem. |

With one category, use **a kind/type/sort of + singular noun**.
