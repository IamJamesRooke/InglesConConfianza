# Language, Nationality, and Citizenship

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| inglés | English |
| español | Spanish |
| una nacionalidad | a nationality |
| una ciudadanía | a citizenship |

English language names begin with a capital letter:

- **English**
- **Spanish**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Hablo inglés y español. | I speak English and Spanish. |
| Quiero saber tu nacionalidad. | I want to know your nationality. |
| Ella tiene ciudadanía colombiana. | She has Colombian citizenship. |

**Nationality** describes national identity. **Citizenship** describes legal membership in a country; they are not always the same.
