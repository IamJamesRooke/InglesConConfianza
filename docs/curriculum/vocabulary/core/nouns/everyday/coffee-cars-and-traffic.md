# Coffee, Cars, and Traffic

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| café | coffee |
| un carro | a car |
| tráfico | traffic |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Quiero un café. | I want a coffee. |
| Tengo un carro. | I have a car. |
| Hay mucho tráfico esta mañana. | There is a lot of traffic this morning. |

**Car** is countable: **one car, two cars**. General **traffic** is uncountable: **a lot of traffic**, not *many traffics*.

In a café, **a coffee** means one serving of coffee. The drink in general is uncountable: **I like coffee**.
