# **By** and **Beside**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Siéntese junto a Ana, al lado de la ventana. | Sit by Ana, beside the window. |

For position, both **by** and **beside** can mean **al lado de** or **junto a**. **By** can also allow the looser meaning **cerca de**, while **beside** more directly presents two things next to each other.

For the contrast with **near** and **next to**, see [**By, Near**, and **Next To**](../../space-direction-and-movement/by-and-through/confusion-sets/03-by-near-next-to.md).
