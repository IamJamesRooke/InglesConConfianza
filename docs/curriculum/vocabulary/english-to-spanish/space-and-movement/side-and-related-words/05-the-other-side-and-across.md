# The **Other Side** and **Across**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El banco queda al otro lado de la calle, frente a la farmacia. | The bank is on the other side of the street, across from the pharmacy. |

Use **on the other side of** to name the opposite position. Use **across from** when two places or people face each other with a space between them.

For movement from one side to the other, use **across** without **from**:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Cruzamos la calle para llegar al banco. | We walked across the street to get to the bank. |

For an interior path versus a path over an area or surface, see [**Through** vs **Across**](../by-and-through/confusion-sets/04-through-vs-across.md).
