# **Go, Come**, and **Get Back**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Vuelve a la oficina, regresa acá cuando termines y llega a casa antes de las diez. | Go back to the office, come back here when you're done, and get back home before ten. |

**Go back** points away from the speaker or reference point; **come back** points toward it. **Get back** focuses on returning or arriving again.
