# Whole Expressions with **Around**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Come Around**](01-come-around.md) | Vino a visitarnos y finalmente cambió de opinión. | She came around to visit and finally came around. |
| [**Get Around**](02-get-around.md) | Se desplaza en bus y encontró una manera de evitar la regla. | She gets around by bus and found a way to get around the rule. |
| [**Go Around**](03-go-around.md) | Hay un rumor circulando y hay suficientes copias para todos. | There's a rumor going around, and there are enough copies to go around. |
| [**Mess Around**](04-mess-around.md) | Deja de perder el tiempo y ponte a trabajar. | Stop messing around and get to work. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ana cambió de opinión, encontró una manera de evitar la regla y dejó de perder el tiempo. | Ana came around, found a way to get around the rule, and stopped messing around. |
