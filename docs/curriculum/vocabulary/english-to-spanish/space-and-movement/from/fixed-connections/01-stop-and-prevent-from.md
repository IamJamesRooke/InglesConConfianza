# **Stop / Prevent Someone From Doing**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La lluvia nos impidió salir. | The rain prevented us from going out. |
| El guardia evitó que el hombre entrara. | The guard stopped the man from going in. |

Use **stop/prevent + person + from + verb-ing**. Spanish often restructures this as **impedir/evitar que + verb**.
