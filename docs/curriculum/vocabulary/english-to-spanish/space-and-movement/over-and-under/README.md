# Contextual Uses of **Over** and **Under**

The core picture is a higher path or position for **over** and a lower path or position for **under**. That picture also supports comparisons with a limit, while prefixes and whole expressions add meanings that context must clarify.

| Section | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Core Picture](core-picture/README.md) | El avión voló sobre el puente y el tren pasó por debajo. | The plane flew over the bridge, and the train passed under it. |
| [Core Uses](core-uses/README.md) | Hay una lámpara encima de la mesa, justo por encima de mi cabeza. | There's a lamp over the table, just above my head. |
| [Predictable Extensions](predictable-extensions/README.md) | No sobreestimes el costo ni subestimes el tiempo. | Don't overestimate the cost or underestimate the time. |
| [Whole Expressions](whole-expressions/README.md) | Repasa el informe; si encuentras otro error, hazlo de nuevo. | Go over the report; if you find another mistake, do it over. |

## Whole-Topic Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El avión pasó sobre nosotros, el tren pasó por debajo del puente y ahora ambos están allá. | The plane passed over us, the train went under the bridge, and now they're both over there. |
| El límite es de 100: estamos justo por encima, pero el otro equipo está por debajo. | The limit is 100: we're just over it, but the other team is under it. |
| No lo pienses demasiado: cuando se acabe la reunión, revisaremos el plan y empezaremos de nuevo. | Don't overthink it: when the meeting is over, we'll look over the plan and start over. |
