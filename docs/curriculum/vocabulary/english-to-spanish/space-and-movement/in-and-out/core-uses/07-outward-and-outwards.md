# **Outward** and **Outwards**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Empuje la puerta hacia afuera. | Push the door outward. |

**Outward** marks direction away from the inside or center. **Outwards** has the same directional meaning and is especially common in British English; **outward** is standard in both British and American English.

Only **outward** normally goes before a noun: **an outward movement**, not *an outwards movement*.

Compare the base position and the direction:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mire afuera y luego extienda los brazos hacia afuera. | Look outside, and then stretch your arms outward. |
