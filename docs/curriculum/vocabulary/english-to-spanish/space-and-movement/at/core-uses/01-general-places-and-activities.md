# **At** for General Places and Activities

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ana está en el trabajo, Luis está en la escuela y nos encontraremos en la tienda. | Ana is at work, Luis is at school, and we'll meet at the store. |

Use **at** when the place is a general location or the site of its usual activity. **At work, at home, at school**, and **at church** normally omit **the**; a specific building can take it: **at the school** or **at the church**.
