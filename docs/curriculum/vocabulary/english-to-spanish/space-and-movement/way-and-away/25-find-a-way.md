# **Find a Way**

**Find a way to do something** means to discover a possible method or solution.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No tenemos suficiente presupuesto, pero encontraremos la manera de terminar el proyecto sin reducir la calidad. | We do not have enough money in the budget, but we will find a way to finish the project without reducing the quality. |

The unknown method will solve the budget problem.
