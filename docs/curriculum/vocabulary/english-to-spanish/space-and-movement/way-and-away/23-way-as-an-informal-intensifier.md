# **Way** as an Informal Intensifier

Before a comparative, informal **way** means **much** or **far**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El segundo apartamento es mucho mejor que el primero porque cuesta menos y queda más cerca del trabajo. | The second apartment is way better than the first because it costs less and is closer to work. |

**Way better** is common in conversation. Use **much better** or **far better** in more formal English.
