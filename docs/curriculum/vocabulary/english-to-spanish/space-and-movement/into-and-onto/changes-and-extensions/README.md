# Changes and Extensions of **Into** and **Onto**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Turn Into](01-turn-into.md) | El agua se convirtió en hielo. | The water turned into ice. |
| [Change and Transform Into](02-change-transform-into.md) | Transformaron la casa en un hotel. | They transformed the house into a hotel. |
| [Translate Into](03-translate-into.md) | Traduzca esto al inglés. | Translate this into English. |
| [Divide Into](04-divide-into.md) | Divida la clase en grupos. | Divide the class into groups. |
| [Look Into](05-look-into.md) | Investigaremos el problema. | We'll look into the problem. |
| [Run Into](06-run-into.md) | Me encontré con Ana. | I ran into Ana. |
| [Be Into](07-be-into.md) | Le interesa mucho la fotografía. | She's really into photography. |
| [Move On To](08-move-on-to.md) | Pasemos al siguiente tema. | Let's move on to the next topic. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Investigamos cómo transformaron la casa en un hotel, tradujimos el informe al inglés y pasamos al siguiente caso. | We looked into how they transformed the house into a hotel, translated the report into English, and moved on to the next case. |
