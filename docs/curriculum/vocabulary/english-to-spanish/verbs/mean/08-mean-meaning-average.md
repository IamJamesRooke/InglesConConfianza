# **Mean** Meaning Average

In mathematics, the **mean** is a type of average found by adding values and dividing by how many there are.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El promedio de 2, 4 y 6 es 4. | The mean of 2, 4, and 6 is 4. |

Everyday **average** is broader; not every statistical average is the arithmetic mean.
