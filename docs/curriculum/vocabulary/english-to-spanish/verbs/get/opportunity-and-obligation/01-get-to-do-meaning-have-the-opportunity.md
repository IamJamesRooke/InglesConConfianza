# **Get To Do**: Have the Opportunity

**Get to + verb** means have the opportunity or privilege to do something.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| En este trabajo puedo viajar y conocer a personas de todo el mundo. | In this job, I get to travel and meet people from all over the world. |

Here, **get to** doesn't mean obligation. It presents the action as something available, special, or enjoyable: **We get to leave early today!**
