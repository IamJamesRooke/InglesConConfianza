# **Get, Obtain**, and **Acquire**

**Get** is broad and conversational. **Obtain** sounds formal or procedural; **acquire** often suggests gaining something gradually, deliberately, or permanently.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Conseguimos el permiso, obtuvimos la licencia oficial y adquirimos el equipo necesario. | We got permission, obtained the official license, and acquired the necessary equipment. |
