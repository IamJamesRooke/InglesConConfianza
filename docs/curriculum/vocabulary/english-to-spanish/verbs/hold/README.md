# Spanish Translations of English **Hold**

English **hold** maps to Spanish verbs for grasping, containing, organizing, maintaining, waiting, supporting, and restraining.

| Lesson | English | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| [**Hold → Sostener / Agarrar**](01-grasp.md) | Hold this while I open the door. | Sostén esto mientras abro la puerta. |
| [**Hold → Tener Capacidad Para**](02-contain.md) | The room holds twenty people. | El salón tiene capacidad para veinte personas. |
| [**Hold → Celebrar / Realizar**](03-organize-an-event.md) | They'll hold a meeting tomorrow. | Celebrarán una reunión mañana. |
| [**Hold → Mantener**](04-maintain-a-position.md) | Hold the door open and hold your position. | Mantén la puerta abierta y mantén tu posición. |
| [**Hold On → Esperar**](05-hold-on-wait.md) | Hold on a moment, please. | Espera un momento, por favor. |
| [**Hold On To → Agarrarse / Conservar**](06-hold-on-grip.md) | Hold on to the rail and hold on to the receipt. | Agárrate de la baranda y conserva el recibo. |
| [**Hold Back → Contener / Frenar**](07-hold-back.md) | She couldn't hold back her tears. | No pudo contener las lágrimas. |
| [**Hold Up → Sostener / Retrasar**](08-hold-up.md) | Traffic held us up for an hour. | El tráfico nos retrasó una hora. |
