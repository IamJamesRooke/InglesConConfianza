# **Once** Meaning One Time

**Once** means **una vez**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Solo he visitado Cartagena una vez, pero quiero volver el próximo año. | I've only visited Cartagena once, but I want to go back next year. |

**I've** is the standard contraction of **I have**. In ordinary speech, prefer **once** to **one time** unless the number itself needs emphasis.
