# **Unlike**

**Unlike** means **a diferencia de** or **a diferencia de lo que ocurre con**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| A diferencia de su hermano, Natalia disfruta hablar en público. | Unlike her brother, Natalia enjoys speaking in public. |

Do not confuse **unlike** with **dislike**:

- **Unlike Carlos, I work from home**: Carlos and I are different;
- **I dislike working from home**: I do not enjoy it.
