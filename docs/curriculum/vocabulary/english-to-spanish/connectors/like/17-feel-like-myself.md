# **Feel Like Myself**

**Feel like myself/yourself** means to feel normal or like one's usual self.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Todavía no me siento como siempre después de la gripe, así que voy a descansar hoy. | I still don't feel like myself after the flu, so I'm going to rest today. |

This differs from **I don't feel like working**, which means **no tengo ganas de trabajar**.
