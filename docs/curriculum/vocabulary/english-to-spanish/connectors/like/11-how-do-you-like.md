# **How Do You Like...?**

**How do you like...?** asks for someone's opinion or reaction after experiencing something.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| —¿Qué te parece tu nuevo trabajo? —Me gusta mucho, aunque el trayecto es largo. | “How do you like your new job?” “I like it a lot, although the commute is long.” |

It does not ask for the method by which the person likes something.
