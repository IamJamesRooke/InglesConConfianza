# **That** Standing Alone

**That** can replace a singular thing or complete idea already visible or understood.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me gusta eso, pero no me gusta aquello que está junto a la puerta. | I like that, but I don't like that one by the door. |

**That one** emphasizes a choice among similar items. Plural **those** normally stands alone without **ones**: **I like those**.
