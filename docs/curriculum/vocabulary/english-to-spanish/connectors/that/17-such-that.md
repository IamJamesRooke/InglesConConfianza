# **Such...That**

Use **such + noun phrase + that + result** when the noun itself is central to the description.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Fue una reunión tan larga que tuvimos que pedir la cena. | It was such a long meeting that we had to order dinner. |

Compare:

- **The meeting was so long that...**
- **It was such a long meeting that...**
