# Prepositions and Relative **That**

Conversational English commonly leaves the preposition at the end:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Esa es la persona con la que hablé y el tema al que me referí. | That's the person that I spoke to and the topic that I referred to. |

In formal fronted patterns, use **whom** or **which**, not **that**:

- **the person to whom I spoke**
- **the topic to which I referred**
- not *the person to that I spoke*.
