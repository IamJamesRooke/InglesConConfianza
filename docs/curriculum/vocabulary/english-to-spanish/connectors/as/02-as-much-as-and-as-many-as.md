# **As Much As** and **As Many As**

The Spanish singular–plural contrast transfers directly:

- **tanto trabajo como → as much work as**
- **tantas reuniones como → as many meetings as**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tengo tanto trabajo y tantas reuniones como Laura. | I've got as much work and as many meetings as Laura. |

When the comparison ends with a complete idea, English often avoids repetition: **as Laura does**.
