# **As Soon As**

Use **as soon as** for an action that follows immediately after another.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Te llamaré tan pronto como llegue a la oficina. | I'll call you as soon as I get to the office. |

After **as soon as**, English normally uses the present form for a future event: **as soon as I get**, not *as soon as I will get*.
