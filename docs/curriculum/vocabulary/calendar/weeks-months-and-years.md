# Weeks, Months, and Years

| Unit | Current | Following | Previous |
|---|---|---|---|
| día | today | tomorrow | yesterday |
| semana | this week | next week | last week |
| mes | this month | next month | last month |
| año | this year | next year | last year |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tengo que terminarlo esta semana. | I have to finish it this week. |
| Voy a viajar el próximo mes. | I am going to travel next month. |
| Empecé a estudiar inglés el año pasado. | I started studying English last year. |
| Viajamos en enero. | We travel in January. |
| Viajamos el próximo enero. | We are traveling next January. |
| Viajamos el enero pasado. | We traveled last January. |

Do not add **in** before these complete expressions: **next week**, not *in next week*.

## Saying years

Years are often divided into two pairs:

| Written | Spoken |
|---|---|
| 1998 | nineteen ninety-eight |
| 2020 | twenty twenty |
| 2026 | twenty twenty-six |

**Two thousand twenty** is also possible for 2020, though **twenty twenty** is more usual. The year in a complete date does not need **in**: **Thursday, January 21st, 1999**, not *Thursday, January 21st, in 1999*.
