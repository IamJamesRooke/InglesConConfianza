# **Other, Another**, and **The Other**

| Form | Core meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| another + singular | one more or a different one | another cat |
| other + plural | additional or different ones | other cats |
| the other + singular | the remaining one of two | the other cat |
| the other + plural | the remaining specific ones | the other cats |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Quiero otro libro. | I want another book. |
| Quiero otros libros. | I want other books. |
| Quiero el otro libro. | I want the other book. |
| Quiero los otros libros. | I want the other books. |

Do not use **another** directly before a normal plural noun: **other books**, not *another books*.
