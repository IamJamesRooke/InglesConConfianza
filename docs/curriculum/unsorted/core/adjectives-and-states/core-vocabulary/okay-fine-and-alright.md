# **Okay, Fine**, and **Alright**

Use **okay, fine**, and informal **alright** for an acceptable or satisfactory state:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Estoy bien. | I am okay. / I am fine. / I am alright. |
| El plan está bien. | The plan is okay. / The plan is fine. |

They can also reassure someone:

- **Don't worry—everything is okay.**
- **Laura felt sick yesterday, but she is fine now.**

**All right** is the traditional two-word spelling. **Alright** is extremely common, especially in informal writing, but some formal style guides still prefer **all right**.

This meaning is different from **good**:

- **The food is good.** — positive quality
- **The food is okay.** — acceptable, but not necessarily impressive
