# Company, Money, Price, and Cost

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| una compañía / una empresa | a company |
| dinero | money |
| un dólar | a dollar |
| un precio | a price |
| un costo | a cost |

## **Price** and **cost**

As nouns, both can name an amount of money, but the viewpoint differs.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El precio del libro es veinte dólares. | The price of the book is twenty dollars. |
| El costo total es treinta dólares. | The total cost is thirty dollars. |

**Price** is the amount requested for a product or service. **Cost** is the amount required, paid, or sacrificed.

**Money** is normally uncountable: **some money / much money**, not *a money* or *many monies* in ordinary conversation.
