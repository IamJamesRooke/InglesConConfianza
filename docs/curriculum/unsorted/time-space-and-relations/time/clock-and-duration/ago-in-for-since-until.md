# **Ago, In, For, Since**, and **Until**

These words place an event relative to now or describe its duration.

| Relationship | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| before now | hace unas horas | a few hours ago |
| after now | en unas horas | in a few hours |
| duration | durante unas horas | for a few hours |
| starting point | desde las doce | since twelve |
| ending point | hasta las doce | until twelve |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Llegué hace dos horas. | I arrived two hours ago. |
| Voy a terminar en dos horas. | I am going to finish in two hours. |
| Trabajé durante dos horas. | I worked for two hours. |
| He estado aquí desde las doce. | I have been here since twelve. |
| Voy a estar aquí hasta las doce. | I am going to be here until twelve. |

Use **for** with an amount of time and **since** with the point when the time began.
