# **At**: A Point, Activity, or General Place

Use **at** when a place functions as a point on a map, the site of an activity, or a general location.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| en un lugar | at a place |
| en la tienda | at the store |
| en la oficina | at the office |
| en el apartamento | at the apartment |
| en el parque | at the park |
| en el aeropuerto | at the airport |
| en el evento | at the event |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Estoy en la oficina para una reunión. | I am at the office for a meeting. |
| Nos vemos en el aeropuerto. | I will see you at the airport. |
| Laura está en el evento. | Laura is at the event. |

Use **at** for the general location. Use **in** when being physically inside is the important meaning.
