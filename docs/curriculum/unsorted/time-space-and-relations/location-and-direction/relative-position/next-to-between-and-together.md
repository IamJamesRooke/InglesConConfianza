# **Next To, Between**, and **Together**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| al lado de / junto a | next to |
| entre dos cosas o personas | between |
| juntos | together |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La tienda está al lado de la oficina. | The store is next to the office. |
| El parque está entre la tienda y la oficina. | The park is between the store and the office. |
| Laura y Diana están juntas en la oficina. | Laura and Diana are together in the office. |

Use **between A and B** to name the two sides: **between the store and the office**.

**Together** describes people or things forming one group; it does not need a second object.
