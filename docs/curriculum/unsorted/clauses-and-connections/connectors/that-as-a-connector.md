# Connector **That**

Use connector **that** to introduce an idea after verbs such as **say, think, believe**, and **know**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Creo que es importante. | I think that it is important. |
| Sé que Laura está aquí. | I know that Laura is here. |
| Laura dice que la reunión empieza a las nueve. | Laura says that the meeting starts at nine. |

In everyday English, connector **that** can often be omitted:

- **I think that it is important.**
- **I think it is important.**

This connector **that** introduces a complete idea. It is different from demonstrative **that** in **that book**.
