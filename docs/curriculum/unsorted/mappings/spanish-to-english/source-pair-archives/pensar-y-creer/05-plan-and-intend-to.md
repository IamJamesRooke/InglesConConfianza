# **Pensar Hacer → Plan/Intend To**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Pienso viajar en diciembre y planeo comprar el tiquete mañana. | I intend to travel in December, and I plan to buy the ticket tomorrow. |

Use **plan to** for an organized intention and **intend to** for a stated purpose or intention. Everyday English often prefers **plan to**.
