# Homophones: **Mind** and **Mined**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No me importa dónde extrajeron el carbón. | I don't mind where they mined the coal. |

**Mind** and **mined** are both pronounced `/maɪnd/`. **Mind** can express objection; **mined** is the past form of **mine**, meaning extract material from the ground.
