# **White → Whiteness**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La blancura de la pared hacía que el cuarto pareciera más luminoso. | The whiteness of the wall made the room look brighter. |

Add **-ness** to name the quality or state of being white.

The final **e** remains: **white + -ness → whiteness**.

**Whiteness** can also have specialized social or cultural meanings. Those meanings depend on context and are not simply descriptions of color.
