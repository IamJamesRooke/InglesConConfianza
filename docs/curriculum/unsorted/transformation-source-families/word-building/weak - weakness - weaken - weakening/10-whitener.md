# **Whiten → Whitener**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Probamos el blanqueador en una parte pequeña de la tela. | We tested the whitener on a small part of the fabric. |

Add **-er** to name a product or substance that whitens something.

English commonly specifies its purpose:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Este blanqueador dental no contiene peróxido. | This tooth whitener doesn't contain peroxide. |

For laundry, **whitening agent** or the specific product name may sound more natural than **whitener**.
