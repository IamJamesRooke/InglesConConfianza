# **Whitewash**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Encalaron la pared para cubrir las manchas. | They whitewashed the wall to cover the stains. |

As a physical verb, **whitewash** means cover a surface with a thin white coating.

It also has a figurative meaning: conceal faults or wrongdoing by presenting a falsely favorable version.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El informe intentó encubrir las fallas de seguridad. | The report tried to whitewash the security failures. |

The related forms are **whitewashing** and **whitewashed**. This is an established compound, not the productive suffix **-wash**.
