# **Look Forward To**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Espero con ilusión conocerlos el lunes. | I look forward to meeting you on Monday. |

**Look forward to** means await something with pleasure or anticipation. Its complete meaning is not a physical direction.

Because **to** belongs to this fixed connection, a following verb takes **-ing**: **look forward to meeting**, not *look forward to meet*.
