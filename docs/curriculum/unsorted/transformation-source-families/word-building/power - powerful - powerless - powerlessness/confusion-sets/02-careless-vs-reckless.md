# **Careless** vs **Reckless**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Fue descuidado al copiar el número y temerario al conducir a esa velocidad. | He was careless when copying the number and reckless when driving at that speed. |

**Careless** means not giving enough attention or care. **Reckless** is stronger: the person disregards a serious and recognizable risk.

Both can translate **imprudente** in some contexts, but they do not describe the same degree of danger.
