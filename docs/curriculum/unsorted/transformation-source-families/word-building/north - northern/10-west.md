# **West**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La Calera queda al este de Bogotá, no al oeste. | La Calera is east of Bogotá, not west. |

**West** names the direction **oeste**. It can also identify a position or side:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Vimos el atardecer desde el lado oeste del edificio. | We watched the sunset from the west side of the building. |
