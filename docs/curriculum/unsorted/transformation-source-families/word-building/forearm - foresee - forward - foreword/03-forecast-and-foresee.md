# **Forecast** and **Foresee**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El pronóstico anuncia lluvia, y prevemos retrasos. | The forecast predicts rain, and we foresee delays. |

**Forecast** is an estimate or prediction, especially for weather, finances, or demand. **Foresee** means anticipate that something will happen.

Both preserve the core idea of knowing or estimating before the event.
