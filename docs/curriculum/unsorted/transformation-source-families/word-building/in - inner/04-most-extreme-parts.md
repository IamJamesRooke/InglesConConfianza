# **Uppermost, Lowermost, Innermost**, and **Outermost**

Add **-most** when the part is at the greatest extreme.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El cajón de más arriba está cerrado y el de más abajo está abierto. | The uppermost drawer is closed, and the lowermost drawer is open. |
| Revisaron desde la capa más interna hasta la más externa. | They checked from the innermost layer to the outermost layer. |

In everyday English, **top** and **bottom** are often more natural than **uppermost** and **lowermost**: **the top drawer**, **the bottom drawer**. Use the **-most** forms when the extreme relative position matters.
