# **Be → Been** and **See → Seen**

Use the base verb as the immediate bridge to the past participle.

## Transformation

| Base | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| be | **been** | ser / estar |
| see | **seen** | ver |

## Examples

**The grass has been mown, and the seeds have been sown.**

**He sido profesor durante diez años y he estado en Bogotá todo este tiempo.**<br>
I have been a teacher for ten years and have been in Bogotá all this time.
