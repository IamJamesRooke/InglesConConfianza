# **Go → Gone** and **Do → Done**

Use the base verb as the immediate bridge to the past participle.

## Transformation

| Base | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| go | **gone** | ir |
| do | **done** | hacer |

## Examples

**I rewrote what I had written and redid what I had done.**

**He hecho mi tarea y he ido a la oficina.**<br>
I have done my homework and gone to the office.
