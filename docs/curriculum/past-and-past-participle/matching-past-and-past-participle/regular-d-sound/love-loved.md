# **Love → Loved → Loved**

The written **-ed** ending is pronounced `/d/`.


After final `/v/`, **-ed** is pronounced `/d/`:

**love → loved**

| Course source | Base | Past and past participle | Spanish bridge |
|---|---|---|---|
| core verb | love | loved | amó / amado |
| core verb | live | lived | vivió / vivido |
| core verb | believe | believed | creyó / creído |
| core verb | receive | received | recibió / recibido |
| core verb | arrive | arrived | llegó / llegado |

- Past: Laura **lived** nearby and **loved** the neighborhood.
- Past participle: Laura has **lived** nearby and has always **loved** the neighborhood.

Keep `/v/` voiced and attach `/d/`: live + `/d/`, love + `/d/`.
