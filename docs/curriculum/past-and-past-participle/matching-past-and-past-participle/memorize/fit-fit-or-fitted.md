# The **Fit → Fit/Fitted** Family

Both verbs allow a no-change form and a regular **-ed** form, depending on meaning and variety.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| fit | **fit/fitted** | **fit/fitted** | caber / quedar bien |
| knit | **knit/knitted** | **knit/knitted** | tejer |
