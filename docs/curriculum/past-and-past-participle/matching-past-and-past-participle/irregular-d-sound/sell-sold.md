# **Sell → Sold** and **Tell → Told**

The irregular past and past participle match. Learn the complete three-form sound set together.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| sell | **sold** | **sold** | vender |
| tell | **told** | **told** | decir / contar |

## Examples

**She told me she had sold the old table.**

**Le dije a Laura que la tienda vendió el último libro.**<br>
I told Laura that the store sold the last book.
