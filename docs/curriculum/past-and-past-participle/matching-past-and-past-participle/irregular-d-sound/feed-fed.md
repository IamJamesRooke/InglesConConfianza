# The **Feed → Fed → Fed** Family

The long **ee** sound shortens before the final **d** sound.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| bleed | **bled** | **bled** | sangrar |
| breed | **bred** | **bred** | criar |
| feed | **fed** | **fed** | alimentar |
| flee | **fled** | **fled** | huir |
| lead | **led** | **led** | liderar / guiar |
| speed | **sped/speeded** | **sped/speeded** | acelerar |

## Examples

**The guide fed the animals after they had already been fed.**
