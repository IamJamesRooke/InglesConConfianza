# **Stand → Stood** and **Understand → Understood**

The irregular past and past participle match. Learn the complete three-form sound set together.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| stand | **stood** | **stood** | estar de pie |
| understand | **understood** | **understood** | entender |

## Examples

**I understood how the old bridge had withstood the storm.**

**Me puse de pie cuando entendí la pregunta.**<br>
I stood up when I understood the question.
