# The **Found, Bound, Ground, Wound** Family

The irregular past and past participle match. Learn the complete three-form sound set together.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| find | **found** | **found** | encontrar |
| bind | **bound** | **bound** | atar |
| grind | **ground** | **ground** | moler |
| wind | **wound** | **wound** | enrollar |

## Examples

**I found the cable wound around the machine that had ground the coffee.**

**Encontré mis llaves debajo de la mesa.**<br>
I found my keys under the table.
