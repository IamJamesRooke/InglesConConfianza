# **Make → Made**, **Pay → Paid**, and **Lay → Laid**

The irregular past and past participle match. Learn the complete three-form sound set together.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| make | **made** | **made** | hacer |
| pay | **paid** | **paid** | pagar |
| lay | **laid** | **laid** | poner / colocar |

## Examples

**I made the order, paid the bill, and laid the receipt on the table.**

**Hice la reserva y pagué el depósito.**<br>
I made the reservation and paid the deposit.
