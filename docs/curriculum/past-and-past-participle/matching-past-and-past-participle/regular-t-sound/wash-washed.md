# **Wash → Washed → Washed**

The written **-ed** ending is pronounced `/t/`.


After final `/ʃ/` written **sh**, **-ed** is pronounced `/t/`:

**wash → washed**

| Course source | Base | Past and past participle | Spanish bridge |
|---|---|---|---|
| model vocabulary | wash | washed | lavó / lavado |
| core verb | finish | finished | terminó / terminado |
| supporting vocabulary | push | pushed | empujó / empujado |

- Past: Laura **washed** the cups after she **finished** lunch.
- Past participle: Laura has **washed** the cups and has **finished** lunch.

There is no additional syllable: wash + `/t/`, finish + `/t/`.
