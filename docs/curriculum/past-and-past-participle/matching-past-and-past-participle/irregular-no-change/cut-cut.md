# **Cut → Cut** and **Shut → Shut**

The base, past, and past participle use the same form.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| cut | **cut** | **cut** | cortar |
| shut | **shut** | **shut** | cerrar |

## Examples

**I cut the paper yesterday, and I have cut the other sheets today.**
