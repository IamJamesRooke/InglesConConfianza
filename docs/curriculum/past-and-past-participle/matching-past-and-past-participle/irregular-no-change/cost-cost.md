# **Cast, Cost, and Broadcast**

The base, past, and past participle use the same form.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| cast | **cast** | **cast** | lanzar / asignar un papel |
| cost | **cost** | **cost** | costar |
| broadcast | **broadcast** | **broadcast** | transmitir |

## Examples

**Puse el libro sobre la mesa; costó veinte mil pesos.**<br>
I put the book on the table; it cost twenty thousand pesos.

**He puesto el libro sobre la mesa; las reparaciones han costado mucho dinero.**<br>
I have put the book on the table; the repairs have cost a lot of money.
