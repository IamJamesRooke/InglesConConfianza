# The **Feel → Felt → Felt** Family

The long **ee** sound shortens before **-lt**; accepted regular alternatives remain visible.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| deal | **dealt** | **dealt** | tratar / repartir |
| feel | **felt** | **felt** | sentir |
| kneel | **kneeled/knelt** | **kneeled/knelt** | arrodillarse |

## Examples

**I felt calmer after I had dealt with the problem.**
