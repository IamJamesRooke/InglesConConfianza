# The **Keep → Kept → Kept** Family

The long **ee** sound shortens before **-pt**; accepted regular alternatives remain visible.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| creep | **crept** | **crept** | avanzar sigilosamente |
| keep | **kept** | **kept** | guardar / mantener |
| leap | **leaped/leapt** | **leaped/leapt** | saltar |
| sleep | **slept** | **slept** | dormir |
| sweep | **swept** | **swept** | barrer |
| weep | **wept** | **wept** | llorar |

## Examples

**I slept well because I had kept the room quiet.**
