# The **Send → Sent → Sent** Family

The final **d** sound changes to **t**.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| bend | **bent** | **bent** | doblar |
| lend | **lent** | **lent** | prestar |
| rend | **rent** | **rent** | desgarrar |
| send | **sent** | **sent** | enviar |
| spend | **spent** | **spent** | gastar / pasar tiempo |

## Examples

**I sent the message after I had spent an hour writing it.**
