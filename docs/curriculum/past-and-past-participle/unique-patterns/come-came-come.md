# Past Participles That Return to the Base

These forms are easiest to retain as one complete three-form pattern rather than as two forced derivations.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| come | **came** | **come** | venir |
| become | **became** | **become** | convertirse / llegar a ser |
| run | **ran** | **run** | correr |

## Examples

**Vine a la oficina, comí con Laura y le di el documento.**<br>
I came to the office, ate with Laura, and gave her the document.

**Volví a la oficina después del almuerzo.**<br>
I came back to the office after lunch.
