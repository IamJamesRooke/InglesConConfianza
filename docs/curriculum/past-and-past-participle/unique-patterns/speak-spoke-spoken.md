# The **Speak → Spoke → Spoken** Family

The past forms rhyme in long **o**, and the participles add **-en**; accepted regular alternatives remain visible.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| awake | **awoke/awakened** | **awoken/awakened** | despertar(se) |
| break | **broke** | **broken** | romper |
| speak | **spoke** | **spoken** | hablar |
| wake | **woke** | **woken** | despertar(se) |

## Examples

**She spoke after she had woken up and broken the silence.**
