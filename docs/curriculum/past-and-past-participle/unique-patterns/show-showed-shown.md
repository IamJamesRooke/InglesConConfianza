# The **Show → Showed → Shown** Family

The base keeps long **o**: the regular past ends in **/d/** and the participle commonly ends in **/n/**.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| mow | **mowed** | **mown/mowed** | cortar el césped |
| sew | **sewed** | **sewn/sewed** | coser |
| show | **showed** | **shown/showed** | mostrar |
| sow | **sowed** | **sown/sowed** | sembrar |

## Examples

**She showed me the jacket she had sewn.**
