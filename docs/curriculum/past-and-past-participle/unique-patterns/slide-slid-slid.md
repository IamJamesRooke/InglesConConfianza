# The **Slide → Slid** and **Hide → Hid** Family

Both bases change **/aɪd/** to **/ɪd/** in the past. Their participles then diverge: **slid** stays unchanged, while **hid** becomes **hidden**.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| hide | **hid** | **hidden** | esconder |
| slide | **slid** | **slid** | deslizarse |

## Examples

**The children slid under the table and hid behind the sofa.**
