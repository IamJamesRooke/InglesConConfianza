# The **Rise → Rose → Risen** Family

The related bases change long **i** to long **o**, then to short **i** before **-en**.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| arise | **arose** | **arisen** | surgir |
| rise | **rose** | **risen** | subir / levantarse |

## Examples

**Prices rose last month and have risen again.**
