# The **Give → Gave → Given** Family

The related bases change short **i** to long **a**, then return to short **i** before **-en**.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| forgive | **forgave** | **forgiven** | perdonar |
| give | **gave** | **given** | dar |

## Examples

**She gave me the apology I had already forgiven her for.**
