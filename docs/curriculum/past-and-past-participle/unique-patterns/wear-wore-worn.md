# The **Wear → Wore → Worn** Family

The rhyming bases change to **-ore** in the past and **-orn/-orne** in the participle.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| bear | **bore** | **borne/born** | soportar / dar a luz |
| swear | **swore** | **sworn** | jurar |
| tear | **tore** | **torn** | rasgar |
| wear | **wore** | **worn** | llevar puesto |

## Examples

**She wore the shirt until it had worn thin and torn.**
