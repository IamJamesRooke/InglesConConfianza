# The **Eat → Ate → Eaten** and **Beat → Beat → Beaten** Family

The bases and participles rhyme, while the past forms contrast.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| beat | **beat** | **beaten** | golpear / vencer |
| eat | **ate** | **eaten** | comer |

## Examples

**We ate before our team was beaten.**
