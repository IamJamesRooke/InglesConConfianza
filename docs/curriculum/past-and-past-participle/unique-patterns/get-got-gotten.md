# The **Get → Got → Gotten** Family

The shared **get** root changes to **got** and then commonly adds **-ten**; accepted alternatives remain visible.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| beget | **begot** | **begotten/begot** | engendrar / producir |
| forget | **forgot** | **forgotten** | olvidar |
| get | **got** | **got/gotten** | obtener / ponerse |

## Examples

**I got the message, but I had forgotten the address.**
