# The **Choose → Chose → Chosen** Family

The long **oo/ee** base vowels converge on long **o** in the past and add **-n** in the participle.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| choose | **chose** | **chosen** | elegir |
| freeze | **froze** | **frozen** | congelar |

## Examples

**We chose the meal after the vegetables had frozen.**
