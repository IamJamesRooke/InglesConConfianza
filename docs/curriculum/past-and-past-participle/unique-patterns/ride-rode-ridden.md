# The **Ride → Rode → Ridden** Family

The long **i** changes to long **o**, then to short **i** before **-en**.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| ride | **rode** | **ridden** | montar / ir en |
| stride | **strode** | **stridden** | caminar a zancadas |

## Examples

**She rode the horse she had ridden before.**
