# **Be → Was/were**

There is no reliable visible transformation. Memorize this past form directly from the base.

## Transformation

| Base | Past | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| be | **was/were** | ser / estar |

## Examples

**Was** and **were** are the past forms of **be**.

**The character was slain after he had trodden the forbidden path.**<br>
El personaje fue asesinado después de haber pisado el camino prohibido.
