# **Down → Downward(s)**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mire abajo mientras el ascensor se desplaza hacia abajo. | Look down while the elevator moves downward. |

**Down** supplies the core lower-direction picture. **Downward/downwards** explicitly marks movement or orientation toward a lower position.

Use **downward** before a noun: **a downward trend**. For direction after a verb, both **downward** and **downwards** are standard.
