# **Grow → Growth**

**Grow** expresses a process of becoming larger or developing. **Growth** names the process or its result.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La empresa quiere crecer este año. | The company wants to grow this year. |
| El crecimiento de las ventas fue constante. | Sales growth was steady. |

The spelling changes from **grow** to **growth**. The **w** remains in writing, but the word must be learned as a complete sound-and-spelling family.
