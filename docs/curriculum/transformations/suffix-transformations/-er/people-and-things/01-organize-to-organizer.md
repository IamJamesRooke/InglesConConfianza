# **Organize → Organizer**

English often adds **-er** or **-or** to a verb to name the person or thing that performs its action. Spanish commonly uses **-ador**, **-edor**, **-idor**, or **-or**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| organizador | **organizer** | **La organizadora confirmó el lugar del evento.**<br>The organizer confirmed the event location. |
| receptor | **receiver** | **El técnico reemplazó el receptor de la señal.**<br>The technician replaced the signal receiver. |
| entrenador | **trainer** | **El entrenador mostró cómo utilizar la máquina.**<br>The trainer demonstrated how to use the machine. |
| presentador | **presenter** | **La presentadora explicó los resultados al público.**<br>The presenter explained the results to the audience. |

The English spelling must be learned with the word: **organizer** uses **-er**, while **actor** uses **-or**.
