# Direction, Region, and Capitalization

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Viajamos hacia el oeste para visitar el Oeste de Estados Unidos. | We traveled west to visit the American West. |

Use lowercase for an ordinary compass direction or general area: **drive north**, **northern Colombia**, **the west side**.

Capitalize a direction when it forms part of a proper name or names a widely recognized cultural or political region: **North America, South Korea, the Middle East, the American West**.

Capitalization depends on whether the expression functions as a name, not simply on physical location.
