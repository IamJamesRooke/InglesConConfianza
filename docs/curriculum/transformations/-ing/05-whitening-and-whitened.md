# **Whitening** and **Whitened**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El blanqueamiento tomó dos horas y dejó la tela blanqueada de manera uniforme. | The whitening took two hours and left the fabric evenly whitened. |

**Whitening** can name the process or describe an action in progress:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El producto está blanqueando la tela. | The product is whitening the fabric. |

**Whitened** describes a completed change or a thing made whiter: **whitened fabric**.
