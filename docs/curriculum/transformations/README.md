# Transformations

Transformations are the productive vocabulary core of the course: a familiar English word or visible form becomes another useful word or form.

| Area | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [Cognates](cognates/README.md) | Use reliable Spanish-English similarities, spelling patterns, memory bridges, and confusion sets. |
| [Suffix Transformations](suffix-transformations/README.md) | Turn a base into a related form with endings such as **-ness, -ish, -er**, and **-tion**. |
| [Prefix Transformations](prefix-transformations/README.md) | Recognize meaning-changing prefixes and memorable beginning families such as **un-, fore-, be-**, and **al-**. |
| [Past and Past Participle](past-and-past-participle/README.md) | Single source of truth for regular forms, matching irregular forms, unique pasts, unique participles, and standard variants. |

The former mixed word-building families have been distributed into the canonical prefix and suffix branches. Material that does not clearly represent a productive transformation remains in the single shared [root curriculum unsorted](../unsorted/README.md) area.
