# Regular Superlative Patterns

Use **the + adjective + -est** to identify the highest or lowest member of a group when the adjective takes the short form.

| Base | Superlative | Spanish bridge |
|---|---|---|
| hard | the hardest | difícil → el más difícil |
| fast | the fastest | rápido → el más rápido |
| slow | the slowest | lento → el más lento |
| cheap | the cheapest | barato → el más barato |
| safe | the safest | seguro → el más seguro |

Some short adjectives double the final consonant: **big → the biggest**.

Adjectives ending in consonant + **y** change **y** to **i**:

| Base | Superlative |
|---|---|
| funny | the funniest |
| happy | the happiest |
| easy | the easiest |

Longer adjectives usually use **the most**: **the most important**, **the most difficult**, and **the most beautiful**. The article **the** normally comes before the superlative.
