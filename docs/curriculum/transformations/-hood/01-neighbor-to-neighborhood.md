# **Neighbor → Neighborhood**

English **-hood** forms nouns describing a stage of life, a state, a relationship, or a surrounding community. Spanish expresses these ideas with several different endings.

| Base word | **-hood** noun | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| adult | **adulthood** | adultez |
| child | **childhood** | niñez, infancia |
| mother | **motherhood** | maternidad |
| father | **fatherhood** | paternidad |
| neighbor | **neighborhood** | barrio, vecindario |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Mi familia se mudó a este barrio durante mi niñez.** | My family moved to this **neighborhood** during my **childhood**. |
| **La maternidad cambió sus prioridades.** | **Motherhood** changed her priorities. |
| **La paternidad requiere paciencia y responsabilidad.** | **Fatherhood** requires patience and responsibility. |

## Additional Examples

| Base word | **-hood** noun | Example |
|---|---|---|

| sister | **sisterhood** | **La experiencia creó un fuerte sentido de hermandad entre las mujeres.**<br>The experience created a strong sense of sisterhood among the women. |
| likely | **likelihood** | **La probabilidad de lluvia aumentó durante la tarde.**<br>The likelihood of rain increased during the afternoon. |
