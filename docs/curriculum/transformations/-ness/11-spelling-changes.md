# Spelling in the **-ness/-en** Pattern

| Base | Transformation | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **happy** | **happiness** | final **y → i** before **-ness** |
| **white** | **whitish** | drop final **e** before **-ish** |
| **white** | **whiteness** | keep final **e** before **-ness** |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Su felicidad aumentó cuando la tela blanquecina recuperó su blancura. | Her happiness increased when the whitish fabric recovered its whiteness. |

These rules describe established words, not permission to attach every suffix to every adjective.
