# Irregular Past: **Make → Made** and **Pay → Paid**

Learn **made** and **paid** together. Both end in the sound `/eɪd/`.

| Spanish | Base verb | Past |
|---|---|---|
| hacer, hizo | **make** | **made** |
| pagar, pagó | **pay** | **paid** |

**Hice la reserva y pagué el depósito.**<br>
I made the reservation and paid the deposit.
