# Irregular Past Participles: **Stood** and **Understood**

The participle **understood** contains the same change as **stood**:

- **stand → stood**
- **understand → understood**

Both participles share the final sound chunk `/stʊd/`.

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| levantarse, haberse puesto de pie | **stand up** | **stood up** |
| entender, entendido | **understand** | **understood** |

**Laura se ha puesto de pie porque ha entendido la pregunta.**<br>
Laura has stood up because she has understood the question.
