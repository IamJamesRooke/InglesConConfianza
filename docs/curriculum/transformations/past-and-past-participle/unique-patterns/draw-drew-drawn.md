# The **Draw → Drew → Drawn** and **Saw → Sawed → Sawn** Family

The bases and **-n** participles rhyme, while the past forms follow different patterns.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| draw | **drew** | **drawn** | dibujar |
| saw | **sawed** | **sawn/sawed** | serruchar |

## Examples

**He drew the boards after they had been sawn.**
