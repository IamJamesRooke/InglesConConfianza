# The **Take → Took → Taken** Family

The related bases use short **oo** in the past and return toward the base vowel before **-en**.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| forsake | **forsook** | **forsaken** | abandonar |
| shake | **shook** | **shaken** | sacudir |
| take | **took** | **taken** | tomar / llevar |

## Examples

**I took the same route I had taken yesterday.**
