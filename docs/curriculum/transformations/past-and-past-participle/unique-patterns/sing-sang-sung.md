# The **Sing → Sang → Sung** Family

The stressed short **i** changes to **a** in the past and **u** in the participle.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| begin | **began** | **begun** | empezar |
| ring | **rang** | **rung** | sonar / llamar |
| sing | **sang** | **sung** | cantar |
| spring | **sprang/sprung** | **sprung** | brotar / saltar |

## Examples

**The concert began after the bell rang, and everyone sang a song they had sung before.**
