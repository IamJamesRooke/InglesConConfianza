# The **Write → Wrote → Written** Family

The rhyming bases converge again on the short **i + -en** participle sound, while **bite → bit** contrasts with **smite → smote** and **write → wrote**.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| bite | **bit** | **bitten** | morder |
| smite | **smote** | **smitten** | formal, literary, or humorous |
| write | **wrote** | **written** | escribir |

## Examples

**I wrote the note after the dog had bitten the paper.**
