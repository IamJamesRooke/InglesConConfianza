# The **Drive → Drove → Driven** Family

The long **i** changes to long **o** in the irregular past and short **i** in the **-en** participle; accepted variants remain visible.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| drive | **drove** | **driven** | conducir |
| strive | **strove/strived** | **striven/strived** | esforzarse |
| thrive | **thrived/throve** | **thrived/thriven** | prosperar |

## Examples

**He drove the route he had driven many times before.**
