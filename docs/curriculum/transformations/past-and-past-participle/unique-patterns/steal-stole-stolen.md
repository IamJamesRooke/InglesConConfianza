# The **Steal → Stole → Stolen** Family

The long **ee** sound changes to long **o**, and the participle adds an unstressed **-en** sound.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| steal | **stole** | **stolen** | robar |
| weave | **wove** | **woven** | tejer |

## Examples

**The thief stole the cloth that had been woven by hand.**
