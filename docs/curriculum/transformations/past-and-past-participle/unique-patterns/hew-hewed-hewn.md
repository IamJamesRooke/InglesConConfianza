# The **Hew → Hewed → Hewn** Family

The regular past ends in **/d/** and the participle commonly contracts to **-wn**.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| hew | **hewed** | **hewn/hewed** | tallar / cortar |
| strew | **strewed** | **strewn/strewed** | esparcir |
