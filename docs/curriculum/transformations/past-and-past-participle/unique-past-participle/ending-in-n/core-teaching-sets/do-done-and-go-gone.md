# Irregular Past Participles: **Done** and **Gone**

Learn these changes together:

- **do → done**
- **go → gone**

Both participles finish with the sound `/n/`.

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| hacer, hecho | **do** | **done** |
| ir, ido | **go** | **gone** |

**He hecho mi tarea y he ido a la oficina.**<br>
I have done my homework and gone to the office.

The same participle appears in **go back**:

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| regresar, regresado | **go back** | **gone back** |

**He regresado a la oficina para buscar mis llaves.**<br>
I have gone back to the office to look for my keys.
