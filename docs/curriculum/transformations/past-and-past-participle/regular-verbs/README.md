# Regular Verbs

Regular verbs use the same written form for the past and past participle. The teaching distinction is the final sound of **-ed**.

| Final sound | Teaching group | Model |
|---|---|---|
| `/d/` | [Pronounced `/d/`](pronounced-d/README.md) | **call → called → called** |
| `/t/` | [Pronounced `/t/`](pronounced-t/README.md) | **help → helped → helped** |
| `/ɪd/` | [Pronounced `/ɪd/`](pronounced-id/README.md) | **want → wanted → wanted** |

The [past overview](past-overview.md) and [participle overview](participle-overview.md) preserve the two original source perspectives. They are supporting material, not separate transformation systems.
