# Regular Past Pronounced `/ɪd/`

When a regular verb ends in the sound `/t/` or `/d/`, its past ends in written **-ted** or **-ded**. Pronounce the **e** as an extra syllable.

Use the simple course rule:

> **Si termina en -ted o -ded, pronunciamos la e.**

## **-ted**

| Spanish | English | Pronunciation bridge |
|---|---|---|
| quiso | **wanted** | “wan-ted” |
| empezó | **started** | “star-ted” |
| esperó | **waited for** | “wai-ted” |

**Quise empezar temprano, pero esperé a Laura.**<br>
I wanted to start early, but I waited for Laura.

## **-ded**

| Spanish | English | Pronunciation bridge |
|---|---|---|
| necesitó | **needed** | “nee-ded” |
| decidió | **decided** | “deci-ded” |

**Necesité más tiempo y decidí terminar mañana.**<br>
I needed more time and decided to finish tomorrow.

The pronunciation is `/ɪd/` in both groups. **Wanted** and **needed** each have two syllables.

## Cognate reinforcement

These direct cognates end in `/t/` before **-ed**, so the ending adds the extra syllable `/ɪd/`:

| Cognate category | Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| direct cognates | aceptar | **accepted** | **La universidad aceptó mi solicitud.**<br>The university accepted my application. |
