# **Amaze → Amazed → Amazed**

The written **-ed** ending is pronounced `/d/`.


After `/z/` written **z** or **ze**, add written **-d** or **-ed** and pronounce the ending `/d/`:

**amaze → amazed**

| Course source | Base | Past and past participle | Spanish bridge |
|---|---|---|---|
| model vocabulary | amaze | amazed | asombró / asombrado |
| cognate reinforcement | organize | organized | organizó / organizado |
| cognate reinforcement | memorize | memorized | memorizó / memorizado |

- Past: The result **amazed** Laura after she **organized** the data.
- Past participle: The result has **amazed** Laura, and the data has been **organized**.

This sound family overlaps with **use → used**. The separate lesson makes the different base spellings visible: **s/se** versus **z/ze**.
