# **Want → Wanted → Wanted**, **Start → Started → Started**, and **Wait → Waited → Waited**

These high-frequency verbs form one practical `/ɪd/` teaching set.


These three high-frequency verbs form one useful `/ɪd/` teaching set. The final `/t/` sound of the base verb makes **-ed** a new syllable.

| Base | Past | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| want | wanted | wanted |
| start | started | started |
| wait | waited | waited |

- **I wanted to start yesterday, but I waited.** — Quería empezar ayer, pero esperé.
- **We have started, and they have waited long enough.** — Hemos empezado y ellos han esperado lo suficiente.

Mastery phrase: **I wanted to start, but I waited.**
