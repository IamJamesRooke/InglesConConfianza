# The **Hit, Quit, Slit, Split** No-Change Family

The base, past, and past participle use the same form.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| hit | **hit** | **hit** | golpear |
| quit | **quit** | **quit** | dejar / renunciar |
| slit | **slit** | **slit** | rajar |
| split | **split** | **split** | dividir |
