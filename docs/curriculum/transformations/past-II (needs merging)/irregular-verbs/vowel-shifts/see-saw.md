# Vowel Shifts: **See → Saw**

The vowel changes from **see** `/siː/` to **saw** `/sɔ/`.

| Spanish | Base form | Past |
|---|---|---|
| ver, vio | **see** | **saw** |

**Vi a Laura en la oficina esta mañana.**<br>
I saw Laura in the office this morning.

The exact vowel in **saw** varies by accent, but the form always differs clearly from **see**.
