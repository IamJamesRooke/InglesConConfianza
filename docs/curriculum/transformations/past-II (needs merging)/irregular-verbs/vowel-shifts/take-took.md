# Vowel Shifts: **Take → Took**

The vowel changes from **take** `/teɪk/` to **took** `/tʊk/`.

Use **took** for the core meanings of taking or carrying something.

| Spanish | Base form | Past |
|---|---|---|
| tomar, tomó | **take** | **took** |
| llevar, llevó | **take** | **took** |

**Tomé las llaves y las llevé a la oficina.**<br>
I took the keys and took them to the office.
