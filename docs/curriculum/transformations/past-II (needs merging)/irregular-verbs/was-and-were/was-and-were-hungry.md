# **Was Hungry** and **Were Hungry**

Spanish uses **tener hambre**, but English uses **be hungry**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| tenía hambre | **I/he/she was hungry** |
| tenías hambre; tenía hambre | **you were hungry** |
| teníamos hambre | **we were hungry** |
| tenían hambre | **you/they were hungry** |

**Tenía hambre después de la reunión.**<br>
I was hungry after the meeting.

**Todos tenían hambre.**<br>
They were all hungry.
