# Irregular Past: **Go → Went**

| Spanish | Base form | Past |
|---|---|---|
| ir, fue | **go** | **went** |

**Fui a la oficina después del almuerzo.**<br>
I went to the office after lunch.

The same change appears in **go back**:

| Spanish | Base form | Past |
|---|---|---|
| regresar, regresó | **go back** | **went back** |

**Regresé a la oficina después de la reunión.**<br>
I went back to the office after the meeting.

**Went** ends with the sound `/t/`.
