# Irregular Past: **Get → Got** and **Forget → Forgot**

The past **forgot** contains the same final form as **got**:

- **get → got**
- **forget → forgot**

Both past forms end with the sound `/t/`.

## Meanings of **got**

| Spanish | Base form | Past |
|---|---|---|
| recibir, obtener, conseguir | **get** | **got** |
| llegar a un lugar | **get to a place** | **got to a place** |
| entender algo | **get something** | **got something** |
| levantarse | **get up** | **got up** |

**Recibí el mensaje cuando llegué a la oficina.**<br>
I got the message when I got to the office.

**Entendí la idea cuando la profesora la explicó.**<br>
I got the idea when the teacher explained it.

## **Forget → Forgot**

| Spanish | Base form | Past |
|---|---|---|
| olvidar, olvidó | **forget** | **forgot** |

**Olvidé responder el mensaje.**<br>
I forgot to respond to the message.
