# Irregular Past Participles: **Come**, **Drunk**, and **Run**

The participles in this memory family share the central vowel sound `/ʌ/`:

- **come → came → come**
- **drink → drank → drunk**
- **run → ran → run**

## **Come**

| Spanish | Base form | Simple past | Past participle |
|---|---|---|---|
| venir, venido | **come** | **came** | **come** |
| volver, vuelto | **come back** | **came back** | **come back** |

**Laura ha venido a la oficina y ha vuelto después del almuerzo.**<br>
Laura has come to the office and come back after lunch.

## **Drink** and **Run**

| Spanish | Base form | Simple past | Past participle |
|---|---|---|---|
| beber, tomar; bebido, tomado | **drink** | **drank** | **drunk** |
| correr, corrido | **run** | **ran** | **run** |

**He corrido en el parque y he tomado mucha agua.**<br>
I have run in the park and drunk a lot of water.
