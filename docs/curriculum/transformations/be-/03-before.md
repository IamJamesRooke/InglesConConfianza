# **Before**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Revise el contrato antes de firmarlo. | Review the contract before signing it. |

**Before** places one time, event, or position earlier than another. It can appear before a thing, an **-ing** action, or a complete idea:

- **before the meeting**
- **before signing**
- **before you sign**

For **beforehand**, see the related [**-ward** family lesson](../-ward/11-beforehand.md).
