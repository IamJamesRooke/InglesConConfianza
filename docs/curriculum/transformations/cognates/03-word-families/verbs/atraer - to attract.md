# Verb Cognates: **-act**

These Spanish and English cognates correspond to English verbs ending in **-act**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| atraer | **to attract** | **Las flores de colores atraen a muchas abejas.**<br>The colorful flowers attract many bees. |
| distraer | **to distract** | **El ruido de la calle me distrae cuando estudio.**<br>The street noise distracts me when I study. |
| extraer | **to extract** | **El dentista necesita extraer la muela dañada.**<br>The dentist needs to extract the damaged tooth. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| contraer | **to contract** | **Una persona puede contraer el virus por contacto directo.**<br>A person can contract the virus through direct contact. |
| sustraer | **to subtract** | **Para resolver la operación, debes sustraer cinco de doce.**<br>To solve the equation, you must subtract five from twelve. |
| contactar | **to contact** | **Puedes contactar al equipo de soporte por correo electrónico.**<br>You can contact the support team by email. |
| impactar | **to impact** | **La nueva ley puede impactar a miles de trabajadores.**<br>The new law may impact thousands of workers. |
| actuar | **to act** | **Debemos actuar rápidamente durante una emergencia.**<br>We must act quickly during an emergency. |
| reaccionar | **to react** | **Algunas personas reaccionan mal a este medicamento.**<br>Some people react badly to this medication. |
| interactuar | **to interact** | **Los estudiantes necesitan interactuar en inglés durante la actividad.**<br>The students need to interact in English during the activity. |

## False Cognates

**Contratar a una persona** normally means **to hire someone**, not **to contract someone**. English can use **contract** when formally arranging for a person or company to provide a specific service.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Voy a contratarte como gerente de ventas.** | I am going to **hire** you as the sales manager. |
| **La empresa va a contratar a un laboratorio para realizar las pruebas.** | The company is going to **contract a laboratory** to perform the tests. |
