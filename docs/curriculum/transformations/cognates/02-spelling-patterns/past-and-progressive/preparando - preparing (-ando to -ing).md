# Cognate Forms: Spanish **-ando** and English **-ing**

Spanish **-ando** forms often correspond to related English forms ending in **-ing**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| preparando | **preparing** | **Estoy preparando la presentación para mañana.**<br>I am preparing the presentation for tomorrow. |
| visitando | **visiting** | **Estamos visitando a unos amigos en Medellín.**<br>We are visiting some friends in Medellín. |

In English, the **-ing** form stays the same; only the form of **be** changes:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Estoy preparando la presentación.** | I am **preparing** the presentation. |
| **Estás preparando la presentación.** | You are **preparing** the presentation. |
| **Estamos preparando la presentación.** | We are **preparing** the presentation. |
| **Están preparando la presentación.** | They are **preparing** the presentation. |
| **Ella está preparando la presentación.** | She is **preparing** the presentation. |

## Spelling

- Usually add **-ing**: **visit → visiting**, **confirm → confirming**.
- Usually remove final **-e** before adding **-ing**: **prepare → preparing**, **communicate → communicating**.
- Keep both **e** letters in verbs ending in **-ee**: **agree → agreeing**.

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| practicando | **practicing** | **Ella está practicando inglés antes de la entrevista.**<br>She is practicing English before the interview. |
| confirmando | **confirming** | **El hotel está confirmando nuestra reservación.**<br>The hotel is confirming our reservation. |
| continuando | **continuing** | **La profesora está continuando la explicación después de la pausa.**<br>The teacher is continuing the explanation after the break. |
| comunicando | **communicating** | **Los equipos están comunicando sus decisiones con claridad.**<br>The teams are communicating their decisions clearly. |
