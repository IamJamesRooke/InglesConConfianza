# Noun Cognates: Spanish **-anza** and English **-ance**

These related nouns connect several Spanish **-anza** forms with English **-ance** forms. The spelling transformation varies by word.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| confianza | **confidence** | **La práctica le dio confianza para hablar en público.**<br>Practice gave her confidence to speak in public. |
| alianza | **alliance** | **Las dos organizaciones formaron una alianza estratégica.**<br>The two organizations formed a strategic alliance. |
| finanzas | **finance** | **Ella estudia finanzas en la universidad.**<br>She studies finance at university. |
| venganza | **vengeance** | **El personaje busca venganza por la muerte de su hermano.**<br>The character seeks vengeance for his brother's death. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| balanza | **balance** | **La balanza muestra el peso exacto del paquete.**<br>The balance shows the exact weight of the package. |

## Meaning Distinctions

**Confianza** can mean **confidence** in your own ability or **trust** in another person.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Tengo confianza para hablar en inglés.** | I have the **confidence** to speak in English. |
| **Tengo confianza en mi médico.** | I **trust** my doctor. |

Spanish **finanzas** commonly names the field of **finance**. English **finances** refers to the money available to a person or organization.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Carlos trabaja en el sector de las finanzas.** | Carlos works in **finance**. |
| **La familia necesita organizar mejor sus finanzas.** | The family needs to organize its **finances** better. |
