# Verb Cognates: **-eive**

In this pattern, Spanish verbs ending in **-cibir** or **-cebir** correspond to English verbs ending in **-ceive**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| recibir | **to receive** | **Marta espera recibir una respuesta antes del viernes.**<br>Marta expects to receive an answer before Friday. |
| percibir | **to perceive** | **Los clientes perciben una mejora en la calidad del servicio.**<br>The customers perceive an improvement in the quality of the service. |
| concebir | **to conceive** | **El equipo logró concebir una solución más sencilla.**<br>The team managed to conceive a simpler solution. |

## False Cognates

**Decepcionar** means **to disappoint**, not **to deceive**. **To deceive** means **engañar**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **La decisión decepcionó a muchos clientes.** | The decision **disappointed** many customers. |
| **El vendedor engañó al cliente con información falsa.** | The salesperson **deceived** the customer with false information. |
