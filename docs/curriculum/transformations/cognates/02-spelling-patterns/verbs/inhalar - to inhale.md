# Verb Cognates: **-ale**

In these cognates, Spanish verbs ending in **-alar** correspond to English verbs ending in **-ale**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| inhalar | **to inhale** | **Debes inhalar lentamente por la nariz durante el ejercicio.**<br>You should inhale slowly through your nose during the exercise. |
| exhalar | **to exhale** | **Después, debes exhalar lentamente por la boca.**<br>Then you should exhale slowly through your mouth. |
