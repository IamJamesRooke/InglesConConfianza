# Cognate Curriculum

The folders progress from the easiest, most reliable inferences to the least mechanical material:

1. **Direct:** Spanish and English forms are identical or nearly identical, or they are foundational reference sets learned without a transformation rule.
2. **Spelling patterns:** a visible, reusable spelling transformation connects the Spanish and English forms.
3. **Word families:** prefixes and shared Latin roots reveal related groups of words.
4. **Memory bridges:** useful associations that do not form a dependable letter-by-letter rule.
5. **Confusion sets:** false cognates and context-dependent translations that can defeat otherwise useful inference.

Parts of speech are secondary folders within each learning tier. Teach the reliable pattern first, then its exceptions and false cognates.
