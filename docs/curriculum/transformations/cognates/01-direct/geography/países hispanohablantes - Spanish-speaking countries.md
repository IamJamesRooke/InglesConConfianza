## Country and Nationality

Use **from + country** or **be + nationality**:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Soy de Colombia.** | I am from **Colombia**. |
| **Soy colombiano.** | I am **Colombian**. |
| **Ana es de México.** | Ana is from **Mexico**. |
| **Ana es mexicana.** | Ana is **Mexican**. |

## Country, Nationality and Language

**Español** can refer to a nationality or the Spanish language. Context identifies the meaning.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Carlos es español.** | Carlos is **Spanish**. |
| **Carlos habla español.** | Carlos speaks **Spanish**. |

## Puerto Rico

Puerto Rico is a Spanish-speaking territory of the United States, not an independent country.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Lucía es de Puerto Rico.** | Lucía is from **Puerto Rico**. |
| **Lucía es puertorriqueña.** | Lucía is **Puerto Rican**. |
