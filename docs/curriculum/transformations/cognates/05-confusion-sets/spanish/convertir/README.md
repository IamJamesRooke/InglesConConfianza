# Spanish **Convertir**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Convirtieron la casa en un hotel. | They turned the house into a hotel. |
| Convierte los dólares en pesos. | Convert the dollars into pesos. |
| El agua se convirtió en hielo. | The water turned into ice. |
| Se convirtió en una experta. | She became an expert. |

Transitive **convertir** can be **turn into, convert**, or **make into**. Pronominal **convertirse en** often becomes **turn into** or **become**.
