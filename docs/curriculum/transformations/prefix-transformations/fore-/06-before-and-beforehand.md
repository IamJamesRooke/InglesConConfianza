# **Before** and **Beforehand**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Prepárese de antemano antes de la reunión. | Prepare beforehand before the meeting. |

**Before** relates one event or time to another. **Beforehand** means in advance and stands alone without a following reference.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Llámeme antes de salir y avíseme de antemano. | Call me before leaving, and let me know beforehand. |
