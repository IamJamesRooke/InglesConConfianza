# **Beside** and **Besides**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Siéntese a mi lado; además, necesito hablar con usted. | Sit beside me; besides, I need to speak with you. |

**Beside** means **al lado de**. **Besides** means **además de** before a thing or person, or **además** when adding another point.
