# **Forward** vs **Foreword**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Avance y lea el prólogo antes del primer capítulo. | Move forward and read the foreword before the first chapter. |

**Forward** expresses movement or orientation toward the front. A **foreword** is a short introductory text at the beginning of a book.

The words are close in sound but differ in spelling and meaning. **Forward** is the established direction word; **foreword** transparently combines **fore-** (“before”) and **word** for words placed before the main text.
