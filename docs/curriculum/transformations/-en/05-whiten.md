# **White → Whiten**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La pasta dental puede blanquear los dientes gradualmente. | The toothpaste can whiten your teeth gradually. |

Add **-en** to build the verb **whiten**, meaning make or become whiter.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Las paredes se blanquearon al secarse la pintura. | The walls whitened as the paint dried. |

The first sentence presents something causing the change. In the second, the subject itself becomes whiter.
