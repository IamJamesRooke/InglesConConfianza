# **North**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tunja queda al norte de Bogotá. | Tunja is north of Bogotá. |

**North** names the direction **norte**. It can also identify a position or side:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La entrada queda en el lado norte del edificio. | The entrance is on the north side of the building. |

Compass directions normally use lowercase unless they belong to a proper name or name a recognized region.
