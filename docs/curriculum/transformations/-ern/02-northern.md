# **Northern**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Visitamos la parte norte de Colombia. | We visited northern Colombia. |

**Northern** describes a place, part, climate, culture, or other thing associated with the north. Depending on context, it can mean **del norte, septentrional**, or **norteño**.

Use **north** for the direction itself and **northern** before the thing being described: **north of Bogotá**, but **northern Colombia**.
