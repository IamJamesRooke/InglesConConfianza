# **South**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Soacha queda al sur de Bogotá. | Soacha is south of Bogotá. |

**South** names the direction **sur**. It can also identify a position or side:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La ventana queda en el lado sur del edificio. | The window is on the south side of the building. |
