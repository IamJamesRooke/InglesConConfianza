# **Up → Upward(s)**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mire arriba mientras el globo se desplaza hacia arriba. | Look up while the balloon moves upward. |

**Up** supplies the core higher-direction picture. **Upward/upwards** explicitly marks movement or orientation toward a higher position.

Use **upward** before a noun: **an upward trend**. For direction after a verb, both **upward** and **upwards** are standard.
