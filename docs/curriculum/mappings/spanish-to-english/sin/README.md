# Spanish **Sin**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Café sin azúcar. | Coffee without sugar. |
| Se fue sin despedirse. | She left without saying goodbye. |
| Sin tu ayuda, no puedo hacerlo. | Without your help, I can't do it. |
| Llevo dos días sin dormir. | I haven't slept for two days. |

**Sin** usually becomes **without**, but duration plus **sin + infinitive** often becomes an English negative perfect construction.
