# **Recordar → Remind**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Recuérdame llamar a Ana y recuérdale a Carlos que la reunión es a las diez. | Remind me to call Ana, and remind Carlos that the meeting is at ten. |

Use **remind + person + to + verb** for a necessary action and **remind + person + that-clause** for information the person should remember.
