# Translations of Spanish **A Pesar De**

Spanish **a pesar de** can become **despite** or **in spite of**. With a complete clause, **a pesar de que** can become **despite the fact that**.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [despite](01-despite.md) | A pesar de la lluvia, salimos. | Despite the rain, we left. |
| [in spite of](02-in-spite-of.md) | A pesar de estar cansada, trabajó. | In spite of being tired, she worked. |
| [despite the fact that](03-despite-the-fact-that.md) | A pesar de que llovió, salimos. | Despite the fact that it rained, we left. |
