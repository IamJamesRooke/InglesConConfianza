# Translations of Spanish **Mirar**

Spanish **mirar** maps to **look at** for directed attention and often to **watch** for sustained attention.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Look At or Watch](01-look-at-or-watch.md) | Mira la foto y el partido. | Look at the photo and watch the game. |
| [Look At vs See](confusion-sets/README.md) | Miro la foto y la veo claramente. | I look at the photo and see it clearly. |
