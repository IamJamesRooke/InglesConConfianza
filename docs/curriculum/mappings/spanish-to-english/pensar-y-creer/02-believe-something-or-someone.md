# **Creer → Believe** Something or Someone

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Creo que la explicación de Ana es cierta y le creo cuando dice que fue un accidente. | I believe Ana's explanation is true, and I believe her when she says it was an accident. |

**Believe + information/person** means accept it or them as truthful. **Believe someone** means accept what that person says.
