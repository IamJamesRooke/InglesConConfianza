# **Think So** and **Believe So**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| —¿Funcionará el plan? —Creo que sí, pero no estoy completamente segura. | “Will the plan work?” “I think so, but I'm not completely sure.” |

**Think so** is the normal conversational form for an opinion. **Believe so** is possible but sounds more formal or deliberate.
