# Spanish **Recibir**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Recibí tu mensaje. | I received your message. |
| Recibió una buena nota. | She got a good grade. |
| Recibieron a los invitados en la puerta. | They welcomed the guests at the door. |
| El médico recibe pacientes por la tarde. | The doctor sees patients in the afternoon. |

**Recibir** can become **receive, get, welcome**, or **see**, depending on what or whom someone receives.
