# **Niño** → **Child**, **Boy**, or **Kid**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El niño necesita ayuda. | The child needs help. |
| El niño está jugando con su hermana. | The boy is playing with his sister. |
| Hay muchos niños en el parque. | There are many kids in the park. |

Use **child** when gender does not matter, **boy** when the male distinction matters, and informal **kid** in everyday conversation.
