# Spanish **Trabajar**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Trabajo en una oficina. | I work in an office. |
| Estamos trabajando en el informe. | We are working on the report. |
| Trabaja la madera a mano. | She works the wood by hand. |

Employment, working on a task, and shaping a material use different English complements or senses of **work**.
