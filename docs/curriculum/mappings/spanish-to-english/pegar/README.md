# Translations of Spanish **Pegar** and **Pegarse**

Spanish **pegar** and **pegarse** map to sticking, gluing, hitting, catching an illness, matching, spreading, and staying close.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Pegar → Stick / Glue**](01-stick-or-glue.md) | Pega la foto en la cartulina. | Stick the photo onto the poster board. |
| [**Pegarse → Stick / Get Stuck**](02-be-stuck.md) | El papel se pegó a la mesa. | The paper stuck to the table. |
| [**Pegar → Hit**](03-hit.md) | Le pegó a la puerta con el martillo. | He hit the door with the hammer. |
| [**Pegar → Give / Catch** an Illness](04-catch-or-spread.md) | Ana me pegó la gripa. | Ana gave me her cold. / I caught Ana's cold. |
| [**Pegar → Match / Go With**](05-suit-or-match.md) | Ese color no pega con la camisa. | That color doesn't match the shirt. |
| [**Pegarse → Rub Off / Catch On**](06-spread-a-habit.md) | Su entusiasmo se me pegó. | Her enthusiasm rubbed off on me. |
| [**Pegarse A → Stay Close / Press Against**](07-stay-close.md) | Pégate a la pared mientras pasan. | Stay close to the wall while they pass. |
| [**Pegársela / Pegarle** in Fixed Uses](08-hit-it-off.md) | Le pegó al premio mayor. | He hit the jackpot. |
