# **Seguro** → **Safe** and **Sure**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| seguro / a salvo | safe |
| seguro de algo | sure about something |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Laura está a salvo en casa. | Laura is safe at home. |
| Estoy seguro de que la reunión es hoy. | I am sure that the meeting is today. |

Spanish **seguro** can express protection from danger or certainty; English uses different words.
