# **Duro** → **Hard**, **Tough**, and **Harsh**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| duro / dura | hard / tough / harsh |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El metal es duro. | The metal is hard. |
| Fue un año duro, pero seguimos adelante. | It was a tough year, but we moved forward. |
| El jefe tiene un tono duro. | The boss has a harsh tone. |

**Hard** describes physical firmness or difficulty. **Tough** emphasizes endurance or difficulty. **Harsh** describes something severe or unpleasant.
