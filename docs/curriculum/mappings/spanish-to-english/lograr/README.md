# Spanish **Lograr**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Logró terminar a tiempo. | She managed to finish on time. |
| Logramos nuestro objetivo. | We achieved our goal. |
| No logro entenderlo. | I can't seem to understand it. |
| El equipo logró una victoria importante. | The team secured an important victory. |

**Lograr** can become **manage to, achieve, succeed in**, or **secure**, and negative **no lograr** often becomes **can't seem to**.
