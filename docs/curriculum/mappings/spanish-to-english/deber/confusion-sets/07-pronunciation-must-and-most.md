# Pronunciation: **Must** and **Most**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La mayoría debe presentar el formulario hoy. | Most people must submit the form today. |

These words are not homophones. **Must** has `/ʌ/` and no diphthong: `/mʌst/`. **Most** has `/oʊ/`: `/moʊst/`. The vowel distinguishes obligation from majority.
