# **Debido a que** → **Because**, **Since**, or **Due to the Fact That**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La reunión se canceló debido a que Laura estaba enferma. | The meeting was canceled because Laura was sick. |
| Debido a que ya terminamos, podemos salir. | Since we have already finished, we can leave. |
| El vuelo se retrasó debido a que había una tormenta. | The flight was delayed due to the fact that there was a storm. |

**Debido a que** gives a reason. **Because** is usually the most natural choice, **since** can introduce an already-known reason, and **due to the fact that** is formal and wordy.
