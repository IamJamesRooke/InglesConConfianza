# Translations of Spanish **Deber**

Spanish **deber** can express debt, obligation, advice, expectation, probability, or a moral duty. English chooses among **owe, must, have to, should, ought to, be supposed to**, and other structures according to the intended force and time.

| Section | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Core Translations](core-translations/README.md) | Debo dinero, debo pagar hoy y debería llamar primero. | I owe money, must pay today, and should call first. |
| [Time and Perfect Forms](time-and-perfect-forms/README.md) | Ayer tuve que pagar, pero debí haber llamado primero. | I had to pay yesterday, but I should have called first. |
| [Negative Contrasts](negative-contrasts/README.md) | No debes entrar, no tienes que esperar y no deberías preocuparte. | You mustn't enter, don't have to wait, and shouldn't worry. |
| [Duty and Related Words](related-words/README.md) | Le debo dinero a la dueña, que es propietaria del edificio. | I owe money to the owner, who owns the building. |
| [Confusion Sets](confusion-sets/README.md) | Debe estar cansada, debe llegar a las diez y tiene que presentar el informe. | She must be tired, is supposed to arrive at ten, and has to submit the report. |

## Whole-Topic Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Debo cincuenta mil pesos y tengo que pagarlos hoy, pero debería llamar primero. | I owe fifty thousand pesos and have to pay it today, but I should call first. |
| Debía llegar a las diez, así que debe estar cerca; si no, tendremos que empezar sin ella. | She was supposed to arrive at ten, so she must be close; otherwise, we'll have to start without her. |
| No debes entrar, pero tampoco tienes que esperar afuera; deberías volver mañana. | You mustn't enter, but you don't have to wait outside either; you should come back tomorrow. |
| Le debo dinero a la dueña del edificio, pero soy dueño de mi apartamento. | I owe money to the owner of the building, but I own my apartment. |
