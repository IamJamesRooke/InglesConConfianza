# Translations of Spanish **Esperar**

Spanish **esperar** can mean remain until something happens, desire a possible result, or believe a result is likely. English separates these meanings with **wait, hope**, and **expect**. Related Spanish **desear/ojalá** can also require **wish**.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Esperar → Wait**](01-esperar-meaning-wait.md) | Espera aquí diez minutos. | Wait here for ten minutes. |
| [**Esperar A → Wait For**](02-wait-for-someone-or-something.md) | Estoy esperando el bus y a Ana. | I'm waiting for the bus and for Ana. |
| [Waiting for an Action](03-wait-for-an-action.md) | Espera a que Ana llegue para empezar. | Wait for Ana to arrive before starting. |
| [**Esperar → Hope**](04-esperar-meaning-hope.md) | Espero que Ana se mejore pronto. | I hope Ana gets well soon. |
| [**Esperar → Expect**](05-esperar-meaning-expect.md) | Esperamos que Ana llegue a las diez. | We expect Ana to arrive at ten. |
| [**Desear/Ojalá → Wish**](06-wish.md) | Ojalá tuviera más tiempo. | I wish I had more time. |
| [Nouns: **Wait, Hope**, and **Expectation**](07-wait-hope-and-expectation.md) | La espera fue larga, pero aún tengo esperanza y expectativas realistas. | The wait was long, but I still have hope and realistic expectations. |
| [**Wait, Hope, Expect**, and **Wish**](confusion-sets/README.md) | Espero el bus, espero que llegue pronto y supongo que llegará a las diez. | I'm waiting for the bus, I hope it comes soon, and I expect it at ten. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Espera a Ana; espero que llegue pronto y calculo que llegará a las diez. | Wait for Ana; I hope she arrives soon, and I expect her to arrive at ten. |
| Ojalá no tuviéramos que esperar, pero tengo muchas ganas de conocerla. | I wish we didn't have to wait, but I can't wait to meet her. |
