# **Escuchar** → **Listen**, **Listen To**, and **Listen Up**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| escuchar | **to listen to** |
| escuchar algo o a alguien | listen to something or someone |
| ¡escucha! | listen up! |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Escucho la letra de la canción con atención. | I listen to the lyrics of the song carefully. |

Use **to** before the thing or person receiving your attention: **Listen to the teacher**, not *listen the teacher*.
