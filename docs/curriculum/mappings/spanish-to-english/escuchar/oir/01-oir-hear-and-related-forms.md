# **Oír** → **Hear**, **Hear From**, and **Hear About**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| oír | **to hear** |
| oír un sonido | hear a sound |
| oír de una persona | hear from someone |
| oír sobre algo | hear about something |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Oigo música en el apartamento de al lado. | I hear music in the apartment next door. |

**Hear** describes a sound reaching your ears. Add **from** for a message or contact and **about** for information.
