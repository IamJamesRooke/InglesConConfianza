# Translations of Spanish **Por**

Spanish **por** covers cause, route, means, exchange, duration, rate, agent, and motive. English translation depends on the relationship expressed.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Por → Because Of**](01-cause-and-reason.md) | Cancelaron el vuelo por la tormenta. | They canceled the flight because of the storm. |
| [**Por → Through, Along**, or **Around**](02-movement-through-or-along.md) | Caminamos por el parque y por la avenida. | We walked through the park and along the avenue. |
| [**Por → By** a Means or Medium](03-means-and-medium.md) | Te mando el documento por correo. | I'll send you the document by email. |
| [**Por → For** an Exchange or Price](04-exchange-and-price.md) | Compré el libro por veinte mil pesos. | I bought the book for twenty thousand pesos. |
| [Duration with **Por**](05-duration.md) | Viví allí por dos años. | I lived there for two years. |
| [**Por → Per**](06-rate-and-distribution.md) | La medicina se toma dos veces por día. | The medicine is taken twice per day. |
| [**Por → By** the Agent](07-passive-agent.md) | La canción fue escrita por Ana. | The song was written by Ana. |
| [**Por → For** a Motive or Cause](08-motive-and-on-behalf-of.md) | Lo hice por ti, no para ti. | I did it because of you, not for you. |
