# **See** and **Look At**

Use **see** when something is visible and **look at** when you intentionally direct your eyes toward it.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ver | **to see** |
| mirar | **to look at** |

**Veo una foto en la pared.**<br>
I see a photo on the wall.

**Miro la foto para ver los detalles.**<br>
I look at the photo to see the details.

Use **at** after **look** when an object follows:

**Look at the photo.**<br>
Not: *Look the photo.*
