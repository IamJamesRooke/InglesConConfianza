# Translations of Spanish **Extrañar**

In Colombian and Latin American Spanish, **extrañar** commonly means miss someone or something. It can also mean find strange, surprise, or puzzle.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Extrañar → Miss** a Person](01-miss-a-person.md) | Extraño a mi familia. | I miss my family. |
| [**Extrañar → Miss** a Place or Thing](02-miss-a-place-or-thing.md) | Extraño la comida de Bogotá. | I miss the food from Bogotá. |
| [**Extrañar → Find Strange**](03-find-strange.md) | Me extraña que no haya llamado. | I find it strange that she hasn't called. |
| [**Extrañarse → Be Surprised**](04-be-surprised.md) | No te extrañes si llega tarde. | Don't be surprised if he arrives late. |
| [**Extrañar → Puzzle / Surprise**](05-puzzle.md) | Su silencio me extrañó. | His silence puzzled me. |
| [**Extraño → Strange / Odd**](06-extraño-adjective.md) | Escuché un ruido extraño. | I heard a strange noise. |
| [**Extrañar vs Perder → Miss**](07-missing-someone-vs-losing.md) | La extraño, pero también perdí su llamada. | I miss her, but I also missed her call. |
| [**Extrañar / Echar De Menos → Miss**](08-echar-de-menos.md) | La extraño y echo de menos nuestras conversaciones. | I miss her and miss our conversations. |
