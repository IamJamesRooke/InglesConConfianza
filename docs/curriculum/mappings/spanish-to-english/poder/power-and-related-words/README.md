# **Poder** as a Noun and Related Words

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Power](01-power.md) | El presidente tiene mucho poder. | The president has a lot of power. |
| [Authority](02-authority.md) | Ella tiene la autoridad para aprobarlo. | She has the authority to approve it. |
| [Powerful](03-powerful.md) | Es una herramienta poderosa. | It's a powerful tool. |
| [Powerless](04-powerless.md) | Se sintieron impotentes. | They felt powerless. |
| [Electrical Power](05-electrical-power.md) | Se fue la luz. | The power went out. |
| [Empower](06-empower.md) | La capacitación les da poder para decidir. | The training empowers them to decide. |
| [All-Powerful](07-all-powerful.md) | Ningún gerente es todopoderoso. | No manager is all-powerful. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La directora tiene la autoridad para decidir, pero no tiene el poder de cambiar la ley; sin electricidad, además, el sistema queda sin energía. | The director has the authority to decide, but she doesn't have the power to change the law; without electricity, the system also has no power. |
