# Translations of Spanish **Poder**

Spanish **poder** can describe ability, permission, possibility, opportunity, or power. English chooses among **can, could, be able to, may, might, be allowed to, get to, manage to**, and **power** according to the meaning and time.

| Section | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Ability](ability/README.md) | Puedo nadar y pude cruzar el río ayer. | I can swim, and I managed to cross the river yesterday. |
| [Permission and Requests](permission-and-requests/README.md) | ¿Puedo entrar? ¿Podrías ayudarme? | May I come in? Could you help me? |
| [Possibility](possibility/README.md) | Puede llover y podría haber tráfico. | It may rain, and there might be traffic. |
| [Time and Perfect Forms](time-and-perfect-forms/README.md) | No he podido llamarla, pero podré hacerlo mañana. | I haven't been able to call her, but I'll be able to tomorrow. |
| [Opportunity](opportunity/README.md) | Por fin pude conocer Cartagena. | I finally got to visit Cartagena. |
| [Power and Related Words](power-and-related-words/README.md) | La alcaldesa tiene poder, pero no es todopoderosa. | The mayor has power, but she isn't all-powerful. |
| [Confusion Sets](confusion-sets/README.md) | Podía nadar, pero ayer no logré cruzar el río. | I could swim, but yesterday I wasn't able to cross the river. |

## Whole-Topic Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Sé nadar y normalmente puedo cruzar el río, pero ayer no pude porque la corriente era muy fuerte. | I know how to swim and can normally cross the river, but yesterday I wasn't able to because the current was too strong. |
| Puede que llueva, así que quizá no podamos salir; si mejora el clima, podremos ir más tarde. | It may rain, so we might not be able to go out; if the weather improves, we'll be able to go later. |
| ¿Podría usar su teléfono? No he podido llamar a mi familia. | Could I use your phone? I haven't been able to call my family. |
| La directora tiene la autoridad para decidir, pero no tiene el poder de cambiar la ley. | The director has the authority to decide, but she doesn't have the power to change the law. |
