# **Could** vs **Was Able To**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| De niño podía nadar, y ayer logré cruzar el río por primera vez. | As a child, I could swim, and yesterday I was able to cross the river for the first time. |

**Could** describes general past ability. **Was/were able to** or **managed to** normally marks one completed success. Negative **couldn't** works for a failed attempt.

| Subject | Specific completed success |
|---|---|
| I/he/she | **was able to do something** |
| you/we/they | **were able to do something** |

**Logré explicar la actividad en inglés.**<br>
I was able to explain the activity in English.

**Can** changes to **could** for general past ability. **Could** does not change with the subject and takes the base verb without **to**:

**Podíamos explicar la actividad en inglés.**<br>
We could explain the activity in English.

**We could do it.**<br>
Not: *We could to do it.*
