# **Can't** vs **May Not**

| Meaning | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| Inability | Ana no puede conducir. | Ana can't drive. |
| Uncertain possibility | Puede que Ana no venga. | Ana may not come. |
| Denied permission | No puede entrar. | Ana may not come in. |

**Can't** commonly expresses inability or impossibility. **May not** can mean either **perhaps not** or formal denied permission, so context is essential.
