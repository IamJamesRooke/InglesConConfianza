# Time and Perfect Forms of **Poder**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Future](will-be-able-to.md) | Podré llamarla mañana. | I'll be able to call her tomorrow. |
| [Present Perfect](have-been-able-to.md) | No he podido llamarla. | I haven't been able to call her. |
| [Conditional](would-be-able-to.md) | Podría ayudar si tuviera tiempo. | I would be able to help if I had time. |
| [Hypothetical Past](could-have.md) | Habría podido ayudar. | I could have helped. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No he podido llamarla hoy, pero podré hacerlo mañana y podría ayudarla si todavía me necesita. | I haven't been able to call her today, but I'll be able to tomorrow and could help her if she still needs me. |
