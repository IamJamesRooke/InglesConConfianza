# **Hacer Que**

Spanish **hacer** and **hacer que** can become English **make** or a causative **have** construction.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Hacer → Make**](01-make.md) | Me hizo reír. | She made me laugh. |
| [**Hacer Que → Have**](02-have.md) | Hice que repararan el carro. | I had the car repaired. |
