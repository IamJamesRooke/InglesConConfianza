# **Importante** → **Important**, **Significant**, and **Major**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| importante | important / significant / major |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Esta reunión es importante. | This meeting is important. |
| La decisión tuvo un efecto significativo. | The decision had a significant effect. |
| Es un problema importante para la ciudad. | It is a major problem for the city. |

**Important** is the everyday choice. **Significant** emphasizes impact or meaning. **Major** emphasizes size, seriousness, or importance.
