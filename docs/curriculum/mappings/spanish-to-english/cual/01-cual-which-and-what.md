# **Cuál** → **Which** or **What**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿Cuál libro quieres, este o ese? | Which book do you want, this one or that one? |
| ¿Cuál es tu nombre? | What is your name? |

Spanish **cuál** often becomes **which** when choosing from a known set, but it can become **what** in an identification question.
