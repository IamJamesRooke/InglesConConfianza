# **Entender → Understand / Get**

Use **understand** when the meaning is clear to you. In informal conversation, **get** can express the same idea.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| entender algo | **to understand something** |
| entender algo, informalmente | **to get something** |

**Entiendo la explicación.**<br>
I understand the explanation.

**Entiendo la idea.**<br>
I get the idea.
