# Translations of Spanish **En**

Spanish **en** can correspond to **at**, **in**, or **on**. See the lessons below for the viewpoint that selects each English preposition.

## Lessons

- [**En** → **At**](01-at.md)
- [**En** → **In**](02-in.md)
- [**En / Sobre** → **On**](03-on.md)

Spanish **en** can correspond to **at, in**, or **on**. English chooses the preposition according to how the speaker views the location.

| View of the location | Preposition | Core example |
|---|---|---|
| a point, activity, or general place | **at** | at the office |
| inside an area, space, or container | **in** | in the office |
| touching a surface or appearing on a screen/page | **on** | on the table |

The categories are meanings, not rigid properties of the place. The same noun can take different prepositions when the viewpoint changes:

| Spanish | English | Viewpoint |
|---|---|---|
| Estoy en el parque para el evento. | I am at the park for the event. | general location |
| Estoy dentro del parque. | I am in the park. | inside its boundaries |
| Estoy en la oficina. | I am at the office. | workplace/general location |
| Estoy dentro de la oficina. | I am in the office. | inside the room |
