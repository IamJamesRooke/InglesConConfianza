# **Amigo/Amiga** → **Friend**, **Buddy**, or **Pal**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Laura es mi amiga. | Laura is my friend. |
| Hola, amigo. | Hi, buddy. |
| Es un buen pal. | He's a good pal. |

**Friend** is the neutral choice. **Buddy** and **pal** are informal and friendly; English does not mark the friend's gender in these nouns.
