# Comparing **Intentar, Tratar De**, and **Probar**

Spanish **intentar**, **tratar de**, and **probar** can all become English **try**, but they do not describe the same kind of trying.

## Attempting an action

Use **try + full infinitive** when you make an effort to perform an action.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| intentar hacer algo, tratar de hacer algo | **to try to do something** |

**Intento hablar en inglés durante la clase.**<br>
I try to speak English during class.

## Testing or sampling something

Use **try + something** when you test, use, or sample it to discover what it is like.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| probar algo | **to try something** |

**Prueba esta sopa antes de pedirla.**<br>
Try this soup before you order it.

## Attempting a task

Spanish can also use **intentar** with a task or challenge as its object.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| intentar algo | **to try something** |

**Voy a intentar el ejercicio otra vez.**<br>
I am going to try the exercise again.
