# Spanish **Encantar**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me encanta esta canción. | I love this song. |
| La noticia encantó a los niños. | The news delighted the children. |
| La historia habla de un bosque encantado. | The story is about an enchanted forest. |
| Me encantaría ir. | I would love to go. |

Everyday **encantar** often becomes **love**. Literal delight or magic uses **delight** or **enchant**, and the conditional forms a polite **would love to**.
