# **Conocer** and **Saber**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Conozco a la profesora, sé su nombre y sé dónde trabaja. | I know the teacher, I know her name, and I know where she works. |

Spanish separates familiarity with a person/place (**conocer**) from facts, answers, and clauses (**saber**). English uses **know** for both, so the object reveals the meaning.
