# **Fácil** → **Easy** and **Simple**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| fácil | easy / simple |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La primera parte es fácil, pero la segunda es difícil. | The first part is easy, but the second is hard. |
| Es una pregunta fácil. | It is a simple question. |

Use **easy** for the level of difficulty. Use **simple** when something is uncomplicated or has few parts.
