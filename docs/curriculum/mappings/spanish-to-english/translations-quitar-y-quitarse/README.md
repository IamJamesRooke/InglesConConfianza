# Translations of Spanish **Quitar** and **Quitarse**

Spanish **quitar** and **quitarse** cover removal, taking away, switching off, prevention, clothing, symptoms, and getting rid of things.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Quitar → Remove**](01-remove.md) | Quitaron la mancha de la camisa. | They removed the stain from the shirt. |
| [**Quitar → Take Away**](02-take-away.md) | Le quitaron el teléfono al estudiante. | They took the phone away from the student. |
| [**Quitar → Turn Off / Switch Off**](03-turn-off.md) | Quita la música antes de contestar. | Turn off the music before answering. |
| [**Quitar → Prevent / Take Away**](04-prevent-or-take-away.md) | El ruido me quita la concentración. | The noise prevents me from concentrating. |
| [**Quitarse → Take Off**](05-take-off-clothing.md) | Quítate los zapatos al entrar. | Take off your shoes when you come in. |
| [**Quitarse / Quitar → Get Rid Of**](06-get-rid-of.md) | No puedo quitarme esta preocupación. | I can't get rid of this worry. |
| [**Quitarse → Go Away / Clear Up**](07-go-away.md) | El dolor no se me quita. | The pain won't go away. |
| [**Quitarse → Move / Get Out of the Way**](08-move-out-of-the-way.md) | Quítate de la puerta, por favor. | Move away from the door, please. |
