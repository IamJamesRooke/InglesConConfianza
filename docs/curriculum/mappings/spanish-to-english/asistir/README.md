# Spanish **Asistir**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Asistí a la reunión. | I attended the meeting. |
| El médico asistió al paciente. | The doctor assisted the patient. |
| Una enfermera la asiste en casa. | A nurse cares for her at home. |

**Asistir a un evento** means **attend**, not *assist*. With a person, formal **asistir** can mean assist or care for.
