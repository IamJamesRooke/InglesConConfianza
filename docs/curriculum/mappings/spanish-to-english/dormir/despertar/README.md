# Spanish **Despertar**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Desperté al niño. | I woke the child. |
| Me desperté a las seis. | I woke up at six. |
| La noticia despertó interés. | The news aroused interest. |
| Ese viaje despertó mi curiosidad. | That trip sparked my curiosity. |

**Despertar** can become **wake, wake up, arouse**, or **spark**, depending on what becomes active.
