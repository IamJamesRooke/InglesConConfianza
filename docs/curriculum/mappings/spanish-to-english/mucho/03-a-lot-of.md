# **Mucho / Muchos** → **A Lot Of**

English **a lot of** works with both amounts and plural items, so Spanish may use singular **mucho/mucha** or plural **muchos/muchas**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Leí gran parte del libro y tomé muchas notas. | I read a lot of the book and took a lot of notes. |

Here **muchas notas** becomes **a lot of notes**. When **a lot of** means a large part of a known whole, Spanish often prefers **gran parte de**, as in **a lot of the book → gran parte del libro**.
