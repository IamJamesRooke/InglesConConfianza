# **Costar → Be Difficult / Struggle**

Spanish **costarle algo a alguien** often says that something is difficult for that person. English normally reorganizes the idea rather than using **cost**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me cuesta hablar en público. | It's difficult for me to speak in public. |
| Me cuesta entenderlo. | I struggle to understand it. |

Use **cost** for a price, but **be difficult** or **struggle** for personal difficulty.
