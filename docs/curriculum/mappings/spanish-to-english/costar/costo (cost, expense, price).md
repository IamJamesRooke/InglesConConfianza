# **Costo** → **Cost**, **Expense**, or **Price**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El costo total es treinta dólares. | The total cost is thirty dollars. |
| El costo del viaje incluye varios gastos. | The cost of the trip includes several expenses. |
| El precio de la entrada es alto. | The price of the ticket is high. |

Use **cost** for what something requires or sacrifices, **expense** for money spent, and **price** for the amount requested for a product or service.

This noun lesson is indexed under the **costar** word family.
