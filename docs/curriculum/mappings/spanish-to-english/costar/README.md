# Translations of Spanish **Costar**

Spanish **costar** maps to monetary **cost**, but **costarle algo a alguien** often becomes **be difficult** or **struggle**.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Cost Money](01-cost-money.md) | El libro cuesta veinte mil pesos. | The book costs twenty thousand pesos. |
| [Be Difficult](02-be-difficult.md) | Me cuesta entenderlo. | I struggle to understand it. |
| [**Costo** → **Cost / Expense / Price**](<costo (cost, expense, price).md>) | El costo total es treinta dólares. | The total cost is thirty dollars. |
