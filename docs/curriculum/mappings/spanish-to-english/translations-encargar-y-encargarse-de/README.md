# Translations of Spanish **Encargar** and **Encargarse De**

Spanish **encargar** and **encargarse de** cover ordering, commissioning, assigning responsibility, taking care of tasks, and being in charge.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Encargar → Order**](01-order.md) | Encargué un libro por internet. | I ordered a book online. |
| [**Encargar → Commission**](02-commission.md) | Le encargaron un retrato a la artista. | They commissioned the artist to paint a portrait. |
| [**Encargar A Alguien De → Put In Charge Of**](03-put-in-charge.md) | Encargaron a Ana del proyecto. | They put Ana in charge of the project. |
| [**Encargarle Algo A Alguien → Ask To Do**](04-ask-to-do.md) | Le encargué a Luis que enviara el informe. | I asked Luis to send the report. |
| [**Encargarse De → Take Care Of**](05-take-care-of-a-task.md) | Yo me encargo de las reservas. | I'll take care of the reservations. |
| [**Encargarse De → Be In Charge Of**](06-be-in-charge-of.md) | Ana se encarga del equipo de ventas. | Ana is in charge of the sales team. |
| [**Dejar Encargado → Leave In Someone's Care**](07-leave-in-someones-care.md) | Dejé el perro encargado con mi hermana. | I left the dog in my sister's care. |
| [**Encargo → Order / Errand / Assignment**](08-encargo.md) | Tengo que recoger un encargo y hacer un mandado. | I have to pick up an order and run an errand. |
