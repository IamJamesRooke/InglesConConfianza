# **A** → **To** a Destination

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| a / hacia un lugar | to a place |
| a un lugar | to a place |
| hacia un lugar | toward a place |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Voy a la oficina. | I am going to the office. |
| Lleva el documento a la tienda. | Take the document to the store. |

Spanish **a** marks a destination. **Hacia** emphasizes direction and can correspond to **toward**. Use **para** when the destination is framed as the purpose or endpoint of a trip: **Salgo para la oficina.** → **I am leaving for the office.**
