# **Cuánto** → **How Much** or **How Long**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿Cuánto necesitas? | How much do you need? |
| ¿Cuánto tiempo necesitas? | How much time do you need? |
| ¿Cuánto tiempo has estado aquí? | How long have you been here? |

With **tiempo**, English uses **how much time** when focusing on the amount and **how long** when asking about duration.
