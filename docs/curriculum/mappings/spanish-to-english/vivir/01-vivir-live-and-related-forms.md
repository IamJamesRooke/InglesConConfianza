# **Vivir** → **Live**, **Live In**, and **Live With**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| vivir | **to live** |
| vivir en un lugar | live in a place |
| vivir con alguien | live with someone |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Vivo en Bogotá con mi familia. | I live in Bogotá with my family. |

Use **live** for the general idea; add **in** for a place and **with** for a person or group.
