# **Necesario** → **Necessary**, **Needed**, and **Required**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| necesario / necesaria | necessary / needed / required |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Esta reunión es necesaria. | This meeting is necessary. |
| Necesitamos los documentos necesarios. | We need the required documents. |
| Se necesita más tiempo. | More time is needed. |

**Necessary** is the general choice. **Required** often describes a rule or condition. **Needed** focuses on what is lacking or wanted for a purpose.
