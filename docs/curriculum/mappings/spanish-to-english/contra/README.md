# Spanish **Contra**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La silla está contra la pared. | The chair is against the wall. |
| Bogotá juega contra Cali. | Bogotá is playing against Cali. |
| Estoy en contra del plan. | I am against the plan. |
| La votación quedó diez contra cinco. | The vote was ten to five. |

**Contra** can become physical or figurative **against**, sports **versus**, or score/vote **to**.
