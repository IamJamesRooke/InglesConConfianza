# **Poder** for Possibility

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Present or Future Possibility](01-may-might-could.md) | Puede que llueva. | It may rain. |
| [**Maybe**](02-maybe.md) | Quizá llueva mañana. | Maybe it'll rain tomorrow. |
| [General Possibility](03-general-possibility-can.md) | Bogotá puede ser fría de noche. | Bogotá can be cold at night. |
| [Past Possibility](04-may-might-could-have.md) | Puede que haya olvidado la cita. | She may have forgotten the appointment. |
| [Possible Ability](05-might-be-able-to.md) | Quizá pueda ayudar mañana. | I might be able to help tomorrow. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Puede que llueva mañana; Bogotá puede ser fría y quizá tengamos que quedarnos en casa. | It might rain tomorrow; Bogotá can be cold, and maybe we'll have to stay home. |
