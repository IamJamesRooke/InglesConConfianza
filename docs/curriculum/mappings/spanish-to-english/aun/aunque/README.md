# Translations of Spanish **Aunque**

Spanish **aunque** changes emphasis and certainty with context. It can become **although**, **though**, **even though**, or **even if**.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [although](01-although.md) | Aunque llovió, salimos. | Although it rained, we left. |
| [though](02-though.md) | Aunque estaba cansada, siguió trabajando. | She kept working, though she was tired. |
| [even though](03-even-though.md) | Aunque sabía la verdad, no dijo nada. | Even though she knew the truth, she didn't say anything. |
| [even if](04-even-if.md) | Aunque llueva, saldremos. | Even if it rains, we'll go out. |
