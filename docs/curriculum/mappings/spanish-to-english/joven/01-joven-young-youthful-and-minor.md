# **Joven** → **Young**, **Youthful**, and **Minor**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| joven | young / youthful / minor |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La empresa es joven. | The company is young. |
| Tiene una energía juvenil. | She has youthful energy. |
| El joven es menor de edad. | The young man is a minor. |

**Young** is the general translation. **Youthful** describes the quality or energy of youth. **Minor** is a legal term for someone under the age of majority.
