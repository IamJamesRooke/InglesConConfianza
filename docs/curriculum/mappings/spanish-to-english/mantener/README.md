# Spanish **Mantener**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mantén la puerta cerrada. | Keep the door closed. |
| Debemos mantener el equipo. | We must maintain the equipment. |
| Ella mantiene a su familia. | She supports her family. |
| El equipo mantiene la ventaja. | The team is holding the lead. |

**Mantener** can become **keep, maintain, support**, or **hold**, depending on what continues or is sustained.
