# Past **Haber → There Was / Were**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Había mucha gente y hubo dos problemas. | There were many people, and there were two problems. |

Use **there was/were** for background existence and often **there was/were** or **there occurred** for completed events. English number agreement matters.

Spanish uses **había** with both singular and plural nouns. English changes the form:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| había + singular | **there was + singular** |
| había + plural | **there were + plural** |

**Había un libro sobre la mesa.**<br>
There was a book on the table.

**Había tres libros sobre la mesa.**<br>
There were three books on the table.
