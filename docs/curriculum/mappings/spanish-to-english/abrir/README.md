# Spanish **Abrir**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Abre la puerta. | Open the door. |
| Abrió el negocio en enero. | She started the business in January. |
| La presidenta abrió la reunión. | The president began the meeting. |
| Se abrió con Laura. | He opened up to Laura. |

Physical opening, starting an organization or event, and sharing personal feelings use different English forms.
