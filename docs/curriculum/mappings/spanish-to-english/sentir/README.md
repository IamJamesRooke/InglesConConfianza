# Translations of Spanish **Sentir** and **Sentirse**

Spanish **sentir** and **sentirse** cover sensations, emotions, sensing, regret, opinions, and desires.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Sentir → Feel** a Sensation](01-feel-a-sensation.md) | Siento frío y siento dolor en la espalda. | I feel cold, and I feel pain in my back. |
| [**Sentirse → Feel**](02-feel-an-emotion.md) | Me siento feliz, pero Ana se siente nerviosa. | I feel happy, but Ana feels nervous. |
| [**Sentir → Sense / Feel / Notice**](03-sense-or-notice.md) | Sentí que alguien me estaba mirando. | I sensed that someone was watching me. |
| [**Lo Siento → I'm Sorry**](04-im-sorry.md) | Lo siento por llegar tarde. | I'm sorry for arriving late. |
| [**Tener Ganas / Sentirse Con Ánimo → Feel Like**](05-feel-like.md) | Tengo ganas de salir, pero no me siento con ánimo de cocinar. | I feel like going out, but I don't feel like cooking. |
| [**Sentir Que → Feel That**](06-feel-that-opinion.md) | Siento que necesitamos más tiempo. | I feel that we need more time. |
| [**Sentirse Bien / Mal → Feel Well / Bad**](07-feel-good-or-bad.md) | No me siento bien hoy. | I don't feel well today. |
| [**Sentir Algo Por Alguien → Feel Something For**](08-feel-something.md) | Todavía siente algo por ella. | He still has feelings for her. |
