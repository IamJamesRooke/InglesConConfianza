# **Think About** and **Think Of**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Considera el plan con cuidado y después dime qué opinión te merece. | Think about the plan carefully and then tell me what you think of it. |

**Think about** means consider over time. **Think of** can mean form an opinion, produce an idea, or bring something to mind.

This contrast also appears in the English-first [**about** map](../../../english-to-spanish/translations-about/confusion-sets/03-think-about-vs-think-of.md).
