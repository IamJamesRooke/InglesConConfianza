# **Vender** → **Sell**, **Sell Out**, and **Sell Off**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| vender | **to sell** |
| vender todas las entradas | sell out |
| vender algo por liquidación | sell off |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La tienda vende libros usados. | The store sells used books. |

**Sell out** means all the available items are sold; **sell off** means sell something, often quickly or cheaply.
