# Translations of Spanish **Parecer** and **Parecerse**

Spanish **parecer** and **parecerse** map to seeming, appearance, opinions, impressions, and resemblance.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Parecer → Seem**](01-seem.md) | Parece cansado, pero parece entender. | He seems tired, but he seems to understand. |
| [**Parecer → Look**](02-look.md) | Pareces preocupado hoy. | You look worried today. |
| [**Parecer → Appear**](03-appear.md) | El problema parece ser más serio. | The problem appears to be more serious. |
| [**¿Qué Te Parece? → What Do You Think?**](04-what-do-you-think.md) | ¿Qué te parece mi propuesta? | What do you think of my proposal? |
| [**¿Qué Te Parece Si...? → How About...?**](05-how-does-it-sound.md) | ¿Qué te parece si salimos temprano? | How about leaving early? |
| [**Parecerse A → Look Like / Resemble**](06-resemble.md) | Ana se parece a su mamá. | Ana looks like her mother. |
| [**Parecer → Seem Like**](07-seem-like.md) | Parece una buena idea. | It seems like a good idea. |
| [**Al Parecer → Apparently**](08-as-far-as-i-can-tell.md) | Al parecer, cancelaron la reunión. | Apparently, they canceled the meeting. |
| [**Parecido** → **Similar / Alike**](<parecido (similar and alike).md>) | Los dos libros son similares. | The two books are similar. |
