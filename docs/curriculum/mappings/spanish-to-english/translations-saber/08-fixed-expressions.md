# Fixed Expressions with **Saber**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Que yo sepa, nadie sabe qué pasó; quién sabe, tal vez lo sepamos mañana. | As far as I know, nobody knows what happened; who knows, maybe we'll know tomorrow. |

Learn **que yo sepa → as far as I know**, **quién sabe → who knows**, **ya sé → I know/I've got it**, and **no sé → I don't know** as complete conversational expressions.
