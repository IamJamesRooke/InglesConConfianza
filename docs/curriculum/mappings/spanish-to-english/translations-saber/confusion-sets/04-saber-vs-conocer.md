# **Saber** and **Conocer**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Sé la dirección y conozco a la dueña, pero no conozco bien el barrio. | I know the address and know the owner, but I don't know the neighborhood well. |

Use **saber** for facts, answers, clauses, and learned skills. Use **conocer** for people, places, and familiarity. English often uses **know** for both, so identify what is known.
