# Translations of Spanish **Llegar**

Spanish **llegar** maps to arrival, reaching a place or level, managing an action, eventual outcomes, and emphatic limits.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Llegar → Arrive**](01-arrive.md) | Llegamos al aeropuerto a las seis. | We arrived at the airport at six. |
| [**Llegar → Get To**](02-get-to.md) | ¿Cómo llego a la estación? | How do I get to the station? |
| [**Llegar → Reach**](03-reach.md) | Llegamos a la cima antes del mediodía. | We reached the summit before noon. |
| [**Llegar A → Manage To**](04-manage-to.md) | Llegó a terminar el informe a tiempo. | She managed to finish the report on time. |
| [**Llegar A Ser → Become / Go On To Be**](05-eventually-become.md) | Llegó a ser directora de la empresa. | She went on to become the company's director. |
| [**Llegar A → Reach** a Level](06-reach-an-amount-or-level.md) | La temperatura llegó a cuarenta grados. | The temperature reached forty degrees. |
| [Emphatic **Llegar A**](07-emphatic-llegar-a.md) | Nunca llegué a decir eso. | I never actually said that. |
| [**Llegar A Un Acuerdo → Reach an Agreement**](08-llegar-a-un-acuerdo.md) | Llegamos a un acuerdo después de dos horas. | We reached an agreement after two hours. |
