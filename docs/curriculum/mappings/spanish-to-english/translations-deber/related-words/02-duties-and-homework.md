# **Deberes → Duties / Homework**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Cumplió con sus deberes laborales y luego ayudó a su hijo con la tarea. | She performed her work duties and then helped her son with his homework. |

Plural **deberes** can mean responsibilities (**duties**) or schoolwork (**homework**). In Colombian Spanish, **tarea** is generally more natural for school homework. English **homework** is not normally plural.
