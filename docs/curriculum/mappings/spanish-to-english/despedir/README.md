# Spanish **Despedir**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Despidieron a Mateo. | They fired Mateo. |
| Fui a despedir a Laura al aeropuerto. | I went to see Laura off at the airport. |
| Se despidió de todos. | She said goodbye to everyone. |
| La estufa despide calor. | The stove gives off heat. |

**Despedir** can become **fire, see off, say goodbye**, or **give off**, depending on what is sent away or emitted.
