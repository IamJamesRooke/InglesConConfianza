# Translations of Spanish **Ni**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [nor](01-nor.md) | No llamó ni escribió. | She neither called nor wrote. |
| [neither-nor](02-neither-nor.md) | Ni Ana ni Luis vinieron. | Neither Ana nor Luis came. |
| [not-even](03-not-even.md) | Ni yo lo sabía. | Not even I knew it. |
| [ni-siquiera](05-ni-siquiera.md) | Ni siquiera respondió. | She didn't even answer. |

The independent [**siquiera** family](siquiera/README.md) remains here because its most frequent negative form is **ni siquiera**.
