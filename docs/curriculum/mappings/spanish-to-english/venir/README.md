# Spanish **Venir**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Laura viene a mi casa. | Laura is coming to my house. |
| Vengo de Colombia. | I am from Colombia. |
| La camisa viene en tres colores. | The shirt comes in three colors. |
| Esta fecha me viene bien. | This date works for me. |
| El bus ya viene. | The bus is on its way. |

Physical **venir** usually becomes **come**. Origin, availability, product options, and something approaching use complete English expressions.
