# Translations of Spanish **De**

Spanish **de** does not have one automatic English translation. First identify the relationship: connection (**of**), origin or separation (**from**), topic (**about**), creator (**by**), possession (**'s**), or an English structure with no separate preposition.

| Section | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Core Translations](core-translations/README.md) | Es una novela de García Márquez sobre una familia de Colombia. | It's a novel by García Márquez about a family from Colombia. |
| [English Restructuring](english-restructuring/README.md) | La taza de café de Ana está en la mesa de la cocina. | Ana's coffee cup is on the kitchen table. |
| [Fixed Verb Connections](fixed-verb-connections/README.md) | Acabo de enterarme de que dejó de depender de ellos. | I've just found out that she stopped depending on them. |
| [Fixed Expressions](fixed-expressions/README.md) | De noche estudio de pie y, de vez en cuando, repito todo de memoria. | At night I study standing up and, from time to time, repeat everything by heart. |
| [Confusion Sets](confusion-sets/README.md) | La taza de café no es una taza para café. | The cup of coffee isn't a coffee cup. |

## Whole-Topic Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La novela de Ana trata de una familia de Bogotá que acaba de regresar de España. | Ana's novel is about a family from Bogotá who have just returned from Spain. |
| Dejé de depender de ellos cuando me di cuenta de que podía hacerlo de otra manera. | I stopped depending on them when I realized I could do it another way. |
| De noche tomo una taza de café en la mesa de la cocina y estudio de vez en cuando con Ana. | At night I have a cup of coffee at the kitchen table and study with Ana from time to time. |
