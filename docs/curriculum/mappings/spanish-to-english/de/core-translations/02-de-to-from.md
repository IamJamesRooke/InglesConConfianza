# **De → From**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| de / desde un lugar | from a place |
| Vengo de la oficina. | I am coming from the office. |
| Trae el documento de la oficina. | Bring the document from the office. |
| Vengo de Bogotá y acabo de salir de la oficina. | I come from Bogotá, and I've just left the office. |

Use **from** when **de** marks origin, source, starting point, or separation: **from Bogotá, from Ana**, and **away from the door**.
