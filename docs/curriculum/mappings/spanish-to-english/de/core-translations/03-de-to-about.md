# **De → About**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Hablamos del problema y me enteré del cambio. | We talked about the problem, and I heard about the change. |
| ¿De qué estás hablando? | What are you talking about? |

Use **about** when **de** introduces a topic or information concerning something: **hablar de, enterarse de**, and **tratarse de** can become **talk about, hear/find out about**, and **be about**.
