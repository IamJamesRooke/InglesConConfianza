# Translations of Spanish **Todavía**

Spanish **todavía** commonly becomes English **still** or **yet**, depending especially on whether the sentence is affirmative, negative, or a question.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [still](01-still.md) | Todavía estoy trabajando. | I'm still working. |
| [yet](02-yet.md) | Todavía no he terminado. | I haven't finished yet. |

The overlapping accented form is also indexed under [**aún**](../aun/README.md).
