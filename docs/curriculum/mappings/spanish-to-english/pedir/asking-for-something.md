# Communication Verbs: Asking for Something

Use **ask for + something** when you request a thing.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| pedir algo | **to ask for something** |

**Le pido ayuda a mi compañero.**<br>
I ask my classmate for help.

Use **for** before the thing you request:

**Ask for help.**<br>
Not: *Ask help.*
