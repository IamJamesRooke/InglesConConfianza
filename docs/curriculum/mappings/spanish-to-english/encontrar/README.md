# Spanish **Encontrar**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Encontré mis llaves. | I found my keys. |
| Me encontré con Laura en el centro. | I ran into Laura downtown. |
| Encuentro difícil esta pregunta. | I find this question difficult. |
| La oficina se encuentra en el segundo piso. | The office is located on the second floor. |
| Hoy me encuentro mejor. | I feel better today. |

Discovery, an accidental meeting, an evaluation, location, and personal condition use different English forms of **find, meet, be**, or **feel**.
