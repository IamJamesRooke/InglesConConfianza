# **Malo** → **Bad**, **Evil**, and **Poor**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| malo / mala | bad / evil / poor |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Es una mala idea. | It is a bad idea. |
| El villano es malo. | The villain is evil. |
| El país tuvo una mala cosecha. | The country had a poor harvest. |

**Bad** is the general translation. **Evil** describes serious moral wrong. **Poor** can describe low quality or an unfavorable result.
