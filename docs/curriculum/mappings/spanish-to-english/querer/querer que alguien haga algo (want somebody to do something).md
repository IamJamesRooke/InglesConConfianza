# **Querer que Alguien Haga Algo** → **Want Somebody to Do Something**

Spanish uses **que + subjunctive** when somebody else performs the action. English normally puts that person directly after **want** and uses **to + base verb**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| querer que alguien haga algo | **want somebody to do something** |

**Quiero que Laura haga la reserva.**  
**I want Laura to make the reservation.**

Do not translate this pattern as *I want that Laura makes the reservation.*
