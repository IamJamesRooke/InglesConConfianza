# **Caminar** → **Walk**, **Walk Into**, and **Walk Away**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| caminar | **to walk** |
| entrar caminando | walk into |
| alejarse caminando | walk away |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Camino al trabajo cuando hace buen tiempo. | I walk to work when the weather is good. |

The particle changes the path or result: **walk into** enters a place, while **walk away** leaves it.
