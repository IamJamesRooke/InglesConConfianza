# **Bien** → **Okay**, **Fine**, and **All Right**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| bien | okay / fine / all right |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Estoy bien. | I am okay. / I am fine. / I am all right. |
| El plan está bien. | The plan is okay. / The plan is fine. |
| No te preocupes; todo está bien. | Don't worry; everything is okay. |
| Laura se sintió mal ayer, pero ahora está bien. | Laura felt sick yesterday, but she is fine now. |

**Okay**, **fine**, and **all right** describe an acceptable state. This differs from **good**, which describes positive quality.

**All right** is the traditional two-word spelling. **Alright** is common in informal writing, but **all right** is the safer standard choice.
