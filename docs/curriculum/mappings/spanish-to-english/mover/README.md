# Spanish **Mover**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mueve la silla. | Move the chair. |
| Movió el dinero a otra cuenta. | She transferred the money to another account. |
| La noticia movió a la comunidad a actuar. | The news prompted the community to act. |
| Muévete, que vamos tarde. | Get moving; we are running late. |

Physical relocation uses **move**. Money transfers, prompting action, and imperative **moverse** may require **transfer, prompt**, or **get moving**.
