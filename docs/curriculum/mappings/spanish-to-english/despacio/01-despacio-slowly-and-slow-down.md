# **Despacio** → **Slowly** and **Slow Down**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| despacio | slowly / slow down |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Laura habla despacio. | Laura speaks slowly. |
| ¡Despacio! | Slow down! |

Use **slowly** when describing how an action happens. As a warning or instruction, **¡despacio!** can become **slow down!**
