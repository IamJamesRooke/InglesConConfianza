# Spanish **Disculpar**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Discúlpame por llegar tarde. | Excuse me for being late. |
| No puedo disculpar su comportamiento. | I can't excuse his behavior. |
| La profesora disculpó la ausencia. | The teacher excused the absence. |
| Disculpa, ¿me puedes ayudar? | Excuse me, can you help me? |

**Disculpar** can mean **excuse** or **forgive**, and **disculpa/disculpe** is also a polite attention-getter.
