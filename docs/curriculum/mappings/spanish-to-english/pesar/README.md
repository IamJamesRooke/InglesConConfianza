# Spanish **Pesar**

Literal **pesar** is **weigh**. Regret, concession with **a pesar de**, and figurative influence use different English expressions.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| Core use | La caja pesa diez kilos. | The box weighs ten kilograms. |
| Regret | Me pesa haber mentido. | I regret having lied. |
| Concession (alternate wording) | A pesar de la lluvia, salimos. | Despite the rain, we went out. |
| [**a pesar de** → **despite**](a%20pesar%20de%20%28despite%29.md) | A pesar de la lluvia, salimos. | Despite the rain, we left. |
| [**a pesar de** → **in spite of**](a%20pesar%20de%20%28in%20spite%20of%29.md) | A pesar de estar cansada, trabajó. | In spite of being tired, she worked. |
| [**a pesar de que** → **despite the fact that**](a%20pesar%20de%20que%20%28despite%20the%20fact%20that%29.md) | A pesar de que llovió, salimos. | Despite the fact that it rained, we left. |
| Figurative influence | La decisión pesó mucho en el resultado. | The decision weighed heavily on the result. |
