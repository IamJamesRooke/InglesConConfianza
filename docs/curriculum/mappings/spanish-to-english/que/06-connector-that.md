# Connector **Que** → **That**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| que | that |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Creo que es importante. | I think that it is important. |
| Sé que Laura está aquí. | I know that Laura is here. |
| Laura dice que la reunión empieza a las nueve. | Laura says that the meeting starts at nine. |

In everyday English, connector **that** can often be omitted: **I think it is important.** This connector **that** introduces a complete idea; it is different from demonstrative **that** in **that book**.
