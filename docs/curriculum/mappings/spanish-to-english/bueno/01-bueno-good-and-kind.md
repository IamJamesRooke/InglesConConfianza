# **Bueno** → **Good** and **Kind**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| bueno / buena | good / kind |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Es una buena idea. | It is a good idea. |
| Mi vecino es muy bueno con los niños. | My neighbor is very kind to children. |

Use **good** for positive quality. Use **kind** for a person whose behavior is considerate or helpful.
