# **Dónde** → **Where**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿Dónde está la oficina? | Where is the office? |
| Quiero saber dónde está la oficina. | I want to know where the office is. |

In a direct question, use **where + be + subject**. In an embedded question, use statement order: **where the office is**.
