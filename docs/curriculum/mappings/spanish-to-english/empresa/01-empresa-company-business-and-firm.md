# **Empresa/Compañía** → **Company**, **Business**, or **Firm**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La empresa ofrece cursos. | The company offers courses. |
| Tiene un negocio pequeño. | She has a small business. |
| La firma publicó un informe. | The firm published a report. |

**Company** is the neutral organization word. **Business** can mean the organization or the activity of commerce, while **firm** is more formal.
