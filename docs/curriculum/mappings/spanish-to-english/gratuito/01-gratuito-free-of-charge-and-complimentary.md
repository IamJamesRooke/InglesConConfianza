# **Gratuito** → **Free**, **Free of Charge**, and **Complimentary**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| gratuito / gratuita | free / free of charge / complimentary |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El evento es gratuito. | The event is free. |
| La entrada es gratuita. | Admission is free of charge. |
| El hotel ofrece un desayuno gratuito. | The hotel offers a complimentary breakfast. |

Use **free** in ordinary speech. **Free of charge** is explicit or formal. **Complimentary** is common for a service or item offered as a courtesy.
