# Translations of Spanish **Conocer**

Spanish **conocer** can describe first contact, an existing relationship, familiarity, or experience with a place. English chooses among **meet, know, get to know, be familiar with**, and **have been to**.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Conocer → Meet**](01-conocer-meaning-meet.md) | Conocí a Ana ayer. | I met Ana yesterday. |
| [**Conocer → Know**](02-conocer-meaning-know.md) | Conozco a Ana desde hace años. | I've known Ana for years. |
| [**Conocer → Get To Know**](03-conocer-meaning-get-to-know.md) | Quiero conocer mejor a mis compañeros. | I want to get to know my coworkers better. |
| [Knowing a Place](04-knowing-a-place.md) | Conozco bien Bogotá. | I know Bogotá well. |
| [**Conocer → Be Familiar With**](05-be-familiar-with.md) | Conozco el sistema, pero no todos sus detalles. | I'm familiar with the system, but not all its details. |
| [**Conocido** and **Conocida**](06-conocido-and-conocida.md) | Es una autora conocida y Ana es una conocida mía. | She's a well-known author, and Ana is an acquaintance of mine. |
| [**Conocer, Saber, Meet**, and **Introduce**](confusion-sets/README.md) | Conocí a Ana, Carlos nos presentó y ahora la conozco bien. | I met Ana, Carlos introduced us, and now I know her well. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Conocí a Ana ayer, pero quiero conocerla mejor antes de decir que la conozco bien. | I met Ana yesterday, but I want to get to know her better before saying I know her well. |
| Conozco Bogotá porque he estado allí muchas veces, y estoy familiarizado con su sistema de transporte. | I know Bogotá because I've been there many times, and I'm familiar with its transportation system. |
