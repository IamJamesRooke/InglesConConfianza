# **Conocido** and **Conocida**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Es una autora muy conocida, pero para mí solo es una conocida. | She's a well-known author, but to me she's only an acquaintance. |

As a description, **conocido/a** can mean **known, well-known**, or **familiar**. As a person noun, **un conocido/una conocida** is **an acquaintance**.
