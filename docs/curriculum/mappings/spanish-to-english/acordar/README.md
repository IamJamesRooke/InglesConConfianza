# Translations of Spanish **Acordar** and **Acordarse De**

Spanish **acordar** and **acordarse de** separate agreement or decision from remembering. English uses distinct structures for each.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Acordar → Agree**](01-agree.md) | Acordamos reunirnos el viernes. | We agreed to meet on Friday. |
| [**Acordar → Decide**](02-decide.md) | El comité acordó cambiar las reglas. | The committee decided to change the rules. |
| [**Acordar → Reach an Agreement**](03-reach-an-agreement.md) | Las partes acordaron el precio. | The parties agreed on the price. |
| [**Acordarse De → Remember**](04-remember.md) | Me acordé de llamar a Ana. | I remembered to call Ana. |
| [**Acordarse De Haber Hecho → Remember Doing**](05-remember-doing.md) | Me acuerdo de haber cerrado la puerta. | I remember closing the door. |
| [**Hacer Acordar → Remind**](06-remind-someone.md) | Recuérdame la reunión de mañana. | Remind me about tomorrow's meeting. |
| [**No Acordarse → Can't Remember**](07-cant-remember.md) | No me acuerdo de su nombre. | I can't remember her name. |
| [**Acordar vs Acordarse De**](08-agree-vs-remember.md) | Acordamos la fecha, pero no me acuerdo de la hora. | We agreed on the date, but I don't remember the time. |
