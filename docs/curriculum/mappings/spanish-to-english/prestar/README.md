# Spanish **Prestar**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿Me prestas tu bolígrafo? | Can you lend me your pen? |
| Presta atención. | Pay attention. |
| La empresa presta servicios legales. | The company provides legal services. |
| Se prestó para ayudar. | She volunteered to help. |

**Prestar** can become **lend, pay, provide**, or **volunteer**, depending on the complete expression.

The owner **lends something to someone**; the receiver **borrows something from someone**. Spanish **pedir prestado** follows the receiver's perspective.
