# **Hombre** → **Man**, **Guy**, or **Male**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Es un hombre amable. | He is a kind man. |
| Ese hombre vive al lado. | That guy lives next door. |
| El paciente es de sexo masculino. | The patient is male. |

Use **man** as the neutral adult noun, informal **guy** in conversation, and **male** when talking about sex or classification.
