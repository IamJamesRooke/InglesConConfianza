# Spanish **Preocupar**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El dinero me preocupa. | Money worries me. |
| La noticia preocupa a la comunidad. | The news concerns the community. |
| No te preocupes. | Don't worry. |
| Se preocupa mucho por sus hijos. | She cares deeply about her children. |

**Preocupar** can become **worry, concern**, or **care about**, and the English subject may differ from the Spanish one.
