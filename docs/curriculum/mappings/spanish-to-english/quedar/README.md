# Translations of Spanish **Quedar**

Spanish **quedar** can become **remain, be left, fit, suit, be located, arrange to meet, stay**, or **turn out/look**. Reflexive and non-reflexive differences stay inside this one source folder.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [remain / be left](01-remain-and-be-left.md) | Solo queda una opción. | Only one option remains. |
| [fit / suit](02-fit-and-suit.md) | La camisa te queda bien. | The shirt fits you and looks good on you. |
| [location](03-location.md) | El banco queda cerca. | The bank is nearby. |
| [arrange to meet](04-arrange-to-meet.md) | Quedamos a las seis. | We arranged to meet at six. |
| [stay](05-stay.md) | Me quedo en casa. | I'm staying home. |
| [turn out / look](06-turn-out-or-look.md) | La foto quedó bien. | The photo turned out well. |
| [items left](01-items-left.md) | Quedan cinco. | There are five left. |
| [time left](02-time-left.md) | Quedan cinco minutos. | There are five minutes left. |
| [remaining option](03-remain.md) | Solo queda una opción. | Only one option remains. |
| [how much is left?](04-question.md) | ¿Cuánto queda? | How much is left? |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Quedan cinco minutos y solo queda una opción. | There are five minutes left, and only one option remains. |
| La camisa te queda bien y el banco queda cerca. | The shirt suits you, and the bank is nearby. |
| Quedamos a las seis, pero me quedé en casa. | We arranged to meet at six, but I stayed home. |
