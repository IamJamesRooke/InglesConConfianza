# Translations of Spanish **Dar** and **Darse**

Spanish **dar** and **darse** extend far beyond physical giving. English selects verbs for feelings, direction, realization, indifference, classes, operation, and events.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Dar → Give**](01-give.md) | Le di el libro a Ana. | I gave Ana the book. |
| [**Dar → Make / Be** a Feeling](02-cause-a-feeling.md) | Me da miedo caminar solo. | Walking alone scares me. / I'm afraid to walk alone. |
| [**Dar A → Face / Overlook**](03-face-a-direction.md) | La ventana da al norte. | The window faces north. |
| [**Darse Cuenta → Realize**](04-darse-cuenta.md) | Me di cuenta de que estaba equivocado. | I realized that I was wrong. |
| [**Dar Igual → Not Matter**](05-dar-igual.md) | Me da igual cuál elijas. | It doesn't matter to me which one you choose. |
| [**Dar Clase → Teach / Take** a Class](06-take-or-teach-a-class.md) | Ana da la clase y yo la tomo. | Ana teaches the class, and I take it. |
| [**Dar → Turn On / Start**](07-turn-on-or-start.md) | Dale al interruptor y luego dale al video. | Hit the switch and then start the video. |
| [Events with **Dar**](08-events-and-results.md) | La reunión se dio ayer. | The meeting took place yesterday. |
| [**Dar poder → Empower**](<dar poder (empower).md>) | La capacitación les da poder para decidir. | The training empowers them to decide. |
| [**Dar poder → Give power**](<dar poder (give power).md>) | La regla les da poder a los supervisores. | The rule gives power to the supervisors. |
| [**Darle poder a alguien → Authorize**](<darle poder a alguien para hacer algo (authorize someone to do something).md>) | Le da poder a Ana para firmar. | It authorizes Ana to sign. |
