# **Tener Que** Across Time

Spanish **tener que + infinitive** carries necessity across present, past, future, and perfect forms. English changes the form of **have to** instead of creating a past form of **must**.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Future necessity](01-will-have-to.md) | Tendrá que presentar el informe. | She will have to submit the report. |
| [No present necessity](02-dont-have-to.md) | No tienes que esperar. | You don't have to wait. |
| [Past no necessity](03-didnt-have-to-and-neednt-have.md) | No tuve que pagar. | I didn't have to pay. |
| [Past necessity](04-had-to.md) | Tuve que trabajar. | I had to work. |
| [Present-perfect necessity](05-have-had-to.md) | He tenido que trabajar. | I've had to work. |

Keep **don't have to** separate from **mustn't**: the first removes necessity, while the second prohibits the action.
