# Translations of Spanish **Tener**

Spanish **tener** maps far beyond literal **have**. Its common expressions become English age, states, success, obligation, desire, and judgments.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [age](01-age.md) | Tengo treinta años. | I'm thirty years old. |
| [states](02-states.md) | Tengo hambre, miedo y calor. | I'm hungry, afraid, and hot. |
| [success-luck](03-success-luck.md) | Tuvo éxito y tuvo suerte. | She succeeded and was lucky. |
| [obligation](04-obligation.md) | Tengo que salir. | I have to leave. |
| [desire](05-desire.md) | Tengo ganas de bailar. | I feel like dancing. |
| [right](06-right.md) | Tienes razón. | You're right. |
| [**Have To / Have Got To**](07-have-to-and-have-got-to.md) | Tengo que hacerlo. | I have to do it / I've got to do it. |
| [Possession](08-possession.md) | Tengo una reserva. | I have a reservation. |
