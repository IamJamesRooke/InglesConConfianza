# Translations of Spanish **Echar**

Spanish **echar** is highly contextual, covering throwing, adding, pouring, sending away, missing, beginning, ruining, and emitting.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Echar → Throw / Toss**](01-throw.md) | Echó la pelota por la ventana. | He threw the ball through the window. |
| [**Echar → Put / Add**](02-put-or-add.md) | Échale sal a la sopa. | Add salt to the soup. |
| [**Echar → Pour**](03-pour.md) | Echa el café en la taza. | Pour the coffee into the cup. |
| [**Echar → Throw Out / Fire**](04-send-away-or-fire.md) | Lo echaron del bar y luego lo echaron del trabajo. | They threw him out of the bar and then fired him. |
| [**Echar De Menos → Miss**](05-miss.md) | Echo de menos a mi familia. | I miss my family. |
| [**Echarse A → Start Doing**](06-start-doing.md) | Se echó a reír cuando vio la foto. | She burst out laughing when she saw the photo. |
| [**Echar A Perder → Ruin / Spoil**](07-ruin.md) | La lluvia echó a perder el picnic. | The rain ruined the picnic. |
| [**Echar → Give Off / Grow**](08-emit-or-produce.md) | La estufa echa mucho humo y la planta echó raíces. | The stove gives off a lot of smoke, and the plant grew roots. |
