# **Mujer** → **Woman**, **Lady**, or **Female**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Es una mujer amable. | She is a kind woman. |
| La señora que llamó fue muy amable. | The lady who called was very kind. |
| La paciente es de sexo femenino. | The patient is female. |

Use **woman** as the neutral adult noun, **lady** as a polite or friendly alternative, and **female** for sex or classification.
