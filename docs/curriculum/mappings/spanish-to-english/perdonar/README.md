# Spanish **Perdonar**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Perdóname por mentir. | Forgive me for lying. |
| Perdona la interrupción. | Excuse the interruption. |
| El banco perdonó parte de la deuda. | The bank forgave part of the debt. |
| Perdón, no te escuché. | Sorry, I didn't hear you. |

**Perdonar** can become **forgive** or **excuse**. Noun/interjection **perdón** often becomes **sorry** or **excuse me**.
