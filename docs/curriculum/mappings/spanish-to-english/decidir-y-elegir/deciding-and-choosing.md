# Handle Verbs: Deciding and Choosing

## Deciding to do an action

Use **decide + full infinitive**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| decidir hacer algo | **to decide to do something** |

**Decidimos hacer la reserva hoy.**<br>
We decided to make the reservation today.

## Choosing something

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| elegir algo | **to choose something** |

**Elegimos la mesa junto a la ventana.**<br>
We chose the table by the window.

## Choosing to do an action

Use **choose + full infinitive**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| elegir hacer algo | **to choose to do something** |

**Elegimos esperar afuera.**<br>
We chose to wait outside.
