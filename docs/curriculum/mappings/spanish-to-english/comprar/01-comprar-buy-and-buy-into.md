# **Comprar** → **Buy** and **Buy Into**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| comprar | **to buy** |
| comprar una idea | buy into an idea |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Compro el libro en la tienda del barrio. | I buy the book at the neighborhood store. |
| No compro esa explicación. | I don't buy that explanation. |

**Buy into** means accept or believe an idea, not simply purchase it.
