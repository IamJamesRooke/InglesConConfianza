# Spanish **Medio**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Comí media manzana. | I ate half an apple. |
| Está en medio de la calle. | It is in the middle of the street. |
| Llegó medio cansado. | He arrived somewhat tired. |
| Los medios informaron la noticia. | The media reported the news. |
| Es un medio de transporte. | It is a means of transportation. |

**Medio** can become **half, middle, somewhat, media**, or **means**, depending on form and context.
