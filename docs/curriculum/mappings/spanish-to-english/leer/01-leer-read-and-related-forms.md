# **Leer** → **Read**, **Read Up On**, and **Read Through**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| leer | **to read** |
| informarse sobre algo leyendo | read up on something |
| leer algo completo | read through something |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Leo las noticias en inglés todas las mañanas. | I read the news in English every morning. |

**Read up on** means study a subject through reading; **read through** means read something from beginning to end or carefully.
