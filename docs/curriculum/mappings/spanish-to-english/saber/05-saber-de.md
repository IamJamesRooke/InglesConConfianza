# **Saber De**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Sé bastante de diseño, pero no sé nada de Ana desde el lunes. | I know a lot about design, but I haven't heard anything from Ana since Monday. |

**Saber de un tema** is **know about a subject**. **No saber nada de alguien** often means **not have heard from someone** or **have no news of them**, depending on context.
