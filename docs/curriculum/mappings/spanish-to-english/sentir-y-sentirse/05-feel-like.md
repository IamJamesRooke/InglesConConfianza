# **Tener Ganas / Sentirse Con Ánimo → Feel Like**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tengo ganas de salir, pero no me siento con ánimo de cocinar. | I feel like going out, but I don't feel like cooking. |

English **feel like + -ing** commonly translates **tener ganas de**; it is not usually literal **sentir como**.
