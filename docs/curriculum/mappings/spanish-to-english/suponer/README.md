# Spanish **Suponer**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Supongo que está en casa. | I suppose she is at home. |
| No supongas que todos están de acuerdo. | Don't assume that everyone agrees. |
| El cambio supone un riesgo. | The change involves a risk. |
| Se supone que llega a las ocho. | She is supposed to arrive at eight. |

**Suponer** can express an opinion, an unsupported assumption, what something entails, or an expectation expressed by **be supposed to**.
