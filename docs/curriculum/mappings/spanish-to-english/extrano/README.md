# Spanish **Extraño**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Es un ruido extraño. | It is a strange sound. |
| Me siento extraño. | I feel odd/weird. |
| Un extraño llamó a la puerta. | A stranger knocked on the door. |
| Extraño a mi familia. | I miss my family. |

**Extraño** can become **strange, odd/weird**, or noun **stranger**; as a verb form of **extrañar**, it means **miss**.
