# English **Talk**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| We need to talk. | Tenemos que hablar. |
| I talked to Laura about the problem. | Hablé con Laura del problema. |
| She talked me into applying. | Me convenció de postularme. |
| He talked me out of quitting. | Me convenció de no renunciar. |

Ordinary conversation uses **hablar** or **conversar**. **Talk someone into/out of** expresses persuasion and requires **convencer** or **disuadir**.
