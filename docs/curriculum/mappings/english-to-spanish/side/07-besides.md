# **Besides**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Además del salario, el trabajo ofrece seguro médico. | Besides the salary, the job offers health insurance. |

**Besides + thing/person** means **además de** or **aparte de**.

**Besides** can also introduce an additional reason or point:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No quiero ese trabajo; además, queda demasiado lejos. | I don't want that job; besides, it's too far away. |

This meaning is not physical: **besides** does not mean **al lado de**.
