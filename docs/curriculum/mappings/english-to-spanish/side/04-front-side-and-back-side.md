# The **Front Side** and the **Back Side**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Escriba su nombre en la parte delantera de la tarjeta y la fecha en la parte de atrás. | Write your name on the front side of the card and the date on the back side. |

**The front side** is the forward-facing or main face. **The back side** is the opposite face.

In many contexts, English naturally shortens these expressions to **the front** and **the back**:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La entrada está en la parte delantera del edificio y el jardín está en la parte de atrás. | The entrance is at the front of the building, and the garden is at the back. |

For the larger physical contrast, see [The **Back** and the **Front**](../back/core-picture/01-the-back-and-the-front.md).
