# **As** for Manner or Shared Information

Use **as + complete idea** for **como**, meaning in the way stated or according to shared information.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Como ya sabes, organizamos los archivos tal como lo pidió el cliente. | As you already know, we organized the files as the client requested. |

Other common frames include **as I said**, **as you can see**, and **as we discussed**.
