# **As** Meaning **Because**

At the beginning of a clause, **as** can give a reason. It usually presents the reason as known or secondary.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Como el banco ya cerró, tendremos que hacer la transferencia mañana. | As the bank's already closed, we'll have to make the transfer tomorrow. |

Use **because** when the reason needs stronger emphasis or could otherwise be unclear.
