# English **Sell**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| They sell coffee. | Venden café. |
| The tickets sold out. | Se agotaron las entradas. |
| I am not sold on the idea. | La idea no me convence. |
| She sold me on the plan. | Me convenció del plan. |

Commercial **sell** uses **vender**. Availability and persuasion in **sell out, be sold on**, and **sell someone on** require other Spanish expressions.
