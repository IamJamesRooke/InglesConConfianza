# Spanish Translations of English **Turn**

English **turn** maps to rotation, direction, switching, transformation, age, sequence, results, and particle meanings.

| Lesson | English | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| [**Turn → Girar / Voltear**](01-rotate-or-face.md) | Turn the handle and turn to face me. | Gira la manija y voltéate para mirarme. |
| [**Turn → Girar / Doblar**](02-directions.md) | Turn left at the next corner. | Gira a la izquierda en la próxima esquina. |
| [**Turn On / Off → Encender / Apagar**](03-switch.md) | Turn on the light and turn off the fan. | Enciende la luz y apaga el ventilador. |
| [**Turn → Volverse / Convertirse**](04-become.md) | The sky turned dark. | El cielo se oscureció. |
| [**Turn → Cumplir** an Age](05-age.md) | She turns thirty tomorrow. | Mañana cumple treinta años. |
| [**Take Turns / Your Turn**](06-sequence.md) | We took turns, and now it's your turn. | Nos turnamos y ahora es tu turno. |
| [**Turn Out → Resultar / Salir**](07-turn-out.md) | The plan turned out well. | El plan salió bien. |
| [Other Particles with **Turn**](08-other-particles.md) | She turned down the offer and turned in the report. | Rechazó la oferta y entregó el informe. |
