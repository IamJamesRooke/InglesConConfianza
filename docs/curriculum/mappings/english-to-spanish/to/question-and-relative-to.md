# **To** at the End of Questions and Relative Clauses

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿Con quién estás hablando? | Who are you talking to? |
| Esa es la persona con quien hablé. | That's the person I spoke to. |
| ¿Con quién está hablando? | To whom are you speaking? |

In ordinary conversation, English often leaves **to** at the end of a question or relative clause. The formal alternative moves **to** to the front: **To whom are you speaking?** Spanish keeps **con** before **quién** or **quien**.
