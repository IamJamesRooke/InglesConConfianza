# English **Offer**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| She offered me coffee. | Me ofreció café. |
| The course offers practical experience. | El curso brinda experiencia práctica. |
| He offered to help. | Se ofreció a ayudar. |
| They offered ten dollars for it. | Propusieron pagar diez dólares por eso. |

Giving an option, providing a benefit, volunteering, and proposing a price use different Spanish constructions.
