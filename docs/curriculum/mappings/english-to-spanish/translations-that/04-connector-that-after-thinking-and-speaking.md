# Connector **That** After Thinking and Speaking

Use **that** to introduce a complete idea after verbs such as **think, believe, know, say, explain, notice, remember**, and **hope**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Creo que Laura sabe que la reunión empieza a las nueve. | I think that Laura knows that the meeting starts at nine. |

This **that** does not point to an object. It introduces what someone thinks or knows.
