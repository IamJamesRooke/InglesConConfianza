# English **Lead**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| Laura leads the team. | Laura dirige el equipo. |
| This road leads to the airport. | Esta carretera lleva al aeropuerto. |
| Bogotá is leading two to one. | Bogotá va ganando dos a uno. |
| Stress can lead to illness. | El estrés puede causar enfermedades. |

Managing people, connecting destinations, being ahead, and causing a result require **dirigir, llevar, ir ganando**, or **causar**.
