# Core Picture of **From**

**From** identifies the point where movement, distance, time, information, or change begins.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Source and Starting Point](01-source-and-starting-point.md) | El bus viene del aeropuerto. | The bus comes from the airport. |
| [Movement Verbs](02-movement-verbs.md) | Caminamos desde la estación. | We walked from the station. |
| [**From ... To ...**](03-from-to.md) | Viajamos de Bogotá a Cali. | We traveled from Bogotá to Cali. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Salimos de la casa, caminamos desde allí hasta la estación y viajamos de Bogotá a Cali. | We left the house, walked from there to the station, and traveled from Bogotá to Cali. |
