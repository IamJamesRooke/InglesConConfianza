# Modern Uses of English **Shall**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿Empezamos? | Shall we start? |
| Empezaremos de inmediato. | We shall begin immediately. |
| El contratista deberá entregar el informe el viernes. | The contractor shall submit the report on Friday. |

**Shall I/we...?** makes an offer or suggestion. Affirmative **shall** can express formal determination and appears in legal requirements. In everyday English, future necessity is normally **will have to**, not **shall**.
