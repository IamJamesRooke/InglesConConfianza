# English **Appear**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| A problem appeared. | Apareció un problema. |
| She appears tired. | Parece cansada. |
| The article appeared in the newspaper. | El artículo salió en el periódico. |
| He appeared in court. | Compareció ante el tribunal. |

Becoming visible, seeming, being published, and formally presenting oneself use different Spanish verbs.
