# English **Across**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| She walked across the street. | Cruzó la calle. |
| The bank is across from the hotel. | El banco está frente al hotel. |
| The news spread across the country. | La noticia se difundió por todo el país. |

Crossing, opposite position, and distribution over an area use **cruzar, frente a**, or **por todo**.
