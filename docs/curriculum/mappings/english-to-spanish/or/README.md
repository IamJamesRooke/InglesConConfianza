# English **Or**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| Coffee or tea? | ¿Café o té? |
| Seven or eight people came. | Vinieron siete u ocho personas. |
| Hurry up, or we will be late. | Apúrate o llegaremos tarde. |
| Whether you like it or not, we must leave. | Te guste o no, tenemos que salir. |

Ordinary **or** is **o**, changing to **u** before an **o/ho** sound. Alternatives, consequences, and **whether...or** may use different complete patterns.
