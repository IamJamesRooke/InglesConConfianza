# **Have You Ever...?**

Use **have/has + person + ever + past participle** to ask whether something has happened at any time in someone's life.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿Alguna vez has viajado sola a otro país? | Have you ever traveled alone to another country? |

The exact time isn't important. If the question names a finished time, use the simple past: **Did you travel alone last year?**
