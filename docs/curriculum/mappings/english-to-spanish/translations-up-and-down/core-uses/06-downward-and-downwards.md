# **Downward** and **Downwards**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mire hacia abajo y mueva la palanca hacia abajo. | Look downward and move the lever downwards. |

**Downward** and **downwards** mark direction toward a lower position. **Downwards** is especially common in British English; **downward** is standard in both British and American English.

Only **downward** normally goes before a noun: **a downward movement**, not *a downwards movement*.

Compare the base direction and the extended form:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mire abajo y apunte hacia abajo. | Look down and point downward. |
