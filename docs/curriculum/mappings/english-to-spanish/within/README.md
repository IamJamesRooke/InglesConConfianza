# English **Within**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| Stay within the marked area. | Quédate dentro del área marcada. |
| Reply within five days. | Responde en un plazo de cinco días. |
| The store is within walking distance. | Se puede llegar caminando a la tienda. |

Physical limits, deadlines, and practical reach use **dentro de, en un plazo de**, or a complete accessibility expression.
