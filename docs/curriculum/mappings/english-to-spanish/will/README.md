# English **Will**

English **will** changes meaning with the speaker's intention. It can express future information, a request, a decision, or willingness. The Spanish mapping depends on that function.

| Lesson | Spanish bridge | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Future or prediction](01-future-or-prediction.md) | Mañana te llamaré. | I will call you tomorrow. |
| [Polite request](02-will-you-please.md) | ¿Podrías sentarte, por favor? | Will you please sit down? |
| [Direct request or decision](03-will-you-do-it.md) | ¿Lo harás? | Will you do it? |
| [Willing](willing/01-be-willing-to.md) | Estoy dispuesto a hacerlo. | I am willing to do it. |

## Mastery phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mañana lo haré; ¿podrías ayudarme y estarías dispuesto a hacerlo si te lo pido? | I will do it tomorrow; will you please help me, and will you be willing to do it if I ask you? |
