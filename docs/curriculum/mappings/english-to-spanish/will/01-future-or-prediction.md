# **Will** for the Future

Use **will + base verb** for a future decision, promise, offer, or prediction. Spanish often uses the simple future or **ir a + infinitive**, depending on the context.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Te llamaré mañana. | I will call you tomorrow. |
| Creo que lloverá esta noche. | I think it will rain tonight. |
| No te preocupes; te ayudaré. | Don't worry; I will help you. |
