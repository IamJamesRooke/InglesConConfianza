# **Will You Please...?**

**Will you please + verb...?** is a request. Depending on tone, Spanish may use **¿podrías...?**, **¿quieres...?**, or an imperative softened with **por favor**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿Podrías cerrar la puerta, por favor? | Will you please close the door? |
| ¿Quieres esperar aquí un momento, por favor? | Will you please wait here for a moment? |
