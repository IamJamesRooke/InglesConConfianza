# **Will You Do It?**

In a direct question, **will you + verb...?** asks whether someone intends or agrees to do something. Spanish commonly uses the simple future, **¿vas a...?**, or **¿quieres...?**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿Lo harás? | Will you do it? |
| ¿Vas a venir mañana? | Will you come tomorrow? |
| ¿Quieres ayudarme? | Will you help me? |
