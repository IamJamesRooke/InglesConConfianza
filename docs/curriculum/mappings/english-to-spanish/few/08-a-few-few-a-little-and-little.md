# **A Few, Few, A Little**, and **Little**

| Noun type | Positive amount | Insufficient amount |
|---|---|---|
| countable plural | a few cats | few cats |
| uncountable | a little time | little time |

The article **a** changes the practical meaning:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tengo unos cuantos libros. | I have a few books. |
| Tengo pocos libros. | I have few books. |
| Tengo un poco de tiempo. | I have a little time. |
| Tengo poco tiempo. | I have little time. |

**A few / a little** present the amount as available. **Few / little** emphasize that the amount is small or not enough.
