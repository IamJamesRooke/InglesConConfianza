# Pronunciation: **While, Wild**, and **Whale**

**While** ends in `/l/`, **wild** adds `/d/`, and **whale** has a different vowel. They aren't homophones.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mientras la ballena salvaje nadaba, esperamos un rato. | While the wild whale swam, we waited for a while. |

The playful sentence contrasts three similar-looking forms and repeats both meanings of **while**.
