# Direction vs Position

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Entró a la oficina y ahora está en la oficina; puso el celular sobre la mesa y ahora está sobre ella. | She went into the office and is in it now; she put her phone onto the table, and it is on it now. |

**Into/onto** emphasize movement or change. **In/on** normally describe the resulting position.
