# **Take In** and **Take Out**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me costó asimilar la noticia, así que saqué a mi hermana a cenar para hablar. | I struggled to take in the news, so I took my sister out to dinner to talk. |

**Take in** can mean absorb or understand; **take out** can mean remove, invite socially, or order prepared food to go.
