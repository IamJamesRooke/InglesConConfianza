# Whole Expressions with **In** and **Out**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Work Out**](01-work-out.md) | Hice ejercicio, resolví el problema y todo salió bien. | I worked out, worked out the problem, and everything worked out. |
| [**Figure Out / Find Out**](02-figure-out-and-find-out.md) | Descubrí cuál era el problema y resolví cómo arreglarlo. | I found out what the problem was and figured out how to fix it. |
| [**Turn In / Turn Out**](03-turn-in-and-turn-out.md) | Entregué el informe, resultó bien y me fui a dormir. | I turned in the report, it turned out well, and I turned in. |
| [**Take In / Take Out**](04-take-in-and-take-out.md) | Asimilé la noticia y saqué a mi hermana a cenar. | I took in the news and took my sister out to dinner. |
| [**Point Out / Leave Out**](05-point-out-and-leave-out.md) | Señaló el error y la información omitida. | She pointed out the error and what they'd left out. |
| [**Check In / Check Out**](06-check-in-and-check-out.md) | Nos registramos, salimos y miramos el museo. | We checked in, checked out, and checked out the museum. |
| [**Make Out / Look Out**](07-make-out-and-look-out.md) | Distinguí el letrero y grité: «¡Cuidado!». | I made out the sign and shouted, “Look out!” |
| [**Bring Out / Carry Out / Put Out**](08-bring-out-carry-out-and-put-out.md) | Sacó lo mejor de ella, ejecutó el plan y apagó el incendio. | It brought out her best, she carried out the plan, and put out the fire. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
