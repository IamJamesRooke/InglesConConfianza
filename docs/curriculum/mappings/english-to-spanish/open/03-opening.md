# **Opening**

English **opening** changes with context.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| la apertura | the opening of a door or account |
| la inauguración | the opening of a business or exhibition |
| la vacante | a job opening |

**Asistí a la inauguración porque había una vacante en la nueva oficina.**<br>
I attended the opening because there was a job opening at the new office.
