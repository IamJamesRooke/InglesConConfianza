# Pronunciation: **One, Won, Once, Only** and **Alone**

- **One** and **won** are homophones: `/wʌn/`.
- **Once** begins with the same sound as **one** and ends in `/s/`: `/wʌns/`.
- **Only** begins with the long **o** sound `/oʊ/`: `/ˈoʊnli/`.
- **Alone** contains that same long **o** sound in its stressed syllable: `/əˈloʊn/`.

The spelling can tempt learners to pronounce all four alike, but **one/once** and **only/alone** form two different vowel groups.
