# **Several** and **Enough**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| varios / varias | several |
| suficientes | enough |
| suficiente | enough |

**Several** goes with plural countable nouns:

**Tengo varias opciones.**<br>
I have several options.

**Enough** works with plural countable or uncountable nouns:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tenemos suficientes libros. | We have enough books. |
| Tenemos suficiente tiempo. | We have enough time. |

Before a noun, use **enough + noun**: **enough time**, not *time enough* in the foundational pattern.
