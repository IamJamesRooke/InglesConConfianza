# English **Tell**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| Tell me the truth. | Dime la verdad. |
| Tell me a story. | Cuéntame una historia. |
| I can't tell the twins apart. | No puedo distinguir a los gemelos. |
| Tell Laura to call me. | Dile a Laura que me llame. |
| I can tell that he is tired. | Me doy cuenta de que está cansado. |

**Tell** can become **decir**, **contar**, **distinguir**, or a perception expression such as **darse cuenta**, depending on what follows.
