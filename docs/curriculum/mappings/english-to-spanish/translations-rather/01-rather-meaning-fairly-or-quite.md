# **Rather** Meaning Fairly or Quite

Before an adjective or adverb, **rather** can mean **bastante**, **algo** or **un tanto**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Es bastante importante guardar una copia del contrato porque el original puede perderse. | It is rather important to keep a copy of the contract because the original could get lost. |

The possible loss explains why the matter has a significant degree of importance. This use of **rather** is especially common in British English.
