# English **During**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| I slept during the movie. | Dormí durante la película. |
| It rained during the night. | Llovió por la noche. |
| We met several times during the year. | Nos reunimos varias veces a lo largo del año. |

**During** may become **durante, por**, or **a lo largo de**, according to whether it marks an event, a period, or distribution across time.
