# **What Is It Like?** Versus **How Is It?**

**What is it like?** asks for a general description. **How is it?** asks about condition, quality or experience in the current context.

| Question | Natural answer |
|---|---|
| **What's Bogotá like?** | **It's large, mountainous, and busy.** |
| **How is Bogotá today?** | **It's cold and rainy.** |

The Spanish contrast depends on context:

- **¿Cómo es Bogotá? → What's Bogotá like?**
- **¿Cómo está Bogotá hoy? → How is Bogotá today?**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿Cómo es Bogotá y cómo está hoy? | What's Bogotá like, and how is it today? |

Do not ask *How is Bogotá?* when you want a general description of the city.
