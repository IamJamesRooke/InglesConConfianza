# **Feel Like + Noun or -ing**

**Feel like** expresses a present desire or inclination.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tengo ganas de tomar café, pero no tengo ganas de salir bajo la lluvia. | I feel like having coffee, but I don't feel like going out in the rain. |

The PING rule applies because **like** is followed by the activity as an **-ing** form: **feel like going**, not *feel like to go*.
