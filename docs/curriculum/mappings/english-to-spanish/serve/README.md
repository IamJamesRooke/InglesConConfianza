# English **Serve**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| They served dinner at seven. | Sirvieron la cena a las siete. |
| This key serves no purpose. | Esta llave no sirve para nada. |
| She served five years in prison. | Cumplió cinco años de prisión. |
| Serve the customer first. | Atiende primero al cliente. |

Food and usefulness use **servir**. A sentence or period of service and helping a customer require **cumplir** or **atender**.
