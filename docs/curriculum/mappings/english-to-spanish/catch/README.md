# English **Catch**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| Catch the ball. | Atrapa la pelota. |
| I caught the bus at seven. | Alcancé el bus de las siete. |
| She caught a cold. | Se resfrió. |
| I didn't catch your name. | No entendí tu nombre. |
| I caught him taking the money. | Lo sorprendí tomando el dinero. |

**Catch** can mean capture, reach transport, contract an illness, understand, or discover someone in the act.
