# English **Believe**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| I believe that she is right. | Creo que ella tiene razón. |
| I believe Laura. | Le creo a Laura. |
| I believe in you. | Creo en ti. |
| I believe this is the best option. | Pienso que esta es la mejor opción. |

**Creer que, creerle a alguien**, and **creer en** have different complements. Opinion-softening **I believe** can also become **pienso que**.
