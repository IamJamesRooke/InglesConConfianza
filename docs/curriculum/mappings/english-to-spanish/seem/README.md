# English **Seem**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| You seem tired. | Pareces cansado. |
| It seems that the office is closed. | Parece que la oficina está cerrada. |
| Apparently, they already left. | Al parecer, ya se fueron. |
| I can't seem to open the door. | No logro abrir la puerta. |

Ordinary **seem** uses **parecer**. English impersonal, evidential, and frustrated-attempt patterns often require a complete Spanish restructuring.
