# Whole Expressions with **Back**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Call Back**](01-call-back.md) | Te devuelvo la llamada en cinco minutos. | I'll call you back in five minutes. |
| [**Pay Back**](02-pay-back.md) | Te devolveré el dinero mañana. | I'll pay you back tomorrow. |
| [**Take Back** Meaning Retract](03-take-back-meaning-retract.md) | Retiro lo que dije. | I take back what I said. |
| [**Get Back To**](04-get-back-to.md) | Te responderé mañana. | I'll get back to you tomorrow. |
| [**Get Back To** an Activity](05-get-back-to-an-activity.md) | Volvamos al trabajo. | Let's get back to work. |
| [**Back Up**](06-back-up.md) | Retrocede el carro, respalda a Ana y haz una copia de los archivos. | Back up the car, back Ana up, and back up the files. |
| [**Push Back**](07-push-back.md) | Se opusieron al cambio y aplazaron la reunión. | They pushed back against the change and pushed the meeting back. |
| [**Look Back On**](08-look-back-on.md) | Recuerdo esos años con cariño. | I look back on those years fondly. |
