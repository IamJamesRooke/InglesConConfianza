# Contextual Uses of **Back**

The reusable picture behind **back** is the rear part of something, movement in a rearward direction, or a return to an earlier place, person, state, or action.

| Section | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Core Picture](core-picture/README.md) | La parte de atrás del carro está detrás de nosotros. | The back of the car is behind us. |
| [Return and Rearward Movement](predictable-extensions/README.md) | Regresa, trae la pelota y vuelve a ponerla en la caja. | Come back, bring the ball back, and put it back in the box. |
| [Whole Expressions](whole-expressions/README.md) | Devuélveme la llamada, págame y respalda los archivos. | Call me back, pay me back, and back up the files. |
| [Confusion Sets](confusion-sets/README.md) | Vuelve acá, pero lleva la silla allá. | Come back here, but take the chair back there. |

## Whole-Topic Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mira hacia atrás, da la vuelta y regresa corriendo; te espero en la parte de atrás del edificio. | Look back, turn back, and run back; I'll wait for you at the back of the building. |
| Tráeme el libro, devuélvelo al estante y luego vuelve al trabajo. | Bring the book back to me, put it back on the shelf, and then get back to work. |
| Me devolvió la llamada, me pagó, retiró lo que había dicho y respaldó los archivos. | She called me back, paid me back, took back what she'd said, and backed up the files. |
