# Contextual Uses of **Up** and **Down**

**Up** often suggests higher position, greater intensity, completion, creation, or appearance. **Down** often suggests lower position, reduced intensity, control, or collapse.

| Section | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Core Uses](core-uses/README.md) | Mira arriba y abajo, desde la parte superior hasta el fondo. | Look up and down, from the top to the bottom. |
| [Movement and Position](movement-and-position/README.md) | Levántate, sube corriendo y luego baja caminando. | Stand up, run up, and then walk down. |
| [Completion and Creation](completion-and-creation/README.md) | Termina la comida, limpia y redacta el informe. | Eat up, clean up, and write up the report. |
| [Intensity and Control](intensity-and-control/README.md) | Habla más fuerte, apresúrate, luego reduce la velocidad y cálmate. | Speak up, hurry up, then slow down and calm down. |
| [Whole Expressions](whole-expressions/README.md) | Se presentó, mencionó el problema y no se rindió cuando el sistema falló. | She showed up, brought up the problem, and didn't give up when the system broke down. |
