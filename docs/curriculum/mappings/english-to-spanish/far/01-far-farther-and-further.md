# **Far → Farther/Further → the Farthest/Furthest**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La estación está lejos. | The station is far away. |
| La otra estación está más lejos. | The other station is farther away. |
| Ese es el punto más lejano. | That is the farthest point. |

**Farther** is especially common for physical distance. **Further** is also common for distance and is the usual choice for additional progress or information: **further discussion**.
