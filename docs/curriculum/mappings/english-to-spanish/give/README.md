# Uses of **Give**

English **give** maps to **dar**, more specific **regalar**, and expressions such as **ceder el paso**.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Give Somebody Something](01-give-somebody-something.md) | Le doy las llaves. | I give her the keys. |
| [Give as a Gift](02-give-as-a-gift.md) | Le regalé un libro. | I gave her a book. |
| [Give Way or Permission](03-give-way-or-permission.md) | Cedió el paso. | He gave way. |
