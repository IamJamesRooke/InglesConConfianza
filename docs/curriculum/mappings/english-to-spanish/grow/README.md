# English **Grow**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| The children are growing quickly. | Los niños están creciendo rápido. |
| They grow coffee in Colombia. | Cultivan café en Colombia. |
| The company grew by ten percent. | La empresa aumentó un diez por ciento. |
| I grew tired of waiting. | Me cansé de esperar. |

Natural development uses **crecer**, cultivation **cultivar**, numerical increase **aumentar**, and **grow + adjective** often uses a change verb.
