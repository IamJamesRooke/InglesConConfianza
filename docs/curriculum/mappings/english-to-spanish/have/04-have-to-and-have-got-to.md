# Having: **Have To** and **Have Got To**

## **Have to**

Use **have to + base verb** for an obligation or something that is necessary.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| tener que hacer algo | **to have to do something** |

**Tengo que hacerlo hoy.**<br>
I have to do it today.

## **Have got to**

In the present, **have got to** also expresses obligation. It commonly becomes shorter in speech:

| Form | Use |
|---|---|
| **I have got to do it.** | full form |
| **I've got to do it.** | standard contraction |
| **I gotta do it.** | very informal spoken form |

All three can express:

**Tengo que hacerlo.**

**I've** is the contraction of **I have**. **Gotta** represents the informal spoken reduction of **got to**; use **have got to** or **I've got to** in standard writing.
