# Uses of **Have**

English **have** maps to Spanish **tener, comer, tomar, haber**, or **tener que**, depending on the complete construction.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Possession](01-possession.md) | Tengo una reserva. | I have a reservation. |
| [Food and Drinks](02-food-and-drinks.md) | Como una arepa y me tomo un café. | I have an arepa and a coffee. |
| [Meals](03-meals.md) | Almuerzo al mediodía. | I have lunch at noon. |
| [**Have To / Have Got To**](04-have-to-and-have-got-to.md) | Tengo que hacerlo. | I have to do it / I've got to do it. |
| [Perfect Infinitive](05-perfect-infinitive.md) | Me alegra haber terminado. | I'm glad to have finished. |

The obligation lesson is deliberately retrievable under [**get**](../get/opportunity-and-obligation/06-have-got-to-core-pattern.md) and Spanish [**tener**](../../spanish-to-english/tener/07-have-to-and-have-got-to.md) as well.
