# English **Realize**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| I realized that I was wrong. | Me di cuenta de que estaba equivocado. |
| She realized her goal. | Alcanzó su meta. |
| They made the plan a reality. | Hicieron realidad el plan. |
| The company carried out the project. | La empresa realizó el proyecto. |

English **realize** meaning become aware is **darse cuenta**, not Spanish **realizar**. Achievement and making something real use **alcanzar** or **hacer realidad**.
