# Uses of **As**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**As...As** for an Equal Description](01-as-as-equal-description.md) | Camila es tan paciente como su hermana, incluso con los clientes difíciles. | Camila's as patient as her sister, even with difficult clients. |
| [**As Much As** and **As Many As**](02-as-much-as-and-as-many-as.md) | Tengo tanto trabajo y tantas reuniones como Laura. | I've got as much work and as many meetings as Laura. |
| [**Not As...As**](03-not-as-as.md) | Este informe no es tan claro como el anterior, así que tendremos que revisarlo. | This report isn't as clear as the previous one, so we'll have to revise it. |
| [**Twice, Half**, and **Just As...As**](04-twice-half-and-just-as.md) | El segundo apartamento cuesta el doble, pero es apenas la mitad de grande que el primero. | The second apartment costs twice as much, but it's only half as big as the first one. |
| [**As** for a Real Role or Function](05-as-a-role-or-function.md) | Laura trabaja como médica y usa su teléfono como linterna durante las visitas nocturnas. | Laura works as a doctor and uses her phone as a flashlight during night visits. |
| [**As** for Manner or Shared Information](06-as-for-manner-or-shared-information.md) | Como ya sabes, organizamos los archivos tal como lo pidió el cliente. | As you already know, we organized the files as the client requested. |
| [**As** and **While** for Simultaneous Actions](07-as-and-while-for-simultaneous-actions.md) | Mientras salíamos del edificio, empezó a llover con más fuerza. | As we were leaving the building, it started raining harder. |
| [**As** Meaning **Because**](08-as-meaning-because.md) | Como el banco ya cerró, tendremos que hacer la transferencia mañana. | As the bank's already closed, we'll have to make the transfer tomorrow. |
| [**Such As**](09-such-as.md) | Necesitamos documentos tales como el pasaporte, la visa y el certificado de vacunación. | We need documents such as your passport, visa, and vaccination certificate. |
| [**The Same As**](10-the-same-as.md) | Pedí lo mismo que ellos porque su plato se veía delicioso. | I ordered the same thing as them because their meal looked delicious. |
| [**As...As Possible**](11-as-as-possible.md) | Explícamelo de la manera más clara posible porque tengo que presentarlo mañana. | Explain it to me as clearly as possible because I've gotta present it tomorrow. |
| [**As Soon As**](12-as-soon-as.md) | Te llamaré tan pronto como llegue a la oficina. | I'll call you as soon as I get to the office. |
| [**As Long As**](13-as-long-as.md) | Puedes quedarte el tiempo que quieras, siempre y cuando cierres la puerta al salir. | You can stay as long as you want as long as you lock the door when you leave. |
| [**As If** and **As Though**](14-as-if-and-as-though.md) | Habla como si supiera la respuesta, pero ni siquiera leyó la pregunta. | He talks as if he knew the answer, but he didn't even read the question. |
| [**As Well As**](15-as-well-as.md) | Natalia habla portugués además de inglés, así que puede atender a ambos clientes. | Natalia speaks Portuguese as well as English, so she can help both clients. |
| [**As Far As**](16-as-far-as.md) | Hasta donde sé, el sendero llega hasta el río, pero no pasa de allí. | As far as I know, the trail goes as far as the river, but no farther. |
| [**As For** and **As To**](17-as-for-and-as-to.md) | En cuanto al precio, todavía no hemos tomado una decisión sobre cuánto cobrar. | As for the price, we haven't made a decision as to how much to charge. |
| [**As Of**](18-as-of.md) | A partir de hoy, la oficina cierra a las cinco; hasta ayer, cerraba a las seis. | As of today, the office closes at five; until yesterday, it closed at six. |
| [**As Usual, As Expected**, and **As Planned**](19-as-usual-expected-and-planned.md) | Como de costumbre, Laura llegó temprano y, como estaba previsto, empezó la reunión a las ocho. | As usual, Laura arrived early and, as planned, started the meeting at eight. |
| [**As a Result**](20-as-a-result.md) | El proveedor entregó tarde; como resultado, no pudimos abrir la tienda el lunes. | The supplier delivered late; as a result, we couldn't open the store on Monday. |
| [Pronunciation: **As, Ass**, and **Ask**](21-pronunciation-as-ass-and-ask.md) | Como asistente, pregunta si el burro puede actuar como mascota. | As an assistant, ask whether the ass can act as a pet. |
| [**As Well** Meaning **Too**](22-as-well.md) | Laura va a revisar el contrato y yo también voy a revisarlo. | Laura's going to review the contract, and I'll review it as well. |
| [**Might As Well**](23-might-as-well.md) | Ya que estamos aquí y la fila es corta, mejor entremos de una vez. | Since we're already here and the line's short, we might as well go in. |
| [Future-time **As Soon As**](24-future-time-as-soon-as.md) | Saldré apenas llegue. | I'll leave as soon as she arrives. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Como ya sabes, trabajo como médica y atiendo a tantos pacientes como Laura, pero no trabajo tantas horas como ella. | As you already know, I work as a doctor and see as many patients as Laura, but I don't work as many hours as she does. |
| Como el paciente parecía estar empeorando mientras hablábamos, le pedí al asistente que llamara a la médica lo antes posible. | As the patient seemed to be getting worse as we spoke, I asked the assistant to call the doctor as soon as possible. |
| En cuanto al burro, puede actuar como mascota siempre y cuando sea tan amable como parece; como resultado, pregúntale al asistente lo antes posible. | As for the ass, it can act as a pet as long as it's as gentle as it looks; as a result, ask the assistant as soon as possible. |
