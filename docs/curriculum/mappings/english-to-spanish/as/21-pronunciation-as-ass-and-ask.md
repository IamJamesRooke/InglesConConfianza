# Pronunciation: **As, Ass**, and **Ask**

**As** normally ends in `/z/`; **ass** ends in `/s/`; **ask** adds `/k/`. They aren't homophones.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Como asistente, pregunta si el burro puede actuar como mascota. | As an assistant, ask whether the ass can act as a pet. |

The intentionally playful sentence contrasts **as**, **assistant**, **ask**, and **ass**. **Ass** literally means **burro**, but it can also be an informal insult or a crude word for someone's backside, so use it carefully.
