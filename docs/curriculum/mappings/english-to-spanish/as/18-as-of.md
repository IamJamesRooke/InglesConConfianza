# **As Of**

Use **as of + time** when a fact or change begins, ends, or is measured from that point.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| A partir de hoy, la oficina cierra a las cinco; hasta ayer, cerraba a las seis. | As of today, the office closes at five; until yesterday, it closed at six. |

This expression is especially common in business, administrative, and news contexts.
