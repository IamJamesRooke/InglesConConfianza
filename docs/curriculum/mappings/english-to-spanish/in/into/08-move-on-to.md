# **Move On To**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ya terminamos este punto; pasemos al siguiente tema. | We've finished this point; let's move on to the next topic. |

For progression, **move on to + noun** means proceed to a new topic, stage, or activity. Write **on to** as two words because **on** belongs to **move on**; reserve **onto** for movement to a surface.
