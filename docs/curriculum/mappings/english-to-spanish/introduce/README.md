# Uses of **Introduce**

English **introduce** maps to **presentar** for people and to **introducir, presentar**, or **implementar** for ideas, products, and systems.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Introduce Somebody](01-introduce-somebody.md) | Le presento a Laura a mi equipo. | I introduce Laura to my team. |
| [Introduce a Topic or System](02-introduce-a-topic-or-system.md) | La empresa implementó un sistema nuevo. | The company introduced a new system. |
