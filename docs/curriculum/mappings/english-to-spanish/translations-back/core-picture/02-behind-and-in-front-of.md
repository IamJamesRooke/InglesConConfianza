# **Behind** and **In Front Of**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El jardín está detrás de la casa y el carro está frente a ella. | The garden is behind the house, and the car is in front of it. |

Use **behind + thing/place** and **in front of + thing/place** to relate two positions. Do not say *in back of* for the general opposite of **in front of** in international English; **behind** is clearer.
