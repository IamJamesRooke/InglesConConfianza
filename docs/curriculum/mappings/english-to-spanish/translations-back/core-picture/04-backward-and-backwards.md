# **Backward** and **Backwards**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Dé dos pasos hacia atrás. | Take two steps backward. |

**Backward** and **backwards** mark direction toward the back. **Backwards** is especially common in British English; **backward** is standard in both British and American English.

Only **backward** normally goes before a noun: **a backward movement**, not *a backwards movement*.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mire atrás antes de caminar hacia atrás. | Look back before walking backward. |
