# **Back Up**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Retrocede el carro, respalda a Ana durante la reunión y haz una copia de seguridad de los archivos. | Back up the car, back Ana up during the meeting, and back up the files. |

**Back up** can mean move backward, support someone or a claim, or make a safety copy of data. With an object it is separable, but the object **files/data** commonly follows the complete verb: **back up the files**.
