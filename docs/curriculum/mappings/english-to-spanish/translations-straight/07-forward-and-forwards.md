# **Forward** and **Forwards**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mire hacia adelante y dé dos pasos hacia adelante. | Look forward and take two steps forwards. |

**Forward** and **forwards** mark direction toward the front. **Forwards** is especially common in British English; **forward** is standard in both British and American English.

Only **forward** normally goes before a noun: **a forward movement**, not *a forwards movement*.

For a physical gaze, **look forward** means look ahead. The fixed connection **look forward to** has the different meaning **esperar con ilusión**.
