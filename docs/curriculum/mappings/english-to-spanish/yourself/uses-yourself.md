# Uses of “Yourself”

English uses `yourself` when `you` refers to one person and:

- the person performs an action on the same person;
- the speaker emphasizes that the person does something without assistance;
- the word follows a preposition.

Spanish changes the form according to:

- informal `tú` or formal `usted`;
- grammatical role;
- the gender of the person when `mismo` or `misma` is used.

# Reflexive Actions

## Informal Singular: Te

`Te` represents one person addressed informally as `tú`.

| Full context | Reflexive form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Tú ves a ti mismo. | Tú te ves. | You see yourself. |
| Tú ayudas a ti mismo. | Tú te ayudas. | You help yourself. |
| Tú preguntas a ti mismo. | Tú te preguntas. | You ask yourself. |
| Tú describes a ti mismo. | Tú te describes. | You describe yourself. |

## Formal Singular: Se

`Se` represents one person addressed formally as `usted`.

| Full context | Reflexive form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Usted ve a usted mismo. | Usted se ve. | You see yourself. |
| Usted ayuda a usted mismo. | Usted se ayuda. | You help yourself. |
| Usted pregunta a usted mismo. | Usted se pregunta. | You ask yourself. |
| Usted describe a usted mismo. | Usted se describe. | You describe yourself. |

# Masculine and Feminine Emphasis

The reflexive pronouns `te` and `se` do not indicate gender.

The words `mismo` and `misma` agree with the person.

## Informal Tú

| Reference | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| 👨 | Tú te ves a ti mismo. | You see yourself. |
| 👩 | Tú te ves a ti misma. | You see yourself. |
| 👨 | Tú te ayudas a ti mismo. | You help yourself. |
| 👩 | Tú te ayudas a ti misma. | You help yourself. |

## Formal Usted

| Reference | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| 👨 | Usted se ve a sí mismo. | You see yourself. |
| 👩 | Usted se ve a sí misma. | You see yourself. |
| 👨 | Usted se ayuda a sí mismo. | You help yourself. |
| 👩 | Usted se ayuda a sí misma. | You help yourself. |

The shorter forms are normally sufficient:

- `Te ves.`
- `Se ve.`

The longer forms add emphasis or clarification:

- `Te ves a ti mismo.`
- `Se ve a sí misma.`

# Yourself as Emphasis

English can use `yourself` to emphasize that the person personally performs an action.

Spanish commonly uses `tú mismo`, `tú misma`, `usted mismo` or `usted misma`.

## Informal Tú

| Reference | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| 👨 | Tú mismo lo hiciste. | You did it yourself. |
| 👩 | Tú misma lo hiciste. | You did it yourself. |
| 👨 | Puedes hacerlo tú mismo. | You can do it yourself. |
| 👩 | Puedes hacerlo tú misma. | You can do it yourself. |

## Formal Usted

| Reference | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| 👨 | Usted mismo lo hizo. | You did it yourself. |
| 👩 | Usted misma lo hizo. | You did it yourself. |
| 👨 | Puede hacerlo usted mismo. | You can do it yourself. |
| 👩 | Puede hacerlo usted misma. | You can do it yourself. |

# By Yourself

`By yourself` can mean either alone or without assistance.

## Alone: Solo or Sola

| Reference | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| 👨 informal | Estás solo. | You are by yourself. |
| 👩 informal | Estás sola. | You are by yourself. |
| 👨 formal | Usted está solo. | You are by yourself. |
| 👩 formal | Usted está sola. | You are by yourself. |

# Giving Something to Yourself

## Informal Tú

| Direct object | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| 📕 masculine singular | Te lo das a ti mismo. | You give it to yourself. |
| feminine singular | Te la das a ti mismo. | You give it to yourself. |
| 📚️ masculine plural | Te los das a ti mismo. | You give them to yourself. |
| feminine plural | Te las das a ti mismo. | You give them to yourself. |

For a woman, use `a ti misma`.

## Formal Usted

| Direct object | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|

| feminine singular | Se la da a sí mismo. | You give it to yourself. |
| 📚️ masculine plural | Se los da a sí mismo. | You give them to yourself. |
| feminine plural | Se las da a sí mismo. | You give them to yourself. |

For a woman, use `a sí misma`.

# Minimal Differences

| Use | Informal tú | Formal usted | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| reflexive | Te ves. | Se ve. | You see yourself. |
| emphasized reflexive | Te ves a ti mismo. | Se ve a sí mismo. | You see yourself. |
| emphasis | Tú mismo lo haces. | Usted mismo lo hace. | You do it yourself. |
| for yourself | Para ti mismo. | Para sí mismo. | For yourself. |
| with yourself | Contigo mismo. | Consigo mismo. | With yourself. |
| give it to yourself | Te lo das. | Se lo da. | You give it to yourself. |

# Summary

| Use of “yourself” | Informal tú | Formal usted |
|---|---|---|
| reflexive pronoun | te | se |
| emphasized reflexive | a ti mismo/misma | a sí mismo/misma |
| emphatic subject | tú mismo/misma | usted mismo/misma |
| by yourself | solo/sola; por ti mismo/misma | solo/sola; por sí mismo/misma |
| for yourself | para ti mismo/misma | para sí mismo/misma |
| with yourself | contigo mismo/misma | consigo mismo/misma |
