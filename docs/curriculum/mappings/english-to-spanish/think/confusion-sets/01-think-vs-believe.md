# **Think** and **Believe**

Spanish **pensar** and **creer** can both correspond to **think** when expressing an opinion.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| pensar, creer | **to think** |
| creer | **to believe** |

**Creo que la respuesta es correcta.**<br>
I think the answer is correct.

Use **believe** when emphasizing that you accept something as true:

**Creo que su explicación es correcta.**<br>
I believe her explanation is correct.
