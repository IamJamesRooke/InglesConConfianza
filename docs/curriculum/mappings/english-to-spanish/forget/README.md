# Uses of **Forget**

English **forget** maps to **olvidar** for missing information and often to **dejar** when an object remains somewhere.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Forget Information](01-forget-information.md) | Olvidé su apellido. | I forgot her last name. |
| [Forget Something Somewhere](02-forget-something-somewhere.md) | Dejé las llaves en la oficina. | I forgot the keys at the office. |
