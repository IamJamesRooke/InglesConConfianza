# English **Include**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| The price includes breakfast. | El precio incluye el desayuno. |
| Include Laura in the meeting. | Invita a Laura a la reunión. |
| Ten people came, including Mateo. | Vinieron diez personas, entre ellas Mateo. |

Literal inclusion uses **incluir**; participation and parenthetical **including** often sound more natural with another structure.
