# Uses of **Make**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Make** Versus **Do**](01-make-versus-do.md) | Hice café mientras Laura hacía un favor para el cliente. | I made coffee while Laura did the client a favor. |
| [**Make** a Thing](02-make-a-thing.md) | Preparé la cena e hice una mesa para el balcón. | I made dinner and made a table for the balcony. |
| [**Make Money** and **Make a Living**](03-make-money-and-a-living.md) | Ella gana dinero diseñando sitios web y se gana la vida trabajando desde casa. | She makes money designing websites and makes a living working from home. |
| [**Make Friends**](04-make-friends.md) | Hice muchos amigos cuando empecé el curso. | I made lots of friends when I started the course. |
| [**Make Someone Feel...**](05-make-someone-feel.md) | Esa noticia me hizo muy feliz. | That news made me really happy. |
| [**Make Someone Do Something**](06-make-someone-do.md) | El retraso nos hizo cambiar todos los planes. | The delay made us change all our plans. |
| [**Be Made to Do Something**](07-be-made-to-do.md) | Nos obligaron a esperar afuera durante una hora. | We were made to wait outside for an hour. |
| [**Made Of, Made From**, and **Made By**](08-made-of-from-and-by.md) | La mesa está hecha de madera, el papel está hecho de madera y ambos fueron hechos por Laura. | The table's made of wood, the paper's made from wood, and both were made by Laura. |
| [**Make a Call** and **Make a Reservation**](09-make-a-call-and-reservation.md) | Hice una llamada para hacer una reserva. | I made a call to make a reservation. |
| [**Make a Decision, Suggestion**, and **Promise**](10-make-a-decision-suggestion-and-promise.md) | Tomé una decisión, hice una sugerencia y prometí llamar. | I made a decision, made a suggestion, and made a promise to call. |
| [**Make a Mistake** and **Make an Effort**](11-make-a-mistake-and-an-effort.md) | Cometí un error, pero hice un esfuerzo por corregirlo. | I made a mistake, but I made an effort to correct it. |
| [**Make Progress** and **Make a Difference**](12-make-progress-and-a-difference.md) | Hemos progresado mucho, y practicar todos los días ha marcado una diferencia. | We've made lots of progress, and practicing every day has made a difference. |
| [**Make Sure**](13-make-sure.md) | Asegúrate de guardar el archivo antes de cerrar. | Make sure you save the file before you close it. |
| [**Make Sense**](14-make-sense.md) | La explicación tiene sentido, pero el precio no. | The explanation makes sense, but the price doesn't. |
| [**Make It**](15-make-it.md) | No podré llegar a la reunión, pero espero que el equipo tenga éxito. | I can't make it to the meeting, but I hope the team makes it. |
| [**Make Do**](16-make-do.md) | No tenemos una mesa, así que tendremos que arreglarnos con estas cajas. | We don't have a table, so we'll have to make do with these boxes. |
| [**Make Up**](17-make-up.md) | Inventó una excusa, luego hizo las paces con Laura y juntos formaron la mitad del equipo. | He made up an excuse, made up with Laura, and together they made up half the team. |
| [Homophones: **Made** and **Maid**](18-homophones-made-and-maid.md) | La empleada hizo café y se aseguró de que estuviera bien hecho. | The maid made coffee and made sure it was well made. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Hice café, hice una llamada, tomé una decisión y me aseguré de no cometer ningún error. | I made coffee, made a call, made a decision, and made sure I didn't make any mistakes. |
| El retraso nos hizo cambiar de plan, pero nos las arreglamos, progresamos y logramos llegar. | The delay made us change our plans, but we made do, made progress, and made it. |
| La empleada hizo una mesa de madera y aseguró que el trabajo bien hecho marcaría una diferencia. | The maid made a table made of wood and made sure the well-made work would make a difference. |
