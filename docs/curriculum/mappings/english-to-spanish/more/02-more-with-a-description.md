# **More + Description**

Use **more + description** when one quality has a higher degree.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La segunda explicación es más útil que la primera. | The second explanation's more helpful than the first one. |
| Esta opción es más importante que la otra. | This option is more important than the other one. |

Some short descriptions change form instead: **clearer**, not normally *more clear*.
