# English **Own**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| I have my own office. | Tengo mi propia oficina. |
| She owns the apartment. | Es dueña del apartamento. |
| He did it on his own. | Lo hizo solo. |
| Own up to the mistake. | Admite el error. |

Adjective **own**, possession as a verb, independence, and **own up** use **propio, ser dueño de, solo**, or **admitir**.

## Owner and ownership

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ana es la dueña de la tienda. | Ana is the owner of the store. |
| Ana es propietaria de la tienda. | Ana owns the store. |
| Conserva la propiedad total del negocio. | She retains full ownership of the business. |

**Own** is the verb for possessing something, **owner** is the person or organization that possesses it, and **ownership** is the state or legal right of possession. Spanish chooses among **ser dueño/a de, poseer, ser propietario/a de**, and **propiedad** according to the sentence.
