# Uses of **But** and **Yet**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**But** Meaning However](01-but-meaning-however.md) | Quería acompañarlos, pero no pude salir de la oficina a tiempo. | I wanted to join you, but I couldn't leave the office in time. |
| [**But** Meaning Except](02-but-meaning-except.md) | Todos entregaron el informe a tiempo menos Andrés, que lo envió al día siguiente. | Everyone but Andrés submitted the report on time; he sent his the next day. |
| [**Not X but Y**](03-not-x-but-y.md) | El problema no es el precio, sino el tiempo que tardará la entrega. | The problem isn't the price but the time the delivery will take. |
| [Spanish **Sino** Versus **Si No**](04-spanish-sino-versus-si-no.md) | No llamaremos hoy, sino mañana. | We won't call today but tomorrow. |
| [Sentence-Final **Though**](../though/14-sentence-final-though.md) | El apartamento es pequeño. Sin embargo, tiene mucha luz natural. | The apartment is small. It has plenty of natural light, though. |
| [Contrasting **Yet**](../yet/06-contrasting-yet.md) | El apartamento es pequeño, pero tiene suficiente espacio para una familia de cuatro personas. | The apartment is small, yet it has enough space for a family of four. |
| [Temporal **Yet**](../yet/07-temporal-yet.md) | —¿Ya terminaste el informe? —No, todavía no lo he terminado. | “Have you finished the report yet?” “No, I haven't finished it yet.” |
| [**Not Yet**](../yet/08-not-yet.md) | El cliente todavía no ha firmado el contrato, pero esperamos recibirlo esta tarde. | The client hasn't signed the contract yet, but we expect to receive it this afternoon. |
| [**Have Yet to**](../yet/09-have-yet-to.md) | La empresa aún no ha explicado cómo protegerá los datos de los usuarios. | The company has yet to explain how it will protect users' data. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Todavía no he terminado, pero ya terminé todo menos el final. | I haven't finished yet, yet I've finished everything but the ending. |
