# English **Look**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| Look at the screen. | Mira la pantalla. |
| You look tired. | Te ves cansado. |
| I am looking for my keys. | Estoy buscando mis llaves. |
| Laura looks after her grandmother. | Laura cuida a su abuela. |
| I look forward to seeing you. | Tengo muchas ganas de verte. |

Visual **look** uses **mirar**. Appearance and the frequent combinations **look for**, **look after**, and **look forward to** require different Spanish structures.
