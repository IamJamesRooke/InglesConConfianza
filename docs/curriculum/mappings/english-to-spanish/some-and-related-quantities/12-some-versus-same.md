# **Some** Versus **Same**

**Some** means an unspecified amount or number; **same** means identical or unchanged.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Algunos estudiantes dieron la misma respuesta de siempre. | Some students gave the same answer as always. |

They aren't homophones: **some** has `/ʌ/`; **same** has `/eɪ/`.
