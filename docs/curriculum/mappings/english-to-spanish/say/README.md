# English **Say**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| She said that the meeting was canceled. | Dijo que cancelaron la reunión. |
| Say hello to your mother. | Saluda a tu mamá. |
| The sign says “Closed.” | El letrero dice «Cerrado». |
| They said a prayer before dinner. | Rezaron antes de cenar. |

**Say** usually becomes **decir**, but greetings, prayers, signs, and other fixed contexts often choose a more specific Spanish verb.
