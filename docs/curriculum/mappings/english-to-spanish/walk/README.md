# English **Walk**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| I walk to work. | Camino al trabajo. |
| We walked around downtown. | Anduvimos por el centro. |
| I walk the dog every morning. | Saco a pasear al perro cada mañana. |
| I walked Laura home. | Acompañé a Laura a su casa. |

Walking oneself, wandering, exercising a dog, and accompanying someone require different Spanish verbs.

## Particle paths

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| walk into | entrar caminando |
| walk away | alejarse caminando |

**I walk to work when the weather is good. → Camino al trabajo cuando hace buen tiempo.**

The particle changes the path or result: **walk into** enters a place, while **walk away** leaves it.
