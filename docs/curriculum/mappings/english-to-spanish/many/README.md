# English **Many**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| Many people came. | Vino mucha gente. / Vinieron muchas personas. |
| How many chairs do we need? | ¿Cuántas sillas necesitamos? |
| Many of them agreed. | Muchos de ellos estuvieron de acuerdo. |
| A great many students work at night. | Muchísimos estudiantes trabajan de noche. |

**Many** may become singular collective **mucha gente**, plural **muchos/muchas**, interrogative **cuántos**, or an intensifier such as **muchísimos**.
