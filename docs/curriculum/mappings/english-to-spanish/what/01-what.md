# **What** for Open Information

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿Qué quieres? | What do you want? |
| ¿Qué libro necesitas? | What book do you need? |
| ¿A qué hora empieza? | At what time does it start? |

Use **what** for an open question or an open category. English can use **what + noun** when the question asks for a type or identity.
