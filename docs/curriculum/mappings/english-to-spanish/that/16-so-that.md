# **So...That**

In **so + description + that + result**, **so** expresses the degree and **that** introduces its consequence.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Estaba tan cansada que se quedó dormida en el bus. | She was so tired that she fell asleep on the bus. |

In conversation, result **that** can sometimes disappear: **She was so tired she fell asleep on the bus.**
