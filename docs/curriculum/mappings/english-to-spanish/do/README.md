# English **Do**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| I do my homework after dinner. | Hago la tarea después de cenar. |
| Laura does the dishes. | Laura lava los platos. |
| Mateo is doing well at work. | A Mateo le está yendo bien en el trabajo. |
| Diana did her hair before the interview. | Diana se arregló el pelo antes de la entrevista. |
| Do you work here? | ¿Trabajas aquí? |

Lexical **do** often becomes **hacer**, but natural combinations may require another verb. Auxiliary **do** normally has no separate Spanish word.
