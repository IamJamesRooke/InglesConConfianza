# Move Someone or Something **Around**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Llevó a los visitantes por la oficina y movió las sillas por el salón. | She took the visitors around the office and moved the chairs around the room. |

Core verbs such as **take, push, pull**, and **throw** can combine compositionally with **around** when context supplies a real object and path: **push the cart around**, **pull the wagon around**, or **throw the ball around**. Not every verb + **around** combination is a separate expression to memorize.
