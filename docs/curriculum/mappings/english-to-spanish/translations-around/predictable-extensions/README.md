# Core-Verb Movement with **Around**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Walk, Run**, and **Drive Around**](01-walk-run-and-drive-around.md) | Caminamos, corrimos y condujimos por el parque. | We walked, ran, and drove around the park. |
| [**Look** and **Turn Around**](02-look-and-turn-around.md) | Mira a tu alrededor y luego date la vuelta. | Look around and then turn around. |
| [Move Something Around](03-move-something-around.md) | Llevó a los visitantes por la oficina y movió las sillas. | She took the visitors around the office and moved the chairs around. |

## Phrasal-Verb Note

These combinations keep the physical picture of **around**. In **turn around** and separable forms such as **move the chairs around / move them around**, **around** acts as a particle.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
