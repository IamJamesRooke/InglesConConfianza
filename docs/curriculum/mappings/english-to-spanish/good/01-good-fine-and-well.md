# **Good**, **Fine**, and **Well**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El café está bueno. | The coffee is good. |
| Estoy bien. | I am fine. |
| Me siento bien hoy. | I feel good today. |
| Laura hace bien su trabajo. | Laura does her work well. |

Use **good** for a person, thing, state, or experience. Use **well** for how an action happens. After **feel**, both **good** and **well** are possible: **feel good** emphasizes a positive state, while **feel well** often emphasizes health.
