# Spanish Translations of English **Put**

English **put** covers placement, expression, writing, investment, assignment, and several high-frequency particle meanings.

| Lesson | English | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| [**Put → Poner**](01-placement.md) | Put the keys on the table. | Pon las llaves sobre la mesa. |
| [**Put → Expresar / Decir**](02-express-or-word.md) | I don't know how to put it politely. | No sé cómo expresarlo con cortesía. |
| [**Put In Writing → Poner Por Escrito**](03-put-in-writing.md) | Please put the agreement in writing. | Por favor, pon el acuerdo por escrito. |
| [**Put → Invertir / Meter**](04-invest.md) | They put all their savings into the business. | Invirtieron todos sus ahorros en el negocio. |
| [**Put → Asignar / Poner**](05-assign-or-place.md) | They put Ana in charge of the team. | Pusieron a Ana a cargo del equipo. |
| [**Put Up With → Aguantar / Tolerar**](06-put-up-with.md) | I won't put up with that noise. | No voy a aguantar ese ruido. |
| [**Put Off → Posponer / Desanimar**](07-put-off.md) | They put off the meeting, and his attitude put me off. | Pospusieron la reunión y su actitud me desanimó. |
| [Other Particles with **Put**](08-other-particles.md) | Put on your coat and put out the fire. | Ponte el abrigo y apaga el incendio. |
