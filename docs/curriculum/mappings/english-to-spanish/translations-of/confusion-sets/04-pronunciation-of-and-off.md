# Pronunciation: **Of** and **Off**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Quitó una de las etiquetas. | She took one of the labels off. |

These words are not homophones. **Of** normally has a voiced `/v/` and an unstressed vowel: `/əv/` or simply `/v/` in connected speech. **Off** ends in unvoiced `/f/` and has a strong vowel: `/ɔf/` or `/ɑf/`, depending on accent.
