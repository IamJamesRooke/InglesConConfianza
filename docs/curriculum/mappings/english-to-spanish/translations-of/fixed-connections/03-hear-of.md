# **Hear Of**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Había oído hablar de la empresa, pero no conocía los detalles. | I'd heard of the company, but I didn't know the details. |

**Hear of** means know that someone or something exists. Compare [**hear about**](../../translations-about/confusion-sets/02-hear-about-vs-hear-of.md) for receiving news or details.
