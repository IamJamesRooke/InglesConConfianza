# Pronunciation: **Ever, Never**, and **Every**

**Ever** has two syllables, **never** adds `/n/`, and **every** normally has two syllables in natural speech. None has a common homophone.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Toda persona inteligente que he conocido nunca dice “nunca jamás”. | Every clever person I've ever met never says “never ever.” |

The playful repetition contrasts **every**, **ever**, **never**, and **clever** without treating them as homophones.
