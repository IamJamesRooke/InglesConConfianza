# **To, Too**, and **Two**

> **I want to go to the store to buy two tools, but I'm too used to going to the closer one to want to walk that far.**

The three words **to**, **too** and **two** share the strong pronunciation `/tuː/`, but they do completely different work.

| Word | Core jobs | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **to** | full infinitive, purpose, destination, recipient, relationship | **to go**, **to buy**, **to the store**, **to me** |
| **too** | excess or also | **too far**, **me too** |
| **two** | the number 2 | **two tools** |

## The Central Contrast

In one sentence, identical-looking **to** sounds can perform different jobs:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Quiero ir a la tienda para comprar algo. | I want **to go** **to the store** **to buy** something. |

1. **want to go**: **to** builds the full infinitive;
2. **to the store**: prepositional **to** points to a destination;
3. **to buy**: the full infinitive expresses purpose.

The difference becomes visible when another verb follows:

- full-infinitive **to + base verb**: **I want to go**;
- prepositional **to + verb-ing** under the PING rule: **I'm used to going**.

## Three Folder-Level Mastery Sentences

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Quiero ir a la tienda para comprar dos herramientas, pero estoy demasiado acostumbrado a ir a la más cercana como para querer caminar tan lejos. | I want to go to the store to buy two tools, but I'm too used to going to the closer one to want to walk that far. |
| Los dos sastres también quieren aprender a coser dos tutús, pero están demasiado cansados para hacerlo hoy. | The two tailors want to learn to sew two tutus too, but they're too tired to do it today. |
| Antes solía conducir al trabajo, pero me acostumbré a ir caminando y ahora también quiero que mis dos compañeros se acostumbren a hacerlo. | I used to drive to work, but I got used to walking, and now I want my two coworkers to get used to doing it too. |

Together, these sentences contrast full-infinitive **to**, bare infinitives, purpose, destination, prepositional **to + -ing**, **used to**, **get used to**, excess **too**, agreement **too**, and the number **two**.
