# **Be Hungry** and **Get Hungry**

English uses **be hungry** for tener hambre and **get hungry** for empezar a tener hambre.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No tenía hambre antes, pero me dio hambre y ahora tengo mucha hambre. | I wasn't hungry before, but I got hungry, and now I'm really hungry. |

Don't translate **tener hambre** with **have**: say **I'm hungry**, not **I have hunger**.
