# **Get In** and **Get Into** a Car

Use **get in** without a named object and **get into + vehicle/place** when entering a car, taxi, or similarly enclosed vehicle.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Súbete al carro; tenemos que irnos ya. | Get in the car; we've gotta leave now. |

**Get into the car** is equally correct and emphasizes movement from outside to inside.
