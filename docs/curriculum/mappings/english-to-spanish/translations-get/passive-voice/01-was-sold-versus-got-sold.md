# **It Was Sold** Versus **It Got Sold**

Both forms can describe a sale without naming the seller, but **get + past participle** often makes the event or change of state feel more dynamic and conversational.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La casa estuvo en venta durante meses; finalmente se vendió ayer y ahora está vendida. | The house was for sale for months; it finally got sold yesterday, and now it's sold. |

- **It was sold yesterday.** — neutral description of the completed event.
- **It got sold yesterday.** — conversational focus on the fact that the sale finally happened.

The compact contrast is: **They sold it, they got it sold, it was sold, and it got sold.**
