# Uses of **Drive**

English **drive** maps to **manejar/conducir** for a vehicle and to **impulsar** or **llevar a** when something causes movement or change.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Driving a Vehicle](01-driving.md) | Manejo al trabajo. | I drive to work. |
| [Driving a Process](02-drive-a-process.md) | La demanda impulsa el crecimiento. | Demand drives growth. |
