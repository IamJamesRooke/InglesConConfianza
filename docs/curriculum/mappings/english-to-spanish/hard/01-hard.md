# **Hard** as Adjective and Adverb

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El examen es difícil. | The test is hard. |
| Trabajó con esfuerzo. | She worked hard. |

As an adjective, **hard** often means **difícil**. As an adverb, it means with a lot of effort: **work hard**. It can also describe a physically firm or solid thing: **a hard chair**.
