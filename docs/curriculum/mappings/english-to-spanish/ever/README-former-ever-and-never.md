# Uses of **Ever** and **Never**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Have You Ever...?**](01-have-you-ever.md) | ¿Alguna vez has viajado sola a otro país? | Have you ever traveled alone to another country? |
| [**If You Ever...**](02-if-you-ever.md) | Si alguna vez necesitas ayuda con el contrato, llámame. | If you ever need help with the contract, call me. |
| [**Don't Ever** and **Never**](03-dont-ever-and-never.md) | Nunca vuelvas a compartir tu contraseña con nadie. | Don't ever share your password with anyone again. |
| [**Never** for Zero Frequency](../never/04-never-for-zero-frequency.md) | Nunca conduzco cuando estoy demasiado cansada. | I never drive when I'm too tired. |
| [**Almost Never** and **Hardly Ever**](05-almost-never-and-hardly-ever.md) | Casi nunca cocinamos entre semana porque llegamos tarde a casa. | We hardly ever cook during the week because we get home late. |
| [**Never Again** and **Never Ever**](../never/06-never-again-and-never-ever.md) | Nunca volveré a reservar ese hotel; jamás volveré a quedarme allí. | I'll never book that hotel again; I'm never ever staying there again. |
| [**The Best Ever**](07-the-best-ever.md) | Esta es la mejor clase que hemos tenido. | This is the best class we've ever had. |
| [**More Than Ever**](08-more-than-ever.md) | Después de perder los archivos, necesitamos copias de seguridad más que nunca. | After losing the files, we need backups more than ever. |
| [**Ever Since**](09-ever-since.md) | Desde que tuve el accidente, siempre he tomado el bus. | I've taken the bus ever since I had the accident. |
| [**Forever**](10-forever.md) | No podemos esperar eternamente; llevamos horas aquí. | We can't wait forever; we've been here for hours. |
| [**Happily Ever After**](11-happily-ever-after.md) | El cuento termina diciendo que vivieron felices para siempre. | The story ends by saying that they lived happily ever after. |
| [**Ever-Growing** and **Ever-Changing**](12-ever-growing-and-ever-changing.md) | Tenemos que adaptarnos a una lista de requisitos que crece y cambia constantemente. | We've gotta adapt to an ever-growing, ever-changing list of requirements. |
| [**Whoever**](13-whoever.md) | Quien haya dejado este paquete debe recogerlo antes de las cinco. | Whoever left this package has to pick it up before five. |
| [**Whatever**](14-whatever.md) | Escoge lo que quieras; yo comeré lo mismo que tú. | Choose whatever you want; I'll have the same thing as you. |
| [**Whenever**](15-whenever.md) | Llámame cuando quieras; casi nunca apago el teléfono. | Call me whenever you want; I hardly ever turn my phone off. |
| [**Wherever**](16-wherever.md) | Dondequiera que trabajes, guarda siempre una copia de tus archivos. | Wherever you work, always keep a copy of your files. |
| [**Whichever**](17-whichever.md) | Puedes tomar cualquiera de los dos buses; ambos paran frente al banco. | You can take whichever bus comes first; both stop in front of the bank. |
| [**However** Meaning **Nevertheless**](../however/18-however-meaning-nevertheless.md) | El apartamento es pequeño; sin embargo, tiene mucha luz natural. | The apartment's small; however, it's got plenty of natural light. |
| [**However** Meaning **No Matter How**](../however/19-however-meaning-no-matter-how.md) | Por muy difícil que parezca el ejercicio, no te rindas. | However difficult the exercise seems, don't give up. |
| [**Never Mind**](../never/20-never-mind.md) | —¿Qué querías preguntarme? —No importa; ya encontré la respuesta. | “What did you wanna ask me?” “Never mind; I've already found the answer.” |
| [**Never** at the Beginning](../never/21-never-at-the-beginning.md) | Nunca había visto un error causar tantos problemas. | Never had I seen one mistake cause so many problems. |
| [Pronunciation: **Ever, Never**, and **Every**](22-pronunciation-ever-never-and-every.md) | Toda persona inteligente que he conocido nunca dice “nunca jamás”. | Every clever person I've ever met never says “never ever.” |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| —¿Alguna vez has tenido el mejor día de tu vida? —Nunca, pero ahora estoy más feliz que nunca. | “Have you ever had the best day ever?” “Never, but I'm happier than ever now.” |
| Quienquiera que te diga lo contrario, no vuelvas jamás a compartir tu contraseña, por muy confiable que parezca. | Whoever tells you otherwise, don't ever share your password again, however trustworthy they seem. |
| Desde que conocí a Laura, puedo llamarla cuando quiera, dondequiera que esté, y casi nunca dice: “No importa”. | Ever since I met Laura, I can call her whenever I want, wherever she is, and she hardly ever says, “Never mind.” |
