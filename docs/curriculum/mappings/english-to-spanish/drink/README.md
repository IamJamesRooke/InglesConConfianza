# Uses of **Drink**

English **drink** maps to Spanish **beber** or **tomar**, while conversational English may replace it with **have** around food and beverages.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Drink Something](01-drink-something.md) | Bebo agua y me tomo un café. | I drink water and have a coffee. |

See also [food and drinks with **have**](../have/02-food-and-drinks.md).
