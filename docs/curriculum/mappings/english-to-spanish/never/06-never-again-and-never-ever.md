# **Never Again** and **Never Ever**

**Never again** rejects any repetition. Informal **never ever** adds emotional emphasis.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Nunca volveré a reservar ese hotel; jamás volveré a quedarme allí. | I'll never book that hotel again; I'm never ever staying there again. |

**Never ever** is natural in speech and informal writing, but it can sound dramatic.
