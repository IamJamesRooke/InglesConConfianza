# **Tomorrow** and the **Day After Tomorrow**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| mañana | tomorrow |
| pasado mañana | the day after tomorrow |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La reunión es mañana. | The meeting is tomorrow. |
| La reunión es pasado mañana. | The meeting is the day after tomorrow. |

English adds **the day after** to express the day immediately following tomorrow.
