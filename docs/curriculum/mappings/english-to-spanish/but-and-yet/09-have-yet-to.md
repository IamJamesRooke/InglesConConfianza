# **Have Yet to**

The more formal structure **have yet to + base verb** means that something has not happened up to now.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La empresa aún no ha explicado cómo protegerá los datos de los usuarios. | The company has yet to explain how it will protect users' data. |

This means **the company hasn't explained it yet**, but the structure is more common in formal reporting.
