# **Likely**

**Likely** means probable.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Es probable que el vuelo se retrase por la tormenta. | The flight is likely to be delayed because of the storm. |

Common patterns are:

- **something is likely to happen**
- **it is likely that something will happen**
- informal **something will likely happen**

Despite containing **like**, **likely** does not mean similar. Use **A is like B** or **A and B are alike** for similarity.
