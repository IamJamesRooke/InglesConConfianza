# Uses of **Up To** and **Down On**

The combinations **up to** and **down on** preserve vertical imagery in some uses, but many meanings must be learned as complete connections.

| Section | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Uses of **Up To**](README.md) | Depende de ti; pueden asistir hasta veinte personas. | It's up to you; up to twenty people can attend. |
| [Uses of **Down On**](../../down/down-on/README.md) | No menosprecies a los principiantes. | Don't look down on beginners. |
| [Confusion Sets](confusion-sets/README.md) | Admiro a Ana, pero ella menosprecia a Carlos. | I look up to Ana, but she looks down on Carlos. |

## Whole-Topic Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Depende de ti: podemos pagar hasta cien mil pesos, pero asegúrate de que el trabajo cumpla con nuestras normas. | It's up to you: we can pay up to one hundred thousand pesos, but make sure the work is up to our standards. |
| —¿Qué estás haciendo? —Estoy poniéndome al día con el trabajo que no alcancé a terminar ayer. | “What are you up to?” “I'm catching up on the work I didn't get to finish yesterday.” |
| Admiro a mi jefa porque nunca menosprecia a los principiantes ni los critica por sus errores. | I look up to my boss because she never looks down on beginners or comes down on them for their mistakes. |
