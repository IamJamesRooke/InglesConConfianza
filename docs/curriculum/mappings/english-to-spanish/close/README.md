# Uses of **Close**

The **close** family maps to **cerrar**, **clausurar**, and nouns such as **cierre** or **clausura**, depending on what ends.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Close Something](01-close-something.md) | Cierro la ventana. | I close the window. |
| [Close Down](02-close-down.md) | Cerraron la fábrica. | They closed down the factory. |
| [Closing](03-closing.md) | El cierre de la tienda. | The store closing. |
