# English **Toward**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| Walk toward the door. | Camina hacia la puerta. |
| The money goes toward the project. | El dinero se destina al proyecto. |
| Her attitude toward work changed. | Su actitud frente al trabajo cambió. |

Direction uses **hacia**, contribution **destinar a**, and an attitude or relationship often **frente a** or **hacia**.
