# English **Follow**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| Follow me. | Sígueme. |
| Follow the instructions. | Sigue las instrucciones. |
| I don't follow your argument. | No entiendo tu argumento. |
| The presentation was followed by questions. | Después de la presentación hubo preguntas. |
| Follow through on your promise. | Cumple tu promesa. |

Movement and instructions use **seguir**. Understanding, sequence, and completing a commitment often require restructuring.
