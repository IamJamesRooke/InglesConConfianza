# Uses of **Stay**

English **stay** maps to **quedarse** for remaining somewhere, **alojarse/hospedarse** for accommodation, and **mantenerse/seguir** for continuing in a condition.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Remain in a Place](01-remain-in-a-place.md) | Me quedo en la oficina. | I stay in the office. |
| [Stay at a Hotel](02-stay-at-a-hotel.md) | Nos alojamos en un hotel. | We stay at a hotel. |
| [Stay in a Condition](03-stay-in-a-condition.md) | Manténgase tranquilo. | Stay calm. |
