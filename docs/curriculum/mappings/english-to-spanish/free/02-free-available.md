# **Free** Meaning Available or Not Busy

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Estoy libre después de la clase. | I am free after the class. |
| Diana está disponible esta tarde. | Diana is free this afternoon. |

For a person who can meet or work, **free** means that the person is not busy. **Available** is often clearer; Spanish can use **libre** or **disponible**.
