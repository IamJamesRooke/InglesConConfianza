# **If I Were**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Si yo fuera usted, no lo haría; si fuera a hacerlo, llamaría primero. | If I were you, I wouldn't do it; if I were going to do it, I'd call first. |

Use **were** for formal or careful hypothetical meaning with every person. Informal **if I was** occurs, but **if I were you** is the standard fixed form.
