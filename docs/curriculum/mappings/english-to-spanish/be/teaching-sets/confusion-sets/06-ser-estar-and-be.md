# Spanish **Ser / Estar** and English **Be**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ana es tranquila, pero hoy está muy callada. | Ana is calm, but she's very quiet today. |

English **be** does not preserve the Spanish verb choice. The adjective and context must communicate identity, characteristic, state, location, or change.

The same collapse happens in the past:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| fui, era; estuve, estaba | **I was** |
| fue, era; estuvo, estaba | **he/she/it was** |
| fuiste, eras; estuviste, estabas | **you were** |
| fuimos, éramos; estuvimos, estábamos | **we were** |
| fueron, eran; estuvieron, estaban | **you/they were** |

**La reunión fue útil y estuve allí toda la mañana.**<br>
The meeting was useful, and I was there all morning.

**Las oficinas estaban abiertas.**<br>
The offices were open.
