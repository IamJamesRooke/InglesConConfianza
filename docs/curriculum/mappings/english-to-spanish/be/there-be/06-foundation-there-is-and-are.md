# **To Be**: **There Is** and **There Are**

Use **there is** or **there are** to say that something exists or is present.

Spanish uses **hay** for both singular and plural nouns. English changes the form to agree with the noun.

## Singular

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| hay + singular | **there is + singular** |

**Hay un libro sobre la mesa.**<br>
There is a book on the table.

## Plural

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| hay + plural | **there are + plural** |

**Hay tres libros sobre la mesa.**<br>
There are three books on the table.

In speech, **there is** commonly contracts to **there's**:

**There's a book on the table.**
