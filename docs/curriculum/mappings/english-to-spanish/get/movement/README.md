# **Get** and Movement

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Get To** a Place](01-get-to-a-place.md) | Llegamos al aeropuerto antes de que cerraran la puerta. | We got to the airport before they closed the gate. |
| [**Get Here, There**, and **Home**](02-get-here-there-and-home.md) | Llegué aquí, llegué allá y finalmente llegué a casa. | I got here, got there, and finally got home. |
| [**Get Up**](03-get-up.md) | Me desperté a las seis, pero no me levanté hasta las seis y media. | I woke up at six, but I didn't get up until six thirty. |
| [**Get Down**](04-get-down.md) | Bájate de la mesa y agáchate. | Get down from the table and get down. |
| [**Get In/Into** a Car](05-get-in-and-into-a-car.md) | Súbete al carro; tenemos que irnos. | Get in the car; we've gotta leave. |
| [**Get Out Of** a Car](06-get-out-of-a-car.md) | Nos bajamos del taxi frente al hotel. | We got out of the taxi in front of the hotel. |
| [**Get On/Onto** Public Transport](07-get-on-and-onto-public-transport.md) | Subimos al avión después de montar en el bus equivocado. | We got on the plane after getting on the wrong bus. |
| [**Get Off** Public Transport](08-get-off-public-transport.md) | Nos bajamos del bus y luego del tren. | We got off the bus and then got off the train. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me levanté, subí al carro, llegué al aeropuerto, subí al avión y bajé en Medellín. | I got up, got into the car, got to the airport, got on the plane, and got off in Medellín. |
| Llegué aquí, llegué allá y llegué a casa sin usar **to**. | I got here, got there, and got home without using **to**. |
