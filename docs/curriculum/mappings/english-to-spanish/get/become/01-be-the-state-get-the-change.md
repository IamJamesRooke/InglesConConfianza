# **Be** the State; **Get** the Change

Use **be + adjective** for a state and **get + adjective** for the transition into it.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Antes era pobre, luego me volví rico y ahora soy rico. | I was poor, then I got rich, and now I'm rich. |

The reusable timeline is **wasn't X → got X → am X**. **Get** doesn't replace every use of **be**; it replaces the moment of becoming.
