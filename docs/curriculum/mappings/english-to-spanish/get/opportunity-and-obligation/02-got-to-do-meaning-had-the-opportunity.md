# **Got To Do**: Had the Opportunity

The past of opportunity **get to** is **got to**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ayer tuve la oportunidad de conocer a mi cantante favorita y hablar con ella. | Yesterday I got to meet my favorite singer and talk to her. |

The time context makes the completed opportunity clear: **Last year, we got to visit Cartagena.**
