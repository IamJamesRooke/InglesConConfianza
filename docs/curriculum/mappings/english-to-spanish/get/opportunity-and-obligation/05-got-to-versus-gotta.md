# **I Got To Do It** Versus **I Gotta Do It**

These phrases look and sound very similar, but the clearest standard contrast is opportunity versus obligation.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ayer tuve la oportunidad de hacerlo; hoy me toca hacerlo. | Yesterday I got to do it; today I gotta do it. |

- **I got to do it.** — I had the opportunity to do it.
- **I gotta do it.** — I have to do it.

In careful speech, opportunity is often **got tə**, while obligation may compress into **gotta**. Real casual pronunciation can overlap, so context matters. The tongue twister fixes the meanings: **Yesterday I got to do it; today I gotta do it.**
