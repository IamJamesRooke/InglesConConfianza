# Opportunity, Possession, and Obligation

This section keeps the easily confused forms **get to**, **got to**, **have got**, **have got to**, and **gotta** together.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Get To Do**: Opportunity](01-get-to-do-meaning-have-the-opportunity.md) | En este trabajo puedo viajar y conocer a muchas personas. | In this job, I get to travel and meet lots of people. |
| [**Got To Do**: Past Opportunity](02-got-to-do-meaning-had-the-opportunity.md) | Ayer tuve la oportunidad de conocer a mi cantante favorita. | Yesterday I got to meet my favorite singer. |
| [**Have** and **Have Got**](03-have-and-have-got-for-possession.md) | Tengo dos entradas, así que podemos ver el concierto. | I've got two tickets, so we get to see the concert. |
| [**Have To, Have Got To**, and **Gotta**](04-have-to-have-got-to-and-gotta.md) | Tengo que terminar esto hoy. | I've gotta finish this today. |
| [**Got To** Versus **Gotta**](05-got-to-versus-gotta.md) | Ayer tuve la oportunidad de hacerlo; hoy me toca hacerlo. | Yesterday I got to do it; today I gotta do it. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tengo una entrada, tuve la oportunidad de ir ayer y hoy me toca ir otra vez. | I've got a ticket, I got to go yesterday, and I gotta go again today. |
| Tengo una entrada, pero tengo que conseguir otra. | I've got a ticket, but I've gotta get another one. |
