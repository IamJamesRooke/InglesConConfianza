# **Did It** Versus **Got It Done**

An active verb says the subject performed the action. **Get + thing + past participle** says the subject caused or arranged the result.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ellos vendieron el carro directamente, pero consiguieron que un agente vendiera la casa. | They sold the car themselves, but they got the house sold by an agent. |

- **They sold it.** — they performed the sale.
- **They got it sold.** — they made sure it was sold; someone else may have performed the sale.
- **They got it done.** — they made sure the unspecified task was completed.

The reusable form is **get + thing + past participle**: **get it sold, get it sent, get it fixed**.
