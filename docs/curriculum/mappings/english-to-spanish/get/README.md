# Uses of **Get**

Use this page for a fast review of the entire **get** concept map. A learner who can explain and produce every contrast below is ready to revisit only the section where something feels uncertain.

| Section | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Obtain](obtain/README.md) | Recibí el mensaje, compré las entradas y obtuve el trabajo. | I got the message, got the tickets, and got the job. |
| [Movement](movement/README.md) | Llegué al aeropuerto, subí al avión, bajé en Medellín y llegué al hotel. | I got to the airport, got on the plane, got off in Medellín, and got to the hotel. |
| [Become](become/README.md) | Antes era pobre y tenía energía; luego me hice rico, me cansé y oscureció. | I was poor and energetic; then I got rich, got tired, and it got dark. |
| [Phrasal Verbs](phrasal-verbs/README.md) | Superé la ruptura, escapé, sobreviví con poco dinero y me llevé bien con todos. | I got over the breakup, got away, got by on little money, and got along with everyone. |
| [Causative](causative/README.md) | Me aseguraré de hacerlo yo o de que quede listo. | I'll make sure I do it or that it gets done. |
| [Passive Voice](passive-voice/README.md) | Ellos lo vendieron; fue vendido y finalmente se vendió. | They sold it; it was sold, and it finally got sold. |
| [Opportunity and Obligation](opportunity-and-obligation/README.md) | Ayer tuve la oportunidad de hacerlo; hoy me toca hacerlo. | Yesterday I got to do it; today I gotta do it. |
| [Everyday Replacements](everyday-replacements/README.md) | Al principio no lo entendía, luego lo entendí y te lo haré llegar mañana. | I didn't get it at first, then I got it, and I'll get it to you tomorrow. |

## Whole-Topic Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Llegué al teatro, me dio hambre y por fin entendí el chiste. | I got to the theater, got hungry, and finally got the joke. |
| Ayer tuve la oportunidad de arreglarlo; hoy me toca asegurarme de que quede listo y hacértelo llegar. | Yesterday I got to fix it; today I gotta get it done and get it to you. |
| Lo contrataron cuando se mejoró, se llevaba bien con todos y sobrevivía con poco; después se hizo rico. | He got hired when he got better, got along with everyone, and got by on little; then he got rich. |

## Final Diagnostic

The learner should be able to answer these without translating word by word:

1. What are the forms **get, got**, and **gotten**, and how do they sound?
2. When does **get** mean receive, buy, or acquire?
3. Which movement patterns use **to**, and which omit it?
4. How does **get + state** differ from **be + state**?
5. What meanings do the particles add in **get over, along, away**, and **by**?
6. Who acts in **I fixed it, I got it fixed**, and **it got fixed**?
7. How does **it got sold** differ from **it was sold**?
8. Why are **I got to do it** and **I gotta do it** different?
9. How can **I got it** and **I'll get it to you** replace more specific verbs?
