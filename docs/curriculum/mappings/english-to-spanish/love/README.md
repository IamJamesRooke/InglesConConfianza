# English **Love**

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| I love my family. | Amo a mi familia. / Quiero mucho a mi familia. |
| I love this song. | Me encanta esta canción. |
| I would love to go. | Me encantaría ir. |
| Love, Laura | Con cariño, Laura |

Deep personal affection may use **amar** or **querer**, strong liking **encantar**, and letter closings a phrase such as **con cariño**.
