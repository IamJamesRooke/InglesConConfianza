# Whole Expressions with **On** and **Off**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Come On**](01-come-on.md) | ¡Vamos! No me digas que no estás listo. | Come on! Don't tell me you're not ready. |
| [**Go On**](02-go-on.md) | Sigue; cuéntame qué pasó. | Go on—tell me what happened. |
| [**Go Off**](../off/03-go-off.md) | La alarma se activó y la leche se dañó. | The alarm went off, and the milk went off. |
| [**Take On**](04-take-on.md) | Aceptó más trabajo y se enfrentó al campeón. | She took on more work and took on the champion. |
| [**Put On / Put Off**](../off/05-put-on-and-put-off.md) | Fingió una sonrisa, me quitó las ganas y aplazamos la reunión. | She put on a smile, put me off, and we put off the meeting. |
| [**Show Off / Tell Off**](../off/06-show-off-and-tell-off.md) | Presumió y el jefe lo regañó. | He showed off, and the boss told him off. |
| [**Call Off / Cut Off**](../off/07-call-off-and-cut-off.md) | Cancelaron la reunión porque se cortó la electricidad. | They called off the meeting because the electricity was cut off. |
| [**Back Off / Log On / Log Off**](../off/08-back-off-and-log-on-or-off.md) | Le dije que se alejara, inicié sesión y luego la cerré. | I told him to back off, logged on, and then logged off. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¡Vamos, sigue! La alarma se activó, pero no presumas ni me regañes. | Come on, go on! The alarm went off, but don't show off or tell me off. |
| Aceptamos el trabajo, aplazamos la reunión y luego la cancelamos porque se cortó la electricidad. | We took on the work, put off the meeting, and then called it off because the power was cut off. |
