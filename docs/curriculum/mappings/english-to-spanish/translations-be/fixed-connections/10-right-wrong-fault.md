# **Be Right, Be Wrong**, and **Be at Fault**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tienes razón, yo estoy equivocado y el error fue culpa mía. | You're right, I'm wrong, and the error was my fault. |

Spanish uses **tener razón** and **equivocarse**; English uses **be right/wrong**. For responsibility, use **be someone's fault** or **be at fault**.
