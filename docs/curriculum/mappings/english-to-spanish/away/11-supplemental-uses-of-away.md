# Uses of **Away**

**Away** resembles **way** in spelling, but it usually expresses movement or distance from a reference point, absence, removal, or disappearance.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Movement Away](01-movement-away.md) | Camila se alejó de la mesa. | Camila walked away from the table. |
| [Distance](02-distance-away.md) | Queda a cinco minutos de aquí. | It's five minutes away from here. |
| [Absence](03-be-away.md) | Ana está de viaje. | Ana is away. |
| [Right Away](04-right-away.md) | Hazlo de inmediato. | Do it right away. |
| [Put Away](05-put-away.md) | Guarda los documentos. | Put the documents away. |
| [Take Away](06-take-away.md) | Retire los platos. | Take the plates away. |
| [Throw Away](07-throw-away.md) | No botes el recibo. | Don't throw the receipt away. |
| [Give Away](08-give-away.md) | Voy a regalar la chaqueta. | I'm going to give the jacket away. |
| [Run Away](09-run-away.md) | El perro se escapó. | The dog ran away. |
| [Toward vs Away From](10-toward-vs-away-from.md) | Empuje hacia la pared, no en dirección contraria. | Push toward the wall, not away from it. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ana está de viaje, pero antes de irse guardó los documentos, botó los recibos viejos y me pidió que respondiera de inmediato. | Ana is away, but before she left, she put the documents away, threw the old receipts away, and asked me to respond right away. |
