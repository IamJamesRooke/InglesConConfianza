# **Would Rather + Different Subject**

When someone prefers another person to act, use **would rather + subject + past form** for a present or future situation.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Preferiría que Laura dirigiera la reunión de mañana porque conoce mejor al cliente. | I would rather Laura led tomorrow's meeting because she knows the client better. |

Although **led** looks past, the meeting is tomorrow. Do not use the same-subject pattern *I would rather Laura lead* in the standard structure taught here.
