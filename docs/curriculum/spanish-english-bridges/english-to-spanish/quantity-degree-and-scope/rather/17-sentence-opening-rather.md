# Sentence-Opening **Rather**

In formal English, **rather** can reject a previous conclusion and introduce the opposite or more accurate interpretation.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La caída de las ventas no demuestra que el producto haya fracasado; más bien, indica que necesitamos cambiar la estrategia de publicidad. | The drop in sales does not prove that the product has failed; rather, it indicates that we need to change the advertising strategy. |

Here **rather** means **más bien**, **por el contrario** or **en cambio**, not **bastante**.
