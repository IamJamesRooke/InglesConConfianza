# Uses of **Less** and **Least**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Less + Description**](01-less-with-a-description.md) | El segundo examen fue menos difícil que el primero. | The second exam was less difficult than the first one. |
| [**The Least + Description**](02-the-least-with-a-description.md) | De los tres exámenes, el segundo fue el menos difícil. | Of the three exams, the second was the least difficult. |
| [**Less** With an Amount](03-less-with-an-amount.md) | Hoy hay menos tráfico y menos ruido que ayer. | There's less traffic and less noise today than yesterday. |
| [**Fewer** With More Than One Thing](04-fewer-with-more-than-one-thing.md) | Hoy hay menos carros y menos buses en la avenida. | There are fewer cars and fewer buses on the avenue today. |
| [**The Fewest**](05-the-fewest.md) | Esta ruta tiene el menor número de carros durante la hora pico. | This route has the fewest cars during rush hour. |
| [**Less Of**](06-less-of.md) | Leí menos del libro de lo que había planeado. | I read less of the book than I'd planned. |
| [**More or Less**](07-more-or-less.md) | El informe está más o menos listo; solo falta revisar una tabla. | The report's more or less ready; we've only gotta check one table. |
| [**At Least**](08-at-least.md) | Necesitamos por lo menos tres horas, pero al menos ya tenemos los datos. | We need at least three hours, but at least we've already got the data. |
| [**Unless**](09-unless.md) | No firmes el contrato a menos que entiendas todas las condiciones. | Don't sign the contract unless you understand all the terms. |
| [**Much Less** and **Far Less**](10-much-and-far-less.md) | La segunda opción es mucho menos costosa y bastante menos riesgosa. | The second option's much less expensive and far less risky. |
| [**Less and Less**](11-less-and-less.md) | Cada semana tengo menos tiempo para cocinar. | I've got less and less time to cook each week. |
| [**The Less..., the More...**](12-the-less-the-more.md) | Cuanto menos duermo, más café necesito. | The less I sleep, the more coffee I need. |
| [**Less Than** With Numbers](13-less-than.md) | La reparación tardó menos de dos horas. | The repair took less than two hours. |
| [**No Less Than**](14-no-less-than.md) | La reunión duró nada menos que cinco horas. | The meeting lasted no less than five hours. |
| [**Much Less** Meaning **Let Alone**](15-much-less-meaning-let-alone.md) | No tengo tiempo para leer el informe, mucho menos para reescribirlo. | I don't have time to read the report, much less rewrite it. |
| [**Couldn't Care Less**](16-couldnt-care-less.md) | No me importa en absoluto lo que él piense de mi decisión. | I couldn't care less what he thinks about my decision. |
| [Pronunciation: **Less** and **Lest**](17-pronunciation-less-and-lest.md) | Lleva menos equipaje para evitar perder el último bus. | Take less luggage lest you miss the last bus. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Hay menos tráfico y menos carros, así que esta es la ruta menos difícil y la que tiene menos carros. | There's less traffic and there are fewer cars, so this is the least difficult route with the fewest cars. |
| Cuanto menos duermo, menos trabajo termino y más o menos cinco tareas quedan sin hacer. | The less I sleep, the less work I finish, and more or less five tasks remain undone. |
| No tengo tiempo para leer el informe, mucho menos para reescribirlo, a menos que tarde menos de una hora. | I don't have time to read the report, much less rewrite it, unless it takes less than an hour. |
