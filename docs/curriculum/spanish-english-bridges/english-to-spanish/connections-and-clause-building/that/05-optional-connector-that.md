# Optional Connector **That**

In everyday English, connector **that** is often omitted.

| With **that** | Without **that** |
|---|---|
| **I think that Laura is right.** | **I think Laura is right.** |
| **She said that she'd call.** | **She said she'd call.** |
| **We know that it works.** | **We know it works.** |

Keep **that** when it makes a long sentence easier to read or prevents the first words of the new idea from being mistaken for part of the earlier phrase.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El gerente confirmó que los cambios que solicitó el cliente estarán listos mañana. | The manager confirmed that the changes the client requested will be ready tomorrow. |

The first **that** clearly opens the confirmed idea; the second relative **that** has been omitted after **changes**.
