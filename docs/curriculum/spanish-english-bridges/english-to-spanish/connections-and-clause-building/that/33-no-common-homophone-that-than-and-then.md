# No Common Homophone: **That, Than**, and **Then**

**That** has no common standard-English homophone. The useful sound-and-meaning contrast is with **than** and **then**:

| Word | Job | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **that** | points or connects | **That book is the one that I need.** |
| **than** | comparison | **That book is better than this one.** |
| **then** | time or next step | **That book was cheaper then.** |

> **That was better than this then, but this is better than that now.**
