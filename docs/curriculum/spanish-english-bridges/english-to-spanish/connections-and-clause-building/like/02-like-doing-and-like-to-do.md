# **Like Doing** and **Like To Do**

Both forms are possible, but they often emphasize slightly different ideas.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me gusta caminar porque disfruto estar al aire libre. | I like walking because I enjoy being outdoors. |
| Me gusta revisar dos veces las cifras antes de enviar un informe. | I like to check the figures twice before I send a report. |

**Like doing** naturally emphasizes enjoyment of the activity. **Like to do** can emphasize a preferred habit, choice or practice. Context matters more than treating this as an absolute rule.
