# **Likelihood, Probability**, and **Probably**

**Likelihood** and **probability** are nouns meaning **probabilidad**. **Likelihood** is common in general judgments; **probability** is also the standard technical or mathematical term. **Probably** is the natural form inside an ordinary prediction.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La probabilidad de lluvia es alta, así que probablemente lloverá esta tarde. | The likelihood of rain is high, so it'll probably rain this afternoon. |

Compare:

- **Rain is likely.**
- **There is a high likelihood/probability of rain.**
- **It'll probably rain.**
