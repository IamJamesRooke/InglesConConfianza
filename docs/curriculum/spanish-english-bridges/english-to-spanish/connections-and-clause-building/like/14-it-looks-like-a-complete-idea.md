# **It Looks Like + Complete Idea**

**It looks like + complete idea** means **parece que** based on visible evidence.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Parece que va a llover porque el cielo está completamente oscuro. | It looks like it's going to rain because the sky is completely dark. |

Related forms use different evidence:

- **It sounds like the neighbors are home.**
- **It feels like the temperature is dropping.**
- **It seems like we're missing a page.**
