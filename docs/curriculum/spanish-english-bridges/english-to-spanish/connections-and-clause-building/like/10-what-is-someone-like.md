# **What Is Someone Like?**

Use **what is someone like?** for personality or general character.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| —¿Cómo es la nueva gerente? —Es paciente, directa y muy organizada. | “What's the new manager like?” “She's patient, direct, and very organized.” |

Compare:

- **What does she look like?** → physical appearance;
- **What is she like?** → personality or general character;
- **How is she?** → her current condition or well-being.
