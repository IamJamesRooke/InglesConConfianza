# **Way to Go!**

In informal American English, **Way to go!** expresses enthusiastic approval.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Terminaste tu primera presentación completa en inglés sin mirar las notas; ¡muy bien hecho! | You finished your first complete presentation in English without looking at your notes; way to go! |

This is praise, not a route or a method. Do not confuse it with **a way to go**, which describes a route or remaining progress.
