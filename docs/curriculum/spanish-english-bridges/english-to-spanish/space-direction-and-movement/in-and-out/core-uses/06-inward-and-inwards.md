# **Inward** and **Inwards**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La puerta se abre hacia adentro. | The door opens inward. |

**Inward** marks direction toward the inside or center. **Inwards** has the same directional meaning and is especially common in British English; **inward** is standard in both British and American English.

Only **inward** normally goes before a noun: **an inward movement**, not *an inwards movement*.

Compare the base position and the direction:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mire adentro y luego dé un paso hacia adentro. | Look inside, and then step inward. |
