# **At the Beginning** and **In the Beginning**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El primero de enero celebramos el Año Nuevo. | On January 1, we celebrated the New Year. |
| Al principio del año fijamos nuestras metas. | At the beginning of the year, we set our goals. |
| Durante los primeros meses del año, trabajamos en la primera meta. | In the first few months of the year, we worked on the first goal. |
| Al comienzo, no sabíamos qué plan funcionaría. | In the beginning, we didn't know which plan would work. |

**At the beginning of + noun** identifies the starting edge or early point of something specific. It can cover a short opening phase, so **at the beginning of the year** does not necessarily mean exactly January 1.

**In the beginning** means initially and normally stands alone; it views the situation during its early phase and contrasts it with what happened later. English does not normally say *in the beginning of the year* for January through March. Use **early in the year** or **in the first few months of the year** for that span.

The related pattern is **in the middle of** for a surrounding central part and **at the end of** for a finishing edge. **In the end** has a separate meaning: finally or after everything was considered.
