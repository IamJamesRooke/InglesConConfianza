# **At, In**, or **On**: Point, Interior, or Surface

| Viewpoint | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| general point or activity | Estoy en la oficina para una reunión. | I'm at the office for a meeting. |
| physically inside | Estoy dentro de la oficina. | I'm in the office. |
| touching a surface | El aviso está en la puerta. | The sign is on the door. |

Do not translate Spanish **en** mechanically. Ask whether the English picture is a point or activity (**at**), an interior (**in**), or a surface (**on**).
