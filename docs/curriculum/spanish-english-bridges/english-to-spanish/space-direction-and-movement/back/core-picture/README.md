# The Core Picture of **Back**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [The Back and the Front](01-the-back-and-the-front.md) | La entrada está en la parte delantera y el jardín está en la parte de atrás. | The entrance is at the front, and the garden is at the back. |
| [**Behind** and **In Front Of**](02-behind-and-in-front-of.md) | El jardín está detrás de la casa y el carro está frente a ella. | The garden is behind the house, and the car is in front of it. |
| [**Be Back**](03-be-back.md) | Ya regresé y Ana regresará pronto. | I'm back, and Ana will be back soon. |
| [**Backward** and **Backwards**](04-backward-and-backwards.md) | Dé dos pasos hacia atrás. | Take two steps backward. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ya regresé: estoy en la parte de atrás de la casa, detrás del carro, no en la entrada de adelante. | I'm back: I'm at the back of the house, behind the car, not at the front entrance. |
