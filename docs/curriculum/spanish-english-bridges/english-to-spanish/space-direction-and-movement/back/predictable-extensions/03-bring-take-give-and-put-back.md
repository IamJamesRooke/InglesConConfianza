# **Bring, Take, Give**, and **Put Back**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tráeme el libro, llévalo a la biblioteca, devuélveselo a Ana y ponlo otra vez en el estante. | Bring the book back to me, take it back to the library, give it back to Ana, and put it back on the shelf. |

**Bring back** returns something toward the speaker; **take back** carries it away to an earlier place. **Give back** returns possession, and **put back** returns something to its earlier position.
