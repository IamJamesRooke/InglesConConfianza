# **Be Confused** and **Get Confused**

**Be confused** describes confusion. **Get confused** means become confused or confundirse.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Entendía al principio, luego me confundí y ahora estoy confundido. | I understood at first, then I got confused, and now I'm confused. |

Keep the sound contrast clear: **I was confused because the confusing instructions confused me.**
