# **Get Here, There**, and **Home**

Don't use **to** before **here, there**, or **home**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Llegué aquí a las ocho, llegué allá al mediodía y llegué a casa después de las seis. | I got here at eight, got there at noon, and got home after six. |

Say **get here / there / home**, not *get to here / to there / to home*.
