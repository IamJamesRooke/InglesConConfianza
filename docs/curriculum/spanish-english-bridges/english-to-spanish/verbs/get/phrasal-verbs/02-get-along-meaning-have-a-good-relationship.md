# **Get Along**: Have a Good Relationship

**Get along with someone** means have a friendly or peaceful relationship with that person.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mi hermano y yo no siempre estamos de acuerdo, pero nos llevamos muy bien. | My brother and I don't always agree, but we get along really well. |

Include **with** before the person: **I get along with her**. Without the person, **We get along** is complete.
