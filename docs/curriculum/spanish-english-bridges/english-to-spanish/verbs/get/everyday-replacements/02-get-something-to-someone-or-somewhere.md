# **Get Something To Someone** or Somewhere

**Get + thing + to + recipient/place** means make sure the thing reaches its destination.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Te enviaré el informe mañana y haré que los originales lleguen a la oficina antes del viernes. | I'll get the report to you tomorrow and get the originals to the office by Friday. |

**Send** names one method. **Get it to you** emphasizes successful delivery and may involve sending it, carrying it, or asking someone else to deliver it.

Keep the two meanings together: **I got it, and I'll get it to you tomorrow.**
