# **Used To, Be Used To**, and **Get Used To**

These three near-identical forms describe a past pattern, a current accustomed state, and the process of becoming accustomed.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Antes conducía, estoy acostumbrada al tráfico y me estoy acostumbrando a ir en bus. | I used to drive, I'm used to traffic, and I'm getting used to taking the bus. |
