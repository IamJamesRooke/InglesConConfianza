# Uses of **Mean**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Mean** Meaning Signify](01-mean-meaning-signify.md) | ¿Qué significa esta palabra? | What does this word mean? |
| [**Mean** Meaning Intend](02-mean-meaning-intend.md) | Quería llamarte, pero se me olvidó. | I meant to call you, but I forgot. |
| [**Mean + -ing**](03-mean-doing.md) | Aceptar el trabajo significaría mudarme a otra ciudad. | Taking the job would mean moving to another city. |
| [**Mean for Someone to Do Something**](04-mean-someone-to-do.md) | Quería que Laura leyera el mensaje, no Carlos. | I meant for Laura to read the message, not Carlos. |
| [**Be Meant To**](05-be-meant-to.md) | Esta herramienta está diseñada para cortar vidrio y se supone que debemos usar guantes. | This tool's meant to cut glass, and we're meant to wear gloves. |
| [**I Mean**](06-i-mean.md) | Nos vemos el jueves; quiero decir, el viernes. | I'll see you Thursday—I mean, Friday. |
| [**Mean** Meaning Unkind](07-mean-meaning-unkind.md) | Fue muy cruel burlarse de él por su error. | It was really mean to make fun of him for his mistake. |
| [**Mean** Meaning Average](08-mean-meaning-average.md) | El promedio de 2, 4 y 6 es 4. | The mean of 2, 4, and 6 is 4. |
| [**Means** Meaning Method](09-means-meaning-method.md) | El correo electrónico es un medio rápido de comunicación. | Email is a quick means of communication. |
| [**Means** Meaning Resources](10-means-meaning-resources.md) | La familia no tiene los medios para pagar el tratamiento. | The family doesn't have the means to pay for the treatment. |
| [**By Means Of**](11-by-means-of.md) | Abrieron la puerta por medio de una llave electrónica. | They opened the door by means of an electronic key. |
| [**By All Means**](12-by-all-means.md) | —¿Puedo usar esta silla? —Claro que sí. | “Can I use this chair?” “By all means.” |
| [**By No Means**](13-by-no-means.md) | El proyecto no está terminado de ninguna manera. | The project's by no means finished. |
| [**A Means to an End**](14-a-means-to-an-end.md) | Para él, ese trabajo es solo un medio para alcanzar una meta. | For him, that job's just a means to an end. |
| [**Meaning**](15-meaning.md) | No entiendo el significado de esa expresión. | I don't understand the meaning of that expression. |
| [**Meaningful** and **Meaningless**](16-meaningful-and-meaningless.md) | Tuvimos una conversación significativa, pero la encuesta produjo datos sin sentido. | We had a meaningful conversation, but the survey produced meaningless data. |
| [**Meanwhile**](17-meanwhile.md) | Laura llamó al cliente; mientras tanto, yo corregí el informe. | Laura called the client; meanwhile, I corrected the report. |
| [Homophones: **Mean** and **Mien**](18-homophones-mean-and-mien.md) | No quería parecer cruel, pero su expresión seria hizo que lo pareciera. | He didn't mean to seem mean, but his serious mien made him look mean. |

## Mastery Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿Qué significa esta palabra? Quería buscar su significado, pero eso significaría usar otro diccionario. | What does this word mean? I meant to look up its meaning, but that would mean using another dictionary. |
| No quería ser cruel; quiero decir que su expresión seria no significa que sea una persona cruel. | I didn't mean to be mean; I mean, his serious mien doesn't mean he's a mean person. |
| El promedio no es de ninguna manera insignificante: es un medio para entender los datos; mientras tanto, Laura toma una decisión significativa. | The mean is by no means meaningless: it's a means of understanding the data; meanwhile, Laura's making a meaningful decision. |
