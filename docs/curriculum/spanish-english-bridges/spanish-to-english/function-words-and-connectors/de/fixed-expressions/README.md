# Fixed Expressions with **De**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [Time Expressions](01-time-expressions.md) | Trabajo de noche y descanso de vez en cuando. | I work at night and rest from time to time. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| De noche estudiaba de pie, repetía todo de memoria y, de vez en cuando, empezaba de nuevo. | At night she studied standing up, repeated everything by heart, and, from time to time, started again. |
