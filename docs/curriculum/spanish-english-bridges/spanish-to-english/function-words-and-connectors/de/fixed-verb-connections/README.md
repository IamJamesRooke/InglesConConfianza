# Fixed Verb Connections with **De**

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Acabar De → Have Just**](01-acabar-de.md) | Acabo de llegar. | I've just arrived. |
| [**Dejar De → Stop**](02-dejar-de.md) | Deja de hacer eso. | Stop doing that. |
| [**Depender De → Depend On**](03-depender-de.md) | Depende de Ana. | It depends on Ana. |
| [**Acordarse De → Remember**](04-acordarse-de.md) | Me acordé de la reunión. | I remembered the meeting. |
| [**Darse Cuenta De → Realize**](05-darse-cuenta-de.md) | Me di cuenta del error. | I realized the mistake. |
| [**Tener Ganas De**](06-tener-ganas-de.md) | Tengo ganas de salir. | I feel like going out. |

## Mastery Phrase

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Acabo de darme cuenta de que dejé de depender de Ana porque recordé cómo hacerlo y tengo ganas de intentarlo. | I've just realized that I stopped depending on Ana because I remembered how to do it, and I feel like trying. |
