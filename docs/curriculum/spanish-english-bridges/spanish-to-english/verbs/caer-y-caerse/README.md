# Translations of Spanish **Caer** and **Caerse**

Spanish **caer** and **caerse** cover falling, dropping, dates, realization, impressions of people, and fixed changes of state.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [**Caer / Caerse → Fall**](01-fall.md) | Se cayó de la bicicleta. | She fell off the bicycle. |
| [**Caerse → Drop / Fall**](02-drop.md) | Se me cayó el vaso. | I dropped the glass. |
| [**Caer → Fall** on a Date](03-land-on-a-date.md) | Este año, mi cumpleaños cae en lunes. | This year, my birthday falls on a Monday. |
| [**Caer En Cuenta → Realize**](04-realize.md) | Caí en cuenta de que estaba equivocado. | I realized that I was wrong. |
| [**Caer Bien / Mal → Like / Dislike**](05-like-or-dislike-someone.md) | Ana me cae bien, pero su jefe me cae mal. | I like Ana, but I don't like her boss. |
| [**Caer Bien / Mal → Make an Impression**](06-make-an-impression.md) | El nuevo gerente cayó bien en el equipo. | The new manager made a good impression on the team. |
| [Fixed States with **Caer**](07-fall-ill-or-asleep.md) | Cayó enfermo después del viaje. | He fell ill after the trip. |
| [**Caerle Algo A Alguien → Suit / Agree With**](08-caerle-algo-a-alguien.md) | La comida pesada me cae mal. | Heavy food doesn't agree with me. |
