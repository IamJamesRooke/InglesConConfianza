# **Meet, Introduce**, and **Know**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Carlos me presentó a Ana; la conocí ayer y ahora ya la conozco. | Carlos introduced me to Ana; I met her yesterday, and now I know her. |

**Presentar a alguien** is **introduce someone**: a third person creates the contact. **Conocer por primera vez** is **meet**. After the relationship exists, **conocer a alguien** is **know someone**.
