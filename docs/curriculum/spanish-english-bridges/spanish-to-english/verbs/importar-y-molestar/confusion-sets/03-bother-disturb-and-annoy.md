# **Bother, Disturb**, and **Annoy**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Perdón por interrumpirte; solo quería decirte que el ruido me está fastidiando. | Sorry to disturb you; I just wanted to say that the noise is annoying me. |

**Disturb** emphasizes interruption, **annoy** emphasizes an unpleasant reaction, and **bother** can cover either meaning more generally.
