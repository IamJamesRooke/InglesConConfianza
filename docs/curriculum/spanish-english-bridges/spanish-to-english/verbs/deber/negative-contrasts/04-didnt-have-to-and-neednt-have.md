# **Didn't Have To** and **Needn't Have**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No tuve que pagar porque Ana ya había pagado, pero pagué otra vez y no era necesario. | I didn't have to pay because Ana had already paid, but I paid again and needn't have done so. |

**Didn't have to** says there was no necessity and leaves the completed action unclear. **Needn't have + past participle** says the action happened but was unnecessary; this form is more common in British English.
