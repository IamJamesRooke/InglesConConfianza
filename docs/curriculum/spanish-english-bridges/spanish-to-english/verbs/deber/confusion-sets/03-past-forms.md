# Past Forms of **Deber**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ayer tuve que llamar, debí haber llamado antes, Ana debe haber llamado después y Carlos debía llamar a las diez. | Yesterday I had to call, I should have called earlier, Ana must have called later, and Carlos was supposed to call at ten. |

Use **had to** for past necessity, **should have** for past advice or regret, **must have** for a conclusion about the past, and **was supposed to** for a past expectation.
