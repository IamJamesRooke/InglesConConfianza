# **Deber Algo → Owe**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Le debo cincuenta mil pesos a Ana y se los pagaré mañana. | I owe Ana fifty thousand pesos, and I'll pay her back tomorrow. |

Use **owe + person + thing/amount** or **owe + thing + to + person** for a debt: **I owe Ana money / I owe money to Ana**.
