# **Hope** and **Wish**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Espero que venga mañana; ojalá ya estuviera aquí. | I hope she comes tomorrow; I wish she were already here. |

Use **hope** for a possible future outcome. Use **wish** for an unreal, unlikely, or contrary-to-fact situation. In careful English, **wish I were** is traditional; **wish I was** is common informally.
