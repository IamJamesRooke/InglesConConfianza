# Spanish–English Bridges

These lessons make language-specific distinctions explicit when Spanish and English organize the same idea differently. They are not secondary enrichment: they prevent predictable interference that structure and ordinary vocabulary study cannot resolve by themselves.

| Area | Direction or purpose |
|---|---|
| [Spanish to English](spanish-to-english/README.md) | Choose an English form from the meaning and function of a Spanish word or construction. |
| [English to Spanish](english-to-spanish/README.md) | Use Spanish contrasts to expose the contextual meanings of English words and constructions. |
| [False Friends and Interference](false-friends-and-interference/README.md) | Correct misleading similarities and recurring transfer errors. |

Direction identifies the learner-facing explanation, not a separate course. All three areas support English production and comprehension by Spanish-speaking learners.
