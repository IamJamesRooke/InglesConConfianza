# **To Be**: Present Forms and Contractions

The present forms of **be** are **am**, **is**, and **are**.

| Subject | Full form | Contraction |
|---|---|---|
| I | **I am** | **I'm** |
| you | **you are** | **you're** |
| he | **he is** | **he's** |
| she | **she is** | **she's** |
| it | **it is** | **it's** |
| we | **we are** | **we're** |
| they | **they are** | **they're** |

The contraction has the same meaning as the full form:

**Estoy listo.**<br>
I am ready.<br>
I'm ready.

Use contractions naturally in conversation and informal writing. Use full forms when formality or emphasis makes them more appropriate.
