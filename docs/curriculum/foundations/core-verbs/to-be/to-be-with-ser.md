# **To Be** with **Ser**

Use English **be** where Spanish uses **ser** for identity, classification, origin, general characteristics, and time.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ser | **to be** |
| soy | **am** |
| es | **is** |
| eres, somos, son | **are** |

## Identity or classification

**Soy profesora de inglés.**<br>
I am an English teacher.

## Origin

**Somos de Bogotá.**<br>
We are from Bogotá.

## General characteristics

**El curso es práctico.**<br>
The course is practical.

## Time

**Son las ocho.**<br>
It is eight o'clock.
