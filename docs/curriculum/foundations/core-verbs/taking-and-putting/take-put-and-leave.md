# Taking and Putting: **Take**, **Put**, and **Leave**

## **Take**

Use **take** when you pick up, select, or carry something.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| tomar | **to take** |

**Tomo las llaves de la mesa.**<br>
I take the keys from the table.

## **Put**

Use **put** when you move something into a position or place.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| poner | **to put** |

**Pongo las llaves en mi bolso.**<br>
I put the keys in my bag.

## **Leave something**

Use **leave + something** when you let it remain in a place.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| dejar algo | **to leave something** |

**Dejo las llaves en la recepción.**<br>
I leave the keys at reception.
