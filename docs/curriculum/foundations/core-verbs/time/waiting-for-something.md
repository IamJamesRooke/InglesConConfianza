# Time: Waiting for Something

Use **wait for + somebody or something**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| esperar a alguien, esperar algo | **to wait for somebody or something** |

**Espero el autobús frente a la oficina.**<br>
I wait for the bus in front of the office.

Keep **for** before the thing or person:

**Wait for the bus.**<br>
Not: *Wait the bus.*
