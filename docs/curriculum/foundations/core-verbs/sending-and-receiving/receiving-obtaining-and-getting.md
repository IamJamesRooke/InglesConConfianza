# Sending and Receiving: Receiving, Obtaining, and Getting

English **get** can correspond to **recibir**, **obtener**, or **conseguir**, depending on the context.

## Receiving something

Use **receive** or the more conversational **get** when something arrives or someone gives it to you.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| recibir algo | **to receive something**, **to get something** |

**Recibo el documento por correo electrónico.**<br>
I receive the document by email.

**I get the document by email** expresses the same core idea more conversationally.

## Obtaining something

Use **obtain** or **get** when you acquire something. **Obtain** is more formal.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| obtener algo | **to obtain something**, **to get something** |

**Obtengo una copia del documento en la recepción.**<br>
I obtain a copy of the document at reception.

**I get a copy at reception** is more common in everyday conversation.

## Getting something successfully

Spanish **conseguir** often emphasizes finding, securing, or succeeding in obtaining something.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| conseguir algo | **to get something** |

**Consigo una cita para el viernes después de llamar varias veces.**<br>
I get an appointment for Friday after calling several times.
