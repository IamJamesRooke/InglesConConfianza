# Sending and Receiving: Sending Something

Use **send** when you cause a message or object to go to another person or place.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| enviar algo | **to send something** |

**Le envío el documento a Natalia por correo electrónico.**<br>
I send the document to Natalia by email.

You can put the receiver before or after the thing:

**I send Natalia the document.**<br>
**I send the document to Natalia.**
