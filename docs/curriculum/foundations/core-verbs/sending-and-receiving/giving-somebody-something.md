# Sending and Receiving: Giving Somebody Something

Use **give** when a person transfers something directly to another person.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| darle algo a alguien | **to give somebody something** |

**Le doy las llaves a Natalia.**<br>
I give Natalia the keys.

You can put the receiver before or after the thing:

**I give Natalia the keys.**<br>
**I give the keys to Natalia.**
