# Thinking and Feeling: Knowing and Understanding

## Knowing information

Use **know** for information already present in your mind.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| saber algo | **to know something** |

**Sé la respuesta.**<br>
I know the answer.

## Understanding information

Use **understand** when the meaning is clear to you.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| entender algo | **to understand something** |

**Entiendo la explicación.**<br>
I understand the explanation.

In informal conversation, **get** can also mean **understand**:

**Entiendo la idea.**<br>
I get the idea.
