# Thinking and Feeling: Remembering and Forgetting

## **Remember**

Use **remember** when information remains in or returns to your mind.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| recordar, no olvidar | **to remember** |

**Recuerdo el nombre de la profesora.**<br>
I remember the teacher's name.

## **Forget**

Use **forget** when information does not remain in your mind.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| olvidar | **to forget** |

**A veces olvido su apellido.**<br>
Sometimes I forget her last name.
