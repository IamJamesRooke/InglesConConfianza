# Eating and Drinking: Having Meals

English commonly uses **have** before the name of a meal.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| desayunar | **to have breakfast** |
| almorzar | **to have lunch** |
| cenar | **to have dinner** |

**Desayuno a las siete.**<br>
I have breakfast at seven.

**Almuerzo con mis compañeros al mediodía.**<br>
I have lunch with my coworkers at noon.

**Ceno con mi familia a las siete.**<br>
I have dinner with my family at seven.
