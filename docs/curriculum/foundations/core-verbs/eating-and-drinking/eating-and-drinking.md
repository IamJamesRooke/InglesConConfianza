# Eating and Drinking

English can use **eat** and **drink**, or the broader verb **have**, for food and beverages.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| comer | **to eat**, **to have** |
| beber, tomar | **to drink**, **to have** |

**Como una arepa en el desayuno.**<br>
I eat an arepa for breakfast.

**Me tomo un café con la arepa.**<br>
I drink a coffee with the arepa.

In everyday conversation, **have** can express either action:

**I have an arepa and a coffee for breakfast.**
