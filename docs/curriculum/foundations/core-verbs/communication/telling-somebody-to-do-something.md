# Communication Verbs: Telling Somebody to Do Something

Use **tell + somebody + full infinitive** when you give a person an instruction.

**subject + tell + somebody + to + base verb**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| decirle a alguien que haga algo | **to tell somebody to do something** |

**La profesora les dice a los estudiantes que abran el libro.**<br>
The teacher tells the students to open the book.

English does not use **that** in this pattern:

**She tells them to open the book.**<br>
Not: *She tells them that they open the book.*
