# Communication Verbs: **Hear** and **Listen**

English distinguishes perceiving a sound from intentionally paying attention to it.

## **Hear**

Use **hear** when a sound reaches your ears.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| oír | **to hear** |

**Oigo música en el apartamento de al lado.**<br>
I hear music in the apartment next door.

## **Listen to**

Use **listen to** when you intentionally pay attention to a sound.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| escuchar | **to listen to** |

**Escucho la letra de la canción con atención.**<br>
I listen to the lyrics of the song carefully.

Use **to** before the thing or person receiving your attention:

**Listen to the teacher.**<br>
Not: *Listen the teacher.*
