# Communication Verbs: Asking Somebody to Do Something

Use **ask + somebody + full infinitive** when you request an action from a person.

**subject + ask + somebody + to + base verb**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| pedirle a alguien que haga algo | **to ask somebody to do something** |

**Le pido a Mateo que cierre la ventana.**<br>
I ask Mateo to close the window.

English does not use **that** in this pattern:

**I ask Mateo to close the window.**<br>
Not: *I ask Mateo that he closes the window.*
