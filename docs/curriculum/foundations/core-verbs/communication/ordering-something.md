# Communication Verbs: Ordering Something

In a restaurant or café, Spanish **pedir** usually corresponds to English **order**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| pedir algo en un restaurante | **to order something** |

**Pido un café y una arepa para el desayuno.**<br>
I order a coffee and an arepa for breakfast.

Use **ask for** when making a general request, but **order** when choosing food or drinks from a business:

**I ask the server for a glass of water.**<br>
**I order a coffee from the menu.**
