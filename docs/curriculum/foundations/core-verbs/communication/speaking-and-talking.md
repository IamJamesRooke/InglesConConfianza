# Communication Verbs: **Speak** and **Talk**

Spanish **hablar** can correspond to either **speak** or **talk**.

## **Speak**

Use **speak** for languages and for the act or ability of speaking.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| hablar | **to speak** |

**Camila habla inglés en el trabajo.**<br>
Camila speaks English at work.

## **Talk**

Use **talk to** or **talk with** when people have a conversation. **Speak to** is also possible and can sound more formal.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| hablar con alguien | **to talk to somebody**, **to talk with somebody** |

**Camila habla con su jefe todas las mañanas.**<br>
Camila talks to her boss every morning.
