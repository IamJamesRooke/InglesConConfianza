# Communication Verbs: Explaining Something to Somebody

Use **explain + something + to + somebody**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| explicarle algo a alguien | **to explain something to somebody** |

**La profesora les explica la actividad a los estudiantes.**<br>
The teacher explains the activity to the students.

Put **to** before the person:

**Explain the problem to me.**<br>
Not: *Explain me the problem.*
