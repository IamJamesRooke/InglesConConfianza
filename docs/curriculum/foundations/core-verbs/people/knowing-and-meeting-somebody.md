# People: Knowing and Meeting Somebody

Spanish **conocer** corresponds to different English verbs depending on whether the relationship already exists.

## **Know somebody**

Use **know** when you are already acquainted with the person.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| conocer a alguien | **to know somebody** |

**Conozco a Laura porque trabajamos juntas.**<br>
I know Laura because we work together.

## **Meet somebody**

Use **meet** for the first introduction to a person.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| conocer a alguien por primera vez | **to meet somebody** |

**Conozco a Laura en mi primer día de trabajo.**<br>
I meet Laura on my first day at work.
