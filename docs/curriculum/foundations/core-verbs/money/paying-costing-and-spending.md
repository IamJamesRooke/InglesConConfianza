# Money: Paying, Costing, and Spending

## **Pay**

The buyer **pays** money for something.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| pagar | **to pay** |

**Pago veinte mil pesos por el libro.**<br>
I pay twenty thousand pesos for the book.

## **Cost**

The thing being purchased **costs** an amount of money.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| costar | **to cost** |

**El libro cuesta veinte mil pesos.**<br>
The book costs twenty thousand pesos.

## **Spend**

Use **spend** with the amount of money the buyer uses.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| gastar dinero | **to spend money** |

**Gasto veinte mil pesos en el libro.**<br>
I spend twenty thousand pesos on the book.
