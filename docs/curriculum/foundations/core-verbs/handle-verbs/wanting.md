# Handle Verbs: Wanting

## Wanting something

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| querer algo | **to want something** |

**Quiero un café.**<br>
I want a coffee.

## Wanting to do an action

Use **want + full infinitive** when you want to perform an action.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| querer hacer algo | **to want to do something** |

**Quiero preparar el café.**<br>
I want to make the coffee.
