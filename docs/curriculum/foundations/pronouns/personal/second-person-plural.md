# Second-Person Plural: **ustedes**

**Ustedes** means **you** when speaking to more than one person.

| Function | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | **Ustedes ven el libro.** | You see the book. |
| Direct object | **Los veo a ustedes.** | I see you. |
| Indirect object | **Les doy el libro a ustedes.** | I give the book to you. |
| Reflexive | **Ustedes se ven.** | You see yourselves. |
| Possessive | **Este es su libro.** | This is your book. |
| After **para** | **Lo hago para ustedes.** | I do it for you. |
| After **con** | **Hablo con ustedes.** | I speak with you. |
| Combined pronouns | **Se lo doy a ustedes.** | I give it to you. |

**Ustedes** uses the same verb forms as **ellos** and **ellas**:

- **Ustedes ven.** — You see.
- **Ellos ven.** — They see.
- **Ellas ven.** — They see.
