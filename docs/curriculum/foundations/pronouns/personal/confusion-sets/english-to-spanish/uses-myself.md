# Spanish Translations of **myself**

English **myself** can be reflexive or emphatic.

| Use | English | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| Reflexive | I see **myself**. | **Me** veo. |
| Emphatic | I do it **myself**. | Lo hago **yo mismo**. |
| After **for** | I do it for **myself**. | Lo hago **para mí mismo**. |
| After **with** | I am at peace with **myself**. | Estoy en paz **conmigo mismo**. |

A female speaker uses **misma**:

**Lo hago yo misma.**  
I do it myself.
