# Uses of “You”

English uses `you` for informal, formal, singular and plural references.

Spanish changes the form depending on:

- who receives the action;
- whether the person is addressed informally or formally;
- whether `you` refers to one person or several people;
- whether `you` is the subject, direct object, indirect object, reflexive object or follows a preposition.

# Subject

| Reference | English | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| 👨 informal singular | You see me. | Tú me ves. |
| 👩 informal singular | You see me. | Tú me ves. |
| 👨 formal singular | You see me. | Usted me ve. |
| 👩 formal singular | You see me. | Usted me ve. |
| 👨👩 plural | You see me. | Ustedes me ven. |

# Direct Object

## Informal Singular

`Te` represents one person addressed informally as `tú`.

| Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Veo a ti. | Te veo. | I see you. |
| Escucho a ti. | Te escucho. | I hear you. |
| Ayudo a ti. | Te ayudo. | I help you. |

## Formal Singular

`Lo` or `la` can represent one person addressed formally as `usted`.

| Reference | Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 👨 male `usted` | Lo veo a usted. | Lo veo. | I see you. |
| 👩 female `usted` | La veo a usted. | La veo. | I see you. |
| 👨 male `usted` | Lo escucho a usted. | Lo escucho. | I hear you. |
| 👩 female `usted` | La escucho a usted. | La escucho. | I hear you. |

## Plural

`Los` or `las` can represent several people addressed as `ustedes`.

| Reference | Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 👨👨 male group | Los veo a ustedes. | Los veo. | I see you. |
| 👨👩 mixed group | Los veo a ustedes. | Los veo. | I see you. |
| 👩👩 female group | Las veo a ustedes. | Las veo. | I see you. |
| 👨👩 mixed group | Los escucho a ustedes. | Los escucho. | I hear you. |
| 👩👩 female group | Las escucho a ustedes. | Las escucho. | I hear you. |

# Indirect Object

## Informal Singular

`Te` represents one person addressed informally.

| Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Digo la verdad a ti. | Te digo la verdad. | I tell you the truth. |
| Doy el libro a ti. | Te doy el libro. | I give you the book. |
| Explico la respuesta a ti. | Te explico la respuesta. | I explain the answer to you. |

## Formal Singular

`Le` represents one person addressed formally as `usted`. It does not change according to gender.

| Reference | Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 👨 formal singular | Le digo la verdad a usted. | Le digo la verdad. | I tell you the truth. |
| 👩 formal singular | Le digo la verdad a usted. | Le digo la verdad. | I tell you the truth. |
| 👨 formal singular | Le doy el libro a usted. | Le doy el libro. | I give you the book. |
| 👩 formal singular | Le doy el libro a usted. | Le doy el libro. | I give you the book. |

## Plural

`Les` represents several people addressed as `ustedes`. It does not change according to gender.

| Reference | Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 👨👨 male group | Les digo la verdad a ustedes. | Les digo la verdad. | I tell you the truth. |
| 👩👩 female group | Les digo la verdad a ustedes. | Les digo la verdad. | I tell you the truth. |
| 👨👩 mixed group | Les doy el libro a ustedes. | Les doy el libro. | I give you the book. |

# Combined Object Pronouns

## Informal Singular

`Te` identifies the recipient. The second pronoun identifies what is given.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Te lo doy a ti. | I give it to you. |
| Te la doy a ti. | I give it to you. |
| Te los doy a ti. | I give them to you. |
| Te las doy a ti. | I give them to you. |

## Formal Singular

`Se` replaces `le` before `lo`, `la`, `los`, or `las`.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Se lo doy a usted. | I give it to you. |
| Se la doy a usted. | I give it to you. |
| Se los doy a usted. | I give them to you. |
| Se las doy a usted. | I give them to you. |

## Plural

`Se` also replaces `les` before `lo`, `la`, `los`, or `las`.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Se lo doy a ustedes. | I give it to you. |
| Se la doy a ustedes. | I give it to you. |
| Se los doy a ustedes. | I give them to you. |
| Se las doy a ustedes. | I give them to you. |

# Summary

| Use of “you” | Informal singular | Formal singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| subject | tú | usted | ustedes |
| direct object | te | lo / la | los / las |
| indirect object | te | le | les |
| after `para` | para ti | para usted | para ustedes |
| after `con` | contigo | con usted | con ustedes |
| reflexive | te | se | se |
| before `lo/la/los/las` | te | se | se |
