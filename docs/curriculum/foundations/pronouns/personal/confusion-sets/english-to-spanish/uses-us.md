# Spanish Translations of **us**

English **us** has different Spanish translations depending on the structure.

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| Juan sees **us**. | Juan **nos** ve. |
| Juan gives the book **to us**. | Juan **nos** da el libro. |
| Juan does it **for us**. | Juan lo hace **para nosotros**. |
| Juan speaks **with us**. | Juan habla **con nosotros**. |

Use **nosotras** instead of **nosotros** when the group is entirely female.
