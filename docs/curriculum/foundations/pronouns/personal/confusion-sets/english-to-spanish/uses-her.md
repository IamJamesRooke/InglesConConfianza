# Uses of “Her”

English uses `her` for a female person or animal receiving an action.

Spanish changes the form depending on whether `her` is:

- a direct object;
- an indirect object;
- used after a preposition;
- the recipient in a combined-pronoun construction.

# Direct Object: La

`La` represents a female person or animal directly receiving an action.

| Reference | Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 👩 la mujer | Veo a la mujer. | La veo. | I see the woman. I see her. |
| 👩 María | Escucho a María. | La escucho. | I hear María. I hear her. |
| 👩 la profesora | Ayudo a la profesora. | La ayudo. | I help the teacher. I help her. |
| 🐕️ la perra | Veo a la perra. | La veo. | I see the dog. I see her. |

The personal `a` appears before a specific person or animal:

- `Veo a la mujer.`
- `Escucho a María.`
- `Ayudo a la profesora.`

The personal `a` disappears when the noun is replaced by `la`:

- `La veo.`
- `La escucho.`
- `La ayudo.`

# Indirect Object: Le

`Le` represents a singular recipient.

| Reference | Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 👩 la mujer | Digo la verdad a la mujer. | Le digo la verdad. | I tell the woman the truth. I tell her the truth. |
| 👩 María | Doy el libro a María. | Le doy el libro. | I give María the book. I give her the book. |
| 👩 la profesora | Hago una pregunta a la profesora. | Le hago una pregunta. | I ask the teacher a question. I ask her a question. |
| 🐕️ la perra | Doy comida a la perra. | Le doy comida. | I give the dog food. I give her food. |

`Le` does not indicate gender. It can also mean `him`, `it` or formal singular `you`.

Context tells us that it means `her`.

# Direct and Indirect Minimal Difference

| Grammatical role | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| direct object | La veo. | I see her. |
| indirect object | Le digo la verdad. | I tell her the truth. |
| direct object | La escucho. | I hear her. |
| indirect object | Le doy el libro. | I give her the book. |

Compare:

| Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Veo a la mujer. | La veo. | I see her. |
| Doy el libro a la mujer. | Le doy el libro. | I give her the book. |

# After a Preposition: Ella

After most prepositions, Spanish uses `ella`.

## Para Ella

| Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Hago el café para María. | Lo hago para ella. | I make the coffee for María. I make it for her. |
| Compro el libro para la profesora. | Lo compro para ella. | I buy the book for the teacher. I buy it for her. |

## Con Ella

| Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Hablo con María. | Hablo con ella. | I speak with María. I speak with her. |
| Trabajo con la profesora. | Trabajo con ella. | I work with the teacher. I work with her. |

## De Ella

| Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|
| El libro es de María. | El libro es de ella. | The book belongs to María. The book belongs to her. |
| Recibo un mensaje de María. | Recibo un mensaje de ella. | I receive a message from María. I receive a message from her. |

## Sobre Ella

| Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Hablo sobre María. | Hablo sobre ella. | I speak about María. I speak about her. |
| Escribo sobre la profesora. | Escribo sobre ella. | I write about the teacher. I write about her. |

# Combined Object Pronouns: Se

When `le` appears before `lo`, `la`, `los` or `las`, it changes to `se`.

In these constructions, `se` can mean `to her`.

## Se Lo

| Full context | Combined form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Doy el libro a María. | Se lo doy a ella. | I give María the book. I give it to her. |
| Envío el documento a la profesora. | Se lo envío a ella. | I send the teacher the document. I send it to her. |
| Muestro el robot a la mujer. | Se lo muestro a ella. | I show the woman the robot. I show it to her. |

## Se La

| Full context | Combined form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Doy la llave a María. | Se la doy a ella. | I give María the key. I give it to her. |
| Explico la respuesta a la estudiante. | Se la explico a ella. | I explain the answer to the student. I explain it to her. |
| Muestro la casa a la mujer. | Se la muestro a ella. | I show the woman the house. I show it to her. |

## Se Los

| Full context | Combined form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Doy los libros a María. | Se los doy a ella. | I give María the books. I give them to her. |
| Envío los documentos a la profesora. | Se los envío a ella. | I send the teacher the documents. I send them to her. |
| Muestro los robots a la mujer. | Se los muestro a ella. | I show the woman the robots. I show them to her. |

## Se Las

| Full context | Combined form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Doy las llaves a María. | Se las doy a ella. | I give María the keys. I give them to her. |
| Explico las respuestas a la estudiante. | Se las explico a ella. | I explain the answers to the student. I explain them to her. |
| Muestro las casas a la mujer. | Se las muestro a ella. | I show the woman the houses. I show them to her. |

The phrase `a ella` clarifies that `se` means `to her`.

Without that context:

- `Se lo doy` could mean `I give it to her`.
- It could also mean `I give it to him`.
- It could also mean `I give it to them`.
- It could also mean `I give it to you`.

# Her Versus Herself

English distinguishes `her` from `herself`.

Spanish distinguishes a normal object from a reflexive object.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| María la ve. | María sees her. |
| María se ve. | María sees herself. |
| Juan la ayuda. | Juan helps her. |
| María se ayuda. | María helps herself. |

`La` refers to another female person.

`Se` refers back to the subject.

# “Her” Showing Possession

English also uses `her` before a noun to show possession. Spanish uses `su`.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Es su libro. | It is her book. |
| Veo su casa. | I see her house. |
| Conozco a su hermano. | I know her brother. |

`Su` does not identify the owner’s gender by itself. It can mean:

- his;
- her;
- its;
- your;
- their.

Context identifies the owner.

To clarify the owner, Spanish can use `de ella`:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Es el libro de ella. | It is her book. / The book is hers. |
| Veo la casa de ella. | I see her house. |
| Conozco al hermano de ella. | I know her brother. |

# Summary

| Use of “her” | Spanish form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| direct object | la | La veo. |
| indirect object | le | Le doy el libro. |
| after a preposition | ella | Hablo con ella. |
| possessive before a noun | su | Es su libro. |
| clarified possession | de ella | Es el libro de ella. |
| recipient before `lo` | se | Se lo doy a ella. |
| recipient before `la` | se | Se la doy a ella. |
| recipient before `los` | se | Se los doy a ella. |
| recipient before `las` | se | Se las doy a ella. |
