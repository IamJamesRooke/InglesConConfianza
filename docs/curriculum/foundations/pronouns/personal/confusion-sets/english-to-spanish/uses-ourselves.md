# Spanish Translations of **ourselves**

English **ourselves** can be reflexive or emphatic.

| Use | English | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| Reflexive | We see **ourselves**. | **Nos** vemos. |
| Emphatic | We do it **ourselves**. | Lo hacemos **nosotros mismos**. |
| After **for** | We do it for **ourselves**. | Lo hacemos **para nosotros mismos**. |
| After **with** | We are honest with **ourselves**. | Somos honestos **con nosotros mismos**. |

An entirely female group uses **nosotras mismas**.
