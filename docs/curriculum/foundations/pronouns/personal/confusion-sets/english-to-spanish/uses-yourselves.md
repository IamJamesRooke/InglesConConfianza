# Uses of “Yourselves”

English uses `yourselves` when `you` refers to more than one person and:

- the people perform an action on themselves;
- the speaker emphasizes that they do something without assistance;
- the word follows a preposition.

In Latin American Spanish, the subject is `ustedes` and the reflexive pronoun is `se`.

Spanish also distinguishes:

- masculine or mixed groups with `mismos`;
- all-female groups with `mismas`.

# Reflexive Actions

`Se` represents the same group identified by `ustedes`.

| Reference | Full context | Reflexive form | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 👨👨 male group | Ustedes ven a ustedes mismos. | Ustedes se ven. | You see yourselves. |
| 👨👩 mixed group | Ustedes ven a ustedes mismos. | Ustedes se ven. | You see yourselves. |
| 👩👩 female group | Ustedes ven a ustedes mismas. | Ustedes se ven. | You see yourselves. |
| 👨👩 mixed group | Ustedes ayudan a ustedes mismos. | Ustedes se ayudan. | You help yourselves. |
| 👩👩 female group | Ustedes ayudan a ustedes mismas. | Ustedes se ayudan. | You help yourselves. |

# Masculine and Feminine Emphasis

The pronoun `se` does not indicate gender.

The words `mismos` and `mismas` agree with the group.

## Masculine or Mixed Group

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ustedes se ven a sí mismos. | You see yourselves. |
| Ustedes se ayudan a sí mismos. | You help yourselves. |
| Ustedes se describen a sí mismos. | You describe yourselves. |
| Ustedes se preguntan a sí mismos. | You ask yourselves. |

## All-Female Group

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ustedes se ven a sí mismas. | You see yourselves. |
| Ustedes se ayudan a sí mismas. | You help yourselves. |
| Ustedes se describen a sí mismas. | You describe yourselves. |
| Ustedes se preguntan a sí mismas. | You ask yourselves. |

The shorter forms are normally sufficient:

- `Ustedes se ven.`
- `Ustedes se ayudan.`

The longer forms add emphasis or clarification:

- `Ustedes se ven a sí mismos.`
- `Ustedes se ayudan a sí mismas.`

# Yourselves as Emphasis

English can use `yourselves` to emphasize that the people personally perform an action.

Spanish uses `ustedes mismos` or `ustedes mismas`.

# Giving Something to Yourselves

`Se` identifies the same group as both the subject and recipient.

The second pronoun identifies the thing being given.

## Masculine or Mixed Group

| Direct object | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| 📕 masculine singular | Ustedes se lo dan a sí mismos. | You give it to yourselves. |
| feminine singular | Ustedes se la dan a sí mismos. | You give it to yourselves. |
| 📚️ masculine plural | Ustedes se los dan a sí mismos. | You give them to yourselves. |
| feminine plural | Ustedes se las dan a sí mismos. | You give them to yourselves. |

## All-Female Group

| Direct object | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| 📕 masculine singular | Ustedes se lo dan a sí mismas. | You give it to yourselves. |
| feminine singular | Ustedes se la dan a sí mismas. | You give it to yourselves. |
| 📚️ masculine plural | Ustedes se los dan a sí mismas. | You give them to yourselves. |
| feminine plural | Ustedes se las dan a sí mismas. | You give them to yourselves. |

The phrase `a sí mismos` or `a sí mismas` clarifies that `se` means `to yourselves`.

Without that clarification, `se lo dan` could also mean:

- you give it to him;
- you give it to her;
- you give it to them;
- you give it to another person addressed formally.

# Yourselves Versus Each Other

The pronoun `se` can mean either `yourselves` or `each other`.

Context determines the meaning.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ustedes se ven en el espejo. | You see yourselves in the mirror. |
| Ustedes se ven todos los días. | You see each other every day. |
| Ustedes se ayudan a sí mismos. | You help yourselves. |
| Ustedes se ayudan mutuamente. | You help each other. |

The phrases `a sí mismos`, `a sí mismas` and `mutuamente` can remove the ambiguity.

# Yourself Versus Yourselves

| Reference | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| 👨 informal singular | Tú te ves. | You see yourself. |
| 👩 informal singular | Tú te ves. | You see yourself. |
| 👨 formal singular | Usted se ve. | You see yourself. |
| 👩 formal singular | Usted se ve. | You see yourself. |
| 👨👨 male plural | Ustedes se ven. | You see yourselves. |
| 👨👩 mixed plural | Ustedes se ven. | You see yourselves. |
| 👩👩 female plural | Ustedes se ven. | You see yourselves. |

Spanish distinguishes singular and plural through the subject and verb:

- `Usted se ve` — singular
- `Ustedes se ven` — plural

The reflexive pronoun `se` remains the same.
