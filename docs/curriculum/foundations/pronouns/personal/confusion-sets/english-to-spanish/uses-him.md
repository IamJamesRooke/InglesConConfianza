# Uses of “Him”

English uses `him` for a male person or animal receiving an action.

Spanish changes the form depending on whether `him` is:

- a direct object;
- an indirect object;
- used after a preposition;
- the recipient in a combined-pronoun construction.

# Direct Object: Lo

`Lo` represents a male person or animal directly receiving an action.

| Reference | Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 👨 el hombre | Veo al hombre. | Lo veo. | I see the man. I see him. |
| 👨 Juan | Escucho a Juan. | Lo escucho. | I hear Juan. I hear him. |
| 👨 el profesor | Ayudo al profesor. | Lo ayudo. | I help the teacher. I help him. |
| 🐕️ el perro | Veo al perro. | Lo veo. | I see the dog. I see him. |

The personal `a` appears before a specific person or animal:

- `Veo al hombre.`
- `Escucho a Juan.`
- `Ayudo al profesor.`

The personal `a` disappears when the noun is replaced by `lo`:

- `Lo veo.`
- `Lo escucho.`
- `Lo ayudo.`

# Indirect Object: Le

`Le` represents a singular recipient.

| Reference | Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 👨 el hombre | Digo la verdad al hombre. | Le digo la verdad. | I tell the man the truth. I tell him the truth. |
| 👨 Juan | Doy el libro a Juan. | Le doy el libro. | I give Juan the book. I give him the book. |
| 👨 el profesor | Hago una pregunta al profesor. | Le hago una pregunta. | I ask the teacher a question. I ask him a question. |
| 🐕️ el perro | Doy comida al perro. | Le doy comida. | I give the dog food. I give him food. |

`Le` does not indicate gender. It can also mean `her`, `it` or formal singular `you`.

Context tells us that it means `him`.

# Direct and Indirect Minimal Difference

| Grammatical role | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| direct object | Lo veo. | I see him. |
| indirect object | Le digo la verdad. | I tell him the truth. |
| direct object | Lo escucho. | I hear him. |
| indirect object | Le doy el libro. | I give him the book. |

Compare:

| Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Veo al hombre. | Lo veo. | I see him. |
| Doy el libro al hombre. | Le doy el libro. | I give him the book. |

# After a Preposition: Él

After most prepositions, Spanish uses `él`.

The accent distinguishes the pronoun `él` from the article `el`.

## Para Él

| Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Hago el café para Juan. | Lo hago para él. | I make the coffee for Juan. I make it for him. |
| Compro el libro para el profesor. | Lo compro para él. | I buy the book for the teacher. I buy it for him. |

## Con Él

| Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Hablo con Juan. | Hablo con él. | I speak with Juan. I speak with him. |
| Trabajo con el profesor. | Trabajo con él. | I work with the teacher. I work with him. |

## De Él

| Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|
| El libro es de Juan. | El libro es de él. | The book belongs to Juan. The book belongs to him. |
| Recibo un mensaje de Juan. | Recibo un mensaje de él. | I receive a message from Juan. I receive a message from him. |

## Sobre Él

| Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Hablo sobre Juan. | Hablo sobre él. | I speak about Juan. I speak about him. |
| Escribo sobre el profesor. | Escribo sobre él. | I write about the teacher. I write about him. |

# Combined Object Pronouns: Se

When `le` appears before `lo`, `la`, `los` or `las`, it changes to `se`.

In these constructions, `se` can mean `to him`.

## Se Lo

| Full context | Combined form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Doy el libro a Juan. | Se lo doy a él. | I give Juan the book. I give it to him. |
| Envío el documento al profesor. | Se lo envío a él. | I send the teacher the document. I send it to him. |
| Muestro el robot al hombre. | Se lo muestro a él. | I show the man the robot. I show it to him. |

## Se La

| Full context | Combined form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Doy la llave a Juan. | Se la doy a él. | I give Juan the key. I give it to him. |
| Explico la respuesta al estudiante. | Se la explico a él. | I explain the answer to the student. I explain it to him. |
| Muestro la casa al hombre. | Se la muestro a él. | I show the man the house. I show it to him. |

## Se Los

| Full context | Combined form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Doy los libros a Juan. | Se los doy a él. | I give Juan the books. I give them to him. |
| Envío los documentos al profesor. | Se los envío a él. | I send the teacher the documents. I send them to him. |
| Muestro los robots al hombre. | Se los muestro a él. | I show the man the robots. I show them to him. |

## Se Las

| Full context | Combined form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Doy las llaves a Juan. | Se las doy a él. | I give Juan the keys. I give them to him. |
| Explico las respuestas al estudiante. | Se las explico a él. | I explain the answers to the student. I explain them to him. |
| Muestro las casas al hombre. | Se las muestro a él. | I show the man the houses. I show them to him. |

The phrase `a él` clarifies that `se` means `to him`.

Without that context:

- `Se lo doy` could mean `I give it to him`.
- It could also mean `I give it to her`.
- It could also mean `I give it to them`.
- It could also mean `I give it to you`.

# Him Versus Himself

English distinguishes `him` from `himself`.

Spanish distinguishes a normal object from a reflexive object.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Juan lo ve. | Juan sees him. |
| Juan se ve. | Juan sees himself. |
| María lo ayuda. | María helps him. |
| Juan se ayuda. | Juan helps himself. |

`Lo` refers to another male person.

`Se` refers back to the subject.

# Summary

| Use of “him” | Spanish form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| direct object | lo | Lo veo. |
| indirect object | le | Le doy el libro. |
| after a preposition | él | Hablo con él. |
| recipient before `lo` | se | Se lo doy a él. |
| recipient before `la` | se | Se la doy a él. |
| recipient before `los` | se | Se los doy a él. |
| recipient before `las` | se | Se las doy a él. |
