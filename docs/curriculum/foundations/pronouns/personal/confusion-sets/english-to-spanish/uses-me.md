# Spanish Translations of **me**

English **me** has different Spanish translations depending on the structure.

| English | Spanish |
|---|---|
| Juan sees **me**. | Juan **me** ve. |
| Juan gives the book **to me**. | Juan **me** da el libro. |
| Juan does it **for me**. | Juan lo hace **para mí**. |
| Juan speaks **with me**. | Juan habla **conmigo**. |

- **me** = me or to me
- **mí** = me after most prepositions
- **conmigo** = with me
