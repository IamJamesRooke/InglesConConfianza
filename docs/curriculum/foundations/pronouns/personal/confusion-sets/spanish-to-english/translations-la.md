# Translations of **la**

**La** is a feminine singular direct-object pronoun.

| Reference | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| Woman 👩 | **Veo a la mujer. La veo.** | I see the woman. I see **her**. |
| Object | **Veo la mesa. La veo.** | I see the table. I see **it**. |
| Formal feminine **you** 👩 | **La veo a usted.** | I see **you**. |

## Combined form: **se la**

**Doy la revista a Juan, a María, a usted o a ellos. Se la doy.**  
I give the magazine to Juan, María, you, or them. I give **it to him, her, you, or them**.

- **se** = to him, her, you, or them
- **la** = it
