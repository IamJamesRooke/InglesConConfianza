# Translations of **me**

The Spanish pronoun **me** refers to the speaker, but its English translation depends on its role.

| Use | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| Direct object | **Juan me ve.** | Juan sees **me**. |
| Indirect object | **Juan me da el libro.** | Juan gives the book **to me**. |
| Reflexive | **Me veo en el espejo.** | I see **myself** in the mirror. |
| Combined with **lo** | **Juan me lo da.** | Juan gives **it to me**. |
| Experiencer | **Me gusta el libro.** | **I** like the book. |
| Affected person | **Se me cayó el libro.** | **I dropped** the book. |

## Related forms

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **me** | me, to me, myself |
| **para mí** | for me |
| **mi libro** | my book |
| **conmigo** | with me |

The complete sentence determines the natural English translation.
