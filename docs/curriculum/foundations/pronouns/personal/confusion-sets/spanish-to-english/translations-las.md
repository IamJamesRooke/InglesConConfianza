# Translations of **las**

**Las** is a feminine plural direct-object pronoun.

| Reference | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| Women 👩👩 | **Veo a las mujeres. Las veo.** | I see the women. I see **them**. |
| Objects | **Veo las mesas. Las veo.** | I see the tables. I see **them**. |
| Plural feminine **you** 👩👩 | **Las veo a ustedes.** | I see **you**. |

## Combined form: **se las**

**Doy las revistas a Juan, a María, a usted o a ellos. Se las doy.**  
I give the magazines to Juan, María, you, or them. I give **them to him, her, you, or them**.

- **se** = to him, her, you, or them
- **las** = them
