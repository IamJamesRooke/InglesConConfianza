# Translations of **los**

**Los** is a masculine plural direct-object pronoun. It also represents mixed groups.

| Reference | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| Men 👨👨 | **Veo a los hombres. Los veo.** | I see the men. I see **them**. |
| Mixed group 👨👩 | **Veo a Juan y María. Los veo.** | I see Juan and María. I see **them**. |
| Objects 📚️ | **Veo los libros. Los veo.** | I see the books. I see **them**. |
| Plural **you** | **Los veo a ustedes.** | I see **you**. |

## Combined form: **se los**

**Doy los libros a Juan, a María, a usted o a ellos. Se los doy.**  
I give the books to Juan, María, you, or them. I give **them to him, her, you, or them**.

- **se** = to him, her, you, or them
- **los** = them
