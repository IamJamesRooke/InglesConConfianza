# Translations of **les**

**Les** is a plural indirect-object pronoun.

| Reference | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| People 👨👩 | **Les doy los libros a Juan y María.** | I give the books **to them**. |
| Plural **you** | **Les doy los libros a ustedes.** | I give the books **to you**. |

Before **lo**, **la**, **los**, or **las**, **les** changes to **se**:

**Les doy los libros. → Se los doy.**  
I give the books to them or you. → I give them to them or you.
