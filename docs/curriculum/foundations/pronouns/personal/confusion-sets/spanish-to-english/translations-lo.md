# Translations of **lo**

**Lo** is a masculine singular direct-object pronoun.

| Reference | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| Man 👨 | **Veo al hombre. Lo veo.** | I see the man. I see **him**. |
| Object 📕 | **Veo el libro. Lo veo.** | I see the book. I see **it**. |
| Formal masculine **you** 👨 | **Lo veo a usted.** | I see **you**. |

## Combined form: **se lo**

**Doy el libro a Juan, a María, a usted o a ellos. Se lo doy.**  
I give the book to Juan, María, you, or them. I give **it to him, her, you, or them**.

- **se** = to him, her, you, or them
- **lo** = it
