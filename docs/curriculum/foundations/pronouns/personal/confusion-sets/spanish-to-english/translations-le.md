# Translations of **le**

**Le** is a singular indirect-object pronoun.

| Reference | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| Man 👨 | **Le doy el libro a Juan.** | I give the book **to him**. |
| Woman 👩 | **Le doy el libro a María.** | I give the book **to her**. |
| Formal **you** | **Le doy el libro a usted.** | I give the book **to you**. |

Before **lo**, **la**, **los**, or **las**, **le** changes to **se**:

**Le doy el libro. → Se lo doy.**  
I give the book to him, her, or you. → I give it to him, her, or you.
