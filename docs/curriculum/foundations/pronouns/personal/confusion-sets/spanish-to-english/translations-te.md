# Translations of **te**

The Spanish pronoun **te** refers to one person addressed informally as **tú**.

| Use | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| Direct object | **Te veo.** | I see **you**. |
| Indirect object | **Te doy el libro.** | I give the book **to you**. |
| Reflexive | **Te ves en el espejo.** | You see **yourself** in the mirror. |
| Combined with **lo** | **Te lo doy.** | I give **it to you**. |
| Experiencer | **Te gusta el libro.** | **You** like the book. |
| Affected person | **Se te cayó el libro.** | **You dropped** the book. |

## Related forms

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **te** | you, to you, yourself |
| **para ti** | for you |
| **tu libro** | your book |
| **contigo** | with you |

**Te** is only used with informal singular **tú**, not with **usted** or **ustedes**.
