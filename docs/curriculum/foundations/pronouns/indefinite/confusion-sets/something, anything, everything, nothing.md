# Things: Something, Anything, Nothing and Everything

Learn this sequence as one complete pattern.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Creo que hay algo en la caja.** | I think something is in the box. |
| **¿Hay algo en la caja?** | Is there anything in the box? |
| **Nada está en la caja.** | Nothing is in the box. |
| **No hay nada en la caja.** | There isn't anything in the box. |
| **No creo que haya nada en la caja.** | I don't think anything is in the box. |
| **Puedes poner cualquier cosa en la caja.** | You can put anything in the box. |
| **Todo está en la caja.** | Everything is in the box. |

## Pattern

| Context | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | **algo** | something |
| Question | **algo** | anything |
| Negative pronoun | **nada** | nothing |
| Negative sentence | **no...nada** | not anything |
| Any possible thing | **cualquier cosa** | anything |
| All things | **todo** | everything |

> **No creo que** is followed here by the subjunctive forms **haya nadie** and **haya nada**. Students can initially learn these as complete expressions.
