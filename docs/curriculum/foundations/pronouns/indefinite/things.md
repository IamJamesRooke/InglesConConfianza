# Indefinite Pronouns: Things

These pronouns refer to things without identifying exactly what they are.

## Core forms

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **algo** | something |
| **todo** | everything |
| **nada** | nothing |
| **cualquier cosa** | anything |

## Something

**Hay algo en la caja.**  
There is something in the box.

## Everything

**Todo está listo.**  
Everything is ready.

## Nothing

**Nada funciona.**  
Nothing works.

## Anything in questions

Spanish normally uses **algo** when asking whether a thing exists:

**¿Quieres algo?**  
Do you want anything?

## Anything in negative sentences

Spanish uses **nada**:

**No quiero nada.**  
I do not want anything.

## Anything meaning “any possible thing”

Use **cualquier cosa** when every option is possible:

**Puedes elegir cualquier cosa.**  
You can choose anything.

## Negative word order

If **nada** comes before the verb, do not use **no**:

**Nada funciona.**  
Nothing works.

If **nada** comes after the verb, use **no**:

**No funciona nada.**  
Nothing works.

## Adjectives come after the pronoun

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **algo interesante** | something interesting |
| **todo importante** | everything important |
| **nada nuevo** | nothing new |

**Quiero aprender algo nuevo.**  
I want to learn something new.

## Adding **else**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **algo más** | something else |
| **nada más** | nothing else |

**¿Quieres algo más?**  
Do you want anything else?
