# Verb Cognates: **-ire**

These Spanish and English cognates correspond to English verbs ending in **-ire**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| admirar | **to admire** | **Admiro a Julia por su paciencia y disciplina.**<br>I admire Julia for her patience and discipline. |
| inspirar | **to inspire** | **La historia puede inspirar a otros estudiantes.**<br>The story may inspire other students. |
| adquirir | **to acquire** | **La empresa planea adquirir un edificio más grande.**<br>The company plans to acquire a larger building. |
| requerir | **to require** | **Este trabajo requiere mucha atención al detalle.**<br>This job requires close attention to detail. |
| retirarse | **to retire** | **Mi padre piensa retirarse el próximo año.**<br>My father plans to retire next year. |
| expirar | **to expire** | **El pasaporte va a expirar en diciembre.**<br>The passport is going to expire in December. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| aspirar a | **to aspire to** | **Lucía aspira a dirigir su propia empresa.**<br>Lucía aspires to run her own company. |
| conspirar | **to conspire** | **Los funcionarios fueron acusados de conspirar contra el gobierno.**<br>The officials were accused of conspiring against the government. |

## False Cognates

**Retirar** usually means **to remove** or **to withdraw**. Use **retirarse** for retiring from work.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Voy a retirar dinero del cajero.** | I am going to **withdraw** money from the ATM. |
| **Mi padre va a retirarse después de treinta años en la empresa.** | My father is going to **retire** after thirty years at the company. |
