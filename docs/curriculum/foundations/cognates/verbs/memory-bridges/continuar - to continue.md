# Verb Cognates: **-ue**

These English cognates share the ending **-ue**. Their Spanish equivalents use different forms, so learn each pair through the shared English pattern.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| continuar | **to continue** | **Vamos a continuar la conversación después del almuerzo.**<br>We are going to continue the conversation after lunch. |
| valorar | **to value** | **La empresa valora la experiencia de sus empleados.**<br>The company values its employees' experience. |
| subvalorar | **to undervalue** | **No debemos subvalorar el trabajo de los cuidadores.**<br>We should not undervalue the work of caregivers. |
| sobrevalorar | **to overvalue** | **Los inversionistas podrían sobrevalorar la empresa.**<br>The investors might overvalue the company. |
