# Verb Cognates: **-nish**

The English verbs **finish** and **punish** end in **-nish**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| terminar | **to finish** | **Necesito terminar el informe antes de las cinco.**<br>I need to finish the report before five o'clock. |
| castigar | **to punish** | **La escuela no debe castigar a todo el grupo por el error de una persona.**<br>The school should not punish the whole group for one person's mistake. |

## Teaching Bridge

> En inglés, “finimos” y “punimos”.

These forms work as humorous bridges to **finish** and **punish**, but they are not invented. **Finir** is a disused verb meaning **terminar** or **acabar** that has been recorded in Colombia, and **punir** is a rare verb meaning **castigar**. Use the common Spanish verbs **terminar** and **castigar** in normal conversation.
