# Verb Cognates: **-pt**

In these cognates, remove the Spanish infinitive ending **-ar** to find the English verb ending in **-pt**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| aceptar | **to accept** | **El vendedor decidió aceptar nuestra oferta.**<br>The seller decided to accept our offer. |
| adoptar | **to adopt** | **La empresa va a adoptar una política más flexible.**<br>The company is going to adopt a more flexible policy. |
| adaptar | **to adapt** | **Necesitamos adaptar el curso a las necesidades del grupo.**<br>We need to adapt the course to the group's needs. |
| encriptar | **to encrypt** | **La aplicación debe encriptar los datos antes de enviarlos.**<br>The application must encrypt the data before sending it. |
