# Verb Cognates: **-end**

In these cognates, remove the Spanish infinitive ending **-ar** or **-er** to find the English verb ending in **-end**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| recomendar | **to recommend** | **Te recomiendo este libro porque explica el tema claramente.**<br>I recommend this book because it explains the topic clearly. |
| depender | **to depend** | **El precio depende del número de personas.**<br>The price depends on the number of people. |
| extender | **to extend** | **Queremos extender el contrato por otro año.**<br>We want to extend the contract for another year. |
| suspender | **to suspend** | **La universidad suspendió las clases por la tormenta.**<br>The university suspended classes because of the storm. |
| ofender | **to offend** | **No quiero ofender a Marta con ese comentario.**<br>I do not want to offend Marta with that comment. |
| defender | **to defend** | **La abogada va a defender a su cliente en el juicio.**<br>The lawyer is going to defend her client at the trial. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ascender | **to ascend** | **Los escaladores comenzaron a ascender la montaña al amanecer.**<br>The climbers began to ascend the mountain at dawn. |
| descender | **to descend** | **El avión empezó a descender antes de llegar a Bogotá.**<br>The plane began to descend before reaching Bogotá. |
| trascender | **to transcend** | **Su música logró trascender las fronteras de Colombia.**<br>Her music managed to transcend Colombia's borders. |

## False Cognates

**Pretender** usually means **to intend**, **to try**, or **to claim**, not **to pretend**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Laura pretende terminar el informe hoy.** | Laura **intends** to finish the report today. |
| **Laura finge estar enferma para no ir a trabajar.** | Laura **pretends** to be sick so she does not have to go to work. |
