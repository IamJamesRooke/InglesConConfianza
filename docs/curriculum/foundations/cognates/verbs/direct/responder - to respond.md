# Verb Cognates: **-ond**

In these cognates, remove the Spanish infinitive ending **-er** to find the English verb ending in **-ond**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| responder | **to respond** | **Voy a responder tu mensaje después de la reunión.**<br>I am going to respond to your message after the meeting. |
| corresponder | **to correspond** | **Cada número corresponde a una pregunta del formulario.**<br>Each number corresponds to a question on the form. |
