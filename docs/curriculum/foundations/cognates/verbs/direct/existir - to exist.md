# Verb Cognates: **-ist**

In these cognates, remove the Spanish infinitive ending **-ir** to find the English verb ending in **-ist**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| existir | **to exist** | **Algunos animales solo existen en esta región.**<br>Some animals only exist in this region. |
| persistir | **to persist** | **El dolor puede persistir durante varios días.**<br>The pain may persist for several days. |
| resistir | **to resist** | **Este material puede resistir temperaturas muy altas.**<br>This material can resist very high temperatures. |
| insistir | **to insist** | **Carlos va a insistir en pagar la cuenta.**<br>Carlos is going to insist on paying the bill. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| consistir en | **to consist of** | **El curso consiste en diez lecciones cortas.**<br>The course consists of ten short lessons. |
| desistir de | **to desist from** | **La empresa debe desistir de usar esa publicidad engañosa.**<br>The company must desist from using that misleading advertising. |
| coexistir | **to coexist** | **Las dos especies pueden coexistir en el mismo ecosistema.**<br>The two species can coexist in the same ecosystem. |

## False Cognates

**Asistir** means **to attend**, while **to assist** normally means **ayudar** or **atender**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Voy a asistir a la conferencia del viernes.** | I am going to **attend** Friday's conference. |
| **Un asesor va a atenderte con la solicitud.** | A representative is going to **assist** you with the application. |
