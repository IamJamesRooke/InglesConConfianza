# False Cognates: Verbs

These Spanish verbs resemble English verbs, but the tempting translation is wrong in this context.

## Presentar / Present

**Quiero presentar a mi amigo.**

❌ I want to present my friend.
✅ I want to introduce my friend.

Use **present** when showing or formally giving something:

**Quiero presentar los resultados.**
✅ I want to present the results.

## Asistir / Assist

**Voy a asistir a la conferencia.**

❌ I am going to assist the conference.
✅ I am going to attend the conference.

**Assist** means **ayudar** or **atender**:

**Un asesor va a ayudarte con la solicitud.**
✅ A representative is going to assist you with the application.

## Atender / Attend

**Voy a atender al cliente.**

❌ I am going to attend the client.
✅ I am going to help the customer.

Use **attend** for going to an event:

**Voy a asistir a la reunión.**
✅ I am going to attend the meeting.

## Pretender / Pretend

**Laura pretende terminar el informe hoy.**

❌ Laura pretends to finish the report today.
✅ Laura intends to finish the report today.

**Pretend** means **fingir**:

**Laura finge estar enferma.**
✅ Laura pretends to be sick.

## Realizar / Realize

**El equipo realizó todas las pruebas necesarias.**

❌ The team realized all the necessary tests.
✅ The team carried out all the necessary tests.

**Realize** normally means **darse cuenta de**:

**Ana se dio cuenta de que había enviado el archivo equivocado.**
✅ Ana realized that she had sent the wrong file.

## Soportar / Support

**No soporto el ruido de la calle.**

❌ I do not support the street noise.
✅ I cannot stand the street noise.

**Support** normally means **apoyar**:

**La comunidad decidió apoyar el proyecto.**
✅ The community decided to support the project.

## Decepcionar / Deceive

**La decisión decepcionó a muchos clientes.**

❌ The decision deceived many customers.
✅ The decision disappointed many customers.

**Deceive** means **engañar**:

**El vendedor engañó al cliente.**
✅ The salesperson deceived the customer.

## Suceder / Succeed

**Nadie sabe qué sucedió después de la reunión.**

❌ Nobody knows what succeeded after the meeting.
✅ Nobody knows what happened after the meeting.

**Succeed** means **tener éxito** or **lograr algo**:

**El equipo trabajó mucho para tener éxito.**
✅ The team worked hard to succeed.

## Imprimir / Impress

**Voy a imprimir el contrato.**

❌ I am going to impress the contract.
✅ I am going to print the contract.

**Impress** means **impresionar**:

**La presentación impresionó a los clientes.**
✅ The presentation impressed the clients.

## Invertir / Invert

**La empresa va a invertir en energía solar.**

❌ The company is going to invert in solar energy.
✅ The company is going to invest in solar energy.

Use **invert** when reversing a position or order:

**Debes invertir el orden de las páginas.**
✅ You must invert the order of the pages.

## Divertirse / Divert

**Los niños se divirtieron mucho en la fiesta.**

❌ The children diverted themselves a lot at the party.
✅ The children had a lot of fun at the party.

**Divert** normally means **desviar**:

**La policía tuvo que desviar el tráfico.**
✅ The police had to divert the traffic.

## Cometer / Commit

**Julián cometió un error en el informe.**

❌ Julián committed a mistake in the report.
✅ Julián made a mistake in the report.

Use **commit** with crimes and offenses:

**El acusado cometió un delito.**
✅ The defendant committed a crime.

## Comprometerse / Compromise

**Julián se comprometió a terminar el informe.**

❌ Julián compromised to finish the report.
✅ Julián committed to finishing the report.

**Compromise** can mean **poner en riesgo** or **llegar a un acuerdo**:

**El error puso en riesgo la seguridad del sistema.**
✅ The error compromised the system's security.

## Calificar / Qualify

**La profesora debe calificar treinta exámenes.**

❌ The teacher must qualify thirty exams.
✅ The teacher must grade thirty exams.

For meeting requirements, **qualify** means **cumplir los requisitos**:

**Esta experiencia te permite cumplir los requisitos para el puesto.**
✅ This experience qualifies you for the position.

## Retirar / Retire

**Voy a retirar dinero del cajero.**

❌ I am going to retire money from the ATM.
✅ I am going to withdraw money from the ATM.

Use **retire** for leaving work permanently:

**Mi padre va a retirarse el próximo año.**
✅ My father is going to retire next year.

## Encontrar / Encounter

**Encontré mis llaves debajo del sofá.**

❌ I encountered my keys under the sofa.
✅ I found my keys under the sofa.

Use **encounter** for coming across someone or something, often unexpectedly:

**Encontramos varios problemas durante la instalación.**
✅ We encountered several problems during the installation.

## Experimentar / Experiment

**El paciente experimentó mucho dolor.**

❌ The patient experimented a lot of pain.
✅ The patient experienced a lot of pain.

Use **experiment with** for trying or testing something:

**Los científicos experimentaron con un material nuevo.**
✅ The scientists experimented with a new material.

## Elegir / Elect

**Debes elegir una opción del menú.**

❌ You must elect an option from the menu.
✅ You must choose an option from the menu.

Use **elect** when choosing someone for an official position by voting:

**Los ciudadanos eligieron a Marta como alcaldesa.**
✅ The citizens elected Marta mayor.

## Poner / Pose

**Voy a poner las llaves sobre la mesa.**

❌ I am going to pose the keys on the table.
✅ I am going to put the keys on the table.

**Pose** can mean **plantear** a question:

**La periodista planteó una pregunta difícil.**
✅ The journalist posed a difficult question.

## Disponer / Dispose

**La oficina dispone de dos salas de reuniones.**

❌ The office disposes of two meeting rooms.
✅ The office has two meeting rooms available.

**Dispose of** means **desechar**:

**Debemos desechar estos productos de forma segura.**
✅ We must dispose of these products safely.

## Conformar / Conform

**Cinco especialistas conforman el comité.**

❌ Five specialists conform the committee.
✅ Five specialists form the committee.

**Conform to** means **ajustarse a** or **cumplir con**:

**La empresa debe ajustarse a las normas de seguridad.**
✅ The company must conform to the safety standards.

## Aplicar / Apply

**Daniela va a aplicar al puesto de diseñadora.**

❌ Daniela is going to apply to the designer position.
✅ Daniela is going to apply for the designer position.

This use of **aplicar** is common in Colombian Spanish. English uses **apply for** with a job and **apply to** with an organization:

**Daniela va a postularse a Google.**
✅ Daniela is going to apply to Google.

When applying one thing to another, Spanish **aplicar** maps directly to English **apply**:

**Puedes aplicar esta regla a todos los ejemplos.**
✅ You can apply this rule to all the examples.

## Complementar / Compliment

**La salsa complementa el sabor del pescado.**

❌ The sauce compliments the flavor of the fish.
✅ The sauce complements the flavor of the fish.

**Compliment** means **felicitar** or **elogiar**:

**El chef felicitó a su asistente por el plato.**
✅ The chef complimented his assistant on the dish.

## Contratar / Contract

**Voy a contratarte como gerente de ventas.**

❌ I am going to contract you as the sales manager.
✅ I am going to hire you as the sales manager.

English can use **contract** when formally arranging for a specific service:

**La empresa va a contratar a un laboratorio para realizar las pruebas.**
✅ The company is going to contract a laboratory to perform the tests.

## Embarazar / Embarrass

**Ana se embarazó el año pasado.**

❌ Ana embarrassed herself last year.
✅ Ana got pregnant last year.

**Embarrass** means **avergonzar**:

**El comentario la avergonzó frente a sus compañeros.**
✅ The comment embarrassed her in front of her classmates.
