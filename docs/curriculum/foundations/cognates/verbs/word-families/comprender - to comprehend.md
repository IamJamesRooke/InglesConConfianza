# Verb Cognates: **-hend**

These Spanish and English cognates correspond to English verbs ending in **-hend**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| comprender | **to comprehend** | **Los estudiantes necesitan comprender la idea principal.**<br>The students need to comprehend the main idea. |
| aprehender | **to apprehend** | **La policía logró aprehender al sospechoso cerca de la frontera.**<br>The police managed to apprehend the suspect near the border. |

## False Cognates

**Aprender** means **to learn**, not **to apprehend**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Los niños aprenden nuevas palabras mediante juegos.** | The children **learn** new words through games. |
