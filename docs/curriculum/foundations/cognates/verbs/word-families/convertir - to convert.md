# Verb Cognates: **-vert**

These Spanish and English cognates correspond to English verbs ending in **-vert**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| convertir | **to convert** | **La empresa quiere convertir el edificio en apartamentos.**<br>The company wants to convert the building into apartments. |
| revertir | **to revert** | **El administrador puede revertir el cambio si causa problemas.**<br>The administrator can revert the change if it causes problems. |
| invertir | **to invert** | **Debes invertir el orden de las dos imágenes.**<br>You must invert the order of the two images. |

## False Cognates

**Invertir** normally means **to invest** when talking about money. Use **to invert** when reversing the position or order of something.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **La empresa va a invertir en energía solar.** | The company is going to **invest** in solar energy. |
| **Debes invertir el orden de las páginas.** | You must **invert** the order of the pages. |

**Divertirse** means **to have fun**, not **to divert**. **To divert** normally means **desviar**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Los niños se divirtieron mucho en la fiesta.** | The children **had a lot of fun** at the party. |
| **La policía tuvo que desviar el tráfico.** | The police had to **divert** the traffic. |
