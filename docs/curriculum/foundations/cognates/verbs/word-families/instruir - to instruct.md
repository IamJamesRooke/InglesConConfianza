# Verb Cognates: **-uct**

In this family, Spanish verbs ending in **-struir** correspond to English verbs ending in **-struct**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| instruir | **to instruct** | **La supervisora va a instruir al equipo sobre el nuevo proceso.**<br>The supervisor is going to instruct the team on the new process. |
| construir | **to construct, to build** | **La ciudad planea construir un nuevo puente.**<br>The city plans to construct a new bridge. |
| reconstruir | **to reconstruct, to rebuild** | **La comunidad espera reconstruir la escuela después del incendio.**<br>The community hopes to reconstruct the school after the fire. |
| obstruir | **to obstruct** | **Un vehículo mal estacionado puede obstruir la entrada.**<br>A badly parked vehicle may obstruct the entrance. |
