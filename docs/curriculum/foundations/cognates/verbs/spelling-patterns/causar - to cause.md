# Verb Cognates: **-ause**

In these cognates, Spanish verbs ending in **-ausar** correspond to English verbs ending in **-ause**. The English verbs also share the same final sound.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| causar | **to cause** | **La lluvia intensa puede causar inundaciones.**<br>Heavy rain can cause flooding. |
| pausar | **to pause** | **Puedes pausar el video para tomar apuntes.**<br>You can pause the video to take notes. |
