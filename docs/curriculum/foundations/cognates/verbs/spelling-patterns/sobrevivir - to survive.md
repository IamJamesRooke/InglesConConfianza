# Verb Cognates: **-ive**

These Spanish and English cognates correspond to English verbs ending in **-ive**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| sobrevivir | **to survive** | **Los excursionistas lograron sobrevivir a la noche en la montaña.**<br>The hikers managed to survive the night on the mountain. |
| revivir | **to revive** | **Los médicos intentaron revivir al paciente.**<br>The doctors tried to revive the patient. |
| derivar | **to derive** | **Muchas palabras inglesas derivan del latín.**<br>Many English words derive from Latin. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| privar de | **to deprive of** | **La falta de sueño puede privar al cerebro del descanso necesario.**<br>Lack of sleep can deprive the brain of necessary rest. |

## False Cognates

**Vivir** means **to live**, not **to vive**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Mis abuelos viven en Cali.** | My grandparents **live** in Cali. |
