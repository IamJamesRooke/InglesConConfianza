# Verb Cognates: **-er**

These Spanish and English cognates correspond to English verbs ending in **-er**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| entrar | **to enter** | **Debes mostrar tu identificación antes de entrar al edificio.**<br>You must show your identification before entering the building. |
| centrar | **to center** | **La profesora va a centrar la clase en la conversación.**<br>The teacher is going to center the class on conversation. |
| encontrar | **to encounter** | **Los viajeros podrían encontrar dificultades durante el recorrido.**<br>The travelers might encounter difficulties during the journey. |

## False Cognates

**Encontrar** usually means **to find**. Use **to encounter** for meeting someone or finding something unexpectedly, especially a difficulty or problem.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Encontré mis llaves debajo del sofá.** | I **found** my keys under the sofa. |
| **Encontramos varios problemas durante la instalación.** | We **encountered** several problems during the installation. |
