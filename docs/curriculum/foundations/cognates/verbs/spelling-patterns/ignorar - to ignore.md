# Verb Cognates: **-ore**

In these cognates, Spanish verbs ending in **-orar** correspond to English verbs ending in **-ore**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ignorar | **to ignore** | **No debes ignorar las señales de advertencia.**<br>You should not ignore the warning signs. |
| explorar | **to explore** | **Queremos explorar el centro histórico de Cartagena.**<br>We want to explore Cartagena's historic center. |
| restaurar | **to restore** | **El museo va a restaurar la pintura dañada.**<br>The museum is going to restore the damaged painting. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| adorar | **to adore** | **Los niños adoran a su nueva profesora.**<br>The children adore their new teacher. |
