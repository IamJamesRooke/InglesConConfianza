# Cognates: **-oso → -ous**

In these cognates, Spanish **-oso** corresponds to English **-ous**. Some words require an additional spelling change.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ambicioso | **ambitious** | **El equipo propuso un plan ambicioso para crecer.**<br>The team proposed an ambitious plan for growth. |
| curioso | **curious** | **La niña curiosa hizo muchas preguntas.**<br>The curious girl asked many questions. |
| delicioso | **delicious** | **El restaurante sirve un postre delicioso.**<br>The restaurant serves a delicious dessert. |
| famoso | **famous** | **El pueblo es famoso por sus artesanías.**<br>The town is famous for its handicrafts. |
| misterioso | **mysterious** | **Recibimos un mensaje misterioso durante la noche.**<br>We received a mysterious message during the night. |
| nervioso | **nervous** | **Andrés estaba nervioso antes de la entrevista.**<br>Andrés was nervous before the interview. |
