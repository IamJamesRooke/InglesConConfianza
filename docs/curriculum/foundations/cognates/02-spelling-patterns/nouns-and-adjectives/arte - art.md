# Cognates: **-arte → -art**

In these cognates, remove the final **-e** from Spanish to find the related English word ending in **-art**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| arte | **art** | **El museo exhibe arte moderno de Colombia.**<br>The museum exhibits modern art from Colombia. |
| parte | **part** | **Esta actividad es una parte importante del curso.**<br>This activity is an important part of the course. |
| aparte | **apart** | **Ellos viven aparte aunque trabajan juntos.**<br>They live apart although they work together. |
