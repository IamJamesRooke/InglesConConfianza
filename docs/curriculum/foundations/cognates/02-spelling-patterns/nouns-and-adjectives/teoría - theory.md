# Cognates: Spanish **t** and English **th**

In many Greek-derived cognates, Spanish **t** corresponds to English **th**. Other parts of the word may also change.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| teoría | **theory** | **La evidencia nueva contradice la teoría original.**<br>The new evidence contradicts the original theory. |
| teatro | **theater** | **El teatro presenta una obra colombiana este mes.**<br>The theater is presenting a Colombian play this month. |
| terapia | **therapy** | **La terapia le ayudó a recuperar el movimiento de la mano.**<br>Therapy helped her regain movement in her hand. |
| método | **method** | **Este método utiliza ejemplos completos para enseñar vocabulario.**<br>This method uses complete examples to teach vocabulary. |
