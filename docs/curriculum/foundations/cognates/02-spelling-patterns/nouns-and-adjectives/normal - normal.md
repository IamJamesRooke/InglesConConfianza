# Cognates: Spanish and English **-al**

These Spanish and English cognates share the ending **-al**. Some words require an additional spelling change.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| normal | **normal** | **Es normal cometer errores al aprender un idioma.**<br>It is normal to make mistakes when learning a language. |
| hospital | **hospital** | **El hospital queda cerca de la estación.**<br>The hospital is near the station. |
| especial | **special** | **Prepararon una comida especial para la celebración.**<br>They prepared a special meal for the celebration. |
| total | **total** | **El costo total incluye el transporte.**<br>The total cost includes transportation. |
| usual | **usual** | **El proceso tardó más de lo usual.**<br>The process took longer than usual. |
| local | **local** | **La tienda vende productos locales.**<br>The store sells local products. |
| nacional | **national** | **El equipo ganó el campeonato nacional.**<br>The team won the national championship. |
| internacional | **international** | **La conferencia reunió a expertos internacionales.**<br>The conference brought together international experts. |

## False Cognates

Spanish **actual** means **current**, not **actual**. English **actual** means **real** or **verdadero**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **El presidente actual asumió el cargo en agosto.** | The **current** president took office in August. |
| **El costo real fue mayor que el estimado.** | The **actual** cost was higher than the estimate. |
