# Noun Cognates: Remove the Final **-o**

In these cognates, removing the final **-o** reveals the related English noun ending in **-nt**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| evento | **event** | **El evento comienza a las siete de la noche.**<br>The event begins at seven in the evening. |
| argumento | **argument** | **Su argumento se basa en evidencia sólida.**<br>Her argument is based on solid evidence. |
| apartamento | **apartment** | **Ellos alquilaron un apartamento cerca de la universidad.**<br>They rented an apartment near the university. |
| departamento | **department** | **El departamento de ventas prepara el informe mensual.**<br>The sales department prepares the monthly report. |
| documento | **document** | **Debes firmar el documento en la última página.**<br>You must sign the document on the last page. |
| experimento | **experiment** | **El experimento produjo un resultado inesperado.**<br>The experiment produced an unexpected result. |
| momento | **moment** | **Espera un momento mientras reviso la información.**<br>Wait a moment while I review the information. |
