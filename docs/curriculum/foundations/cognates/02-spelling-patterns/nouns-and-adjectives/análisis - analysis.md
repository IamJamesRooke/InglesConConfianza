# Cognates: Spanish **-sis** and English **-sis**

Many Greek-derived Spanish nouns ending in **-sis** have English cognates with the same ending. The stem may require an additional spelling change.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| análisis | **analysis** | **El análisis explica por qué aumentaron los costos.**<br>The analysis explains why the costs increased. |
| crisis | **crisis** | **La empresa cambió su estrategia durante la crisis.**<br>The company changed its strategy during the crisis. |
| tesis | **thesis** | **La estudiante defendió su tesis ante tres profesores.**<br>The student defended her thesis before three professors. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| psicosis | **psychosis** | **El paciente recibió tratamiento después de un episodio de psicosis.**<br>The patient received treatment after an episode of psychosis. |
| síntesis | **synthesis** | **La conclusión presenta una síntesis de los resultados.**<br>The conclusion presents a synthesis of the results. |
| parálisis | **paralysis** | **La lesión le causó una parálisis temporal en una pierna.**<br>The injury caused temporary paralysis in one leg. |
