# Cognates: Spanish **-ible** and English **-ible**

These Spanish and English cognates share the ending **-ible**, although some require a spelling adjustment.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| posible | **possible** | **Es posible terminar el trabajo antes del viernes.**<br>It is possible to finish the work before Friday. |
| imposible | **impossible** | **Es imposible abrir la puerta sin la llave.**<br>It is impossible to open the door without the key. |
| flexible | **flexible** | **Necesitamos un horario flexible para el equipo.**<br>We need a flexible schedule for the team. |
| inflexible | **inflexible** | **La política parece demasiado inflexible.**<br>The policy seems too inflexible. |
| visible | **visible** | **La montaña es visible desde la ventana.**<br>The mountain is visible from the window. |
| invisible | **invisible** | **El gas es invisible, pero tiene un olor fuerte.**<br>The gas is invisible, but it has a strong smell. |
