# Cognates: **-ivo → -ive**

In these cognates, Spanish **-ivo** corresponds to English **-ive**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| activo | **active** | **Mi abuelo permanece activo después de jubilarse.**<br>My grandfather remains active after retiring. |
| pasivo | **passive** | **El estudiante adoptó un papel pasivo durante la actividad.**<br>The student took a passive role during the activity. |
| positivo | **positive** | **Recibimos una respuesta positiva del cliente.**<br>We received a positive response from the client. |
| negativo | **negative** | **El comentario tuvo un efecto negativo en el equipo.**<br>The comment had a negative effect on the team. |
| decisivo | **decisive** | **Su voto fue decisivo para aprobar la propuesta.**<br>Her vote was decisive in approving the proposal. |
| efectivo | **effective** | **El nuevo método es más efectivo que el anterior.**<br>The new method is more effective than the previous one. |

## False Cognates

As an adjective, **efectivo** means **effective**. As a noun referring to money, **efectivo** means **cash**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **El tratamiento fue efectivo.** | The treatment was **effective**. |
| **Necesito pagar en efectivo.** | I need to pay in **cash**. |
