# Noun Cognates: Remove the Final **-a**

In this pattern, removing the final **-a** from Spanish nouns ending in **-ista** reveals related English nouns ending in **-ist**. Some words also require a small English spelling change, as in **turista → tourist** and **pesimista → pessimist**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| lista | **list** | **La lista contiene todos los materiales del curso.**<br>The list contains all the course materials. |
| turista | **tourist** | **La turista pidió un mapa del centro de Bogotá.**<br>The tourist asked for a map of downtown Bogotá. |
| artista | **artist** | **La artista va a exhibir sus pinturas en el museo.**<br>The artist is going to exhibit her paintings at the museum. |
| dentista | **dentist** | **El dentista examinó la muela que me dolía.**<br>The dentist examined the tooth that was hurting. |
| optimista | **optimist** | **Mi hermano es optimista y siempre espera buenos resultados.**<br>My brother is an optimist and always expects good results. |
| pesimista | **pessimist** | **Un pesimista podría concentrarse únicamente en los riesgos.**<br>A pessimist might focus only on the risks. |
| realista | **realist** | **Como realista, Marta reconoce tanto las oportunidades como los problemas.**<br>As a realist, Marta recognizes both the opportunities and the problems. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| especialista | **specialist** | **La clínica remitió al paciente a una especialista.**<br>The clinic referred the patient to a specialist. |
| capitalista | **capitalist** | **El libro analiza la función del capitalista en la economía.**<br>The book analyzes the role of the capitalist in the economy. |
| comunista | **communist** | **El candidato negó ser comunista durante el debate.**<br>The candidate denied being a communist during the debate. |
| ciclista | **cyclist** | **La ciclista usa casco cada vez que entrena.**<br>The cyclist wears a helmet every time she trains. |
| violinista | **violinist** | **La violinista interpretó una pieza colombiana.**<br>The violinist performed a Colombian piece. |

## False Cognates

The noun **lista** means **list**, but **listo/lista** as an adjective normally means **ready**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **La lista está sobre la mesa.** | The **list** is on the table. |
| **Marta está lista para salir.** | Marta is **ready** to leave. |
