# Cognates: Spanish **-able** and English **-able**

These Spanish and English cognates share the ending **-able**, although some require a spelling adjustment.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| probable | **probable** | **Es probable que la reunión termine temprano.**<br>It is probable that the meeting will end early. |
| improbable | **improbable** | **Ese resultado parece improbable sin más evidencia.**<br>That result seems improbable without more evidence. |
| razonable | **reasonable** | **El cliente hizo una solicitud razonable.**<br>The client made a reasonable request. |
| aceptable | **acceptable** | **La calidad del producto es aceptable.**<br>The quality of the product is acceptable. |
| disponible | **available** | **El informe estará disponible mañana.**<br>The report will be available tomorrow. |
| sensible | **sensitive** | **Esta información es sensible y debe permanecer privada.**<br>This information is sensitive and must remain private. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| admirable | **admirable** | **Su compromiso con la comunidad es admirable.**<br>Her commitment to the community is admirable. |
| favorable | **favorable** | **El proyecto recibió una evaluación favorable.**<br>The project received a favorable evaluation. |
| considerable | **considerable** | **La reparación requiere una inversión considerable.**<br>The repair requires a considerable investment. |
| inevitable | **inevitable** | **Después de tantos retrasos, el cambio era inevitable.**<br>After so many delays, the change was inevitable. |

## False Cognates

Spanish **sensible** means **sensitive**, while English **sensible** means **sensato** or **razonable**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Esta información es sensible.** | This information is **sensitive**. |
| **Esperar parece una decisión sensata.** | Waiting seems like a **sensible** decision. |
