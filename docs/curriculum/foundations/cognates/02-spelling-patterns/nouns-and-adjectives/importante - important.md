# Cognates: **-ante → -ant**

In these cognates, remove the final **-e** from the Spanish ending **-ante** to find the English ending **-ant**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| importante | **important** | **La seguridad es una parte importante del proyecto.**<br>Security is an important part of the project. |
| restaurante | **restaurant** | **El restaurante sirve comida colombiana tradicional.**<br>The restaurant serves traditional Colombian food. |
| distante | **distant** | **Desde la finca se ve una montaña distante.**<br>A distant mountain is visible from the farm. |
| relevante | **relevant** | **Incluye únicamente la información relevante en el informe.**<br>Include only the relevant information in the report. |
| arrogante | **arrogant** | **El candidato pareció arrogante durante la entrevista.**<br>The candidate seemed arrogant during the interview. |
| ignorante | **ignorant** | **Su comentario demuestra que es ignorante sobre el tema.**<br>His comment shows that he is ignorant about the subject. |
