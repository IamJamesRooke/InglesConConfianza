# Cognates: Spanish **-el** and English **-el**

These Spanish and English cognates share the ending **-el**, although some require a spelling adjustment.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| hotel | **hotel** | **El hotel queda cerca del aeropuerto.**<br>The hotel is near the airport. |
| ángel | **angel** | **La pintura muestra un ángel sobre la ciudad.**<br>The painting shows an angel above the city. |
| cruel | **cruel** | **El tribunal describió el castigo como cruel.**<br>The court described the punishment as cruel. |
| túnel | **tunnel** | **El tren atraviesa un túnel bajo la montaña.**<br>The train passes through a tunnel under the mountain. |
| coronel | **colonel** | **El coronel habló con los soldados antes de la operación.**<br>The colonel spoke with the soldiers before the operation. |

> **Pronunciation:** English **colonel** sounds like “kernel.”
