# Noun Cognates: Spanish **-aje / -agen** and English **-age**

These related Spanish nouns correspond to English nouns ending in **-age**. The spelling transformation varies by word.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| mensaje | **message** | **Recibí tu mensaje esta mañana.**<br>I received your message this morning. |
| garaje | **garage** | **El carro está en el garaje.**<br>The car is in the garage. |
| porcentaje | **percentage** | **El informe muestra el porcentaje de respuestas correctas.**<br>The report shows the percentage of correct answers. |
| lenguaje | **language** | **El documento utiliza un lenguaje sencillo.**<br>The document uses simple language. |
| imagen | **image** | **La imagen aparece en la primera página.**<br>The image appears on the first page. |

## Meaning Distinctions

Spanish **viaje** normally means **trip** or **journey**. English **voyage** is mainly a long journey by sea or through space.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Hicimos un viaje corto a Villa de Leyva.** | We took a short **trip** to Villa de Leyva. |
| **La nave comenzó su viaje a Marte.** | The spacecraft began its **voyage** to Mars. |

Spanish **equipaje** means **luggage** or **baggage**, not “equipage.”

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Dejamos el equipaje en el hotel.** | We left our **luggage** at the hotel. |

## False Cognates

**Paisaje** means **landscape** or **scenery**, not **passage**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **El paisaje cambia durante la temporada de lluvias.** | The **landscape** changes during the rainy season. |
| **Leí un pasaje del libro.** | I read a **passage** from the book. |
