# Cognates: One Spanish Consonant and Two English Consonants

English sometimes doubles a consonant that appears only once in the related Spanish word. This is a useful spelling signal, not a rule for every cognate.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| profesor | **professor** | **El profesor publicó los resultados de su investigación.**<br>The professor published the results of his research. |
| posible | **possible** | **Es posible terminar el informe antes del viernes.**<br>It is possible to finish the report before Friday. |
| necesario | **necessary** | **Es necesario confirmar la reservación antes del viaje.**<br>It is necessary to confirm the reservation before the trip. |
| diferente | **different** | **Cada estudiante necesita una estrategia diferente.**<br>Each student needs a different strategy. |
