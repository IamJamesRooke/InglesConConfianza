# Cognates: **-ente → -ent**

In this family, removing the final **-e** from Spanish **-ente** reveals a related English **-ent** form. Some words require an additional spelling change, as in **diferente → different** and **estudiante → student**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| diferente | **different** | **Cada estudiante necesita un método diferente.**<br>Each student needs a different method. |
| inteligente | **intelligent** | **Laura es una investigadora inteligente y cuidadosa.**<br>Laura is an intelligent and careful researcher. |
| presente | **present** | **Todos los miembros del equipo estuvieron presentes.**<br>All the team members were present. |
| paciente | **patient** | **El paciente espera los resultados del examen.**<br>The patient is waiting for the test results. |
| cliente | **client** | **El cliente pidió una copia del contrato.**<br>The client requested a copy of the contract. |
| estudiante | **student** | **La estudiante practica inglés después del trabajo.**<br>The student practices English after work. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| excelente | **excellent** | **El restaurante ofrece un servicio excelente.**<br>The restaurant provides excellent service. |
| evidente | **evident** | **El cambio fue evidente después de la primera semana.**<br>The change was evident after the first week. |
| permanente | **permanent** | **La tinta dejó una marca permanente en la mesa.**<br>The ink left a permanent mark on the table. |
| urgente | **urgent** | **El hospital atendió primero el caso urgente.**<br>The hospital handled the urgent case first. |

## False Cognates

**Pariente** means **relative**, not **parent**. English **parent** means **padre** or **madre**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Un pariente vino a visitarnos desde Cali.** | A **relative** came to visit us from Cali. |
| **Cada padre o madre debe firmar el formulario.** | Each **parent** must sign the form. |
