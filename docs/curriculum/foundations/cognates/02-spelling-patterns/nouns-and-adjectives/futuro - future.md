# Cognates: **-uro / -ura → -ure**

In these cognates, Spanish **-uro** or **-ura** corresponds to English **-ure**. Some words require an additional spelling change.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| futuro | **future** | **Nadie puede predecir el futuro con certeza.**<br>No one can predict the future with certainty. |
| puro | **pure** | **El laboratorio necesita una muestra de agua pura.**<br>The laboratory needs a sample of pure water. |
| impuro | **impure** | **El material impuro produjo un resultado diferente.**<br>The impure material produced a different result. |
| maduro | **mature** | **Felipe es lo suficientemente maduro para aceptar la responsabilidad.**<br>Felipe is mature enough to accept the responsibility. |
| inmaduro | **immature** | **Su reacción fue impulsiva e inmadura.**<br>His reaction was impulsive and immature. |
| literatura | **literature** | **La estudiante disfruta la literatura latinoamericana.**<br>The student enjoys Latin American literature. |
