# Noun Cognates: **-ismo → -ism**

In these cognates, Spanish **-ismo** corresponds to English **-ism**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| turismo | **tourism** | **El turismo genera muchos empleos en la región.**<br>Tourism creates many jobs in the region. |
| optimismo | **optimism** | **Su optimismo ayudó al equipo durante la crisis.**<br>Her optimism helped the team during the crisis. |
| pesimismo | **pessimism** | **El informe refleja cierto pesimismo sobre la economía.**<br>The report reflects some pessimism about the economy. |
| realismo | **realism** | **La novela combina fantasía y realismo.**<br>The novel combines fantasy and realism. |
| idealismo | **idealism** | **Su idealismo inspiró a muchos jóvenes.**<br>His idealism inspired many young people. |
| capitalismo | **capitalism** | **El curso examina la historia del capitalismo.**<br>The course examines the history of capitalism. |
| socialismo | **socialism** | **El libro compara el capitalismo con el socialismo.**<br>The book compares capitalism with socialism. |
