# Verb Cognates: **-ude**

In these cognates, Spanish verbs ending in **-luir** or **-ludir** correspond to English verbs ending in **-lude**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| incluir | **to include** | **El precio debe incluir todos los impuestos.**<br>The price must include all taxes. |
| excluir | **to exclude** | **No podemos excluir a nadie de la actividad.**<br>We cannot exclude anyone from the activity. |
| concluir | **to conclude** | **La investigadora pudo concluir que el tratamiento era seguro.**<br>The researcher was able to conclude that the treatment was safe. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| aludir a | **to allude to** | **El director aludió a los problemas sin explicarlos directamente.**<br>The director alluded to the problems without explaining them directly. |
| eludir | **to elude** | **El sospechoso logró eludir a la policía durante varias horas.**<br>The suspect managed to elude the police for several hours. |
