# Direct Noun Cognates: **-or / -ctor**

These Spanish and English nouns have the same spelling.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| actor | **actor** | **El actor respondió preguntas después de la película.**<br>The actor answered questions after the movie. |
| instructor | **instructor** | **El instructor explicó cómo utilizar la máquina.**<br>The instructor explained how to use the machine. |
| director | **director** | **La directora presentó el plan al equipo.**<br>The director presented the plan to the team. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| doctor | **doctor** | **El doctor examinó al paciente esa mañana.**<br>The doctor examined the patient that morning. |
| profesor | **professor** | **El profesor publicó un artículo sobre el estudio.**<br>The professor published an article about the study. |
| conductor | **conductor** | **El conductor detuvo el bus frente al hospital.**<br>The driver stopped the bus in front of the hospital. |
| factor | **factor** | **El costo es un factor importante en la decisión.**<br>Cost is an important factor in the decision. |

## Meaning Distinction

Spanish **conductor** normally means the driver of a vehicle. English **conductor** commonly refers to the person who directs an orchestra or works on a train.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **El conductor estacionó el carro.** | The **driver** parked the car. |
| **El director dirigió la orquesta.** | The **conductor** directed the orchestra. |
