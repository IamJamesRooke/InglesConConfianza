# Major English-Speaking Countries

These countries use English as an official or dominant language. Some also have other official or widely spoken languages.

| Spanish country | English country | Spanish nationality | English nationality |
|---|---|---|---|
| Australia | Australia | australiano / australiana | Australian |
| Canadá | Canada | canadiense | Canadian |
| Irlanda | Ireland | irlandés / irlandesa | Irish |
| Nueva Zelanda | New Zealand | neozelandés / neozelandesa | New Zealander |
| el Reino Unido | the United Kingdom / the UK | británico / británica | British |
| los Estados Unidos | the United States / the US | estadounidense | American |

## Country and Nationality

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Emma es de Australia.** | Emma is from **Australia**. |
| **Emma es australiana.** | Emma is **Australian**. |
| **Liam es del Reino Unido.** | Liam is from **the United Kingdom**. |
| **Liam es británico.** | Liam is **British**. |

## New Zealand

Use **New Zealander** for a person and **New Zealand** before a noun.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Mia es neozelandesa.** | Mia is a **New Zealander**. |
| **Mia estudia la cultura de Nueva Zelanda.** | Mia studies **New Zealand culture**. |

## United States

English normally uses **American** for a person from the United States. Spanish **estadounidense** is more precise because **americano** can refer more broadly to the Americas.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Daniel es estadounidense.** | Daniel is **American**. |
| **Daniel es de los Estados Unidos.** | Daniel is from **the United States**. |

## Languages

Nationality and language are not always the same:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Sophie es canadiense y habla inglés y francés.** | Sophie is **Canadian** and speaks **English** and **French**. |
| **Sean es irlandés y habla inglés e irlandés.** | Sean is **Irish** and speaks **English** and **Irish**. |
