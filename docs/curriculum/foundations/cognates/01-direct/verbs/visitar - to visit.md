# Verb Cognates: **-it**

In these cognates, remove the Spanish infinitive ending **-ar** to find the English verb ending in **-it**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| visitar | **to visit** | **Vamos a visitar a mis abuelos este domingo.**<br>We are going to visit my grandparents this Sunday. |
| limitar | **to limit** | **La escuela decidió limitar el tamaño de las clases.**<br>The school decided to limit class sizes. |
| acreditar | **to credit** | **El banco va a acreditar el dinero en tu cuenta mañana.**<br>The bank is going to credit the money to your account tomorrow. |
| depositar | **to deposit** | **Necesito depositar este cheque antes del viernes.**<br>I need to deposit this check before Friday. |
