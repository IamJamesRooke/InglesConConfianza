# Noun Cognates: Add **-e**

These English nouns resemble shorter Spanish forms with an added final **-e**, but they do not follow one complete spelling rule.

## Tiempo / Vez

English **time** can refer to an amount of time or to an occasion.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **No tengo tiempo para terminar el informe.** | I do not have **time** to finish the report. |
| **Esta es la tercera vez que llamo.** | This is the third **time** I have called. |

## Tipo

English uses **type**, **sort**, or **kind** according to the context.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Este tipo de café viene de Colombia.** | This **type** of coffee comes from Colombia. |
| **¿Qué tipo de música escuchas?** | What **kind** of music do you listen to? |
| **No me gusta ese tipo de comportamiento.** | I do not like that **sort** of behavior. |

## Café / Coffee

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Tomo una taza de café cada mañana.** | I drink a cup of **coffee** every morning. |

## Precio / Price

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **El precio incluye todos los impuestos.** | The **price** includes all taxes. |
