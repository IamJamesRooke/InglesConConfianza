# Verb Cognates: **-cur**

These Spanish and English cognates correspond to English verbs ending in **-cur**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ocurrir | **to occur** | **El accidente ocurrió cerca de la entrada del túnel.**<br>The accident occurred near the tunnel entrance. |
| incurrir en | **to incur** | **La empresa podría incurrir en gastos adicionales.**<br>The company might incur additional expenses. |

## False Cognates

**Recurrir a** means **to resort to** or **to turn to**, not normally **to recur**. **To recur** means **repetirse**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Tuvimos que recurrir a un especialista.** | We had to **turn to** a specialist. |
| **El mismo problema comenzó a repetirse cada semana.** | The same problem began **to recur** every week. |

**Concurrir** normally means **to attend** or **to come together**, not **to concur**. **To concur** means **estar de acuerdo**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Miles de personas concurrieron al evento.** | Thousands of people **attended** the event. |
| **Los dos expertos están de acuerdo con la conclusión.** | The two experts **concur** with the conclusion. |
