# Verb Cognates: **-ect**

These Spanish and English cognates correspond to English verbs ending in **-ect**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| corregir | **to correct** | **La profesora va a corregir los errores del ejercicio.**<br>The teacher is going to correct the mistakes in the exercise. |
| dirigir | **to direct** | **Camilo va a dirigir la nueva película.**<br>Camilo is going to direct the new movie. |
| elegir | **to elect** | **Los ciudadanos van a elegir al alcalde en octubre.**<br>The citizens are going to elect the mayor in October. |

## False Cognates

**Elegir** normally means **to choose**. Use **to elect** when people choose someone for an official position by voting.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Debes elegir una opción del menú.** | You must **choose** an option from the menu. |
| **Los ciudadanos eligieron a Marta como alcaldesa.** | The citizens **elected** Marta mayor. |
