# **Good** and **Well**

Use adjective **good** to describe a person, thing, state, or experience. Use adverb **well** to describe how an action is performed.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| estar bien | to be good / to be fine |
| sentirse bien | to feel good / to feel well |
| hacer algo bien | to do something well |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El café está bueno. | The coffee is good. |
| Estoy bien. | I am fine. |
| Me siento bien hoy. | I feel good today. |
| Laura hace bien su trabajo. | Laura does her work well. |

After **feel**, both **good** and **well** are possible. **Feel good** describes a positive state; **feel well** often emphasizes health.
