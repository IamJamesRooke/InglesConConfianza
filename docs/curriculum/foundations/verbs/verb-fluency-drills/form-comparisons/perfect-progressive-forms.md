# Perfect Progressive Forms

Perfect-progressive forms use a form of **have + been + verb-ing**.

| Form | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| present | Lo he estado haciendo. | I have been doing it. |
| past | Lo había estado haciendo. | I had been doing it. |
| future | Lo habré estado haciendo. | I will have been doing it. |
| conditional | Lo habría estado haciendo. | I would have been doing it. |
| with **could** | Podría haberlo estado haciendo. | I could have been doing it. |
| with **should** | Debería haberlo estado haciendo. | I should have been doing it. |
