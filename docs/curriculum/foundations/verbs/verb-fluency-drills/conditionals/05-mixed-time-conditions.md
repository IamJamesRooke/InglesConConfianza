# Mixed-Time Conditions

The condition and result do not have to refer to the same time. Choose each verb form for its own meaning.

## Past condition, present result

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Si hubiera aceptado ese trabajo, ahora viviría en Medellín. | If I had accepted that job, I would live in Medellín now. |

A different past decision would produce a different present reality.

## Present condition, past result

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Si fuera más organizado, no habría perdido la fecha límite ayer. | If I were more organized, I would not have missed the deadline yesterday. |

A continuing present characteristic explains a past result.

The time words **now** and **yesterday** help make the relationship visible.
