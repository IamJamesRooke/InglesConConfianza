# Real Future Possibilities

Use a present form after **if** and **will + base verb** for a possible future result. This pattern is traditionally called the **first conditional**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Si tengo suficiente tiempo mañana, terminaré el informe. | If I have enough time tomorrow, I will finish the report. |
| Si ella llama esta noche, le contaré lo que pasó. | If she calls tonight, I will tell her what happened. |

The future meaning is clear from the complete sentence, so English normally does not use **will** in the condition:

- **If I have time, I will do it.**
- Not normally: *If I will have time, I will do it.*

The result can also be a command or use another modal:

- **If she calls, tell me.**
- **If we finish early, we can go together.**
