# Hypothetical Present or Future

Use a past form after **if** and **would + base verb** for a situation presented as imaginary, unlikely, or contrary to the present. This pattern is traditionally called the **second conditional**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Si tuviera suficiente tiempo, terminaría el informe. | If I had enough time, I would finish the report. |
| Si pudiera hacerlo, lo haría. | If I could do it, I would do it. |

Here **had** and **could** do not place the imagined situation in past time. They create distance from present reality.

With **be**, **were** is the traditional form for every subject in a hypothetical condition:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Si yo fuera tú, no aceptaría la oferta. | If I were you, I would not accept the offer. |

**Was** is common in informal speech with **I, he, she**, and **it**, but **If I were you** is the standard fixed expression.
