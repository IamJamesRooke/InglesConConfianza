# Fluency Drill: **There + Be**

Use **there + be** to say that something exists. The form of **be** agrees with what comes next.

| Time and number | Statement | Question | Negative |
|---|---|---|---|
| present singular | There is a problem. | Is there a problem? | There isn't a problem. |
| present plural | There are two problems. | Are there two problems? | There aren't two problems. |
| past singular | There was a problem. | Was there a problem? | There wasn't a problem. |
| past plural | There were two problems. | Were there two problems? | There weren't two problems. |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Quiero saber si hay un problema. | I want to know if there is a problem. |
| ¿Por qué no hay una solución? | Why isn't there a solution? |
| Quiero saber por qué no hay una solución. | I want to know why there isn't a solution. |

Direct questions invert **be + there**: **Is there...?** Embedded clauses keep statement order: **if there is...** and **why there isn't...**
