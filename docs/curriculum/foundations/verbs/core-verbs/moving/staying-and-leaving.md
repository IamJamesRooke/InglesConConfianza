# Moving: **Stay** and **Leave**

## **Stay**

Use **stay** when someone remains in a place.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| quedarse | **to stay** |

**Me quedo en la oficina hasta las cinco.**<br>
I stay in the office until five.

## **Leave**

Use **leave** when someone goes away from a place.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| salir, irse | **to leave** |

**Salgo de la oficina a las cinco.**<br>
I leave the office at five.

When the place is the direct object of **leave**, do not put **from** before it:

**I leave the office at five.**<br>
Not: *I leave from the office at five.*
