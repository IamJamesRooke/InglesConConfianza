# **To Be** with **Estar**

Use English **be** where Spanish uses **estar** for states and locations.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| estar | **to be** |
| estoy | **am** |
| está | **is** |
| estás, estamos, están | **are** |

## State

**Estoy cansado después del trabajo.**<br>
I am tired after work.

## Location

**La oficina está en el centro de Bogotá.**<br>
The office is in downtown Bogotá.

English does not use different forms of **be** to mark the Spanish distinction between **ser** and **estar**. Context communicates the intended meaning.
