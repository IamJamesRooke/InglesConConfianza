# Core Verbs: **Do** and **Make**

Spanish **hacer** can mean either **to do** or **to make** in English.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| hacer | **to do**, **to make** |

## **Do** for tasks and activities

Use **do** when the focus is completing a task or performing an activity.

**Hago mi tarea después de cenar.**<br>
I do my homework after dinner.

## **Make** for creating and preparing

Use **make** when the action creates, produces, or prepares something.

**Hago café para mi familia todas las mañanas.**<br>
I make coffee for my family every morning.

## Common expressions with **make**

Some expressions use **make** as a fixed combination.

**Hago una reserva para cuatro personas.**<br>
I make a reservation for four people.
