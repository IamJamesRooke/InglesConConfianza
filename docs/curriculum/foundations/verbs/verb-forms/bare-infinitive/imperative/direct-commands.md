# Direct Commands

Use the bare infinitive to give a direct command. English does not show the Spanish **tú/usted/ustedes** distinction in the verb form.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Hazlo. | Do it. |
| Hágalo. | Do it. |
| Háganlo. | Do it. |
| Háganoslo. | Do it for us. |

The understood subject is **you**, but English normally does not say it:

- **Do it.**
- Not normally: *You do it.*

For a negative command, use **don't + bare infinitive**:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No lo hagas. | Don't do it. |
