# **Let's + Bare Infinitive**

Use **let's + bare infinitive** to propose doing something together.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Hagámoslo. | Let's do it. |
| Vamos. | Let's go. |

**Let's** is the contraction of **let us** in this suggestion pattern.

Do not add **to**:

- **Let's do it.**
- Not: *Let's to do it.*

Use **let's not + bare infinitive** for a negative suggestion:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No lo hagamos. | Let's not do it. |
