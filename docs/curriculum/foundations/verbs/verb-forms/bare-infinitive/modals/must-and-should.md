# **Must** and **Should + Bare Infinitive**

Use the bare infinitive after **must** and **should**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Él lo debe hacer. | He must do it. |
| Él lo debería hacer. | He should do it. |

- **must do**
- **should do**
- Not: *must to do* or *should to do*

**Must** expresses strong obligation in this pattern. **Should** expresses advice or what is advisable.
