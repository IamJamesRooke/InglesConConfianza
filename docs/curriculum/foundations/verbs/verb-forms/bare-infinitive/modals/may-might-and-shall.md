# **May, Might**, and **Shall** + Bare Infinitive

Like other modal verbs, **may, might**, and **shall** select the bare infinitive.

| Meaning | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| possibility | Es posible que ella lo haga. | She may do it. |
| weaker possibility | Puede que ella lo haga. | She might do it. |
| formal suggestion | ¿Lo hacemos? | Shall we do it? |

Never add **to** or an **-s** ending after the modal:

- **She might do it.**
- Not: *She might to do it.*
- Not: *She might does it.*

**Shall we...?** remains useful for suggestions, especially in British English. In everyday American English, **Should we...?** is more common.
