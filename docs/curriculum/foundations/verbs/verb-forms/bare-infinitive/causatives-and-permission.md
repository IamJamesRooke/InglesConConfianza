# **Let, Make, Have**, and **Help** + Bare Infinitive

These verbs can put a person directly before a second action without **to**:

**subject + let/make/have/help + somebody + bare infinitive**

| Meaning | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| permission | Me dejan hacerlo. | They let me do it. |
| obligation or pressure | Me obligan a hacerlo. | They make me do it. |
| arrangement or instruction | Hacen que yo lo haga. | They have me do it. |
| assistance | Me ayudan a hacerlo. | They help me do it. |

Compare the forms:

- They **let me do** it.
- They **make me do** it.
- They **have me do** it.
- They **help me do** it.

Do not insert **to** after **let, make**, or causative **have**. **Help me to do it** is also standard, but **help me do it** is more compact and very common.
