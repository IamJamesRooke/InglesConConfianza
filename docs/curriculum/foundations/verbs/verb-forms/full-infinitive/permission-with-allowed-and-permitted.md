# **Allowed** and **Permitted** + Full Infinitive

Use **be allowed/permitted + full infinitive** to say that someone has permission to act:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me permiten hacerlo. | I am allowed to do it. |
| Ella tiene permiso para entrar. | She is permitted to enter. |

**Allowed** is the normal everyday choice. **Permitted** is more formal and common in rules or notices.

The active sentence uses a different pattern:

- They **let me do** it. — **let + somebody + bare infinitive**
- I am **allowed to do** it. — **allowed + full infinitive**
