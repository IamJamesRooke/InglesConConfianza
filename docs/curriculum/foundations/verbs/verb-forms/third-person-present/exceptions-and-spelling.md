# Third-Person Exceptions and Spelling

## Foundational irregular forms

| Spanish present | English base | Third-person form | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|---|
| tiene | have | has | `/hæz/` |
| va | go | goes | `/goʊz/` |
| hace | do | does | `/dʌz/` |
| dice | say | says | `/sɛz/` — sounds like **sez** |
| es / está | be | is | `/ɪz/` |

Learn these as complete forms:

- **She has it.**
- **He goes to the office.**
- **She does it.**
- **He says it.**
- **She is here.**

## **Have to** and **have got to**

The change from **have** to **has** also appears when **have** expresses obligation.

| Spanish | I / you / we / they | He / she / it |
|---|---|---|
| tener que hacerlo | have to do it | has to do it |
| tener que hacerlo | have got to do it | has got to do it |

**I have to do it.**<br>
**She has to do it.**

**I've got to do it.**<br>
**She's got to do it.**

## Add **-es**

After English verbs ending in **-s, -sh, -ch, -x, -z**, and many ending in **-o**, add **-es**:

| Base | Third-person form |
|---|---|
| finish | finishes |
| go | goes |
| do | does |

## Consonant + **y**

Change consonant + **y** to **-ies**:

| Base | Third-person form |
|---|---|
| study | studies |
| try | tries |

After a vowel + **y**, simply add **-s**:

| Base | Third-person form |
|---|---|
| pay | pays |
| buy | buys |
| say | says |

**Says** follows the normal spelling pattern but has the exceptional pronunciation `/sɛz/`.

## Modal verbs do not add **-s**

Modal verbs use the same written form with every subject:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Él puede hacerlo. | He can do it. |
| Ella debería hacerlo. | She should do it. |
| Él lo hará. | He will do it. |

Do not say *he cans, she shoulds,* or *he wills* in these modal patterns.
