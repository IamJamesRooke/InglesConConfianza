# **Be** and **Get** + Past Participle

Use **be + past participle** when the focus is on the person or thing affected by an action:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Laura lo hizo. | It was done by Laura. |
| No se puede hacer. | It cannot be done. |

Use **get + past participle** for an event or change, often in less formal English:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Se hizo a tiempo. | It got done on time. |
| La ventana se rompió durante la tormenta. | The window got broken during the storm. |

Compare:

- **It was done by Laura.** — neutral passive
- **It got done on time.** — emphasizes that the result happened

The past participle does not carry the tense. **Be** or **get** does: **is done, was done, will be done, got done**.
