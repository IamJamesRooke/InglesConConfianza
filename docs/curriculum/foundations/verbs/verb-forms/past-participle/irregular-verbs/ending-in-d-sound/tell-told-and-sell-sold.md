# Irregular Past Participles: **Told** and **Sold**

Learn these two changes together:

- **tell → told**
- **sell → sold**

Both participles end in the sound `/oʊld/`.

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| decirle a alguien, dicho | **tell somebody** | **told somebody** |
| vender, vendido | **sell** | **sold** |

**Le he dicho a Laura que la tienda ha vendido el último libro.**<br>
I have told Laura that the store has sold the last book.
