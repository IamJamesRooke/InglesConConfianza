# Irregular Past Participles: **Known** and **Shown**

These forms add written **-n** to the base form:

- **know → known**
- **show → shown**

Both finish with the sound `/n/`.

## **Know → Known**

Use **known** for both knowing information and knowing a person.

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| saber, sabido | **know something** | **known something** |
| conocer, conocido | **know somebody** | **known somebody** |

**Siempre he sabido la respuesta.**<br>
I have always known the answer.

**Conozco a la profesora desde hace años.**<br>
I have known the teacher for years.

Spanish normally uses the present **conozco** for this continuing relationship, while English uses **have known**.

## **Show → Shown**

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| mostrar, mostrado | **show** | **shown** |

**La profesora nos ha mostrado un ejemplo claro.**<br>
The teacher has shown us a clear example.
