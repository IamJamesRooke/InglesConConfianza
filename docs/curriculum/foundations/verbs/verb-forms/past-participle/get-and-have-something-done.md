# **Get** and **Have Something Done**

Use **get/have + something + past participle** when someone arranges for another person to complete the action:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mandé reparar el teléfono. | I got the phone repaired. |
| Ella hace revisar el carro cada año. | She has the car checked every year. |

The object receives the action:

- They **got it done** before Friday.
- She **had the report translated** into English.

This is not the ordinary past:

- **They did it.** — They performed the action.
- **They got it done.** — They caused or arranged for the result.

The command **Get it done!** means “Make sure it is completed.”
