# The **PING** Rule: Preposition + **-ing**

When a verb follows a preposition, use its **-ing** form:

**P + ING = preposition + verb-ing**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| antes de hacerlo | before doing it |
| después de hacerlo | after doing it |
| por hacerlo | for doing it |
| sin decirlo | without saying it |

This includes complete expressions such as:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Gracias por ayudarme. | Thank you for helping me. |
| Antes de hacerlo, quiero hablar con Laura. | Before doing it, I want to speak with Laura. |
| Después de hacerlo, voy a llamarte. | After doing it, I am going to call you. |

Never leave the verb in its base form after the preposition:

- **Thank you for helping me.**
- Not: *Thank you for help me.*

The advanced curriculum can distinguish infinitive **to** from prepositional **to**. For this foundation rule, focus on clear prepositions such as **before, after, for**, and **without**.
