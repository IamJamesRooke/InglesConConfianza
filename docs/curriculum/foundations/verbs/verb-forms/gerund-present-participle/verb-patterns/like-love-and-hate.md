# **Like, Love**, and **Hate + Verb-ing**

Use **like, love**, or **hate + verb-ing** to express a general attitude toward an activity.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me gusta hacerlo. | I like doing it. |
| Me encanta leer. | I love reading. |
| Odio esperar. | I hate waiting. |

With **like, love**, and **hate**, English can also use the full infinitive:

| **-ing** pattern | Full-infinitive pattern |
|---|---|
| I like doing it. | I like to do it. |
| I love reading. | I love to read. |
| I hate waiting. | I hate to wait. |

The meanings often overlap in foundational conversation. The advanced curriculum can teach preferences and context differences between the two forms.
