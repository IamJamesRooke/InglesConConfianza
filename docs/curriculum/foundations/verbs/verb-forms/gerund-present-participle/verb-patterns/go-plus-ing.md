# **Go** + **-ing** for Activities

Use **go + verb-ing** for many recreational and movement activities:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Voy de compras los sábados. | I go shopping on Saturdays. |
| Vamos a correr en el parque. | We go running in the park. |
| Ellos fueron a bailar anoche. | They went dancing last night. |

The form of **go** carries the time; the activity stays in **-ing**:

- **go shopping**
- **went shopping**
- **will go shopping**

This is different from **go + bare infinitive** in a direction or command such as **Go ask Laura**.
