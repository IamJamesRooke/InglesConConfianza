# **Keep** and **Continue** + **-ing**

Use **keep + verb-ing** when an activity does not stop:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ella sigue trabajando a pesar del ruido. | She keeps working despite the noise. |

Use **continue + verb-ing** when an activity carries on:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ellos continúan hablando después de la reunión. | They continue talking after the meeting. |

**Continue to talk** is also standard and often has little difference in meaning. **Keep to talk** is not standard; use **keep talking**.
