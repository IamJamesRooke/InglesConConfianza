# Present Participle: **Be + Verb-ing**

Use **be + verb-ing** for an action presented as in progress.

| Spanish pattern | English pattern |
|---|---|
| estar + **-ando/-iendo** | be + **verb-ing** |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Estoy comiendo. | I am eating. |
| Ella está haciendo el informe. | She is doing the report. |
| Estamos esperando a Laura. | We are waiting for Laura. |

The **-ing** form follows **be** directly:

- **I am eating.**
- Not: *I am to eat* when the intended meaning is **estoy comiendo**.
