# Verb Cognates: **-ose**

In this family, Spanish verbs built from **poner** correspond to English verbs ending in **-pose**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| proponer | **to propose** | **Ana va a proponer una solución durante la reunión.**<br>Ana is going to propose a solution during the meeting. |
| oponerse | **to oppose** | **Los vecinos se oponen a la construcción de la carretera.**<br>The neighbors oppose the construction of the road. |
| exponer | **to expose** | **El informe puede exponer varios problemas de seguridad.**<br>The report may expose several security problems. |
| suponer | **to suppose** | **Supongo que Carlos llegará después del almuerzo.**<br>I suppose Carlos will arrive after lunch. |
| componer | **to compose** | **Luisa quiere componer una canción para su hija.**<br>Luisa wants to compose a song for her daughter. |
| imponer | **to impose** | **El gobierno decidió imponer nuevas restricciones.**<br>The government decided to impose new restrictions. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| deponer | **to depose** | **Los generales intentaron deponer al presidente.**<br>The generals tried to depose the president. |
| interponer | **to interpose** | **El mediador trató de interponer una barrera entre los dos grupos.**<br>The mediator tried to interpose a barrier between the two groups. |
| predisponer | **to predispose** | **La falta de sueño puede predisponer a una persona a enfermarse.**<br>Lack of sleep can predispose a person to illness. |

## False Cognates

**Poner** means **to put**, not **to pose**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Voy a poner las llaves sobre la mesa.** | I am going to **put** the keys on the table. |
| **La periodista planteó una pregunta difícil.** | The journalist **posed** a difficult question. |

**Disponer de** usually means **to have available**, while **to dispose of** means **desechar**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **La oficina dispone de dos salas de reuniones.** | The office **has** two meeting rooms available. |
| **Debemos desechar estos productos de forma segura.** | We must **dispose of** these products safely. |
