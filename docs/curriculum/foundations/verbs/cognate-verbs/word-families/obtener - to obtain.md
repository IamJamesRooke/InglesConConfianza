# Verb Cognates: **-ain**

These related Spanish and English verbs use different forms, but the English cognates share the ending **-ain**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| obtener | **to obtain** | **Debes obtener un permiso antes de comenzar la obra.**<br>You must obtain a permit before starting the project. |
| mantener | **to maintain** | **Es importante mantener el equipo en buenas condiciones.**<br>It is important to maintain the equipment in good condition. |
| sostener | **to sustain** | **La empresa necesita más ingresos para sostener su crecimiento.**<br>The company needs more revenue to sustain its growth. |
| contener | **to contain** | **Esta botella contiene un litro de agua.**<br>This bottle contains one liter of water. |
| retener | **to retain** | **Este método ayuda a los estudiantes a retener información nueva.**<br>This method helps students retain new information. |
| explicar | **to explain** | **La profesora va a explicar la diferencia con otro ejemplo.**<br>The teacher is going to explain the difference with another example. |
