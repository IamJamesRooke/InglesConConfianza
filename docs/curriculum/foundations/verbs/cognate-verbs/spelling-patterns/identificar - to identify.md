# Verb Cognates: **-ify**

In these cognates, Spanish verbs ending in **-ificar** correspond to English verbs ending in **-ify**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| identificar | **to identify** | **La policía pudo identificar al conductor en el video.**<br>The police were able to identify the driver in the video. |
| verificar | **to verify** | **Debes verificar la dirección antes de enviar el paquete.**<br>You must verify the address before sending the package. |
| justificar | **to justify** | **Ninguna excusa puede justificar ese comportamiento.**<br>No excuse can justify that behavior. |
| unificar | **to unify** | **El nuevo sistema busca unificar los procesos de las oficinas.**<br>The new system aims to unify the offices' processes. |
| cuantificar | **to quantify** | **El estudio intenta cuantificar el impacto de la contaminación.**<br>The study attempts to quantify the impact of pollution. |

| Spanish | English | Example |

| clasificar | **to classify** | **Los biólogos van a clasificar las nuevas especies.**<br>The biologists are going to classify the new species. |

## False Cognates

**Calificar** normally means **to grade** or **to rate**, not **to qualify** for a position. In that context, **to qualify** means **cumplir los requisitos**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **La profesora debe calificar treinta exámenes.** | The teacher must **grade** thirty exams. |
| **Esta experiencia te permite cumplir los requisitos para el puesto.** | This experience **qualifies** you for the position. |
