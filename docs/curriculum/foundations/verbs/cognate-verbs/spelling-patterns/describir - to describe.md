# Verb Cognates: **-scribe**

In these cognates, Spanish verbs ending in **-scribir** correspond to English verbs ending in **-scribe**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| describir | **to describe** | **La testigo pudo describir al hombre que vio salir del edificio.**<br>The witness was able to describe the man she saw leaving the building. |
| inscribir | **to inscribe** | **El artista decidió inscribir su nombre en la escultura.**<br>The artist decided to inscribe his name on the sculpture. |
| prescribir | **to prescribe** | **La médica puede prescribir un tratamiento diferente.**<br>The doctor may prescribe a different treatment. |
| suscribirse | **to subscribe** | **Puedes suscribirte al boletín para recibir las noticias.**<br>You can subscribe to the newsletter to receive the news. |
| transcribir | **to transcribe** | **Necesito transcribir la entrevista antes de analizarla.**<br>I need to transcribe the interview before analyzing it. |
