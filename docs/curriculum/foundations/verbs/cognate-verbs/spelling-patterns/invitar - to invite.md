# Verb Cognates: **-ite**

These Spanish and English cognates correspond to English verbs ending in **-ite**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| invitar | **to invite** | **Vamos a invitar a todos los vecinos a la reunión.**<br>We are going to invite all the neighbors to the meeting. |
| unir | **to unite** | **La campaña busca unir a las comunidades de la región.**<br>The campaign aims to unite the region's communities. |
| reunir | **to reunite** | **El programa espera reunir a las familias separadas.**<br>The program hopes to reunite separated families. |
| citar | **to cite** | **Debes citar todas las fuentes del informe.**<br>You must cite every source in the report. |
| incitar | **to incite** | **El mensaje podría incitar a la violencia.**<br>The message could incite violence. |
| recitar | **to recite** | **La estudiante va a recitar un poema en la ceremonia.**<br>The student is going to recite a poem at the ceremony. |
