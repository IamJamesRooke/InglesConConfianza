# Verb Cognates: **-serve**

In these cognates, Spanish verbs ending in **-servar** correspond to English verbs ending in **-serve**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| observar | **to observe** | **Los científicos van a observar el comportamiento de las aves.**<br>The scientists are going to observe the birds' behavior. |
| conservar | **to conserve** | **La comunidad trabaja para conservar el bosque.**<br>The community works to conserve the forest. |
| preservar | **to preserve** | **El museo controla la humedad para preservar los documentos.**<br>The museum controls the humidity to preserve the documents. |
| reservar | **to reserve** | **Debes reservar una mesa antes de llegar al restaurante.**<br>You should reserve a table before arriving at the restaurant. |
