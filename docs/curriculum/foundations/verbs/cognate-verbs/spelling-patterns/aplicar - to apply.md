# Verb Cognates: **-ply**

Some Spanish verbs ending in **-plicar** correspond to English verbs ending in **-ply**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| aplicar | **to apply** | **Puedes aplicar esta regla a todos los ejemplos.**<br>You can apply this rule to all the examples. |
| multiplicar | **to multiply** | **Para resolver el problema, debes multiplicar seis por cuatro.**<br>To solve the problem, you must multiply six by four. |
| implicar | **to imply** | **Los resultados implican que el tratamiento no fue eficaz.**<br>The results imply that the treatment was not effective. |

## False Cognates

**Explicar** means **to explain**, not “to exply.”

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **La profesora va a explicar la diferencia con otro ejemplo.** | The teacher is going to **explain** the difference with another example. |

English also uses **apply** when requesting a job or program. In standard Spanish, use **postularse** or **solicitar**, rather than mechanically extending **aplicar** to this context.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Daniela va a postularse al puesto de diseñadora.** | Daniela is going to **apply for** the designer position. |
