# Verb Cognates: **-are**

In these cognates, Spanish verbs ending in **-arar** correspond to English verbs ending in **-are**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| preparar | **to prepare** | **Voy a preparar la presentación para la reunión de mañana.**<br>I am going to prepare the presentation for tomorrow's meeting. |
| comparar | **to compare** | **Necesitamos comparar los precios antes de comprar el computador.**<br>We need to compare the prices before buying the computer. |
| declarar | **to declare** | **Todos los viajeros deben declarar estos productos en la aduana.**<br>All travelers must declare these products at customs. |
| reparar | **to repair** | **El técnico puede reparar mi celular esta tarde.**<br>The technician can repair my cell phone this afternoon. |

## False Cognates

**Parar** means **to stop**, not **to pare**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **El conductor paró el bus frente al hospital.** | The driver **stopped** the bus in front of the hospital. |
| **El chef va a pelar la manzana con un cuchillo.** | The chef is going to **pare** the apple with a knife. |
