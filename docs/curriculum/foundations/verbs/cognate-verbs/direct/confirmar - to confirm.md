# Verb Cognates: **-rm**

In these cognates, remove the Spanish infinitive ending **-ar** to find the English verb ending in **-rm**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| confirmar | **to confirm** | **Por favor, confirma tu asistencia antes del jueves.**<br>Please confirm your attendance before Thursday. |
| formar | **to form** | **Los estudiantes van a formar cuatro grupos.**<br>The students are going to form four groups. |
| informar | **to inform** | **La clínica debe informar a los pacientes sobre el cambio.**<br>The clinic must inform the patients about the change. |
| transformar | **to transform** | **La tecnología puede transformar la educación.**<br>Technology can transform education. |
| reformar | **to reform** | **El Congreso quiere reformar el sistema tributario.**<br>Congress wants to reform the tax system. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| afirmar | **to affirm** | **La testigo afirmó que había visto el accidente.**<br>The witness affirmed that she had seen the accident. |
| alarmar | **to alarm** | **La noticia podría alarmar a los residentes.**<br>The news might alarm the residents. |
| armar | **to arm** | **El gobierno decidió armar a los guardias fronterizos.**<br>The government decided to arm the border guards. |
| desarmar | **to disarm** | **La policía logró desarmar al atacante.**<br>The police managed to disarm the attacker. |

## False Cognates

**Conformar** usually means **to form** or **to constitute**, and **conformarse con** means **to settle for**. English **to conform to** normally means **ajustarse a** or **cumplir con**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Cinco especialistas conforman el comité.** | Five specialists **form** the committee. |
| **La empresa debe ajustarse a las normas de seguridad.** | The company must **conform to** the safety standards. |
