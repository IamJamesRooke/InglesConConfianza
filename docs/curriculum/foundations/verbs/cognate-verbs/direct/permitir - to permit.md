# Verb Cognates: **-mit**

These Spanish and English cognates correspond to English verbs ending in **-mit**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| permitir | **to permit** | **El edificio no permite el ingreso de mascotas.**<br>The building does not permit pets to enter. |
| admitir | **to admit** | **El hospital va a admitir al paciente esta noche.**<br>The hospital is going to admit the patient tonight. |
| omitir | **to omit** | **No debes omitir información importante del informe.**<br>You should not omit important information from the report. |
| transmitir | **to transmit** | **Esta antena puede transmitir la señal a toda la región.**<br>This antenna can transmit the signal throughout the region. |
| cometer | **to commit** | **El tribunal determinó que el acusado había cometido un delito.**<br>The court determined that the defendant had committed a crime. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| emitir | **to emit** | **La fábrica no puede emitir gases tóxicos cerca de la ciudad.**<br>The factory cannot emit toxic gases near the city. |
| remitir | **to remit** | **La clínica va a remitir el pago al proveedor mañana.**<br>The clinic is going to remit payment to the supplier tomorrow. |
| someterse | **to submit** | **Todos los candidatos deben someterse a una verificación de antecedentes.**<br>All candidates must submit to a background check. |

## False Cognates

**Cometer** means **to commit** a crime or an offense. Spanish **cometer un error** is normally **to make a mistake**, not **to commit a mistake**. To commit yourself to a plan or responsibility, use **comprometerse**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Julián cometió un error en el informe.** | Julián **made a mistake** in the report. |
| **Julián se comprometió a terminar el informe hoy.** | Julián **committed himself** to finishing the report today. |
