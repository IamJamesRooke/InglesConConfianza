# Verb Cognates: **-ion**

In these cognates, remove the Spanish infinitive ending **-ar** to find the English verb ending in **-ion**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| funcionar | **to function** | **El sistema puede funcionar sin conexión a internet.**<br>The system can function without an internet connection. |
| mencionar | **to mention** | **Nadie mencionó el cambio durante la reunión.**<br>No one mentioned the change during the meeting. |
| cuestionar | **to question** | **Los periodistas comenzaron a cuestionar la versión oficial.**<br>The journalists began to question the official account. |
| posicionar | **to position** | **Debes posicionar la cámara frente a la ventana.**<br>You must position the camera in front of the window. |
