# Verb Cognates: **-ent**

In these cognates, remove the Spanish infinitive ending **-ar** to find the English verb ending in **-ent**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| presentar | **to present** | **Daniela va a presentar los resultados mañana.**<br>Daniela is going to present the results tomorrow. |
| representar | **to represent** | **Esta gráfica representa el crecimiento de las ventas.**<br>This graph represents the growth in sales. |
| inventar | **to invent** | **El equipo quiere inventar una herramienta más sencilla.**<br>The team wants to invent a simpler tool. |
| comentar | **to comment** | **El director no quiso comentar la decisión.**<br>The director did not want to comment on the decision. |
| experimentar | **to experiment** | **Los científicos van a experimentar con un material nuevo.**<br>The scientists are going to experiment with a new material. |

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| complementar | **to complement** | **La práctica diaria puede complementar las clases.**<br>Daily practice can complement the classes. |
| implementar | **to implement** | **La empresa va a implementar el nuevo sistema en agosto.**<br>The company is going to implement the new system in August. |
| orientar | **to orient** | **Este mapa ayuda a orientar a los visitantes.**<br>This map helps orient visitors. |

## False Cognates

**To complement** means **complementar** or **completar** something. **To compliment** means **felicitar** or **elogiar** someone.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **La salsa complementa el sabor del pescado.** | The sauce **complements** the flavor of the fish. |
| **El chef felicitó a su asistente por el plato.** | The chef **complimented** his assistant on the dish. |
