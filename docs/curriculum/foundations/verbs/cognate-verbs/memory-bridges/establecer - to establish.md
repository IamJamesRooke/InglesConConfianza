# Verb Cognates: **-lish**

These Spanish and English cognates correspond to English verbs ending in **-lish**.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| abolir | **to abolish** | **El Congreso votó para abolir la ley.**<br>Congress voted to abolish the law. |
| pulir | **to polish** | **El joyero va a pulir el anillo.**<br>The jeweler is going to polish the ring. |
| distinguir | **to distinguish** | **Es difícil distinguir los dos tonos con esta luz.**<br>It is difficult to distinguish the two shades in this light. |
| extinguir | **to extinguish** | **Los bomberos lograron extinguir el incendio.**<br>The firefighters managed to extinguish the fire. |
| establecer | **to establish** | **La empresa quiere establecer una oficina en Medellín.**<br>The company wants to establish an office in Medellín. |
| demoler | **to demolish** | **La ciudad decidió demoler el edificio abandonado.**<br>The city decided to demolish the abandoned building. |
| florecer | **to flourish** | **Estas plantas pueden florecer en climas cálidos.**<br>These plants can flourish in warm climates. |
| publicar | **to publish** | **La editorial va a publicar la novela en septiembre.**<br>The publisher is going to publish the novel in September. |
