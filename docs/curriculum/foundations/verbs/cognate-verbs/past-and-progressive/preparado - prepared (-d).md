# Regular **-ed** Pronounced `/d/`: **prepared**

After a vowel or voiced sound other than `/d/`, regular English **-ed** is pronounced `/d/`. It does not add another syllable.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| preparado | **prepared** | **Preparé la presentación anoche.**<br>I prepared the presentation last night. |
| confirmado | **confirmed** | **El hotel confirmó nuestra reservación ayer.**<br>The hotel confirmed our reservation yesterday. |

## Pronunciation

Attach `/d/` directly to the final sound of the base verb:

| Form | Pronunciation bridge |
|---|---|
| prepared `/prɪˈperd/` | **pre-PARED** — two syllables |
| confirmed `/kənˈfɝːmd/` | **con-FIRMD** — two syllables |

The written **e** in **-ed** is silent. Do not say `pre-par-ed`.

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| recibido | **received** | **Recibimos la confirmación esta mañana.**<br>We received the confirmation this morning. |
