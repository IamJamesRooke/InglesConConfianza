# Cognate Forms: Spanish **-tado / -tido** and English **-ted**

When a regular English verb ends in the sound `/t/`, its **-ed** ending is pronounced `/ɪd/`. This adds another syllable.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| visitado | **visited** | **Visitamos el Museo del Oro durante las vacaciones.**<br>We visited the Gold Museum during our vacation. |
| aceptado | **accepted** | **La universidad aceptó mi solicitud esta mañana.**<br>The university accepted my application this morning. |

## Pronunciation

The final **-ted** is pronounced `/tɪd/`:

| Form | Syllable bridge |
|---|---|
| visited `/ˈvɪz.ɪ.tɪd/` | **VIS-i-ted** — three syllables |
| accepted `/əkˈsep.tɪd/` | **ac-CEP-ted** — three syllables |

Do not pronounce **visited** as if it ended in a single `/t/` or `/d/` sound.

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| conectado | **connected** | **Conectamos el computador al proyector.**<br>We connected the computer to the projector. |
| contactado | **contacted** | **La empresa contactó a todos los candidatos.**<br>The company contacted every candidate. |
| permitido | **permitted** | **El museo permitió que los visitantes tomaran fotografías.**<br>The museum permitted visitors to take photographs. |
