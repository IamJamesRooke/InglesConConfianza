# Cognate Forms: Spanish **-dido** and English **-ded**

When a regular English verb ends in the sound `/d/`, its **-ed** ending is pronounced `/ɪd/`. This adds another syllable.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| decidido | **decided** | **Decidimos cambiar la fecha de la reunión.**<br>We decided to change the date of the meeting. |
| dividido | **divided** | **La profesora dividió la clase en cuatro grupos.**<br>The teacher divided the class into four groups. |

## Pronunciation

The final **-ded** is pronounced `/dɪd/`:

| Form | Syllable bridge |
|---|---|
| decided `/dɪˈsaɪ.dɪd/` | **de-CI-ded** — three syllables |
| divided `/dɪˈvaɪ.dɪd/` | **di-VI-ded** — three syllables |

Do not remove the middle vowel sound: **decided** has three syllables, not two.

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| incluido | **included** | **El precio incluyó el desayuno y los impuestos.**<br>The price included breakfast and taxes. |
| recomendado | **recommended** | **La médica recomendó un tratamiento diferente.**<br>The doctor recommended a different treatment. |
| extendido | **extended** | **El hotel extendió nuestra reservación por dos noches.**<br>The hotel extended our reservation by two nights. |
