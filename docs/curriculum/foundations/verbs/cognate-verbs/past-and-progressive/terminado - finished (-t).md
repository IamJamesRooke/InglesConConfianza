# Regular **-ed** Pronounced `/t/`: **finished**

After a voiceless sound other than `/t/`, regular English **-ed** is pronounced `/t/`. It does not add another syllable.

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| terminado | **finished** | **Terminamos el informe antes del almuerzo.**<br>We finished the report before lunch. |
| practicado | **practiced** | **Practicamos las preguntas antes de la entrevista.**<br>We practiced the questions before the interview. |

## Pronunciation

Attach `/t/` directly to the final sound of the base verb:

| Form | Pronunciation bridge |
|---|---|
| finished `/ˈfɪn.ɪʃt/` | **FIN-isht** — two syllables |
| practiced `/ˈpræk.tɪst/` | **PRAC-tist** — two syllables |

The written **e** in **-ed** is silent. Do not say `fin-ish-ed`.

## Additional Cognates

| Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| introducido | **introduced** | **La profesora introdujo un tema nuevo.**<br>The teacher introduced a new topic. |
| producido | **produced** | **La fábrica produjo mil unidades este mes.**<br>The factory produced one thousand units this month. |
| notado | **noticed** | **Noté un error en la última página.**<br>I noticed an error on the last page. |
