# Irregular Past: **Feel → Felt** and **Meet → Met**

In both families, the long vowel in the base form becomes the shorter vowel in the past:

- **feel → felt**
- **meet → met**

Both past forms finish with the sound `/t/`.

| Spanish | Base form | Past |
|---|---|---|
| sentirse, se sintió | **feel** | **felt** |
| conocer a alguien por primera vez | **meet somebody** | **met somebody** |

**Me sentí tranquilo cuando conocí a Laura.**<br>
I felt calm when I met Laura.
