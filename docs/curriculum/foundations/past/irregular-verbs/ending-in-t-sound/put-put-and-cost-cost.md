# Irregular Past: **Put → Put** and **Cost → Cost**

The past forms of **put** and **cost** do not change in spelling.

| Spanish | Base form | Past |
|---|---|---|
| poner, puso | **put** | **put** |
| costar, costó | **cost** | **cost** |

**Puse el libro sobre la mesa; costó veinte mil pesos.**<br>
I put the book on the table; it cost twenty thousand pesos.

Both past forms finish with the sound `/t/`.
