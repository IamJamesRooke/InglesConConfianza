# Irregular Past: **Say → Said** and **Read → Read**

Learn **said** and past **read** together. They both end in the sound `/ɛd/`.

| Spanish | Base verb | Past |
|---|---|---|
| decir, dijo algo | **say** | **said something** |
| leer, leyó | **read** `/riːd/` | **read** `/rɛd/` |

**Dije la respuesta después de leer el mensaje.**<br>
I said the answer after I read the message.

The spelling of **read** does not change, but its pronunciation does:

**read** `/riːd/` → **read** `/rɛd/`, pronounced like **red**
