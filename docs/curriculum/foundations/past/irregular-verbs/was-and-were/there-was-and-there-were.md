# **There Was** and **There Were**

Use **there was** and **there were** to say that something existed or was present in the past.

Spanish uses **había** for both singular and plural nouns. English changes the form.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| había + singular | **there was + singular** |
| había + plural | **there were + plural** |

**Había un libro sobre la mesa.**<br>
There was a book on the table.

**Había tres libros sobre la mesa.**<br>
There were three books on the table.
