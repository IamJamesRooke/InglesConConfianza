# **Was** and **Were** with **Ser** and **Estar**

English uses the same past forms of **be** where Spanish distinguishes **ser** and **estar**.

## **Was**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| fui, era | **I was** |
| fue, era | **he/she/it was** |
| estuve, estaba | **I was** |
| estuvo, estaba | **he/she/it was** |

**La reunión fue útil y estuve allí toda la mañana.**<br>
The meeting was useful, and I was there all morning.

## **Were**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| fuiste, eras; fue, era | **you were** |
| fuimos, éramos | **we were** |
| fueron, eran | **you/they were** |
| estuviste, estabas; estuvo, estaba | **you were** |
| estuvimos, estábamos | **we were** |
| estuvieron, estaban | **you/they were** |

**Las oficinas estaban abiertas.**<br>
The offices were open.
