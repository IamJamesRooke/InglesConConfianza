# **Was Going To** and **Were Going To**

Use **was/were going to + base verb** for a past plan or expectation.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| iba a hacer algo | **I/he/she was going to do something** |
| ibas a hacer algo; iba a hacer algo | **you were going to do something** |
| íbamos a hacer algo | **we were going to do something** |
| iban a hacer algo | **you/they were going to do something** |

**Iba a terminar el informe ayer.**<br>
I was going to finish the report yesterday.

**Íbamos a terminar el informe juntos.**<br>
We were going to finish the report together.
