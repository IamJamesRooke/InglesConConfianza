# **Was Able To**, **Were Able To**, and **Could**

Use **was/were able to + base verb** or **could + base verb** to express past ability.

## **Was/Were able to**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| podía hacer algo | **I/he/she was able to do something** |
| podías hacer algo; podía hacer algo | **you were able to do something** |
| podíamos hacer algo | **we were able to do something** |
| podían hacer algo | **you/they were able to do something** |

**Podía explicar la actividad en inglés.**<br>
I was able to explain the activity in English.

## **Could**

**Can** changes to **could** in the past. **Could** does not change with the subject and does not use **to** before the next verb.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| podía, podían hacer algo | **could do something** |

**Podíamos explicar la actividad en inglés.**<br>
We could explain the activity in English.

**We could do it.**<br>
Not: *We could to do it.*
