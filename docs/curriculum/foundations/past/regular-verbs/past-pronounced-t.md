# Regular Past Pronounced `/t/`

When a regular past form does not end in **-ted** or **-ded**, the written **e** is silent. The verbs in this lesson end with the sound `/t/`.

## **Asked**, **walked**, and **talked**

Learn these similar-sounding forms together:

| Spanish | English | Pronunciation bridge |
|---|---|---|
| preguntó | **asked** | ask + `/t/` |
| caminó | **walked** | walk + `/t/` |
| habló | **talked** | talk + `/t/` |

**Pregunté dónde estaba la oficina.**<br>
I asked where the office was.

The same pronunciation remains when **ask** is part of a longer pattern:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| pidió algo | **asked for something** |

## **Liked**, **looked**, and **worked**

| Spanish | English | Pronunciation bridge |
|---|---|---|
| gustó | **liked** | like + `/t/` |
| miró | **looked at** | look + `/t/` |
| buscó | **looked for** | look + `/t/` |
| trabajó, funcionó | **worked** | work + `/t/` |

**Me gustó la foto y la miré otra vez.**<br>
I liked the photo and looked at it again.

## **Finished**

The final sound `/ʃ/` joins directly to `/t/`:

| Spanish | English | Pronunciation bridge |
|---|---|---|
| terminó | **finished** | finish + `/t/` → “finisht” |

**Terminé el informe ayer.**<br>
I finished the report yesterday.

## **Helped**

The final sound `/p/` joins directly to `/t/`:

| Spanish | English | Pronunciation bridge |
|---|---|---|
| ayudó | **helped** | help + `/t/` → “helpt” |

**Ayudé a Laura con el informe.**<br>
I helped Laura with the report.

## **Introduced**

The final sound `/s/` joins directly to `/t/`:

| Spanish | English | Pronunciation bridge |
|---|---|---|
| presentó, introdujo | **introduced** | introduce + `/t/` |

**Presenté a Laura al equipo.**<br>
I introduced Laura to the team.

Do not add another syllable: **walked** is one syllable and **finished** is two.

## Cognate reinforcement

These examples come from different cognate categories and follow the same `/t/` rule:

| Cognate category | Spanish | English | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| spelling patterns | introducir | **introduced** | **Introdujimos un tema nuevo.**<br>We introduced a new topic. |
| word families | comprimir | **compressed** | **Comprimimos el archivo antes de enviarlo.**<br>We compressed the file before sending it. |
| memory bridges | practicar | **practiced** | **Practicamos las preguntas antes de la entrevista.**<br>We practiced the questions before the interview. |
