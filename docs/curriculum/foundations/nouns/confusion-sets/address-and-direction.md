# **Address** and **Direction**

Spanish **dirección** has two common English translations.

| Meaning | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| location information | Necesito la dirección de la oficina. | I need the office address. |
| way or orientation | La oficina está en esa dirección. | The office is in that direction. |

Use **address** for the information needed to locate a home, office, or website. Use **direction** for the way something faces or the route toward it.

Do not ask for *the direction of the office* when you need its street address.
