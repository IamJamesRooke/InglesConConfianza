# Decimals and Phone Numbers

Number formats are not always read like ordinary quantities.

## Alternative reading of hundreds

For 1,500, both forms are natural:

- **one thousand five hundred**
- **fifteen hundred**

The second pattern is common for prices, addresses, and numbers between 1,100 and 9,999 that divide naturally into hundreds.

## Decimals

Say **point**, then read each digit separately:

**3.14159 → three point one four one five nine**

Spanish decimal comma normally becomes a decimal point in English writing: **3,14 → 3.14**.

## Phone numbers

Read phone numbers digit by digit:

**5718 → five seven one eight**

Zero may be pronounced **zero** or **oh**. Repeated digits may be read separately or with **double**: **55 → five five / double five**.
