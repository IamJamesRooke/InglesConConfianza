# Fractions

Use **half** for one of two equal parts. Use an ordinal number for many other fractions:

| Fraction | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| 1/2 | la mitad | half / one half |
| 1/3 | un tercio | a third / one third |
| 1/4 | un cuarto | a quarter / one fourth |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Leí la mitad del libro. | I read half the book. / I read half of the book. |
| Leí un tercio del libro. | I read a third of the book. |
| Leí un cuarto del libro. | I read a quarter of the book. |

Do not normally say *a half of the book*. Use **half the book** or **half of the book**.

For a numerator larger than one, make the denominator plural: **two thirds, three quarters**.
