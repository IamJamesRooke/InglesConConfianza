# **Sometime, Anytime, No Time**, and **Every Time**

| Form | Core meaning | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| sometime | at an unspecified time | Llámame algún día la próxima semana. | Call me sometime next week. |
| anytime | whenever; at any time | Puedes llamarme cuando quieras. | You can call me anytime. |
| no time | no available time | Hoy no tengo tiempo para almorzar. | I have no time for lunch today. |
| every time | on every occasion | Ella sonríe cada vez que escucha la canción. | She smiles every time she hears the song. |

## Spelling contrasts

- **sometime** = at an unspecified time: **Let's meet sometime.**
- **some time** = a period of time: **The repair will take some time.**
- **sometimes** = on some occasions: **I sometimes walk to work.**

**Anytime** is common as an adverb. The two-word form remains standard after **at**: **Call me at any time**.

Always write **no time** and **every time** as two words. Do not write *notime* or *everytime*.
