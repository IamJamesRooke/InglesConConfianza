# **Somewhere, Anywhere, Nowhere**, and **Everywhere**

Use this family to talk about places without naming the place.

| Form | Core meaning | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| somewhere | an unspecified place | Laura está en algún lugar tranquilo. | Laura is somewhere quiet. |
| anywhere | a place in a question or negative | ¿Podemos reunirnos en algún lugar? | Can we meet anywhere? |
| nowhere | no place | No hay dónde sentarse. | There is nowhere to sit. |
| everywhere | every place | Busqué las llaves por todas partes. | I looked everywhere for the keys. |

In ordinary negative English, use either **not + anywhere** or **nowhere**, not both:

- We **didn't go anywhere**.
- We **went nowhere**.
- Not: *We didn't go nowhere.*

**Anywhere** can also mean any possible place:

**Puedes sentarte en cualquier lugar. → You can sit anywhere.**

All four place compounds are written as one word.
