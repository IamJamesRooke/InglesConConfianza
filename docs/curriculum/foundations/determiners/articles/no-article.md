# No Article

English often uses no article when speaking generally about plural countable nouns or uncountable ideas.

| General meaning | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| plural category | Me gustan los gatos. | I like cats. |
| uncountable idea | Necesito información. | I need information. |

Use **the** when the meaning is specific:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me gustan los gatos de Laura. | I like Laura's cats. |
| Necesito la información del documento. | I need the information from the document. |

The absence of a determiner is sometimes called the **zero article**.
