# Possessive Determiners

Possessive determiners identify whose noun it is.

| Person | Determiner | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | my | my cat |
| you | your | your cat |
| he | his | his cat |
| she | her | her cat |
| it | its | its name |
| we | our | our cat |
| they | their | their cat |

They do not change for the gender or number of the thing possessed:

- **her cat / her cats**
- **his office / his offices**

Do not confuse **its** with **it's**:

- **its name** = su nombre
- **it's here** = it is here

A possessive determiner needs a noun. The corresponding possessive pronoun stands alone: **my cat → mine**.
