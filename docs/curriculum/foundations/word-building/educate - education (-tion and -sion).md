# Word Building: English **-tion** and **-sion**

English often changes a verb ending to **-tion** or **-sion** to form the related noun. The exact spelling must be learned with each family.

| Verb | Noun | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| educate | **education** | educación |
| organize | **organization** | organización |
| communicate | **communication** | comunicación |
| decide | **decision** | decisión |
| conclude | **conclusion** | conclusión |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **La educación puede transformar una comunidad.** | **Education** can transform a community. |
| **La organización confirmó su decisión esta mañana.** | The **organization** confirmed its **decision** this morning. |
| **La evidencia respalda nuestra conclusión.** | The evidence supports our **conclusion**. |
