# Word Building: English Nouns with **-ing**

English can use a verb's **-ing** form as the name of an activity, process, or event. Spanish often uses a separate noun.

| Spanish verb | English verb | Spanish noun | English **-ing** noun |
|---|---|---|---|
| leer | to read | lectura | **reading** |
| escribir | to write | escritura | **writing** |
| abrir | to open | apertura | **opening** |
| cerrar | to close | cierre, clausura | **closing** |
| comprender | to understand | comprensión | **understanding** |
| reunirse | to meet | reunión | **meeting** |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **La lectura diaria ayuda a ampliar el vocabulario.** | Daily **reading** helps expand your vocabulary. |
| **La apertura del nuevo hospital será en agosto.** | The **opening** of the new hospital will be in August. |
| **La reunión comienza a las nueve.** | The **meeting** begins at nine. |

The article helps reveal a countable event: **the meeting** means **la reunión**, not the action **reuniéndose**.
