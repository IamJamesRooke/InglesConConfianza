# Word Building: English **-ness**

Add **-ness** to many adjectives to form a noun describing their quality or state.

| Adjective | **-ness** noun | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| happy | **happiness** | felicidad |
| kind | **kindness** | amabilidad |
| dark | **darkness** | oscuridad |
| weak | **weakness** | debilidad |
| aware | **awareness** | conciencia, conocimiento |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Su felicidad aumentó cuando encontró un trabajo estable.** | Her **happiness** increased when she found a stable job. |
| **Nunca olvidé la amabilidad de aquella enfermera.** | I never forgot that nurse's **kindness**. |
| **La campaña busca crear conciencia sobre el fraude digital.** | The campaign aims to raise **awareness** of digital fraud. |

## Spelling

Change final **y** to **i** before **-ness**: **happy → happiness**.
