# Word Building: English **-ship**

English **-ship** forms nouns describing relationships, roles, abilities, or states. Spanish expresses these ideas with several different words and endings.

| Base word | **-ship** noun | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| friend | **friendship** | amistad |
| leader | **leadership** | liderazgo |
| member | **membership** | membresía |
| champion | **championship** | campeonato |
| owner | **ownership** | propiedad |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Nuestra amistad comenzó en la universidad.** | Our **friendship** began at university. |
| **Su liderazgo ayudó al equipo durante la crisis.** | Her **leadership** helped the team during the crisis. |
| **La membresía incluye acceso a todas las instalaciones.** | The **membership** includes access to all the facilities. |

## Additional Examples

| Base word | **-ship** noun | Example |
|---|---|---|
| partner | **partnership** | **Las dos empresas formaron una sociedad para desarrollar el proyecto.**<br>The two companies formed a partnership to develop the project. |
| relation | **relationship** | **La relación entre las dos familias mejoró con el tiempo.**<br>The relationship between the two families improved over time. |
| citizen | **citizenship** | **Ella solicitó la ciudadanía después de vivir diez años en el país.**<br>She applied for citizenship after living in the country for ten years. |
