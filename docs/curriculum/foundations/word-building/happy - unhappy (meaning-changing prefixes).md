# Word Building: Meaning-Changing Prefixes

English prefixes can reverse, repeat, oppose, misdirect, increase, or reduce the meaning of a familiar word.

| Prefix | Meaning | Transformation | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| **un-** | not, opposite | happy → **unhappy** | feliz → infeliz |
| **re-** | again | write → **rewrite** | escribir → reescribir |
| **dis-** | not, opposite | agree → **disagree** | estar de acuerdo → estar en desacuerdo |
| **mis-** | wrongly | understand → **misunderstand** | entender → malinterpretar |
| **over-** | too much | cook → **overcook** | cocinar → cocinar demasiado |
| **under-** | too little | estimate → **underestimate** | estimar → subestimar |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **Andrés está inconforme con el resultado.** | Andrés is **unhappy** with the result. |
| **Necesito reescribir el último párrafo.** | I need to **rewrite** the final paragraph. |
| **No subestimes el tiempo necesario para terminar.** | Do not **underestimate** the time needed to finish. |

## Additional Examples

| Transformation | Spanish | Example |
|---|---|---|
| connect → **disconnect** | desconectar | **Desconecta el equipo antes de limpiarlo.**<br>Disconnect the equipment before cleaning it. |
| inform → **misinform** | desinformar | **La publicación puede desinformar a los lectores.**<br>The post may misinform readers. |
| charge → **overcharge** | cobrar de más | **El restaurante nos cobró de más por error.**<br>The restaurant overcharged us by mistake. |
