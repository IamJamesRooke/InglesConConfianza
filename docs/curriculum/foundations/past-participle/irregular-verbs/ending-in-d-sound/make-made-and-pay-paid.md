# Irregular Past Participles: **Made** and **Paid**

Learn **made** and **paid** together. Both participles end in the sound `/eɪd/`.

| Spanish | Base form | Simple past | Past participle |
|---|---|---|---|
| hacer, hecho | **make** | **made** | **made** |
| pagar, pagado | **pay** | **paid** | **paid** |

**He hecho la reserva y he pagado el depósito.**<br>
I have made the reservation and paid the deposit.
