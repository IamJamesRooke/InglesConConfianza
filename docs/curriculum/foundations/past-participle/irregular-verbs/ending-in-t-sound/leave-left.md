# Irregular Past Participle: **Leave → Left**

Use **left** for both leaving a place and leaving something in a place.

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| salir de un lugar, salido | **leave a place** | **left a place** |
| dejar algo, dejado | **leave something** | **left something** |

**Laura ha salido de la oficina, pero ha dejado las llaves en la recepción.**<br>
Laura has left the office, but she has left the keys at reception.

**Left** ends with the sound `/t/`.
