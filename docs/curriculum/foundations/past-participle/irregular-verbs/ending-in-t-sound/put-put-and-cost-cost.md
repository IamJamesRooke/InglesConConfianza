# Irregular Past Participles: **Put** and **Cost**

The participles of **put** and **cost** do not change in spelling.

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| poner, puesto | **put** | **put** |
| costar, costado | **cost** | **cost** |

**He puesto el libro sobre la mesa; las reparaciones han costado mucho dinero.**<br>
I have put the book on the table; the repairs have cost a lot of money.

Both participles finish with the sound `/t/`.
