# Irregular Past Participles: **Gotten** and **Forgotten**

The participle **forgotten** contains the same final form as **gotten**:

- **get → got → gotten**
- **forget → forgot → forgotten**

This course uses American English, where **gotten** is the usual participle for many meanings involving receiving, obtaining, reaching, or changing state.

## Meanings of **gotten**

| Spanish | Base form | Simple past | Past participle |
|---|---|---|---|
| conseguir, conseguido | **get** | **got** | **gotten** |
| obtener, obtenido | **get** | **got** | **gotten** |
| recibir, recibido | **get** | **got** | **gotten** |
| llegar a un lugar | **get to a place** | **got to a place** | **gotten to a place** |
| levantarse, levantado | **get up** | **got up** | **gotten up** |

**He recibido el mensaje y he llegado a la oficina.**<br>
I have gotten the message and gotten to the office.

**Laura ya se ha levantado.**<br>
Laura has already gotten up.

## **Forget → Forgotten**

| Spanish | Base form | Simple past | Past participle |
|---|---|---|---|
| olvidar, olvidado | **forget** | **forgot** | **forgotten** |

**He olvidado responder el mensaje.**<br>
I have forgotten to respond to the message.
