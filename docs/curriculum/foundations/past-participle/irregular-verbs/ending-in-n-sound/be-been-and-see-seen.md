# Irregular Past Participles: **Been** and **Seen**

The participles **been** and **seen** share the written ending **-een** and finish with `/n/`.

## **Be → Been**

Use **been** where Spanish uses a participle of **ser** or **estar**.

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| ser, sido | **be** | **been** |
| estar, estado | **be** | **been** |

**He sido profesor durante diez años y he estado en Bogotá todo este tiempo.**<br>
I have been a teacher for ten years and have been in Bogotá all this time.

Use **been able to + base verb** as the participle construction for ability:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| podido hacer algo | **been able to do something** |

**He podido explicar la actividad en inglés.**<br>
I have been able to explain the activity in English.

## **See → Seen**

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| ver, visto | **see** | **seen** |

**He visto a Laura en la oficina esta semana.**<br>
I have seen Laura in the office this week.

The pronunciation of **been** varies by accent, but both forms always finish with `/n/`.
