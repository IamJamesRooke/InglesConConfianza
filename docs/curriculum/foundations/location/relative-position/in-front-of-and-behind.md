# **In Front Of** and **Behind**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| delante de / frente a | in front of |
| detrás de | behind |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Estoy frente a la oficina. | I am in front of the office. |
| El parque está detrás de la oficina. | The park is behind the office. |

Use the complete expression **in front of**. Do not omit **of** before the place.
