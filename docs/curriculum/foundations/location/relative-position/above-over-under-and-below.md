# **Above, Over, Under**, and **Below**

| Spanish | English | Core relationship |
|---|---|---|
| encima de / arriba de | above | higher than |
| sobre / por encima de | over | higher than or covering |
| debajo de | under | directly lower or covered |
| debajo de / más abajo de | below | lower than |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La oficina está arriba de la tienda. | The office is above the store. |
| La lámpara está sobre la mesa. | The lamp is over the table. |
| El bolso está debajo de la mesa. | The bag is under the table. |
| La tienda está debajo de la oficina. | The store is below the office. |

For the simplest physical contrast, learn **above / below** for relative height and **over / under** when direct position or covering matters.
