# **Inside** and **Outside**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| adentro / dentro | inside |
| afuera / fuera | outside |
| dentro de un lugar | inside a place |
| fuera de un lugar | outside a place |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Estoy adentro. | I am inside. |
| Estoy afuera. | I am outside. |
| Laura está dentro de la oficina. | Laura is inside the office. |
| Diana está fuera de la oficina. | Diana is outside the office. |

**Inside** and **outside** can stand alone or go directly before a place. They do not require **of** in these foundational patterns.
