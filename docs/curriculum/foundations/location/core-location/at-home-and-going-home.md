# **At Home** and **Going Home**

**Home** has two essential patterns:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| en casa | at home |
| a casa | home |
| de casa | from home |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Estoy en casa. | I am at home. |
| Voy a casa. | I am going home. |
| Trabajo desde casa. | I work from home. |

Do not use **to** before **home** after a movement verb: **go home**, not *go to home*.
