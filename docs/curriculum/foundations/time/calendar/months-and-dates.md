# Months and Dates

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| enero | January |
| febrero | February |
| marzo | March |
| abril | April |
| mayo | May |
| junio | June |
| julio | July |
| agosto | August |
| septiembre | September |
| octubre | October |
| noviembre | November |
| diciembre | December |

English months begin with a capital letter. Use **in** with a month or year and **on** with a specific date:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La reunión es en agosto. | The meeting is in August. |
| El curso empieza en 2027. | The course starts in 2027. |
| La reunión es el 28 de agosto. | The meeting is on August 28th. |

In American English, say **August twenty-eighth** for **August 28th**.
