# Translations of **De**

Spanish **de** can express material, contents, purpose, possession, or origin. English does not always use **of**.

## **Of**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| una taza de café | a cup of coffee |
| el nombre de la tienda | the name of the store |

## One noun describing another

English often places the describing noun first:

| Spanish | English | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| leche de chocolate | chocolate milk | milk with chocolate flavor |
| chocolate de leche | milk chocolate | chocolate made with milk |
| taza de café | coffee cup | a cup made for coffee |
| taza de café | cup of coffee | a cup containing coffee |

## Possession

For people and other possessors, English often uses **'s**:

**La leche de chocolate de John.**<br>
John's chocolate milk.

Use context to decide whether **de** describes contents, purpose, type, or possession.
