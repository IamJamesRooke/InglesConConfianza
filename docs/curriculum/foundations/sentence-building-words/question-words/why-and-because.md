# **Why** and **Because**

| Spanish | English | Function |
|---|---|---|
| ¿por qué? | why? | asks for a reason |
| porque | because | gives a reason |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| ¿Por qué lo necesitas? | Why do you need it? |
| Porque tengo que terminar el informe. | Because I have to finish the report. |

In Spanish, the question is written **por qué** and the answer connector is **porque**. English uses two different words: **why** and **because**.
