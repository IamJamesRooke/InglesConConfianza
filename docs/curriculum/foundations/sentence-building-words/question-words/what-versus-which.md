# **What** Versus **Which**

Use **what** for an open question and **which** when choosing from a limited or understood set.

| Situation | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| open information | ¿Qué quieres? | What do you want? |
| open category | ¿Qué libro necesitas? | What book do you need? |
| known alternatives | ¿Cuál libro quieres, este o ese? | Which book do you want, this one or that one? |

Spanish **qué** and **cuál** do not map mechanically to English **what** and **which**. The size and clarity of the choice set determine the natural English word.
