# Fluency Drills: Perfect Auxiliaries

The perfect forms use **have, has**, or **had + past participle**:

| Subject or time | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **I, you, we, they** in the present | **have** | You **have done** it. |
| **he, she, it** in the present | **has** | He **has done** it. |
| every subject in the past | **had** | They **had done** it. |

In a direct question, move **have, has**, or **had** before the subject. In an embedded clause, keep statement order.
