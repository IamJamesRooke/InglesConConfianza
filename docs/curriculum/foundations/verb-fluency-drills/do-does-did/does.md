# Fluency Drill: **Does**

Use **does** with **he, she**, and **it** in present questions, emphasis, and negations.

| Drill step | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| embedded **if** clause | Quiero saber si él lo hace. | I want to know if he does it. |
| direct question | ¿Lo hace? | Does he do it? |
| affirmative answer | Sí, lo hace. | Yes, he does it. |
| emphatic affirmative | Sí, sí lo hace. | Yes, he **does** do it. |
| negative answer | No, no lo hace. | No, he doesn't do it. |
| negative **why** question | ¿Por qué no lo hace? | Why doesn't he do it? |
| embedded **why** clause | Quiero saber por qué no lo hace. | I want to know why he doesn't do it. |

The third-person marker moves to **does**, so the main verb returns to base **do**.

The auxiliary **does** is stressed because the speaker is correcting doubt or contradiction.

The embedded clause uses statement order: **why he doesn't**, not *why doesn't he*.
