# Fluency Drill: **Do**

Use **do** with **I, you, we**, and **they** in present questions, emphasis, and negations.

| Drill step | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| embedded **if** clause | Quiero saber si tú lo haces. | I want to know if you do it. |
| direct question | ¿Lo haces? | Do you do it? |
| affirmative answer | Sí, lo hago. | Yes, I do it. |
| emphatic affirmative | Sí, sí lo hago. | Yes, I **do** do it. |
| negative answer | No, no lo hago. | No, I don't do it. |
| negative **why** question | ¿Por qué no lo haces? | Why don't you do it? |
| embedded **why** clause | Quiero saber por qué no lo haces. | I want to know why you don't do it. |

The first **do** is stressed because the speaker is correcting doubt or contradiction.

The embedded clause uses statement order: **why you don't**, not *why don't you*.
