# Fluency Drill: **Will**

Use **will + base verb** to talk about the future.

| Drill step | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| embedded **if** clause | Quiero saber si tú lo harás. | I want to know if you will do it. |
| direct question | ¿Lo harás? | Will you do it? |
| affirmative answer | Sí, lo haré. | Yes, I will do it. |
| negative answer | No, no lo haré. | No, I won't do it. |
| negative **why** question | ¿Por qué no lo harás? | Why won't you do it? |
| embedded **why** clause | Quiero saber por qué no lo harás. | I want to know why you won't do it. |

The direct question uses **will you**. The embedded clauses use statement order: **if you will** and **why you won't**.
