# Fluency Drill: **Can**

Use **can + base verb** to talk about ability or possibility.

| Drill step | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| embedded **if** clause | Quiero saber si lo puedes hacer. | I want to know if you can do it. |
| direct question | ¿Lo puedes hacer? | Can you do it? |
| affirmative answer | Sí, lo puedo hacer. | Yes, I can do it. |
| negative answer | No, no lo puedo hacer. | No, I can't do it. |
| negative **why** question | ¿Por qué no lo puedes hacer? | Why can't you do it? |
| embedded **why** clause | Quiero saber por qué no lo puedes hacer. | I want to know why you can't do it. |

The direct question uses **can you**. The embedded clauses use statement order: **if you can** and **why you can't**.
