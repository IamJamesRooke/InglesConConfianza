# Fluency Drill: **Is He Doing It?**

Use **is + verb-ing** for an action that **he** is doing now or around now.

| Drill step | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| embedded **if** clause | Quiero saber si él lo está haciendo. | I want to know if he is doing it. |
| direct question | ¿Lo está haciendo? | Is he doing it? |
| affirmative answer | Sí, lo está haciendo. | Yes, he is doing it. |
| negative answer | No, no lo está haciendo. | No, he isn't doing it. |
| negative **why** question | ¿Por qué no lo está haciendo? | Why isn't he doing it? |
| embedded **why** clause | Quiero saber por qué no lo está haciendo. | I want to know why he isn't doing it. |

The direct question uses inversion: **is he**.

The embedded clauses use statement order: **if he is** and **why he isn't**.
