# Fluency Drill: **Was He Going to Do It?**

Use **was going to + base verb** to ask about **his** past plan or intention.

| Drill step | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| embedded **if** clause | Quiero saber si él lo iba a hacer. | I want to know if he was going to do it. |
| direct question | ¿Lo iba a hacer? | Was he going to do it? |
| affirmative answer | Sí, lo iba a hacer. | Yes, he was going to do it. |
| negative answer | No, no lo iba a hacer. | No, he wasn't going to do it. |
| negative **why** question | ¿Por qué no lo iba a hacer? | Why wasn't he going to do it? |
| embedded **why** clause | Quiero saber por qué no lo iba a hacer. | I want to know why he wasn't going to do it. |

The direct question uses inversion: **was he going to**.

The embedded clauses use statement order: **if he was going to** and **why he wasn't going to**.
