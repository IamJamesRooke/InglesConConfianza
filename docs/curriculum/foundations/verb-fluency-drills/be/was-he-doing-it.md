# Fluency Drill: **Was He Doing It?**

Use **was + verb-ing** for an action that **he** was doing at a past moment.

| Drill step | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| embedded **if** clause | Quiero saber si él lo estaba haciendo. | I want to know if he was doing it. |
| direct question | ¿Lo estaba haciendo? | Was he doing it? |
| affirmative answer | Sí, lo estaba haciendo. | Yes, he was doing it. |
| negative answer | No, no lo estaba haciendo. | No, he wasn't doing it. |
| negative **why** question | ¿Por qué no lo estaba haciendo? | Why wasn't he doing it? |
| embedded **why** clause | Quiero saber por qué no lo estaba haciendo. | I want to know why he wasn't doing it. |

The direct question uses inversion: **was he**.

The embedded clauses use statement order: **if he was** and **why he wasn't**.
