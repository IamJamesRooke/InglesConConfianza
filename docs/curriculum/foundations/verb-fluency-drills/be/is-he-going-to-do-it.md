# Fluency Drill: **Is He Going to Do It?**

Use **is going to + base verb** to ask about **his** plan or intention.

| Drill step | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| embedded **if** clause | Quiero saber si él lo va a hacer. | I want to know if he is going to do it. |
| direct question | ¿Lo va a hacer? | Is he going to do it? |
| affirmative answer | Sí, lo va a hacer. | Yes, he is going to do it. |
| negative answer | No, no lo va a hacer. | No, he isn't going to do it. |
| negative **why** question | ¿Por qué no lo va a hacer? | Why isn't he going to do it? |
| embedded **why** clause | Quiero saber por qué no lo va a hacer. | I want to know why he isn't going to do it. |

The direct question uses inversion: **is he going to**.

The embedded clauses use statement order: **if he is going to** and **why he isn't going to**.
