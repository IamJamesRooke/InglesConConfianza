# Fluency Drill: **Are You Going to Do It?**

Use **are going to + base verb** to ask **you** about a plan or intention.

| Drill step | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| embedded **if** clause | Quiero saber si tú lo vas a hacer. | I want to know if you are going to do it. |
| direct question | ¿Lo vas a hacer? | Are you going to do it? |
| affirmative answer | Sí, lo voy a hacer. | Yes, I'm going to do it. |
| negative answer | No, no lo voy a hacer. | No, I'm not going to do it. |
| negative **why** question | ¿Por qué no lo vas a hacer? | Why aren't you going to do it? |
| embedded **why** clause | Quiero saber por qué no lo vas a hacer. | I want to know why you aren't going to do it. |

The direct question uses inversion: **are you going to**.

The embedded clauses use statement order: **if you are going to** and **why you aren't going to**.
