# The Three Jobs of **Have**

The word **have** appears in three foundational structures. The words after it determine which question pattern to use.

| Job | Statement | Question | Negative |
|---|---|---|---|
| possession: **have + thing** | She has it. | Does she have it? | She doesn't have it. |
| obligation: **have to + base verb** | You have to do it. | Do you have to do it? | You don't have to do it. |
| perfect: **have + past participle** | You have done it. | Have you done it? | You haven't done it. |

With possession and **have to**, use **do, does**, or **did** to build the question or negative. With the perfect form, **have** itself moves before the subject.

Compare the meanings:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ella lo tiene. | She has it. |
| Tienes que hacerlo. | You have to do it. |
| Lo has hecho. | You have done it. |

**You don't have to do it** means **No tienes que hacerlo / No es necesario hacerlo**. It does not mean that doing it is prohibited.
