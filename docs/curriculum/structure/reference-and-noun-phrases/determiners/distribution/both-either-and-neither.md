# **Both, Either**, and **Neither**

Use these determiners when there are exactly two options.

| Spanish | English | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ambos / los dos | both | the two together |
| cualquiera de los dos | either | one or the other |
| ninguno de los dos | neither | not one and not the other |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ambos libros son útiles. | Both books are useful. |
| Cualquiera de los dos libros está bien. | Either book is fine. |
| Ninguno de los dos libros está disponible. | Neither book is available. |

Use a plural noun after **both**, but a singular noun after determiner **either** and **neither**.
