# Uses of “Them”

English uses `them` for plural people, animals and objects.

Spanish changes the form depending on:

- whether `them` is a direct or indirect object;
- the grammatical gender of the people, animals or objects;
- whether the group is masculine, feminine or mixed;
- whether `them` follows a preposition;
- whether it identifies the recipient in a combined-pronoun construction.

# Direct Object

## Masculine or Mixed: Los

`Los` represents masculine plural direct objects and mixed groups.

| Reference | Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 👨👨 los hombres | Veo a los hombres. | Los veo. | I see the men. I see them. |
| 👨👩 el hombre y la mujer | Veo al hombre y a la mujer. | Los veo. | I see the man and woman. I see them. |
| 🐕️🐕️ los perros | Veo a los perros. | Los veo. | I see the dogs. I see them. |
| 🤖🤖 los robots | Veo los robots. | Los veo. | I see the robots. I see them. |
| 📚️ los libros | Veo los libros. | Los veo. | I see the books. I see them. |

## Feminine: Las

`Las` represents feminine plural direct objects.

| Reference | Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 👩👩 las mujeres | Veo a las mujeres. | Las veo. | I see the women. I see them. |
| 🐕️🐕️ las perras | Veo a las perras. | Las veo. | I see the dogs. I see them. |
| las mesas | Veo las mesas. | Las veo. | I see the tables. I see them. |
| las casas | Veo las casas. | Las veo. | I see the houses. I see them. |

## Direct-Object Minimal Difference

| Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Veo a los hombres. | Los veo. | I see them. |
| Veo a las mujeres. | Las veo. | I see them. |
| Veo los libros. | Los veo. | I see them. |
| Veo las mesas. | Las veo. | I see them. |

English uses `them` in every sentence.

Spanish distinguishes masculine or mixed groups with `los` and feminine groups with `las`.

# Indirect Object: Les

`Les` represents plural recipients. It does not indicate gender.

| Reference | Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 👨👨 los hombres | Digo la verdad a los hombres. | Les digo la verdad. | I tell the men the truth. I tell them the truth. |
| 👩👩 las mujeres | Digo la verdad a las mujeres. | Les digo la verdad. | I tell the women the truth. I tell them the truth. |
| 👨👩 mixed group | Doy el libro al hombre y a la mujer. | Les doy el libro. | I give the man and woman the book. I give them the book. |
| 🐕️🐕️ los perros | Doy comida a los perros. | Les doy comida. | I give the dogs food. I give them food. |
| 🤖🤖 los robots | Doy instrucciones a los robots. | Les doy instrucciones. | I give the robots instructions. I give them instructions. |

`Les` can refer to:

- men;
- women;
- mixed groups;
- animals;
- objects or organizations.

Context identifies the recipients.

| Grammatical role | Spanish | English |

# After a Preposition

Spanish uses `ellos` for masculine or mixed groups and `ellas` for feminine groups.

## Para Ellos and Para Ellas

| Full context | Pronoun form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Hago el café para los hombres. | Lo hago para ellos. | I make the coffee for the men. I make it for them. |
| Hago el café para el hombre y la mujer. | Lo hago para ellos. | I make the coffee for the man and woman. I make it for them. |
| Hago el café para las mujeres. | Lo hago para ellas. | I make the coffee for the women. I make it for them. |

# Combined Object Pronouns: Se

When `les` appears before `lo`, `la`, `los` or `las`, it changes to `se`.

In these constructions, `se` can mean `to them`.

## Se Lo

| Full context | Combined form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Doy el libro a los hombres. | Se lo doy a ellos. | I give the men the book. I give it to them. |
| Doy el libro a las mujeres. | Se lo doy a ellas. | I give the women the book. I give it to them. |
| Muestro el robot a los estudiantes. | Se lo muestro a ellos. | I show the students the robot. I show it to them. |

## Se La

| Full context | Combined form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Doy la llave a los hombres. | Se la doy a ellos. | I give the men the key. I give it to them. |
| Doy la llave a las mujeres. | Se la doy a ellas. | I give the women the key. I give it to them. |
| Explico la respuesta a los estudiantes. | Se la explico a ellos. | I explain the answer to the students. I explain it to them. |

## Se Los

| Full context | Combined form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Doy los libros a los hombres. | Se los doy a ellos. | I give the men the books. I give them to them. |
| Doy los libros a las mujeres. | Se los doy a ellas. | I give the women the books. I give them to them. |
| Muestro los robots a los estudiantes. | Se los muestro a ellos. | I show the students the robots. I show them to them. |

## Se Las

| Full context | Combined form | English |
|---|---|---|
| Doy las llaves a los hombres. | Se las doy a ellos. | I give the men the keys. I give them to them. |
| Doy las llaves a las mujeres. | Se las doy a ellas. | I give the women the keys. I give them to them. |
| Explico las respuestas a los estudiantes. | Se las explico a ellos. | I explain the answers to the students. I explain them to them. |

The phrases `a ellos` and `a ellas` clarify that `se` means `to them`.

# Them Versus Themselves

English distinguishes `them` from `themselves`.

Spanish distinguishes normal direct objects from reflexive pronouns.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Los hombres ven a los niños. Los ven. | The men see the children. They see them. |
| Los hombres se ven en el espejo. | The men see themselves in the mirror. |
| Las mujeres ayudan a los estudiantes. Las ayudan. | The women help the students. They help them. |
| Las mujeres se ayudan. | The women help themselves. |

For normal direct objects:

- `los` represents masculine or mixed `them`;
- `las` represents feminine `them`.

For reflexive actions:

- `se` represents `themselves`.

# Reciprocal Se

`Se` can also mean `each other`.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| El hombre y la mujer se ven. | The man and woman see each other. |
| Los amigos se ayudan. | The friends help each other. |
| Las mujeres se escriben. | The women write to each other. |

Context distinguishes `themselves` from `each other`:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Los hombres se ven en el espejo. | The men see themselves in the mirror. |
| Los hombres se ven todos los días. | The men see each other every day. |
