# Translations of **se**

In pronoun structures, **se** can have several meanings.

| Use | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| Reflexive singular | **Juan se ve en el espejo.** | Juan sees **himself** in the mirror. |
| Reflexive plural | **Ellos se ven en el espejo.** | They see **themselves** in the mirror. |
| Reciprocal | **Juan y María se ayudan.** | Juan and María help **each other**. |
| Formal **yourself** | **Usted se ve en el espejo.** | You see **yourself** in the mirror. |
| Formal **yourselves** | **Ustedes se ven en el espejo.** | You see **yourselves** in the mirror. |
| Replacement for **le/les** | **Se lo doy.** | I give it **to him, her, you, or them**. |
| Pronominal verb | **María se llama Ana.** | Her name is Ana. |

## Key distinction

In **se lo**, **se la**, **se los**, and **se las**, **se** replaces **le** or **les**. The following pronoun identifies the thing:

- **Se lo doy.** — I give **it** to him, her, you, or them.
- **Se los doy.** — I give **them** to him, her, you, or them.
