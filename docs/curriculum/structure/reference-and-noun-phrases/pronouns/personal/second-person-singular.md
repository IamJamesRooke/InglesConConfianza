# Second-Person Singular: Informal

> **you · your · yours · yourself**

| Spanish | English | |
|---|---|---|
| tú | **you** | *Tú lo haces.*<br>You do it. |
| te | **you** | *Yo te veo.*<br>I see you. |
| para ti | **for you** | *Es para ti.*<br>It is for you. |
| contigo | **with you** | *Yo lo hago contigo.*<br>I do it with you. |
| te lo | **it to you** | *Yo te lo doy.*<br>I give it to you. |
| te los | **them to you** | *Yo te los doy.*<br>I give them to you. |
| tu libro | **your book** | *Yo veo tu libro.*<br>I see your book. |
| tuyo, tuya | **yours** | *Es tuyo.*<br>It is yours. |
| tú mismo, tú misma, ti mismo, ti misma | **yourself** | *Tú mismo lo haces.*<br>You do it yourself. |
