# Pronouns

Pronouns identify participants and other referents without repeating a noun.

| Area | Focus |
|---|---|
| [Personal Pronouns](personal/) | Subject, object, possessive, and reflexive forms organized by person and number. |
| [Indefinite Pronouns](indefinite/) | People and things expressed with **some, any, no**, and **every** families. |

The English pronoun system belongs here. Context-dependent Spanish equivalents belong to the directional maps under [Spanish–English Bridges](../../../spanish-english-bridges/README.md).
