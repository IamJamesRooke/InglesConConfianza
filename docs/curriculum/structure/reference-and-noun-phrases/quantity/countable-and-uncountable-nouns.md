# Countable and Uncountable Nouns

Quantity determiners depend on whether the noun can be counted individually.

| Type | Examples | Compatible quantity words |
|---|---|---|
| countable | one cat, two cats | many, a few, fewer |
| uncountable | traffic, information, money | much, a little, less |
| either type | cats / traffic | some, any, no, a lot of, more, enough |

Use a singular or plural form with countable nouns:

- **a cat / twelve cats**

Foundational uncountable nouns do not normally take **a/an** or a plural **-s**:

- **some information**, not *an information* or *informations*
- **a lot of traffic**, not *many traffics*
- **some money / much money**, not *a money* or *many monies* in ordinary conversation
