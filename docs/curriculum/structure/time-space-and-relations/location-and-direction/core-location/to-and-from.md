# **To** and **From**

Use **to** for a destination and **from** for an origin.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| a / hacia un lugar | to a place |
| de / desde un lugar | from a place |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Voy a la oficina. | I am going to the office. |
| Vengo de la oficina. | I am coming from the office. |
| Lleva el documento a la tienda. | Take the document to the store. |
| Trae el documento de la oficina. | Bring the document from the office. |

English **for** can translate Spanish **para** when it expresses purpose or recipient, but not when it names a destination:

- **Salgo para la oficina.** → **I am leaving for the office.**
- **Voy a la oficina.** → **I am going to the office.**
