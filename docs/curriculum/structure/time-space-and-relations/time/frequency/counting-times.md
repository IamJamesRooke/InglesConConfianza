# Counting How Many Times

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| una vez | once |
| dos veces | twice |
| tres veces | three times |
| unas cuantas veces | a few times |
| muchas veces | many times |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Lo hice una vez. | I did it once. |
| He llamado dos veces. | I have called twice. |
| He estado allí muchas veces. | I have been there many times. |

Use **once** and **twice** in normal conversation. **One time** and **two times** are possible when the number needs special emphasis, but they are not the default forms.
