# **Start** and **Finish** with **-ing**

Use **start + verb-ing** for the beginning of an activity and **finish + verb-ing** for its completion.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ella empieza a hacerlo. | She starts doing it. |
| Ella termina de hacerlo. | She finishes doing it. |

**Start** can also take the full infinitive with little difference in many foundational contexts:

- **She starts doing it.**
- **She starts to do it.**

Use **-ing** after **finish**:

- **She finishes doing it.**
- Not: *She finishes to do it.*
