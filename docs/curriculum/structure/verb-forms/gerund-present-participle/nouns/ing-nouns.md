# Nouns Built with **-ing**

English can use a verb's **-ing** form as the name of an activity, process, or event. Spanish often uses a separate noun.

| Spanish verb | English verb | Spanish noun | English **-ing** noun |
|---|---|---|---|
| reunirse | to meet | reunión | meeting |
| leer | to read | lectura | reading |
| escribir | to write | escritura | writing |
| abrir | to open | apertura | opening |
| cerrar | to close | cierre / clausura | closing |
| aprender | to learn | aprendizaje | learning |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La reunión empieza a las nueve. | The meeting starts at nine. |
| La lectura es importante. | Reading is important. |
| La apertura del evento es mañana. | The opening of the event is tomorrow. |

The determiner can reveal a countable event: **the meeting** means **la reunión**, not the action **reuniéndose**.

This lesson intentionally overlaps with [**Meet → Meeting**](../../../../transformations/-ing/01-meet-to-meeting.md): that lesson teaches the transformation, while this one identifies the noun-like job of the **-ing** form.
