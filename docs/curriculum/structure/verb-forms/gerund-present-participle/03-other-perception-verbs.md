# **Hear, Watch, Feel**, and **Notice**

| Whole event | Event in progress |
|---|---|
| We heard him open the door. | We heard him opening the door. |
| Lo oímos abrir la puerta. | Lo oímos abriendo la puerta. |

The same whole-event versus ongoing contrast appears after **hear, watch, feel**, and **notice**. Passive versions use **to**: **He was seen to cross the street.**
