# Another Person + Full Infinitive

When the second action has a different person as its subject, put that person before the full infinitive.

**subject + first verb + somebody + to + bare infinitive**

## Would like involving another person

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me gustaría que ellos lo hicieran. | I would like them to do it. |

Spanish normally uses **que + conjugated verb**. English uses **somebody + to + bare infinitive**:

- **I would like them to do it.**
- Not: *I would like that they do it.*
