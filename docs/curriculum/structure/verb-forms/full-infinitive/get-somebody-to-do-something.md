# **Get Somebody to Do Something**

Use **get + somebody + full infinitive** when one person persuades, arranges for, or succeeds in causing another person to act:

**subject + get + somebody + to + bare infinitive**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Conseguí que Laura lo hiciera. | I got Laura to do it. |
| Ellos consiguen que yo lo haga. | They get me to do it. |

Compare two ways to cause an action:

- They **got me to do** it. — The result may involve persuasion or effort.
- They **made me do** it. — I had no real choice.

Unlike **make**, causative **get** requires **to**.
