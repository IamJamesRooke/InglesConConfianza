# **Have to Do Something**

Use **have to + bare infinitive** for an obligation or necessity:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tengo que hacerlo hoy. | I have to do it today. |
| Ella tendrá que hacerlo mañana. | She will have to do it tomorrow. |

The second verb is a full infinitive: **to do**.

The tense appears on **have** or on its auxiliary:

- I **had to do** it yesterday.
- She **will have to do** it tomorrow.
- **Do** you **have to do** it today?

Do not add a second **to** after a modal: **will have to do**, not *will have to to do*.
