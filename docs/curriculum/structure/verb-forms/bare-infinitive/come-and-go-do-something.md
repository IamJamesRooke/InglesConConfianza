# **Come** and **Go** + Bare Infinitive

In conversational English, **come** or **go** can be followed directly by another action:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ven a verlo. | Come see it. |
| Ve a hacerlo. | Go do it. |

The second verb is a bare infinitive:

- **Come talk to me.**
- **Go ask Laura.**

Forms with **and** are also standard—**Come and see it** and **Go and ask Laura**—but the shorter pattern is especially common in American English.
