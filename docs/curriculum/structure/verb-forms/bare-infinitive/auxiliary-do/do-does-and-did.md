# **Do, Does**, and **Did + Bare Infinitive**

When auxiliary **do, does**, or **did** builds a question, negative, or emphatic statement, the main verb stays in the bare infinitive.

| Structure | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| present question | ¿Lo haces? | Do you do it? |
| third-person question | ¿Ella lo hace? | Does she do it? |
| past question | ¿Ellos lo hicieron? | Did they do it? |
| present negative | No lo hago. | I don't do it. |
| past negative | No lo hicieron. | They didn't do it. |
| emphasis | Sí lo hago. | I do do it. |

The tense or third-person marker moves to the auxiliary:

- **Does she do it?**, not *Does she does it?*
- **Did they do it?**, not *Did they did it?*

Do not add **to** after the auxiliary.
