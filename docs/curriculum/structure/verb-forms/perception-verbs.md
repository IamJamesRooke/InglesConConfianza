# Perception Verb Forms

Perception verbs use **object + base verb** for a whole event and **object + -ing** for an event seen or heard in progress.

| Lesson | English | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| [Whole Event](bare-infinitive/01-object-plus-base-verb.md) | I saw her cross the street. | La vi cruzar la calle. |
| [Event in Progress](gerund-present-participle/02-object-plus-ing.md) | I saw her crossing the street. | La vi cruzando la calle. |
| [Hear, Watch, Feel, and Notice](bare-infinitive/03-other-perception-verbs.md) | We heard him open the door. | Lo oímos abrir la puerta. |
