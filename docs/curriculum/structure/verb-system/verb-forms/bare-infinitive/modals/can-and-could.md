# **Can** and **Could + Bare Infinitive**

Use the bare infinitive after **can** and **could**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Él lo puede hacer. | He can do it. |
| Él lo podía hacer. | He could do it. |
| Él lo podría hacer. | He could do it. |

Spanish **podía** can describe past ability, while **podría** can describe hypothetical ability or possibility. English **could** can express either meaning in context.

- **can do**
- **could do**
- Not: *can to do* or *could to do*
