# **Will** and **Would + Bare Infinitive**

Use the bare infinitive after **will** and **would**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Él lo hará. | He will do it. |
| Él lo haría. | He would do it. |

The modal carries the future or conditional meaning. The next verb stays in its base form:

- **will do**
- **would do**
- Not: *will to do* or *would to do*
