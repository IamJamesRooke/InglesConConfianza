# Third-Person Present

Use the third-person present form when the subject is **he, she, it**, or one singular person or thing.

| Subject | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I / you / we / they | bare infinitive | They speak. |
| he / she / it | third-person present | She speaks. |

## Spanish **-a/-e** memory bridge

For Spanish speakers, the third-person Spanish form provides a strong signal:

> If a present-tense Spanish verb ends in **-a** or **-e**, the corresponding English verb almost always ends in **-s** in a third-person statement.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ella habla. | She speaks. |
| Él corre. | He runs. |

This is a memory bridge, not a spelling rule for deriving unknown vocabulary. The English spelling sometimes requires **-es** or **-ies**, and a small foundational group is irregular.
