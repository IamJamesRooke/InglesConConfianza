# Another Person + Full Infinitive

When the second action has a different person as its subject, put that person before the full infinitive.

**subject + first verb + somebody + to + bare infinitive**

## Wants and needs involving another person

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Quiero que lo hagas. | I want you to do it. |
| Necesito que Laura lo haga. | I need Laura to do it. |
| Me gustaría que ellos lo hicieran. | I would like them to do it. |

Spanish normally uses **que + conjugated verb**. English uses **somebody + to + bare infinitive**:

- **I want you to do it.**
- Not: *I want that you do it.*

## Requests and instructions

The same form follows **ask** and **tell**:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Te pido que lo hagas. | I ask you to do it. |
| Te digo que lo hagas. | I tell you to do it. |

These patterns are sometimes taught as indirect requests or commands because one person causes, requests, or wants another person's action.
