# Irregular Past: **Have → Had**

| Spanish | Base verb | Past |
|---|---|---|
| tener, tuvo | **have** | **had** |

**Tuve una reunión esta mañana.**<br>
I had a meeting this morning.

Use **had** for meals in the past:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| desayunó | **had breakfast** |
| almorzó | **had lunch** |
| cenó | **had dinner** |

**Almorcé con mis compañeros.**<br>
I had lunch with my coworkers.

Use **had to + base verb** as the past of **have to**:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| tuvo que hacer algo | **had to do something** |

**Tuve que terminar el informe ayer.**<br>
I had to finish the report yesterday.

**Had** ends with the sound `/d/`.
