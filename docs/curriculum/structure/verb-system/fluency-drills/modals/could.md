# Fluency Drill: **Could**

Use **could + base verb** here for a hypothetical ability or possibility.

| Drill step | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| embedded **if** clause | Quiero saber si lo podrías hacer. | I want to know if you could do it. |
| direct question | ¿Lo podrías hacer? | Could you do it? |
| affirmative answer | Sí, lo podría hacer. | Yes, I could do it. |
| negative answer | No, no lo podría hacer. | No, I couldn't do it. |
| negative **why** question | ¿Por qué no lo podrías hacer? | Why couldn't you do it? |
| embedded **why** clause | Quiero saber por qué no lo podrías hacer. | I want to know why you couldn't do it. |

The direct question uses **could you**. The embedded clauses use statement order: **if you could** and **why you couldn't**.
