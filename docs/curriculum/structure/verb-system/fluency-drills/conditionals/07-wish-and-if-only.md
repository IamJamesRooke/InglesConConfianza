# **Wish** and **If Only**

**Wish** and **if only** use the same distancing forms as hypothetical conditions. **If only** is normally more emphatic.

## A different present

Use a past form for a present situation the speaker wants to be different.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ojalá tuviera más tiempo. | I wish I had more time. |
| Ojalá pudiera hacerlo. | If only I could do it. |

## A different past

Use **had + past participle** for regret about a completed past.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ojalá hubiera leído el contrato. | I wish I had read the contract. |
| Ojalá me hubieras llamado. | If only you had called me. |

## A desired change

Use **would** when the speaker wants a person, behavior, or situation to change.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Ojalá dejaran de hacer tanto ruido. | I wish they would stop making so much noise. |

Do not normally use **would** for your own present state: say **I wish I had more time**, not *I wish I would have more time*.
