# Impossible Past Conditions

Use **had + past participle** in the condition and **would have + past participle** in the result when imagining a different past. This pattern is traditionally called the **third conditional**.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Si hubiera tenido suficiente tiempo, habría terminado el informe. | If I had had enough time, I would have finished the report. |
| Si me hubieras llamado, te habría ayudado. | If you had called me, I would have helped you. |

The real past was different: I did not have enough time, and I did not finish the report.

Do not put **would have** in the ordinary **if** clause:

- **If I had known, I would have called.**
- Not: *If I would have known, I would have called.*

Other modals can change the result:

- **If you had asked, I could have helped.**
- **If we had left earlier, we might have arrived on time.**
