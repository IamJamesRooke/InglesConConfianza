# Fluency Drill: **Did**

Use **did** with every subject in past questions, emphasis, and negations.

| Drill step | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| embedded **if** clause | Quiero saber si ellos lo hicieron. | I want to know if they did it. |
| direct question | ¿Lo hicieron? | Did they do it? |
| affirmative answer | Sí, lo hicieron. | Yes, they did it. |
| emphatic affirmative | Sí, sí lo hicieron. | Yes, they **did** do it. |
| negative answer | No, no lo hicieron. | No, they didn't do it. |
| negative **why** question | ¿Por qué no lo hicieron? | Why didn't they do it? |
| embedded **why** clause | Quiero saber por qué no lo hicieron. | I want to know why they didn't do it. |

The past marker moves to **did**, so the main verb returns to base **do**.

The auxiliary **did** is stressed because the speaker is correcting doubt or contradiction.

The embedded clause uses statement order: **why they didn't**, not *why didn't they*.
