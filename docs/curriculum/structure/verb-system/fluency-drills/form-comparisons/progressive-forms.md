# Progressive Forms

Progressive forms use a form of **be + verb-ing**.

| Form | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| present | Lo estoy haciendo. | I am doing it. |
| past | Lo estaba haciendo. | I was doing it. |
| future | Lo estaré haciendo. | I will be doing it. |
| conditional | Lo estaría haciendo. | I would be doing it. |
| with **could** | Podría estar haciéndolo. | I could be doing it. |
| with **should** | Debería estar haciéndolo. | I should be doing it. |
