# **Accept → Accepted**

Many established cognates end in the cluster **-ct**. The final sound is `/t/`, so **-ed** adds the syllable `/ɪd/`:

**accept → accepted**

| Base | Past and past participle | Spanish bridge |
|---|---|---|
| accept | accepted | aceptó / aceptado |
| contact | contacted | contactó / contactado |
| connect | connected | conectó / conectado |
| correct | corrected | corrigió / corregido |
| direct | directed | dirigió / dirigido |
| protect | protected | protegió / protegido |

- Past: Laura **contacted** the office, and they **accepted** her application.
- Past participle: Laura has **contacted** the office, and they have **accepted** her application.

Do not compress the ending into a bare `/t/`. **Accepted** has three syllables: ac-cep-ted.
