# **Call → Called**

After final `/l/`, add the voiced sound `/d/`:

**call → called**

| Course source | Base | Past and past participle | Spanish bridge |
|---|---|---|---|
| core verb | call | called | llamó / llamado |
| cognate reinforcement | cancel | canceled | canceló / cancelado |
| cognate reinforcement | control | controlled | controló / controlado |

- Past: Laura **called** the hotel yesterday.
- Past participle: Laura has **called** the hotel twice.

**Called** has one syllable: call + `/d/`.
