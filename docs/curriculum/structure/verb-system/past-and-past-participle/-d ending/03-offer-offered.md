# **Offer → Offered**

After the voiced ending of **offer**, **-ed** is pronounced `/d/`:

**offer → offered**

| Course source | Base | Past and past participle | Spanish bridge |
|---|---|---|---|
| core verb | remember | remembered | recordó / recordado |
| core verb | order | ordered | pidió / pedido en un restaurante |
| established cognate | offer | offered | ofreció / ofrecido |
| cognate reinforcement | prepare | prepared | preparó / preparado |

- Past: The hotel **offered** breakfast, and Laura **ordered** coffee.
- Past participle: The hotel has **offered** breakfast, and Laura has **ordered** coffee.

In rhotic accents, the `/d/` follows `/r/`. In many British accents, the written **r** is not pronounced here, so `/d/` follows the final vowel sound. Both remain in the voiced `/d/` group.
