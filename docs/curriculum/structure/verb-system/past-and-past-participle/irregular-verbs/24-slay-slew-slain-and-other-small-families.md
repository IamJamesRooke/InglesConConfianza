# **Slay → Slew → Slain** and Other Small Families

These standard verbs are irregular but do not support a large, high-value rhyme family.

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| behold | beheld | beheld | contemplar |
| clothe | clothed / clad | clothed / clad | vestir |
| light | lit / lighted | lit / lighted | encender |
| shoe | shod / shoed | shod / shoed | herrar / poner zapatos |
| slay | slew | slain | matar |
| slink | slunk | slunk | escabullirse |
| tread | trod | trodden / trod | pisar |

Learn these after the high-frequency families.

**The character was slain after he had trodden the forbidden path.**
