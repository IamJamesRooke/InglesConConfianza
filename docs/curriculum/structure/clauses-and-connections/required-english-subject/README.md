# Required English Subjects

Spanish often leaves a subject unstated when the verb, context, or construction makes it clear. English normally supplies **it**, **they**, or a specific person or thing.

| Lesson | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| [weather](01-weather.md) | Llueve mucho. | It rains a lot. |
| [reporting](02-reporting.md) | Dicen que cerraron. | They say it closed. |
| [evaluation](03-evaluation.md) | Es importante practicar. | It's important to practice. |
| [contextual subject](04-contextual-subject.md) | Llegó tarde. | She arrived late. |
