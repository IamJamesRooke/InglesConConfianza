# **And, Or, But**, and **With**

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| y / e | and |
| o / u | or |
| pero | but |
| con | with |

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Laura y Diana trabajan juntas. | Laura and Diana work together. |
| ¿Quieres café o agua? | Do you want coffee or water? |
| Quiero ir, pero tengo que trabajar. | I want to go, but I have to work. |
| Trabajo con Laura. | I work with Laura. |

Spanish changes **y → e** before certain **i** sounds and **o → u** before certain **o** sounds. English keeps **and** and **or**.
