# Past, Present, Future, Time, and Tense

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| el pasado | the past |
| el presente | the present |
| el futuro | the future |
| tiempo pasado | past time |
| tiempo presente | present time |
| tiempo futuro | future time |

Use these words to locate an event in time:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Eso ocurrió en el pasado. | That happened in the past. |
| Necesito hacerlo en el presente. | I need to do it in the present. |
| Voy a hacerlo en el futuro. | I am going to do it in the future. |

## **Time** and **tense**

English **time** refers to when something happens. **Tense** refers to the grammatical form used to present it.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| No tengo tiempo. | I don't have time. |
| El pasado es un tiempo verbal. | The past is a verb tense. |

The real time and the grammatical tense do not always match exactly, so do not use **time** when the meaning is **tiempo verbal**.
