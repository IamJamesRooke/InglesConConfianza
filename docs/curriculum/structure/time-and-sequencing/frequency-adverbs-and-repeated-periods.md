# Frequency Adverbs and Repeated Periods

## Adverbs of frequency

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| nunca | never |
| casi nunca | almost never |
| a veces | sometimes |
| a menudo / con frecuencia | often |
| casi siempre | almost always |
| siempre | always |

Frequency adverbs normally go before a main verb but after **be**:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Siempre preparo café. | I always make coffee. |
| A veces llego tarde. | I sometimes arrive late. |
| Ella casi siempre está aquí. | She is almost always here. |

## Repeated periods and duration

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| todos los días | every day |
| todo el día | all day |
| todas las semanas | every week |
| toda la semana | all week |

**Every day** describes repetition; **all day** describes the duration of one day.
