# Adjective Position

An adjective commonly appears before a noun or after a linking verb such as **be** or **feel**.

| Position | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| before a noun | un libro importante | an important book |
| after **be** | El libro es importante. | The book is important. |
| after **feel** | Me siento bien. | I feel good. |

English normally places the adjective before the noun: **an important meeting**, not *a meeting important*.

Do not add an adverb ending after **be** when describing the subject: **The meeting is important**, not *The meeting is importantly*.
