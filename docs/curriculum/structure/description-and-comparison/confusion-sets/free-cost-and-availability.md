# Two Meanings of **Free**

English **free** has two foundational meanings.

| Meaning | Spanish | English |
|---|---|---|
| no cost | gratuito | The event is free. |
| available / not busy | libre / disponible | I am free this afternoon. |

Use context to distinguish them:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| La clase es gratuita. | The class is free. |
| Estoy libre después de la clase. | I am free after the class. |

For a person who can meet or work, **available** is often clearer than **free**.
