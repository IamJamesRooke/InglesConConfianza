# **Breathe → Breathed**

The written **-ed** ending is pronounced `/d/`.

## **Breathe → Breathed**

Verb **breathe** ends in voiced `/ð/`, so **-ed** is pronounced `/d/`:

**breathe → breathed**

| Course source | Base | Past and past participle | Spanish bridge |
|---|---|---|---|
| model vocabulary | breathe | breathed | respiró / respirado |
| supporting vocabulary | bathe | bathed | se bañó / bañado |

- Past: Laura **breathed** slowly after she **bathed**.
- Past participle: Laura has **breathed** more easily since she **bathed**.

Compare the two **th** sounds:

- voiced **breathe** `/ð/` → **breathed** with `/d/`
- voiceless **unearth** `/θ/` → **unearthed** with `/t/`

This pair demonstrates why pronunciation follows the final sound rather than the letters **th**.
