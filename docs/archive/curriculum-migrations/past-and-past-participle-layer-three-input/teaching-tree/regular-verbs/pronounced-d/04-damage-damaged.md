# **Damage → Damaged**

The written **-ed** ending is pronounced `/d/`.

## **Damage → Damaged**

After final `/dʒ/`, **-ed** is pronounced `/d/`:

**damage → damaged**

| Course source | Base | Past and past participle | Spanish bridge |
|---|---|---|---|
| model vocabulary | damage | damaged | dañó / dañado |
| cognate reinforcement | imagine | imagined | imaginó / imaginado |

- Past: Laura **imagined** that the storm **damaged** the sign.
- Past participle: Laura has **imagined** many problems, but the sign has never been **damaged**.

The ending joins the existing voiced sound: damage + `/d/`. It does not add an **-ed** syllable.
