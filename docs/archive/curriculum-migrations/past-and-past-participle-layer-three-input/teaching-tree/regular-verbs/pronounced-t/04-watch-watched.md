## **Watch → Watched**

After final `/tʃ/` written **ch**, **-ed** is pronounced `/t/`:

**watch → watched**

| Course source | Base | Past and past participle | Spanish bridge |
|---|---|---|---|
| model vocabulary | watch | watched | vio / miró / visto |
| supporting vocabulary | reach | reached | alcanzó / alcanzado |
| supporting vocabulary | touch | touched | tocó / tocado |

- Past: Laura **watched** the screen and **reached** the final page.
- Past participle: Laura has **watched** the screen and has **reached** the final page.

The ending joins the existing sound cluster without adding an **-ed** syllable: watch + `/t/`.
