# **Unearth → Unearthed**

The written **-ed** ending is pronounced `/t/`.

## **Unearth → Unearthed**

After voiceless `/θ/` written **th**, **-ed** is pronounced `/t/`:

**unearth → unearthed**

| Course source | Base | Past and past participle | Spanish bridge |
|---|---|---|---|
| model vocabulary | unearth | unearthed | desenterró / desenterrado; descubrió / descubierto |

- Past: The researchers **unearthed** new evidence last year.
- Past participle: The researchers have **unearthed** new evidence.

## **Unearthed** versus **breathed**

The source chart placed **breathed** in the `/t/` group, but the verbs end in different **th** sounds:

- **unearth** ends in voiceless `/θ/` → **unearthed** ends in `/t/`
- **breathe** ends in voiced `/ð/` → **breathed** ends in `/d/`

The letters alone cannot choose the ending; listen for whether the final sound is voiced.
