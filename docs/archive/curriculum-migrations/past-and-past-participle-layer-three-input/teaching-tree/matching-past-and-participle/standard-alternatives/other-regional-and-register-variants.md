# Other Matching Regional and Register Variants

The past and participle still match; dialect, meaning, or register changes the accepted form.

| Base | Past = participle | Note |
|---|---|---|
| clad | clad / cladded | **clad** is most common as a participle or adjective |
| clothe | clothed / clad | vestir |
| shit | shit / shat / shitted | vulgar; forms vary by dialect and speaker |
| shoe | shod / shoed | herrar / poner zapatos |
| sneak | sneaked / snuck | **snuck** is especially common in American English |
| stave | staved / stove | **stove** is rare; **staved off** is the ordinary current form |
| sweat | sweated / sweat | **sweated** is the safest general form |

**He snuck out, but he has never sneaked back in unnoticed.**<br>
Se escapó, pero nunca ha vuelto a entrar a escondidas sin que lo noten.
