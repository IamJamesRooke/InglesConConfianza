# **Hung, Clung, Flung**, and Related Forms

The past and participle share the same short-vowel form.

## **Hung, Clung, Flung, Slung, Stung, Strung, Swung, Wrung**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| hang | hung | hung | colgar |
| cling | clung | clung | aferrarse |
| fling | flung | flung | arrojar |
| sling | slung | slung | lanzar / colgar |
| sting | stung | stung | picar |
| string | strung | strung | ensartar |
| swing | swung | swung | balancearse |
| wring | wrung | wrung | retorcer |

All target forms rhyme.

**The bee stung me while I swung the bag that had hung from my shoulder.**
