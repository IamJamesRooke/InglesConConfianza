# **Thought, Bought, Brought**, and Related Forms

The **-ought/-aught** spelling represents one compact family of matching past and participle forms.

## **Think → Thought → Thought** and **-ought/-aught**

| Base | Past = participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| bring | brought | traer |
| buy | bought | comprar |
| fight | fought | pelear |
| seek | sought | buscar |
| think | thought | pensar |
| catch | caught | atrapar |
| teach | taught | enseñar |

The spelling splits into **-ought** and **-aught**, but all seven target forms rhyme.

**The teacher taught what she thought the students had sought.**

## Irregular Past: **Bought**, **Thought**, and **Brought**

Learn these written **-ought** forms together:

- **buy → bought**
- **think → thought**
- **bring → brought**

They share a similar rhyme and finish with the sound `/t/`. The exact vowel varies by accent.

| Spanish | Base form | Past |
|---|---|---|
| comprar, compró | **buy** | **bought** |
| pensar, pensó | **think** | **thought** |
| traer, trajo | **bring** | **brought** |

**Compré el libro que Laura trajo a clase y pensé que era útil.**<br>
I bought the book Laura brought to class and thought it was useful.

## Irregular Past Participles: **Bought**, **Thought**, and **Brought**

Learn these written **-ought** forms together:

- **buy → bought**
- **think → thought**
- **bring → brought**

They share a similar rhyme and finish with the sound `/t/`. The exact vowel varies by accent.

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| comprar, comprado | **buy** | **bought** |
| pensar, pensado | **think** | **thought** |
| traer, traído | **bring** | **brought** |

**He comprado el libro, he pensado en la lección y lo he traído a clase.**<br>
I have bought the book, thought about the lesson, and brought it to class.
