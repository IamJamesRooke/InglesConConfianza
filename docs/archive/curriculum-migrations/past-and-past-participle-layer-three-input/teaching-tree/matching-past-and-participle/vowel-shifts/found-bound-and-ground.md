# **Found, Bound, and Ground**

The past and participle share one transformed sound.

## **Find → Found → Found** and the **-ound** Family

| Base | Past = participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| bind | bound | atar |
| find | found | encontrar |
| grind | ground | moler |
| wind `/waɪnd/` | wound `/waʊnd/` | enrollar |

The target forms rhyme. Do not confuse past **wound** `/waʊnd/` with the noun **wound** `/wuːnd/`, una herida.

**I found the cable wound around the machine that had ground the coffee.**

## Irregular Past: **Find → Found**

| Spanish | Base verb | Past |
|---|---|---|
| encontrar, encontró | **find** | **found** |

**Encontré mis llaves debajo de la mesa.**<br>
I found my keys under the table.

**Found** ends with the sound `/d/`.

## Irregular Past Participle: **Find → Found**

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| encontrar, encontrado | **find** | **found** |

**Hemos encontrado las llaves debajo de la mesa.**<br>
We have found the keys under the table.

**Found** ends with the sound `/d/`.
