# **Hear → Heard → Heard**

The past and participle share one transformed sound.

## **Hear → Heard → Heard**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| hear | heard | heard | oír |

**I heard that song yesterday, and I have heard it twice today.**

## Irregular Past: **Hear → Heard**

| Spanish | Base verb | Past |
|---|---|---|
| oír, oyó | **hear** | **heard** |

**Oí la noticia en la radio esta mañana.**<br>
I heard the news on the radio this morning.

**Heard** ends with the sound `/d/`.

## Irregular Past Participle: **Hear → Heard**

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| oír, oído | **hear** | **heard** |

**He oído esa noticia varias veces.**<br>
I have heard that news several times.

**Heard** ends with the sound `/d/`.
