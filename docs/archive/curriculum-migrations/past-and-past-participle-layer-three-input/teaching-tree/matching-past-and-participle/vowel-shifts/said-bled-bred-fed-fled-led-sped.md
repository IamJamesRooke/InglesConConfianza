# **Said, Bled, Bred, Fed, Fled, Led, Sped**

The past and participle share one transformed sound.

## **Said, Bled, Bred, Fed, Fled, Led, Sped**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| say | said | said | decir |
| bleed | bled | bled | sangrar |
| breed | bred | bred | criar |
| feed | fed | fed | alimentar |
| flee | fled | fled | huir |
| lead | led | led | liderar / guiar |
| speed | sped | sped | acelerar |

All target forms rhyme with **red**. Written **said** does not rhyme with **paid**.

**She said the farmer bred and fed the horses before they fled.**

## Irregular Past: **Say → Said** and **Read → Read**

Learn **said** and past **read** together. They both end in the sound `/ɛd/`.

| Spanish | Base verb | Past |
|---|---|---|
| decir, dijo algo | **say** | **said something** |
| leer, leyó | **read** `/riːd/` | **read** `/rɛd/` |

**Dije la respuesta después de leer el mensaje.**<br>
I said the answer after I read the message.

The spelling of **read** does not change, but its pronunciation does:

**read** `/riːd/` → **read** `/rɛd/`, pronounced like **red**

## Irregular Past Participles: **Said** and **Read**

Learn **said** and past participle **read** together. They both end in the sound `/ɛd/`.

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| decir, dicho algo | **say** | **said something** |
| leer, leído | **read** `/riːd/` | **read** `/rɛd/` |

**Laura ha dicho que la reunión empezó después de haber leído el mensaje.**<br>
Laura has said that the meeting started after having read the message.

The spelling of **read** does not change, but its pronunciation does:

**read** `/riːd/` → **read** `/rɛd/`, pronounced like **red**
