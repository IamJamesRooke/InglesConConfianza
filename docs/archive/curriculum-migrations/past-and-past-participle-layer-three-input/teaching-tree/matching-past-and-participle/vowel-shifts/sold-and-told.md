# **Sold and Told**

The past and participle share one transformed sound.

## **Sold, Told**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| sell | sold | sold | vender |
| tell | told | told | decir / contar |

**She told me she had sold the old table.**

## Irregular Past: **Tell → Told** and **Sell → Sold**

Learn these two changes together:

- **tell → told**
- **sell → sold**

Both past forms end in the sound `/oʊld/`.

| Spanish | Base verb | Past |
|---|---|---|
| decirle a alguien, le dijo | **tell somebody** | **told somebody** |
| vender, vendió | **sell** | **sold** |

**Le dije a Laura que la tienda vendió el último libro.**<br>
I told Laura that the store sold the last book.

## Irregular Past Participles: **Told** and **Sold**

Learn these two changes together:

- **tell → told**
- **sell → sold**

Both participles end in the sound `/oʊld/`.

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| decirle a alguien, dicho | **tell somebody** | **told somebody** |
| vender, vendido | **sell** | **sold** |

**Le he dicho a Laura que la tienda ha vendido el último libro.**<br>
I have told Laura that the store has sold the last book.
