# **Have → Had → Had**

The past and participle share one transformed sound.

## **Have → Had → Had**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| have | had | had | tener |

**I had the key yesterday, and I have had it all week.**

## Irregular Past: **Have → Had**

| Spanish | Base verb | Past |
|---|---|---|
| tener, tuvo | **have** | **had** |

**Tuve una reunión esta mañana.**<br>
I had a meeting this morning.

Use **had** for meals in the past:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| desayunó | **had breakfast** |
| almorzó | **had lunch** |
| cenó | **had dinner** |

**Almorcé con mis compañeros.**<br>
I had lunch with my coworkers.

Use **had to + base verb** as the past of **have to**:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| tuvo que hacer algo | **had to do something** |

**Tuve que terminar el informe ayer.**<br>
I had to finish the report yesterday.

**Had** ends with the sound `/d/`.

## Irregular Past Participle: **Have → Had**

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| tener, tenido | **have** | **had** |

**He tenido tres reuniones esta semana.**<br>
I have had three meetings this week.

Use **had** with meals:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| desayunado | **had breakfast** |
| almorzado | **had lunch** |
| cenado | **had dinner** |

**Ya hemos almorzado.**<br>
We have already had lunch.

Use **have had to + base verb** when an obligation has occurred:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| haber tenido que hacer algo | **have had to do something** |

**He tenido que terminar varios informes esta semana.**<br>
I have had to finish several reports this week.

**Had** ends with the sound `/d/`.
