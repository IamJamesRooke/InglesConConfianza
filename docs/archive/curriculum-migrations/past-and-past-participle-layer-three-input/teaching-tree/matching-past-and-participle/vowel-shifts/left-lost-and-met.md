# **Left, Lost, and Met**

These are short high-frequency matching forms.

## **Left, Lost, Met**: Three One-Offs

These do not rhyme, so treat them as three isolated micro-patterns.

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| leave | left | left | salir / dejar |
| lose | lost | lost | perder |
| meet | met | met | conocer / encontrarse |

Do not chant these as one family. Review each row independently.

## Irregular Past: **Leave → Left**

Use **left** for both leaving a place and leaving something in a place.

| Spanish | Base form | Past |
|---|---|---|
| salir de un lugar, salió | **leave a place** | **left a place** |
| dejar algo, dejó algo | **leave something** | **left something** |

**Salí de la oficina, pero dejé las llaves en la recepción.**<br>
I left the office, but I left the keys at reception.

**Left** ends with the sound `/t/`.

## Irregular Past Participle: **Leave → Left**

Use **left** for both leaving a place and leaving something in a place.

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| salir de un lugar, salido | **leave a place** | **left a place** |
| dejar algo, dejado | **leave something** | **left something** |

**Laura ha salido de la oficina, pero ha dejado las llaves en la recepción.**<br>
Laura has left the office, but she has left the keys at reception.

**Left** ends with the sound `/t/`.
