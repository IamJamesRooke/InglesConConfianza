# **Made, Paid, Laid**

The past and participle share one transformed sound.

## **Made, Paid, Laid**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| make | made | made | hacer |
| pay | paid | paid | pagar |
| lay | laid | laid | poner / colocar |

The targets rhyme: **made–paid–laid**.

**I made the order, paid the bill, and laid the receipt on the table.**

## Irregular Past: **Make → Made** and **Pay → Paid**

Learn **made** and **paid** together. Both end in the sound `/eɪd/`.

| Spanish | Base verb | Past |
|---|---|---|
| hacer, hizo | **make** | **made** |
| pagar, pagó | **pay** | **paid** |

**Hice la reserva y pagué el depósito.**<br>
I made the reservation and paid the deposit.

## Irregular Past Participles: **Made** and **Paid**

Learn **made** and **paid** together. Both participles end in the sound `/eɪd/`.

| Spanish | Base form | Simple past | Past participle |
|---|---|---|---|
| hacer, hecho | **make** | **made** | **made** |
| pagar, pagado | **pay** | **paid** | **paid** |

**He hecho la reserva y he pagado el depósito.**<br>
I have made the reservation and paid the deposit.
