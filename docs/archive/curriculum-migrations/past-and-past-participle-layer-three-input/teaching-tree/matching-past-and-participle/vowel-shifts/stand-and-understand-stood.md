# **Stand and Understand → Stood**

The past and participle share one transformed sound.

## **Stand → Stood → Stood**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| stand | stood | stood | estar de pie |
| understand | understood | understood | entender |
| withstand | withstood | withstood | resistir |

The compounds preserve **stand → stood → stood**, although only **stood** stands alone as a complete rhyme.

**I understood how the old bridge had withstood the storm.**

## Irregular Past: **Stand → Stood** and **Understand → Understood**

The past of **understand** contains the same change as the past of **stand**:

- **stand → stood**
- **understand → understood**

Both past forms share the final sound chunk `/stʊd/`.

| Spanish | Base verb | Past |
|---|---|---|
| ponerse de pie, se puso de pie | **stand up** | **stood up** |
| entender, entendió | **understand** | **understood** |

**Me puse de pie cuando entendí la pregunta.**<br>
I stood up when I understood the question.

## Irregular Past Participles: **Stood** and **Understood**

The participle **understood** contains the same change as **stood**:

- **stand → stood**
- **understand → understood**

Both participles share the final sound chunk `/stʊd/`.

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| levantarse, haberse puesto de pie | **stand up** | **stood up** |
| entender, entendido | **understand** | **understood** |

**Laura se ha puesto de pie porque ha entendido la pregunta.**<br>
Laura has stood up because she has understood the question.
