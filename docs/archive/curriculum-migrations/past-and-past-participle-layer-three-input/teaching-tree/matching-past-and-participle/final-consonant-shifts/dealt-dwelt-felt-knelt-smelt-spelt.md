# **Dealt, Dwelt, Felt, Knelt, Smelt, Spelt**

The matching irregular forms end in a shared consonant pattern.

## **Dealt, Dwelt, Felt, Knelt, Smelt, Spelt**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| deal | dealt | dealt | tratar / repartir |
| dwell | dwelt / dwelled | dwelt / dwelled | habitar |
| feel | felt | felt | sentir |
| kneel | knelt / kneeled | knelt / kneeled | arrodillarse |
| smell | smelt / smelled | smelt / smelled | oler |
| spell | spelt / spelled | spelt / spelled | deletrear |

The irregular targets rhyme with **felt**.

**I felt that the word she spelt described what I had smelt.**

## Irregular Past: **Feel → Felt** and **Meet → Met**

In both families, the long vowel in the base form becomes the shorter vowel in the past:

- **feel → felt**
- **meet → met**

Both past forms finish with the sound `/t/`.

| Spanish | Base form | Past |
|---|---|---|
| sentirse, se sintió | **feel** | **felt** |
| conocer a alguien por primera vez | **meet somebody** | **met somebody** |

**Me sentí tranquilo cuando conocí a Laura.**<br>
I felt calm when I met Laura.

## Irregular Past Participles: **Felt** and **Met**

In both families, the long vowel in the base form becomes the shorter vowel in the participle:

- **feel → felt**
- **meet → met**

Both participles finish with the sound `/t/`.

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| sentirse, sentido | **feel** | **felt** |
| conocer a alguien por primera vez | **meet somebody** | **met somebody** |

**Me he sentido tranquilo esta semana.**<br>
I have felt calm this week.

**He conocido a varios compañeros nuevos.**<br>
I have met several new coworkers.
