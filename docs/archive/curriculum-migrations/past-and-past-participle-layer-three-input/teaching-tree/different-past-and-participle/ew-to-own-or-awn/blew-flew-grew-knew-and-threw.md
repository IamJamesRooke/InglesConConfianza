# **Blew, Flew, Grew, Knew, Threw → -own**

The past **-ew** sound leads to the participle **-own** sound.

## **Blew, Flew, Grew, Knew, Threw → Blown, Flown, Grown, Known, Thrown**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| blow | blew | blown | soplar |
| fly | flew | flown | volar |
| grow | grew | grown | crecer |
| know | knew | known | saber / conocer |
| throw | threw | thrown | lanzar |

Every past form rhymes, and every participle rhymes.

**The wind blew after the plane had flown and the trees had grown.**
