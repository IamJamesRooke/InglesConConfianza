# **Wear → Wore → Worn**

The final **e** disappears before the participle **-n** sound.

## **Wear → Wore → Worn**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| bear | bore | borne / born | soportar / dar a luz |
| swear | swore | sworn | jurar |
| tear | tore | torn | rasgar |
| wear | wore | worn | llevar puesto |

Use **born** only for birth: **Laura was born in Bogotá**. Use **borne** for carrying or enduring: **The cost was borne by the company**.

**She wore the shirt until it had worn thin and torn.**
