# **Steal → Stole → Stolen** and **Weave → Wove → Woven**

The participle grows visibly from the long-**o** past form.

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| steal | stole | stolen | robar |
| weave | wove | woven | tejer |

Use the familiar **chose–frozen–spoken** families as bridges.

**She chose the scarf that had been woven, not the one that had been stolen.**
