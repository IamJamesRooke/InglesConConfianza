# **Speak → Spoke → Spoken**

The participle grows directly from the past sound: **spoke + n → spoken**.

## **Speak → Spoke → Spoken**: **-oke → -oken**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| break | broke | broken | romper |
| speak | spoke | spoken | hablar |
| wake | woke | woken | despertar(se) |

**She spoke after everyone had woken up and discovered what had broken.**

## Irregular Past Participles: **Spoken** and **Chosen**

Learn the full three-form families:

- **speak → spoke → spoken**
- **choose → chose → chosen**

The participles share the vowel sound `/oʊ/` and finish with `/n/`.

| Spanish | Base form | Simple past | Past participle |
|---|---|---|---|
| hablar, hablado | **speak** | **spoke** | **spoken** |
| elegir, elegido | **choose** | **chose** | **chosen** |

**He hablado con Laura y hemos elegido una fecha.**<br>
I have spoken with Laura, and we have chosen a date.
