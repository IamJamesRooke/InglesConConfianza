# **Slay → Slew → Slain**

This literary but current verb does not support a larger high-value rhyme family.

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| slay | slew | slain | matar |

**The character was slain after he had trodden the forbidden path.**<br>
El personaje fue asesinado después de haber pisado el camino prohibido.
