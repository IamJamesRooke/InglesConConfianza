# **Take → Took → Taken**

Learn **took** and **taken** as one three-form set.

## **Take → Took → Taken**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| forsake | forsook | forsaken | abandonar |
| mistake | mistook | mistaken | confundir / equivocarse |
| shake | shook | shaken | sacudir |
| take | took | taken | tomar / llevar |

Compounds inherit **take → took → taken**: **overtake, retake, undertake**.

**I mistook the bottle she had taken for the one she had shaken.**

## Vowel Shifts: **Take → Took**

The vowel changes from **take** `/teɪk/` to **took** `/tʊk/`.

Use **took** for the core meanings of taking or carrying something.

| Spanish | Base form | Past |
|---|---|---|
| tomar, tomó | **take** | **took** |
| llevar, llevó | **take** | **took** |

**Tomé las llaves y las llevé a la oficina.**<br>
I took the keys and took them to the office.
