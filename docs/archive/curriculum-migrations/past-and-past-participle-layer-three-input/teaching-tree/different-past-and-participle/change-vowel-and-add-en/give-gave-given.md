# **Give → Gave → Given**

Learn **gave** and **given** as one three-form set.

## **Give → Gave → Given**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| give | gave | given | dar |
| forgive | forgave | forgiven | perdonar |

The prefix stays attached while the internal chain remains visible.

**She gave an apology, and I have forgiven her.**
