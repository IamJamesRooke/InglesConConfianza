# **Arise, Awake, Forsake, and Smite**

These forms combine a changed past vowel with an **-n/-en** participle.

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish or register |
|---|---|---|---|
| arise | arose | arisen | surgir |
| awake | awoke / awakened | awoken / awakened | despertar(se) |
| forsake | forsook | forsaken | abandonar |
| smite | smote | smitten | formal, literary, or humorous |

**They forsook the old plan, and the building has been forsaken for years.**<br>
Abandonaron el plan anterior y el edificio lleva años abandonado.
