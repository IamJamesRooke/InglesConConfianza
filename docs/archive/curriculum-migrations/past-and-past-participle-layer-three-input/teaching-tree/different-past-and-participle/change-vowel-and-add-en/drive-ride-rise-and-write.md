# **Drive, Ride, Rise, and Write**

The past uses long **o**; the participle returns to a short **i** sound and adds **-en**.

## **Drive–Drove–Driven, Strive–Strove–Striven**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| drive | drove | driven | conducir |
| strive | strove / strived | striven / strived | esforzarse |

The base forms rhyme, the irregular past forms rhyme, and the irregular participles rhyme.

## **Ride → Rode → Ridden**

**I rode yesterday, and I have ridden here before.**

## **Rise → Rose → Risen**

**Prices rose last year, and they have risen again.**

## **Write → Wrote → Written**

**I wrote yesterday, and I have written again today.**

## Irregular Past Participles: **Written** and **Driven**

Learn these similar base and participle shapes together:

- **write → written**
- **drive → driven**

Both participles finish with written **-en** and the sound `/n/`.

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| escribir, escrito | **write** | **written** |
| manejar, conducir; manejado, conducido | **drive** | **driven** |

**He escrito el mensaje y he manejado hasta la oficina.**<br>
I have written the message and driven to the office.

## Vowel Shifts: **Spoke**, **Drove**, **Wrote**, and **Chose**

The past forms in this family share the vowel sound `/oʊ/`.

| Spanish | Base form | Past |
|---|---|---|
| hablar, habló | **speak** | **spoke** |
| manejar, conducir; manejó, condujo | **drive** | **drove** |
| escribir, escribió | **write** | **wrote** |
| elegir, eligió | **choose** | **chose** |

**Hablé con Laura, manejé a la oficina, escribí el mensaje y elegí una fecha.**<br>
I spoke with Laura, drove to the office, wrote the message, and chose a date.

Listen for the shared past vowel:

**spoke — drove — wrote — chose**
