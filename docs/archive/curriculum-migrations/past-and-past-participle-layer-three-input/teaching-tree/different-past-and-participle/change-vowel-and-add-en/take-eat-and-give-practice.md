# **Take, Eat, and Give** Practice

This retrieval set keeps all three forms visible.

## Irregular Past Participles: **Taken**, **Eaten**, and **Given**

These participles finish with written **-en** and the sound `/n/`:

- **take → taken**
- **eat → eaten**
- **give → given**

| Spanish | Base form | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| tomar, tomado | **take** | **taken** |
| llevar, llevado | **take** | **taken** |
| comer, comido | **eat** | **eaten** |
| dar, dado | **give** | **given** |

**He tomado las llaves, las he llevado a la oficina y se las he dado a Laura.**<br>
I have taken the keys, taken them to the office, and given them to Laura.

**Ya hemos comido.**<br>
We have already eaten.

## Vowel Shifts: **Came**, **Ate**, and **Gave**

The past forms **came**, **ate**, and **gave** share the vowel sound `/eɪ/`, even though their spelling differs.

| Spanish | Base form | Past |
|---|---|---|
| venir, vino | **come** | **came** |
| comer, comió | **eat** | **ate** |
| dar, dio | **give** | **gave** |

**Vine a la oficina, comí con Laura y le di el documento.**<br>
I came to the office, ate with Laura, and gave her the document.

The same change appears in **come back**:

| Spanish | Base form | Past |
|---|---|---|
| volver, volvió | **come back** | **came back** |

**Volví a la oficina después del almuerzo.**<br>
I came back to the office after lunch.
