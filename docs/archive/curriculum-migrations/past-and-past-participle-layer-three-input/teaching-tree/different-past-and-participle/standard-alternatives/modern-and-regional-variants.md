# Different Forms with Modern or Regional Variants

These current forms have a past option that differs from the usual participle, or an **-n/-en** participle alongside a regular alternative.

| Base | Past | Past participle | Use or register |
|---|---|---|---|
| beget | begot | begotten / begot | formal: engendrar, producir |
| cleave | cleaved / cleft / clove | cleaved / cleft / cloven | rare outside formal or literary uses; meaning affects the preferred form |
| crow | crowed / crew | crowed | **crew** is a rare past for a rooster crowing |
| dive | dived / dove | dived | **dove** is a common American past; **dived** is the standard participle |
| hew | hewed | hewn / hewed | tallar |
| strew | strewed | strewn / strewed | **strewn** remains current |
| thrive | thrived / throve | thrived / thriven | prosperar |
| tread | trod | trodden / trod | pisar |

**She dove into the pool yesterday, but she has never dived from that height before.**<br>
Se lanzó a la piscina ayer, pero nunca se ha lanzado desde esa altura.

**The papers were strewn across the floor.**<br>
Los papeles estaban esparcidos por el piso.

The character was **slain** after he had **trodden** the forbidden path.
