# **Get → Got → Got/Gotten** and **Forget → Forgot → Forgotten**

The participle varies by dialect and meaning; **gotten** remains especially productive in North American English.

## **Get–Got–Gotten, Forget–Forgot–Forgotten**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| get | got | gotten / got | obtener / ponerse |
| forget | forgot | forgotten | olvidar |

The prefix preserves the inner rhythm. American English commonly uses **gotten**; British English more often uses **got**.

## Irregular Past Participles: **Gotten** and **Forgotten**

The participle **forgotten** contains the same final form as **gotten**:

- **get → got → gotten**
- **forget → forgot → forgotten**

This course uses American English, where **gotten** is the usual participle for many meanings involving receiving, obtaining, reaching, or changing state.

## Meanings of **gotten**

| Spanish | Base form | Simple past | Past participle |
|---|---|---|---|
| conseguir, conseguido | **get** | **got** | **gotten** |
| obtener, obtenido | **get** | **got** | **gotten** |
| recibir, recibido | **get** | **got** | **gotten** |
| llegar a un lugar | **get to a place** | **got to a place** | **gotten to a place** |
| levantarse, levantado | **get up** | **got up** | **gotten up** |

**He recibido el mensaje y he llegado a la oficina.**<br>
I have gotten the message and gotten to the office.

**Laura ya se ha levantado.**<br>
Laura has already gotten up.

## **Forget → Forgotten**

| Spanish | Base form | Simple past | Past participle |
|---|---|---|---|
| olvidar, olvidado | **forget** | **forgot** | **forgotten** |

**He olvidado responder el mensaje.**<br>
I have forgotten to respond to the message.

## Irregular Past: **Get → Got** and **Forget → Forgot**

The past **forgot** contains the same final form as **got**:

- **get → got**
- **forget → forgot**

Both past forms end with the sound `/t/`.

## Meanings of **got**

| Spanish | Base form | Past |
|---|---|---|
| recibir, obtener, conseguir | **get** | **got** |
| llegar a un lugar | **get to a place** | **got to a place** |
| entender algo | **get something** | **got something** |
| levantarse | **get up** | **got up** |

**Recibí el mensaje cuando llegué a la oficina.**<br>
I got the message when I got to the office.

**Entendí la idea cuando la profesora la explicó.**<br>
I got the idea when the teacher explained it.

## **Forget → Forgot**

| Spanish | Base form | Past |
|---|---|---|
| olvidar, olvidó | **forget** | **forgot** |

**Olvidé responder el mensaje.**<br>
I forgot to respond to the message.
