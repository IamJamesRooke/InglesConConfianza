# **Swim → Swam → Swum**

Keep the three vowel positions together as one sound transformation.

## **Swim → Swam → Swum**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| swim | swam | swum | nadar |

It shares the **i–a–u** vowel rhythm, but its final sound prevents it from rhyming with **sing** or **drink**.

**I swam yesterday, and I have swum here before.**
