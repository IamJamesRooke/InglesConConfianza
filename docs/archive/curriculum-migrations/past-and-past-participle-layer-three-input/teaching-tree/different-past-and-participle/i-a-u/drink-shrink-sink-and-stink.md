Keep the three vowel positions together as one sound transformation.

## **Drink–Drank–Drunk, Shrink–Shrank–Shrunk, Sink–Sank–Sunk, Stink–Stank–Stunk**

| Base | Past | Participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| drink | drank | drunk | beber |
| shrink | shrank | shrunk | encogerse |
| sink | sank | sunk | hundirse |
| stink | stank | stunk | apestar |

Every column rhymes, and every row follows **i–a–u**.

**I drank before the bottle shrank, the boat sank, and the water stank.**
