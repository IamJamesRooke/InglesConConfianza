# **Begin → Began → Begun**

Keep the three vowel positions together as one sound transformation.

## **Begin → Began → Begun**

**Begin** follows the same short **i–a–u** memory rhythm as **sing → sang → sung**, but it has two syllables.

| Base | Past | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| begin | began | begun |

- **The class began at seven.** — La clase empezó a las siete.
- **The class has already begun.** — La clase ya empezó.

Do not exchange the two forms:

- **began** stands as the past: **It began yesterday.**
- **begun** follows a helper such as **has, had**, or **was**: **It has begun.**

Mastery phrase: **It began yesterday, but the real work has only just begun.**
