# The **Crept, Kept, Slept, Swept, Wept** Family

The irregular past and past participle match. Learn the complete three-form sound set together.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| creep | **crept** | **crept** | avanzar sigilosamente |
| keep | **kept** | **kept** | guardar / mantener |
| sleep | **slept** | **slept** | dormir |
| sweep | **swept** | **swept** | barrer |
| weep | **wept** | **wept** | llorar |

## Examples

**She slept while the cat crept in, so I kept quiet and swept later.**
