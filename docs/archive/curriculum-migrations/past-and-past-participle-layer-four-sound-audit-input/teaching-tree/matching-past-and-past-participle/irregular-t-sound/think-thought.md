# The **Thought, Bought, Brought, Caught, Fought, Sought, Taught** Family

The irregular past and past participle match. Learn the complete three-form sound set together.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| think | **thought** | **thought** | pensar |
| buy | **bought** | **bought** | comprar |
| bring | **brought** | **brought** | traer |
| catch | **caught** | **caught** | atrapar |
| fight | **fought** | **fought** | pelear |
| seek | **sought** | **sought** | buscar |
| teach | **taught** | **taught** | enseñar |

## Examples

**The teacher taught what she thought the students had sought.**

**Compré el libro que Laura trajo a clase y pensé que era útil.**<br>
I bought the book Laura brought to class and thought it was useful.
