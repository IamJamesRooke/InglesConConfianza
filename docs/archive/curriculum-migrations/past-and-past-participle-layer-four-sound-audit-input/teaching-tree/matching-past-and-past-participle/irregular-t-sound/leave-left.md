# **Leave → Left**, **Lose → Lost**, and **Meet → Met**

The irregular past and past participle match. Learn the complete three-form sound set together.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| leave | **left** | **left** | salir / dejar |
| lose | **lost** | **lost** | perder |
| meet | **met** | **met** | conocer / encontrarse |

## Examples

**Me sentí tranquilo cuando conocí a Laura.**<br>
I felt calm when I met Laura.

**He conocido a varios compañeros nuevos.**<br>
I have met several new coworkers.
