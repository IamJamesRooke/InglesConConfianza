# **Dance → Danced → Danced**

The written **-ed** ending is pronounced `/t/`.


When **c/ce** represents final `/s/`, **-ed** is pronounced `/t/`:

**dance → danced**

| Course source | Base | Past and past participle | Spanish bridge |
|---|---|---|---|
| model vocabulary | dance | danced | bailó / bailado |
| core source verb | introduce | introduced | presentó / presentado |
| cognate reinforcement | produce | produced | produjo / producido |
| cognate reinforcement | reduce | reduced | redujo / reducido |

- Past: Laura **introduced** the musicians before they **danced**.
- Past participle: Laura has **introduced** the musicians, and they have **danced**.

Do not pronounce the written **e** as another syllable: dance + `/t/`, introduce + `/t/`.
