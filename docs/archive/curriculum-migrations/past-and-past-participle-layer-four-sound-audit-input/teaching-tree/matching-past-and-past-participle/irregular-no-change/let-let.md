# The **Bet, Set, Let** No-Change Family

The base, past, and past participle use the same form.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| bet | **bet** | **bet** | apostar |
| set | **set** | **set** | poner / establecer |
| let | **let** | **let** | dejar / permitir |
