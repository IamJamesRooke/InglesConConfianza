# The **Said, Bled, Bred, Fed, Fled, Led** Family

The irregular past and past participle match. Learn the complete three-form sound set together.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| say | **said** | **said** | decir |
| bleed | **bled** | **bled** | sangrar |
| breed | **bred** | **bred** | criar |
| feed | **fed** | **fed** | alimentar |
| flee | **fled** | **fled** | huir |
| lead | **led** | **led** | liderar / guiar |

## Examples

**She said the farmer bred and fed the horses before they fled.**

**Dije la respuesta después de leer el mensaje.**<br>
I said the answer after I read the message.
