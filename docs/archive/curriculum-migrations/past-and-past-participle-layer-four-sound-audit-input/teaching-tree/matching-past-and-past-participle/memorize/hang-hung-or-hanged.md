# Meaning-Dependent Matching Forms

The two target forms match, but accepted alternatives, meaning, or register must be learned with the set.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| bid | **bid** | **bid; bade → bidden** | ofrecer una cantidad |
| hang | **hung/hanged** | **hung/hanged** | colgar |
| shine | **shone** | **shone** | brillar |

## Examples

**The bee stung me while I swung the bag that had hung from my shoulder.**
