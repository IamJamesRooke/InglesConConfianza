# Regional and Register-Sensitive Matching Forms

The two target forms match, but accepted alternatives, meaning, or register must be learned with the set.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| clad | **clad/cladded** | **clad/cladded** | vestir / revestir |
| clothe | **clothed/clad** | **clothed/clad** | vestir |
| shit | **shit/shat/shitted** | **shit/shat/shitted** | cagar (vulgar) |
| shoe | **shod/shoed** | **shod/shoed** | calzar / herrar |
| sneak | **sneaked/snuck** | **sneaked/snuck** | escabullirse |
| stave | **staved/stove** | **staved/stove** | evitar (stave off) |
| sweat | **sweated/sweat** | **sweated/sweat** | sudar |
| spit | **spat/spit** | **spat/spit** | escupir |

## Examples

**He snuck out, but he has never sneaked back in unnoticed.**<br>
Se escapó, pero nunca ha vuelto a entrar a escondidas sin que lo noten.

**Sat** and **spat** rhyme.
