# **Slay → Slew → Slain**

These forms are easiest to retain as one complete three-form pattern rather than as two forced derivations.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| slay | **slew** | **slain** | matar |

## Examples

**The character was slain after he had trodden the forbidden path.**<br>
El personaje fue asesinado después de haber pisado el camino prohibido.
