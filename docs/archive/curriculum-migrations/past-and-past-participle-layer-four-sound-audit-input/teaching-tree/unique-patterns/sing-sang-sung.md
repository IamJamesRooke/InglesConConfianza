# The **Sing–Sang–Sung** Family

These forms are easiest to retain as one complete three-form pattern rather than as two forced derivations.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| sing | **sang** | **sung** | cantar |
| ring | **rang** | **rung** | sonar / llamar |
| spring | **sprang/sprung** | **sprung** | brotar / saltar |

## Examples

**Begin** follows the same short **i–a–u** memory rhythm as **sing → sang → sung**, but it has two syllables.

**The bell rang after I had rung it, and everyone sang a song they had sung before.**
