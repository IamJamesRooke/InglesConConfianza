# The **Drink–Drank–Drunk** Family

These forms are easiest to retain as one complete three-form pattern rather than as two forced derivations.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| drink | **drank** | **drunk** | beber |
| shrink | **shrank/shrunk** | **shrunk** | encogerse |
| sink | **sank/sunk** | **sunk** | hundirse |
| stink | **stank/stunk** | **stunk** | apestar |

## Examples

**Corrí en el parque y tomé agua después.**<br>
I ran in the park and drank water afterward.

**I drank before the bottle shrank, the boat sank, and the water stank.**
