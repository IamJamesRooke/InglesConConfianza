# Current and Regional Three-Form Variants

These forms are easiest to retain as one complete three-form pattern rather than as two forced derivations.

## Forms

| Base | Past | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| awake | **awoke/awakened** | **awoken/awakened** | despertar(se) |
| cleave | **cleaved/cleft/clove** | **cleaved/cleft/cloven** | partir / adherirse |
| crow | **crowed/crew** | **crowed** | cantar un gallo / alardear |
| dive | **dived/dove** | **dived** | zambullirse |
| hew | **hewed** | **hewn/hewed** | tallar / cortar |
| prove | **proved** | **proven/proved** | demostrar |
| shave | **shaved** | **shaven/shaved** | afeitar |
| shear | **sheared** | **shorn/sheared** | esquilar |
| sow | **sowed** | **sown/sowed** | sembrar |
| strew | **strewed** | **strewn/strewed** | esparcir |
| thrive | **thrived/throve** | **thrived/thriven** | prosperar |
| tread | **trod** | **trodden/trod** | pisar |

## Examples

**The grass has been mown, and the seeds have been sown.**

**I saw the result that has proven the method works.**
