# **Eat → Ate**

Build the irregular past from the base. The visible or spoken change is the memory bridge.

## Transformation

| Base | Past | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| eat | **ate** | comer |

## Examples

**We ate before our team beat the rival; we have eaten, and they have been beaten.**

**Vine a la oficina, comí con Laura y le di el documento.**<br>
I came to the office, ate with Laura, and gave her the document.
