# **Speak → Spoke**

Build the irregular past from the base. The visible or spoken change is the memory bridge.

## Transformation

| Base | Past | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| speak | **spoke** | hablar |

## Examples

**She spoke after everyone had woken up and discovered what had broken.**

**Hablé con Laura, manejé a la oficina, escribí el mensaje y elegí una fecha.**<br>
I spoke with Laura, drove to the office, wrote the message, and chose a date.
