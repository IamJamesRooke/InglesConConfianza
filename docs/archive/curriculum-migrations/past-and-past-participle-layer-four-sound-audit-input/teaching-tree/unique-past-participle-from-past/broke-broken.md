# The **Spoke–Spoken** Family

Use the irregular past as the immediate bridge to the past participle.

## Transformation

| Past | Past participle | Base verb | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| spoke | **spoken** | speak | hablar |
| broke | **broken** | break | romper |
| woke | **woken** | wake | despertar(se) |

## Examples

**She spoke after everyone had woken up and discovered what had broken.**

**He hablado con Laura y hemos elegido una fecha.**<br>
I have spoken with Laura, and we have chosen a date.
