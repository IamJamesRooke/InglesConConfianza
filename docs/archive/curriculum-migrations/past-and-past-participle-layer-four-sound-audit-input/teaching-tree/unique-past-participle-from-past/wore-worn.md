# The **Bore–Borne, Swore–Sworn, Tore–Torn, Wore–Worn** Family

Use the irregular past as the immediate bridge to the past participle.

## Transformation

| Past | Past participle | Base verb | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| bore | **borne/born** | bear | soportar / dar a luz |
| swore | **sworn** | swear | jurar |
| tore | **torn** | tear | rasgar |
| wore | **worn** | wear | llevar puesto |

## Examples

**She wore the shirt until it had worn thin and torn.**
