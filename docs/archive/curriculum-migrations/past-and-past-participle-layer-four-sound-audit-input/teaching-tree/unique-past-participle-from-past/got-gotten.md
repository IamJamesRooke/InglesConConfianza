# **Got → Gotten** and **Forgot → Forgotten**

Use the irregular past as the immediate bridge to the past participle.

## Transformation

| Past | Past participle | Base verb | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| got | **got/gotten** | get | obtener / ponerse |
| forgot | **forgotten** | forget | olvidar |

## Examples

**He olvidado responder el mensaje.**<br>
I have forgotten to respond to the message.

**Recibí el mensaje cuando llegué a la oficina.**<br>
I got the message when I got to the office.
