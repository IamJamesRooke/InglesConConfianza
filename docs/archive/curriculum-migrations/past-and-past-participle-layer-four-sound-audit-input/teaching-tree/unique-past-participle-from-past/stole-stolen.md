# **Stole → Stolen** and **Wove → Woven**

Use the irregular past as the immediate bridge to the past participle.

## Transformation

| Past | Past participle | Base verb | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| stole | **stolen** | steal | robar |
| wove | **woven** | weave | tejer |

## Examples

**She chose the scarf that had been woven, not the one that had been stolen.**
