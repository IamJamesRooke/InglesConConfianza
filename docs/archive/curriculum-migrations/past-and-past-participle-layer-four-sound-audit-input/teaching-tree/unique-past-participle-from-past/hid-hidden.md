# **Hid → Hidden**

Use the irregular past as the immediate bridge to the past participle.

## Transformation

| Past | Past participle | Base verb | Spanish |
|---|---|---|---|
| hid | **hidden** | hide | esconder |

## Examples

**Hidden** and **forbidden** share the final rhythm, but their past forms do not rhyme.

**I hid the object that had been forbidden and then hidden before.**
