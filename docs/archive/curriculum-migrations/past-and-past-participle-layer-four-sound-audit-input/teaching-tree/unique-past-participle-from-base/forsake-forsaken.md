# **Forsake → Forsaken**

Use the base verb as the immediate bridge to the past participle.

## Transformation

| Base | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| forsake | **forsaken** | abandonar |

## Examples

**They forsook the old plan, and the building has been forsaken for years.**<br>
Abandonaron el plan anterior y el edificio lleva años abandonado.
