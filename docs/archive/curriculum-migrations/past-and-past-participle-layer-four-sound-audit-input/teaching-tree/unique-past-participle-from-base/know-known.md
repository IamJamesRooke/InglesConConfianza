# **Know → Known** and **Show → Shown**

Use the base verb as the immediate bridge to the past participle.

## Transformation

| Base | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| know | **known** | saber / conocer |
| show | **shown/showed** | show |

## Examples

**Siempre he sabido la respuesta.**<br>
I have always known the answer.

**Conozco a la profesora desde hace años.**<br>
I have known the teacher for years.
