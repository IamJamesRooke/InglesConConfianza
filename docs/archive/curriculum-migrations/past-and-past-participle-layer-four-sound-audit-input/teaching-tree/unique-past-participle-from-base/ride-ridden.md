# **Ride → Ridden** and **Stride → Stridden**

Use the base verb as the immediate bridge to the past participle.

## Transformation

| Base | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| ride | **ridden** | montar / ir en |
| stride | **stridden** | caminar a zancadas |

## Examples

**I rode yesterday, and I have ridden here before.**
