# **Give → Given** and **Forgive → Forgiven**

Use the base verb as the immediate bridge to the past participle.

## Transformation

| Base | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| give | **given** | dar |
| forgive | **forgiven** | perdonar |

## Examples

**She gave an apology, and I have forgiven her.**

**He tomado las llaves, las he llevado a la oficina y se las he dado a Laura.**<br>
I have taken the keys, taken them to the office, and given them to Laura.
