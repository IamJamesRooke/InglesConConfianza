# The **Blown, Flown, Grown, Thrown** Family

Use the base verb as the immediate bridge to the past participle.

## Transformation

| Base | Past participle | Spanish |
|---|---|---|
| blow | **blown** | soplar |
| fly | **flown** | volar |
| grow | **grown** | crecer |
| throw | **thrown** | lanzar |

## Examples

**The wind blew after the plane had flown and the trees had grown.**
