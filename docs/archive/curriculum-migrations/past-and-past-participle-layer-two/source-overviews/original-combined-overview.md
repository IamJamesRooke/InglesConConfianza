# Original Combined Past and Past-Participle Overview

This map treats verb forms as transformations of a known base verb.

| Base | Past | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| call | called | called |
| go | went | gone |

For regular verbs, the past and past participle have the same written form. Their sentence jobs differ:

- **Laura called me yesterday.** — past
- **Laura has called me twice.** — past participle after **has**

Irregular verbs may change differently in the two slots: **go → went → gone**.

## Regular pronunciation groups

The pronunciation of regular **-ed** depends on the final sound of the base verb, not simply its final letter.

| Group | Model |
|---|---|
| [final voiced sound + `/d/`](../../regular-verbs/pronounced-d/README.md) | **call → called** |
| [final voiceless sound + `/t/`](../../regular-verbs/pronounced-t/README.md) | **help → helped** |
| [final `/d/` + `/ɪd/`](../../regular-verbs/pronounced-id/after-d/README.md) | **need → needed** |
| [final `/t/` + `/ɪd/`](../../regular-verbs/pronounced-id/after-t/README.md) | **want → wanted** |

## Irregular memory families

[Irregular verbs by memory family](original-complete-family-index.md) keep the base, past, and participle together. Students learn a limited set of sound transformations such as **sing → sang → sung**, then attach the other verbs in the same family instead of memorizing a long alphabetical table.

The [complete coverage index](../complete-three-form-index.md) provides the reference table without controlling the teaching order.
