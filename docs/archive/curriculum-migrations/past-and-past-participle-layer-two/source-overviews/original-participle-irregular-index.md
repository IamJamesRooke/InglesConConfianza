# Irregular Past Participles

Irregular past participles do not follow the regular **-ed** spelling rule.

Some are identical to their irregular simple-past forms:

| Base form | Simple past | Past participle |
|---|---|---|
| tell | **told** | **told** |
| send | **sent** | **sent** |
| buy | **bought** | **bought** |

The foundation includes the irregular participles needed for the closed core-verb curriculum. The advanced curriculum contains the complete irregular-verb inventory.
